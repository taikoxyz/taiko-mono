# 12 — review notes

**Non-normative.** Nothing here can be conformed to or failed against. This is what reading [SPEC.md](../../SPEC.md) section 12 as a limitations register turned up. Each item says what would close it. Only the spec owner decides.

## Normativity boundary

Section 12 is different from the other sections: its 21 items carry IDs of type `limitation` (see [../README.md](../README.md)), so an ID marks "precise and citable", not "binding". Nothing here is a rule and nothing conforms to it. Several items nevertheless contain clauses that read like rules; they are deliberately left without a rule ID, because putting a rule inside a limitation would make the limitation binding.

## Q12-1

**Recovery duration includes five phases and is bounded by none**

**Text.** "Ordinary recovery discards the suffix above c, and its duration includes the DAO authorization delay, finality, reconstruction, signer migration and first settlement. There is no non-governance escape hatch."

**Why it matters.** The item discloses that recovery is the only path back and that its duration is the sum of a DAO authorization (veto plus timelock), L1 finality, EL reconstruction, signer migration and a first landing. No bound is given, and no drill result is cited, so operators and users cannot size an outage. ACCEPTANCE may test recovery, but the item does not say so, and "success is not guaranteed" leaves the failure probability unstated too.

**Would close it.** a measured recovery drill with a recorded duration, or an explicit statement that no duration bound is claimed and what users should assume.

## Q12-2

**Same-round-only slashing plus missed gossip is the whole economic margin, with no stated detection assumption**

**Text.** "Cross-round amnesia is not covered, and gossip can miss evidence".

**Why it matters.** 4.1 slashes only same-round conflicting votes, and this item admits both that cross-round equivocation is out of scope and that evidence can be missed. Together they mean the expected penalty for an equivocating validator is less than "all principal", and the design gives no detection rate or relay assumption to bound the shortfall. A reviewer cannot tell whether this is accepted risk with a stated assumption or an open item.

**Would close it.** state the detection assumption (for example "assume honest peers relay evidence within the withdrawalDelay window") and whether cross-round evidence is permanently out of scope — then either close the item or move it behind that assumption.

## Q12-6

**"Pending acceptance" and "must pass acceptance" have no thresholds in the spec**

**Text.** Item 16: "Overlapping proofs provide short-landing protection, pending acceptance." Item 17: "U=3,600, approximately 4 s blocks, 20 min landings, about 900 unsettled blocks ... are illustrative, not measured, and must pass Etna Acceptance and Launch Checklist."

**Why it matters.** Both items defer to acceptance, but the spec states neither the measurement nor the threshold that would retire them. Item 16 in particular reads as a guarantee ("cannot make an honest submission fail") while being labelled pending. Without a named test, the item stays open indefinitely, and a deployment can claim the protection while never having exercised it.

**Would close it.** name the acceptance test and its pass condition for short-landing protection and for the latency figures (or record the measurement result), so each item can be retired rather than restated.

Raised by: DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash (dantaik)

## Q12-7

**Who decides and discloses an economic-security shortfall**

**Text.** "Operation and recovery may continue with publicly disclosed economic-security shortfalls."

**Why it matters.** The sentence permits continuing with a shortfall as long as it is disclosed, but names no authority, trigger, threshold or channel. "Publicly disclosed" could be a blog post or an on-chain record. Because this sits next to the launch gate in the checklist, the permission can be used to launch while the gate that would have blocked launch is failing. That is a governance decision hidden in a limitation.

**Would close it.** name the authority, the measurement that triggers disclosure and the disclosure channel, or state that this is policy outside the spec and cannot override the launch gate.
