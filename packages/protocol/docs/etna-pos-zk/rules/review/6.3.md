## Q6.3-1

**The 131,072-header bound is stated as a "reference value" but enforced as a rule**

**Text.** "Landers and provers need a resource bound on the reference-header chain (reference value 131,072 headers, about 18 days of L1 blocks at 12 s); a suffix beyond it cannot be landed the ordinary way".

**Why it matters.** "Reference value" reads like a deployable number, while "cannot be landed" reads like a consensus rule. Section 11 has no row for this bound, so its layer (config hash, Inbox immutable, or protocol constant) is unstated, and it is unclear who may change it and what a node does at the boundary. If it is only a sizing assumption, then "cannot be landed" overstates it; if it is a rule, its value and layer belong in section 11 like `U` and `K`.

**Would close it.** add the bound to section 11 in the correct layer, or state that it is a fixed constant and that 131,072 is the value.

## Q6.3-2

**"Signed job" is undefined and unenforceable**

**Text.** "Reference expiry, rule/image changes or a generation transition can still invalidate a job; signed jobs must not be rewritten to bypass those boundaries."

**Why it matters.** 6.3 never defines a job, who signs one, what the signature covers, or who checks it. The next sentence explicitly permits copying a transaction and says copying cannot change proven content, so the boundary between an allowed copy and a forbidden rewrite is not stated. As written the sentence cannot be conformed to or failed against: no observer can tell whether a job was rewritten, because there is no artifact that carries the signature.

**Would close it.** define the signed job and what a rewrite is (and who verifies it), or move the sentence to implementation guidance.

## Q6.3-3

**How the reference-header window is counted after a generation transition**

**Text.** "the outage before R does not count toward this bound (8.4)".

**Why it matters.** 8.4 says every anchor and L1 root used by the new generation's proofs is no earlier than `R`, but it does not restate the 131,072-header bound or say from where the window is counted. Counting from `R`, from the first anchor of the new generation, or globally all give different answers for a very old suffix, and the difference decides whether that suffix is landable after recovery or must be discarded. The parenthetical points at 8.4, but 8.4 does not contain the rule.

**Would close it.** state the origin and length of the window in a new generation, and make either 6.3 or 8.4 the owner of the rule rather than a cross-reference.
