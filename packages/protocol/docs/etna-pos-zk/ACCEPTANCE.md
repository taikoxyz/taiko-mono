# Etna Acceptance and Launch Checklist

This page collects the acceptance, shared vectors and launch gate for [Etna PoS + ZK Design Spec](SPEC.md); the spec body remains authoritative for validity rules.

## 1. Resource and latency acceptance (spec 6.4)

1. Pin K, U, L, n_max, maxBatchBlocks, EL gas/zk-gas limits, maxAnchorAge and software/hardware configuration. Test an anchor spanning U/K single-segment landings, combined with rotation, all 4096 registry slots, maximum-committee commits and an 8,192-byte FI payload. Bound the complete Protobuf block conservatively and demonstrate a legal proposal always fits 22,020,096 bytes and all other limits; optional transactions may be reduced but the anchor witness, committee rotation data and forced-inclusion data may not be omitted. Merely rejecting oversized proposals is insufficient.
2. Measure propagation, decoding, MPT checks, ProcessProposal/FinalizeBlock, memory, signatures and real-guest cycles/latency for legal maximum combinations. Validate ordinary L+2 history and recovery's current origin, R registry and necessary L1 ancestry. Every valid block must be provable by at least one legal two-proof combination, and any grid segment of K consecutive blocks, worst case included, must be provable together in one landing (this sets the value of K); measure any claimed fallback combination too. Publish high percentiles, maxima, hardware and samples. Mock/host results do not replace real guests, and nodes cannot trim validation to local resource budgets.
3. Jointly validate 4 s production, 20 min landing cadence and the capacity budget under sustained target load across multiple complete epochs and settlement cycles, a saturated FI queue, blocks filled with incompressible data, competing/stale landing jobs, retries and L1 finality delays. Record peaks and headroom of height − anchor.lastCheckpoint.height directly. Cover U−1/U/U+1, single/multiple landings, several landings within one L1 block, switch boundaries, cold sync and governance recovery. Normal target scenarios must sustain progress with headroom; over-budget scenarios must pause normally and resume under the rules when the cause clears. Missing the 20 min target does not itself authorize recovery.
4. Budget proving for full B=h−p, not suffix h−c, and check B and the leaf count before allocation. Calldata carries the leaves of every grid point in (p,h), B/K − 1 leaves of 32 bytes each for the chosen K (for 300 blocks, 99 leaves and 3,168 bytes at K = 3, or 49 leaves and 1,568 bytes at K = 6). Add ABI, statement, two proofs and transaction overhead; these are byte counts, not measured gas. Validate both verifiers, grid and leaf checks, landing storage writes within the L1 transaction gas, six-blob and guest limits; the guest authenticates all B+1 cuts and computes cutHash over the grid-point leaves. The worst single grid segment (K maximum-size blocks) must land independently, and target multi-segment batches must pass too. Do not skip grid or leaf checks, remove a proof or raise limits to pass. DAO costs include repeated prefix blobs, submissions that fail because the target was already settled, and retries.
5. Reuse the same immutable two-proof result across p=c, p\<c\<h, c≥h, c at p and at every grid point in (p,h), the same transaction succeeding unchanged after a competitor lands first, repeated frontier advances in one L1 block, epoch boundaries, historical origins, cross-generation rejection, tampering with the leaf at c or other leaves, out-of-range heights/FI cursors and public replay. The settled prefix is never executed or consumed twice, and slot 285 always records actual c→h. During sustained shortest-landing interference (one grid segment each), the same transaction for an honest large batch must still advance to h or observe h already settled; one successful overlap is insufficient. Under 8.3/8.4, test recovery preconditions, replay protection, atomicity and list identity, separately for empty, exclusion, FI-blanking and combined cases.
6. Unified zk-gas acceptance must cover ordinary EVM revert/OOG versus zk discard, uint64 overflow in intrinsic/addition/multiplication, exact-limit versus over-limit cases, direct and nested precompile calls, and atomic rollback of nonce/authorizations/logs/receipts/fees. Resource budgets must include the actual block, FI singleton counterfactual and the entire first crossing atomic operation/precompile, not merely final difficulty. Even discarded unpaid FI trials require real legal two-proof acceptance for the worst valid grid segment, multi-segment batches, repeated expensive FI entries and sustained queue service. A guest-verifiable cost check may end work certain to be discarded only if proved equivalent to the shared execution result, EVM failure precedence and metering; a host estimate or timeout is insufficient. Do not deploy a configuration unable to prove these valid blocks: adjust and pin EL/guest/limits and repeat acceptance, never skip at runtime based on local budgets.

Do not claim deployment readiness or target attainment before acceptance; reassess resources/scheduling and review the spec after failures.

## 2. Recovery acceptance (spec section 8)

Sections 8, 8.2, 8.3, 8.4 and 9.1 require joint implementation and acceptance. Editing the spec, passing mock/host checks or producing blocks after recovery does not replace a first real two-proof landing becoming finalized.

1. Exercise empty-list, offline-member exclusion, FI blanking and combined recovery from stall through DAO scheduling to the first real two-proof landing becoming finalized. Check c, generation, FI head and the Inbox balance, and that confirmed exits keep aging across recovery.
2. Cover c=D(t), S(t), H(t)−1 (c can only be a grid point, so test with values of L that put these heights on grid points), first block at S(t), repeated recovery at one c, admission of newly eligible standby candidates, excluded entries staying out, slot reuse and entryKey mismatch. Nodes/guests must derive identical R snapshots, genesis, powers, schedules and proposers.
3. Cover non-owner calls, execution via both the standard path and an emergency path that skips the veto period (Inbox does not distinguish them), changed lists and replay of executed calls. Test stale generation/c, reverted-operation retries, and failures/reentrancy at every atomic step without partial effects.
4. Cover landing-before-recovery and recovery-before-landing, plus missing checkpoint/origin records. Recovery preserves the settled prefix and never revives old authorizations or old-generation signatures. Test that signers keep HRS state through crashes, restarts and failover without reset or rollback.
5. Rebuild and settle using an independent archive. Publish the L1 ancestry from R to the reference block, R registry witness, necessary ordinary committee history, bundle size, node resources, both guests' cycles/memory/latency and L1 gas under combined measured loads; do not omit authentication. Separately publish the maximum supported outage for proving and landing an old suffix without recovery (spec 6.3).

## 3. Shared vectors (spec appendix A)

Compare config, committee/set/registry encodings, statements, the four-word cutLeaf encoding and cutHash, land/recover/sweepProtocolFees interfaces and storage layouts, including packed-field widths and order, byte for byte. Cover rejection of other formats, canonical ABI/RLP, trailing data, overflow/nonminimal integers, wrong proofs/accounts, vp_unit bound edges (867,361,737 rejected, 867,361,738 accepted), ETH sent to any Inbox entry point other than saveForcedInclusion reverting, and selection ties. Cover envelope, FI eligibility, ExecTxResult, secp256k1eth low-s, nil/different-round, full chain-id/Inbox/generation, uint48, Base64URL, historical evidence and conflicting votes signed under this deployment's chain id before Inbox initialization and submitted afterward, with no Ed25519 or local-signing fallback. Cover all p/c/h relations, h or p not a multiple of K, one leaf too many or too few, h = p+K (no leaves), c at p and at every grid point in (p,h), multiple landings in one block, a leaf at c whose height/EL/Comet/FI differs from the slot 285 record (revert before proof verification), a proof whose own cut at c differs from that record (proof verification fails when the leaf computed from the record is supplied instead), tampered or misordered other leaves (proof verification fails), out-of-range heights/FI cursors, current and recovery-origin records, rejection of an anchor whose lastCheckpoint is above the parent height or not on the local chain, skipping an FI transaction whose maxFeePerGas is below the base fee because its single build is empty, fixed R genesis, direct reselection, rejection of a new generation's first-block anchor earlier than R, a first post-recovery landing whose c anchor is beyond the reference-header bound, p+1 inheriting p's anchor, the S(t) committee-witness root (S(t) with and without an anchor change, a pause before S(t) longer than maxAnchorAge, c = D(t) (with an L that makes it a grid point), a root block equal to the snapshot block, cp(i+1) present and absent, a batch containing S(t)), old-pending cancellation, entryKey reuse, no inherited exit confirmation after slot reuse, two or more released (all-zero) slots not breaking selection or recovery, exitEffectiveL1 = 2\^48 − 1 when no exit is set, slot 284 equal to genesisCutoff at Inbox initialization, verifier-failure rollback re-checking exit confirmations against the restored snapshot (including a confirmation in the same block as the last trusted landing), confirmExit/slashDoubleSign failing before Inbox initialization, Inbox initialization failing when the registry's Inbox address or l2ChainId does not match, recover owner-check, rejection on mismatched expected values, replay and atomic failures, plus writing the current block's EIP-4788 root before any transaction (including retained anchors) and execution with extraData\[0\]=100 paying all base fees and tips to the beneficiary. Acceptance of U=K and of the largest multiple of K not above 65,536, rejection of U=0, of U above 65,536, of U not a multiple of K and of maxBatchBlocks below K, and the resource and real-two-proof acceptance in sections 1–2 are mandatory. The production manifest pins CometBFT, signer and HSM/KMS versions and measures maximum-committee verification, signature tail latency and guest cycles/memory; mock/host results are insufficient. Check the register/slashDoubleSign ABI, which takes no coordinate arguments; real MODEXP/curve decoding and PoP implementations must pass acceptance.

## 4. Launch gate (spec section 10)

- Committee-0 total effStake ≥ manifest S_MIN_TOTAL (chosen from the capital needed to gain more than 1/3 of the voting power and reviewed periodically), with each entry and known operator aggregate below 1/3 power, and the ZkRequiredVerifier in 6.2.
- If operating collateral falls below S_MIN_TOTAL, the DAO supplies treasury TAIKO to multiple independent online operators, each below 1/3, publicly reporting reduced security until replenished. The vp_unit lower bound prevents arithmetic overflow; it neither replaces concentration review nor guarantees DAO reaction time against takeover.
- Pass section 1's resource/latency acceptance and jointly validate two-proof settlement, latest-checkpoint checks, grid cuts and recovery under 8.3/8.4. Do not claim readiness until real guests, recover's execution-time checks, signers, independent archives and first real settlement pass, or advertise unmeasured recovery duration as a service commitment.

## 5. FI backlog example (spec 7.3)

For illustration only, use a fee of 0.01 ETH, threshold = 50 and initial pending = 0, with no L1 head advancement while submitting 900 entries. The total is `0.01 × (900 + 900 × 899 / (2 × 50)) = 89.91 ETH`. Once the entries are due, consuming 900 entries takes about 60 min at a sustained target interval of 4 s without additional stalls. This excludes waiting for entries to become due, L1 finality and settlement pauses and is not a wall-clock guarantee. Production fees, threshold, service rate under sustained attack and honest-user costs still need evaluation; this example is not production anti-backlog acceptance.
