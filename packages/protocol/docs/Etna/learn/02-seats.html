Lesson 2: seats and sortition

Prerequisite: lesson 1. Design pages: Sequencing rights §2, §4 to §6, §8, §9, Roles §1 and §2.

Goal

Know how anyone becomes a sequencer, how the holder of each term is drawn, and what happens when nobody eligible exists.

The idea

Replace the roster with seats: one seat is one unit of bond (B_SEAT = 20,000 TAIKO) in the existing L1 ledger and one slot Seat{owner, activeFrom, activeUntil} in an append-only array of at most 65,535 (identity: a seat's index never moves), while the owner's operator record keeps its strikes, its suspension intervals and a validity interval for each of its keys, so that "was seat i eligible at instant T" is computable from current storage for any T inside the 34-hour role horizon. The sortition domain is the set of seats registered and inside [activeFrom, activeUntil) at the term's start, suspended ones included, and a draw is a rank among them. Anyone registers who posts 1.05 × B_SEAT per seat (the 5 % buffer, 1,000 TAIKO, absorbs 20 timeout debits before seat stake) plus a 14,400-TAIKO landing reserve per seat (two terms of the 7,161-TAIKO worst-case reward exposure, round 6, R6H-3; 35,400 TAIKO of entry capital per seat; while any seat is active the reserve cannot fall below one term per seat, 7,161, without the owner losing its draws, lesson 7), with registerKeys binding a code-less sequencer key that signs keccak(DOMAIN_KEYREG ‖ chainId ‖ owner) as a proof of control (so nobody can bind another party's address first; round 6, judge's note) and a BLS attester key with a proof of possession over (pubkey, owner, chainId), unique across the registry, then register(count) with indices assigned by the contract (recycled holes first, then appends); the effect is future-dated (lesson 1). Each term, a hash walk over those ranks, seeded by randomness fixed an hour ahead, picks a primary holder (view 0) and up to four backups (views 1 to 4). A second walk picks the committee of up to K = 32 seats that will certify the term's blocks. Because the walk is a pure function of the seed, the term number and the set of seats in the domain at the term's start, every node and the inbox compute it identically, whatever registry writes took effect later (invariant I1).

The rules

  • Seed. Every landing, registry write or public poke() mixes block.prevrandao into an accumulator, at most once per L1 block, and emits an event so nodes replay the mix log. The seed of cycle c is keccak(DOMAIN ‖ chainId ‖ seedAcc at T_seed(c) ‖ c) with T_seed(c) = cycleStart(c) − LOOKBACK; the first mixing call at or after T_seed(c) fixes it on-chain, and nodes compute the same value from the log without waiting. It is fresh if some mix happened after cycleStart(c) − 3,660 s, the last instant a registry write could affect the cycle; then no registrant could have known the seed when it last had a chance to change its inputs (claim 5, proven).
  • Walk. idx(t, k) = keccak(seed ‖ 0x01 ‖ t ‖ k) mod E_t for k = 0 to 63, where E_t is the number of seats in the domain at termStart(t) (registered, with activeFrom ≤ termStart(t) < activeUntil) and idx is a rank among them in seat-id order, so exited or never-active seats never dilute the draw (round 3, H5). Skip visited ranks; append each new seat's owner to the holder list H; stop at five distinct owners. Views 0 to 4 are H in order; if fewer than five owners were found, view v beyond |H| is the owner of the seat at rank idx(t, v), so a term always has five views whenever E_t ≥ 1. Suspension is a separate per-owner flag read only after the draw: a suspended seat stays in the domain and keeps its rank, and if it is drawn as a holder its view is void (skipped without a timeout), if as a committee slot the slot is empty (the committee shrinks by one and the quorum scales), never a re-draw that would shift later ranks (round 5, R5S-2: this is what lets a suspension take effect in 22 minutes while entry and exit, the only inputs that move ranks, wait two hours). So the committee's size, and the number of slashable double-signers behind a lock, falls with the suspended share of the domain; an owner that parks seats in a suspension before a seed is fixed shrinks every committee they are drawn into, but its parked seats sit in none, which lowers its own chance of a quorum, and the seats of an owner suspended or lapsed for more than STALE_EXIT = 7 days without reactivating can be exited by anyone with exitStale, two hours later like any exit (round 6, R6-C1). The contract keeps the domain as a persistent (versioned) segment tree over seat ids: every entry or exit creates a new version by path copying, about 16 fresh nodes, tagged with its effective time τ + DELAY_REG, and term t reads the latest version effective at or before its start, so a write that takes effect later changes nothing about term t (round 4, R4H-1: round 3's dense eligible list with swap-and-pop moved positions under in-flight terms and made them unlandable; the round-5 fix pass replaced round 4's mutation ring, whose correction step made reading an old term cost more the more writes had happened since). A seat stays in the domain for at least MIN_TENURE = 7 days after activeFrom (requestExit is accepted only once τ + DELAY_REG ≥ activeFrom + MIN_TENURE, which also keeps the version log sorted), so churn is priced, not capped, and each write, an exit as much as an entry, pays for its own version (about 1.6 M gas of state per seat plus about 60k of execution under EIP-8037, outside the per-transaction gas cap, so register(8) fits in one call; round 6, R6S-4). No rule limits how many writes take effect per minute, so no incumbent can delay an entrant (round-5 fix pass). The walk is done once per term by whoever pins it first, with recordAssignment(t) (callable only once termStart(t) has passed) or the first landing that settles t, reads each drawn owner's attester key valid at the term's start (every key carries a validity interval, so a later rotation changes nothing; round 6, R6S-4), and costs about 1 M gas at 128 seats, about 5 M at 4,096 and about 10 M at the 65,535-seat maximum (assumed until measured; the round-6 simulation measured 5.4 M and 10.1 M, so a 10 M-gas block carries a pin through 4,096 seats and well beyond, and only the maximum, a registry above the token supply, needs a larger block), whatever happened since; the pinner is paid PIN_REWARD from the holder's reserve, ramping from 0 to PIN_MAX = 400 TAIKO over the 30 minutes after landableFrom(t), skipped when the pinner is the holder (round 6, R6H-2: a recordAssignment pin is paid only for a term at or below the first unlanded term, so pinning a backlog during an outage pays nothing beyond its head). The committee walk, keccak(seed ‖ 0x02 ‖ t ‖ m) over at most 512 tries, takes the first 32 distinct domain seats, skipping every seat of the primary holder (by owner) and the backups' drawn seats (by seat only), so a committee never certifies its own primary's blocks but the honest set's seats stay in it even when the honest set is a few large owners (red-team round 2, F1); owner repeats among the rest are allowed, so the committee is stake-weighted and drawn without replacement. Full committees need at least 44 domain seats (K + CAP + V_MAX) with no suspended seat drawn; the published launch condition of eight distinct owners and 72 seats is a heuristic no rule enforces (round 4, R4-C5).
  • Share. An owner with w of A domain seats is drawn primary with probability w/A (if it is suspended, its draw is a void view and moves no other owner's rank); an address holds at most CAP = 8 seats (a cap on addresses, not on entities: lesson 9).
  • Open-empty term. The domain is empty at the term's start, or every holder view the walk draws (views 0 to 4) is void because every drawn owner is suspended or lapsed; the two cases are treated alike (round 6, R6H-2): any address with one seat's bond may sequence with the sentinel view 255, and the first landing on L1 wins. Before the term an owner can void only its own views, so a term opens only when all five drawn owners are suspended (about q5 for a void share q spread over many owners, 0.4 % at a third), and opening it adds sequencing rights without removing any (claim 16, proven).
  • Dead mode. If termOf(now) − lastLandedTerm > DEAD_TERMS = 75, any bonded address may sequence from the last landed block, opening its chain with keccak("ETNA_DEAD" ‖ lastLanded.blockHash ‖ t), and land without a certificate; the first landing on L1 wins. This is the recovery floor that needs no attester, no registration and no DAO: one honest bonded party recovers the chain within about 75 minutes plus proving. It is the one rule that is not a pure function of S(t), it only ever adds authorized sequencers, and like every certificate-free landing it needs two ZK systems (limitation L19).
  • No-committee mode. Fewer than K_MIN_CERT = 8 non-empty committee slots (a seat drawn while its owner is suspended is an empty slot): holders are still drawn, there are no certificates, landings are accepted with an empty certificate, users get the sequenced level only, and the landing right is reserved to the holder until its economic deadline, termEnd + LAND_WINDOW (round 2, S9). Between 8 and 32 slots the committee is smaller and the quorum Q(m) = ⌊2m/3⌋ + 1 scales with it. The published launch condition (8 owners, 72 seats) is meant to keep launch out of this mode; it is a heuristic over a Sybil-able, revocable count that no rule enforces or protects, the DAO arms T0 with a second call armEtna(T0) after reading the domain count, and launch may begin in no-committee mode if the condition fails (round 4, R4-C5).

Worked example

Ten seats: A owns 0-3, B 4-5, C 6, D 7, E 8-9 (the sequencing page's §9 table, recomputed with a reference keccak). For term 604 every seat is in the domain, so rank equals seat id, and the first tries are 6, 3, 2, 9, 2, 1, 0, 0: view 0 is C (seat 6), view 1 is A (seat 3), rank 2 is A again and is skipped, view 2 is E (seat 9), and later tries give view 3 D (seat 7) and view 4 B (seat 4). If C is dark, the committee closes view 0 after 5 seconds (lesson 5), A starts at the lock plus one, and C loses 50 TAIKO. D exits from term 605, so E_t falls to 9 and ranks run over seats 0-6, 8, 9; E is suspended for terms 607-609 after a third strike. In term 607 the ranks 6, 6, 5, 4, 3, 1, 2, 7 are seats 6, 6, 5, 4, 3, 1, 2, 8: C (6), B (5), A (3), then E (8), whose view 3 is void and skipped without any timeout, and view 4 is A (seat 3) again by rank. E's suspension changed neither E_t nor any rank, so no other owner's view moved, whoever submitted the evidence; had E's seat 9 been drawn into the committee, the committee would simply have one seat fewer; and had all five drawn owners been suspended, the term would be open-empty (round 6, R6H-2). Over terms 600-609 the primaries are A 4, B 3, C 3, D 0, E 0, within the noise of ten draws against the seat shares.

Self-check

Why is a consecutive-term cooldown not used?

Two addresses defeat it, and it couples term t to term t−1's evaluation. Consecutive terms by one honest owner cost nothing; consecutive censorship is bounded by forced inclusion, not by rotation.

How many bits of bias can L1 proposers put into a seed?

At most two per proposer of the last m blocks before the seed instant (reveal or skip; include or exclude a mixing transaction), so m colluders choose among at most 4m seeds for one 60-term cycle. The same bound as every RANDAO consumer.

What does an entrant need to obtain its proportional share of terms?

A key registration and a register(count) call on L1 with the bond and reserve; two hours later its seat is in the domain, and it stays there (drawn and bled) for at least a week. No vote, no sponsor, no roster edit exists, no incumbent can delay the write, and a full seat array costs about 2.3 billion TAIKO, more than the token supply.

Challenge this

The seed can be stale if nobody lands or pokes for 41 minutes. Design the cheapest attack that makes it stale on purpose, then compute what it buys (the design says: a few bits of seat placement in a registration already submitted). Is that worth 41 minutes of total censorship of every Etna transaction on L1?
