Lesson 2: seats and sortition

Prerequisite: lesson 1. Design pages: Sequencing rights, Roles.

Goal

Know how anyone becomes a sequencer, how the holder of each term is drawn, and what happens when nobody eligible exists.

The idea

Replace the roster with seats: one seat is one unit of bond (20,000 TAIKO) in the existing L1 ledger and one slot in an append-only array (identity) that records activeFrom, activeUntil and its owner's suspension intervals, so that "was seat i eligible at instant T" is computable from current storage for any T inside the 34-hour role horizon; the sortition domain is the rank among the seats eligible at the term's start. Anyone with the bond plus an 9,200-TAIKO landing reserve per seat (non-withdrawable while any seat is active), a code-less signing key and a BLS attester key (proof of possession over key, owner and chain id; a key binds once) registers by count, with indices assigned by the contract; the effect is future-dated (lesson 1). Each term, a hash walk over the eligible ranks, seeded by randomness fixed an hour ahead, picks a primary holder (view 0) and up to four backups (views 1 to 4). A second walk picks the 32-seat committee that will certify the term's blocks. Because the walk is a pure function of the seed, the term number and the set of seats eligible at the term's start, every node and the inbox compute it identically, whatever registry writes took effect later (invariant I1).

The rules

  • Seed. Every landing, registry write or public poke() mixes block.prevrandao into an accumulator once per L1 block. The seed of cycle c is the accumulator as of cycleStart(c) − LOOKBACK, hashed with the cycle number. It is fresh if some mix happened after the last instant a registry write could affect the cycle; then no registrant could have known the seed when it last had a chance to change its inputs.
  • Walk. idx(t, k) = keccak(seed ‖ 0x01 ‖ t ‖ k) mod E_t for k = 0, 1, 2, …, where E_t is the number of seats in the domain at termStart(t) (registered, with activeFrom ≤ termStart(t) < activeUntil) and idx is a rank among them, so exited or never-active seats never dilute the draw. Suspension is a separate per-owner flag read only after the draw: a suspended seat stays in the domain and keeps its rank, and if it is drawn as a holder its view is void (skipped without a timeout), if as a committee slot the slot is empty (the committee shrinks by one and the quorum scales), never a re-draw that would shift later ranks (round 5, R5S-2: this is what lets a suspension take effect in 22 minutes while entry and exit, the only inputs that move ranks, wait two hours). The contract keeps a Fenwick (prefix-count) tree over "eligible now" and a mutation ring of (effectiveTime, seatId, +1 or −1) entries, applied lazily in (effectiveTime, seatId) order by the first land, registry call or poke after the effective time, and always before that call computes any right. The seat at rank r for term t is found by descending the current tree corrected by the ring's entries with effective time in (termStart(t), now]: seats that left are added back, seats that entered are removed (round 4, R4H-1: round 3's dense eligible list with swap-and-pop moved positions under in-flight terms and made them unlandable). A seat stays eligible for at least MIN_TENURE = 7 days after activeFrom, so the corrections are bounded and priced: an owner with the 8-seat cap produces at most 16 entry or exit mutations a week (each seat costing B_SEAT for at least that week, however requestExit groups its seats), suspensions add nothing to the ring, and at most MUT_PER_SLOT = 2 mutations take effect per 60-s slot (a write whose slot is full waits for the next), so the correction set is at most 4,096 over the 34-hour horizon and the on-chain committee walk stays inside a stated gas bound (about 1.0 M gas with 200 corrections, about 9.2 M at the cap, which still fits one transaction). The walk is done once per term by whoever pins it first, with recordAssignment or the first landing, and that party is paid a pin reward from the holder's reserve, ramping to 400 TAIKO over the 30 minutes after term end (enough for the pin's gas at 100 gwei), so pinning promptly, when the correction set is empty, is the paid path (round 5, R5H-2). Skip visited ranks; the first five distinct owners are views 0 to 4. The committee walk uses a different domain byte and skips every seat of the primary holder (by owner) and the backups' drawn seats (by seat only), so a committee never certifies its own primary's blocks but the honest set's seats stay in it even when the honest set is a few large owners (red-team round 2). Activation needs eight distinct owners and 72 seats for full committees, a heuristic no rule enforces (round 4, R4-C5).
  • Share. An owner with w of A eligible seats is primary with probability w/A; an address holds at most 8 seats (a cap on addresses, not on entities: lesson 9).
  • Open-empty term. No eligible seat found: any address with one seat's bond may sequence with the sentinel view 255.
  • Dead mode. If 75 terms pass without any landing, any bonded address may sequence from the last landed block and land without a certificate; the first landing on L1 wins. This is the recovery floor that needs no attester, no registration and no DAO.
  • No-committee mode. Fewer than K_MIN_CERT = 8 eligible committee seats: holders are still drawn, there are no certificates, and landings are certificate-free but reserved to the holder until its economic deadline. The published launch condition (8 owners, 72 seats) is meant to keep launch out of this mode; it is a heuristic over a Sybil-able, revocable count that no rule enforces or protects, the DAO arms T0 with a second call armEtna(T0) after reading the domain count, and launch may begin in no-committee mode if the condition fails (round 4, R4-C5).

Worked example

Ten seats: A owns 0-3, B 4-5, C 6, D 7, E 8-9. For term 604 the walk yields 5, 3, 4, 5, 1, 4, 9, 8: view 0 is B (seat 5), view 1 is A (seat 3), view 2 is E (seat 9), view 3 is D (seat 7), view 4 is C (seat 6). If B is dark, the committee closes view 0 after 5 seconds (lesson 5) and A starts. If B had been suspended before the term (by evidence, 22 minutes earlier at the least), the draw is identical, view 0 is void and A starts at term start without any timeout; had E's seat 9 been drawn into the committee while E was suspended, the committee would simply have one seat fewer. Over ten terms the primaries land within sampling noise of the seat shares.

Self-check

Why is a consecutive-term cooldown not used?

Two addresses defeat it, and it couples term t to term t−1's evaluation. Consecutive terms by one honest owner cost nothing; consecutive censorship is bounded by forced inclusion, not by rotation.

How many bits of bias can L1 proposers put into a seed?

At most two per proposer of the last m blocks before the seed instant (reveal or skip; include or exclude a mixing transaction), so m colluders choose among at most 4m seeds for one 60-term cycle. The same bound as every RANDAO consumer.

What does an entrant need to obtain its proportional share of terms?

One L1 transaction with the bond and reserve; two hours later its seat is eligible, and it stays eligible (drawn and bled) for at least a week. No vote, no sponsor, no roster edit exists, and a full seat array costs more than the token supply.

Challenge this

The seed can be stale if nobody lands or pokes for 41 minutes. Design the cheapest attack that makes it stale on purpose, then compute what it buys (the design says: a few bits of seat placement in a registration already submitted). Is that worth 41 minutes of total censorship of every Etna transaction on L1?
