Lesson 8: slashing and bonds

Prerequisites: lessons 5 and 7. Design page: Slashing & economics §1 to §4 and §6; constants on the parameters page.

Goal

List every slashable offence with its evidence, and explain why nobody can be slashed by accusation.

The idea

A rule that accepts a claim needs a counter-claim game, a challenger bond and a judge. Etna's rules accept only the accused key's own signatures over conflicting messages, a signed object naming an invalid preimage, a signed L1 reference that is false, or a code-size fact; no rule infers fault from the absence of a landing (invariant I5; round 4). The absence bleed is not a slashing rule: it is applied by a TIMEOUT view change carried in a landing. A challenger controls none of these inputs, so false accusations are impossible rather than merely unprofitable, and challengers need no bond. The offender can always report itself, so the deterrent of a rule is the debit minus the challenger share, min(10 %, 2,000 TAIKO); and because a suspension changes no rank (lesson 2), self-reporting buys no influence over any draw either (round 5, R5S-2).

The slashable identity is the owner, which binds a code-less sequencer key (secp256k1) and an attester key (BLS12-381, with a proof of possession, so a certificate pair cannot frame a stranger). The sequencer key signs keccak(DOMAIN_KEYREG ‖ chainId ‖ owner) at registration, so nobody can bind an address whose key it does not hold (round 6). Each key carries a validity interval, and every committee walk and holder check reads the key valid at termStart(t), so a rotation after a term starts changes nothing for that term (round 6, R6S-4); a retired key stays liable for the 7-day evidence window (§1).

Sizing the bond (§4)

Inputs, all assumed until measured: a term's revenue V_term ≈ 0.01 ETH (the whole base fee, since BASEFEE_SHARING_PCTG = 100 after Proposal0026, plus tips and MEV; corrected after reading PR #22188) and 1 TAIKO ≈ 1.5·10−4 ETH; every TAIKO figure is a formula instantiated with them (limitation L15).

  1. Reverting a locked block needs 12 attester keys plus the leader, all class A: at least 13 × B. With B = 20,000 TAIKO that is 260,000 TAIKO, about 39 ETH at the assumed rate: the published credit bound for "locked", at a full committee. For m non-empty slots it is (2Q(m) − m + 1) × B, and m falls with the suspended share of the domain, since a suspended or lapsed owner's drawn seats are empty slots; nodes publish m beside the label, with the other exceptions to "locked" (a redraw, a private-quorum stall, the fallback residual, no-committee terms, provisional landings; certificate page §4, after the #22188 review, 2026-10-01; round 6, R6-C1). It is a deterrent, not compensation: reorg victims are not identifiable on L1. It holds at every lock (round 5, R5S-1) and against a later committee's fallback, which L1 gates by time, first record and a certificate at the lock (R5S-5) and refuses below the term's own committee's highest recorded certificate, so the gate holds when a blocking minority of that committee withholds its term-end votes (round 6, R6S-3; the residual, an L1-ordering race against the record, is limitation L5).
  2. One uncertified tip block (S1): B minus the challenger cap must exceed what users act on within one second.
  3. Liveness: B ≥ 100 terms of revenue (B_SEAT = 20,000 is 300 × V_term); the absence penalty (MISS_PENALTY = 50 TAIKO) applies only to a leader silent to the whole committee, so an honest online leader is never bled; a fresh owner's 5 % buffer (1,000 TAIKO per seat) absorbs 20 timeouts before seat stake is touched, and 220 bleeds of one seat lie between the buffer and the hard floor. The landing reserve (14,400 TAIKO per seat) covers two terms of the 7,161-TAIKO worst-case reward exposure per seat (60 × 4 + 15 × 1 + 5 × 1,300 + 60 × 0.1 + 400: five paid landings of LANDING_UNIT = 254,000 bytes at every slot time, round 6, R6H-3, and R_BLOB paid for at most 15 blobs per term, so the sum bounds every landing pattern, round 6 fix pass) and is at most B; it is lowered by a self-landing only by the attester share (offset in expectation), is refilled first by every reward the owner earns on other owners' terms (round 5, R5S-4), and must stay above one term per seat for the owner to keep its draws: below seats × 7,161 the owner lapses 22 minutes later for at least 36 h, which is not a slashing (nothing is debited) but a suspension that, like every suspension, moves no rank (round 6, R6S-2); after 7 days lapsed without reactivating, anyone may exit its seats with exitStale (round 6, R6-C1).
  4. Sybil invariance: class A takes the whole ledger and the floor is seats × B, so splitting seats across owners changes nothing; and no owner-keyed progressive rule can tax a Sybil beyond the seat price, which is why none exists.

Self-check

An attester's key is stolen. What is the worst the thief can do?

Equivocate and self-report: the victim loses its whole ledger, the thief nets at most 2,000 TAIKO (the challenger cap, limitation L10).

Why does EIP-8151 matter here and how is it handled?

It restricts ecrecover for accounts with code, so a sequencer could attach code to make its own equivocation unprovable. The evidence function has two branches: if the key has code, slash for that fact (S8); else recover the signatures. Attesters use BLS and never touch ecrecover.

What is the accounting invariant?

TAIKO: the inbox's balance equals the sum of ledger balances plus the pending burn; announcement bonds and landing reserves are ledger entries inside that sum, the reserve being a sub-balance that only reward-type debits and exit can lower and that reward credits refill before the withdrawable balance (round 5); every credit is a same-transaction debit of another entry, except that a self-landing skips both; nothing is minted, and a shortfall lowers a credit, never raises it. ETH: the inbox's balance equals the unconsumed forced-inclusion fees and bonds plus the claimable amounts, that is the includers' fee ledger and the bond and refund of every entry whose status is refundable and not yet claimed, each pulled per entry with withdrawFiRefund(id) (round 6 fix pass); what a stall burns leaves at once to the dead address (§6).

Challenge this

Try to construct evidence that slashes an honest party under any rule in the table. For each attempt, name the input you would need and who controls it. The design's claim is that you will always need the victim's own signature or a quorum object plus an L1 fact. Then try the reserve lapse, which is not a slashing: find a third party that can make an honest, funded owner lose its draws, and check it against the ReserveLow headroom of one full term.
