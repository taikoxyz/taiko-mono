Follow the assumptions

An argument can explain why a bad result is rejected without promising that a good result will arrive.

The problem: rejection and progress are different guarantees

Separate soundness from availability

Questions to ask before reading an argument
QuestionPremise to inspect
Could a false execution result be accepted?Assumed: proof soundness, correct execution rules and authenticated inputs. Soundness concerns what an accepted proof establishes.
Can someone produce and land the required proofs?Assumed: proving availability, retained bytes, a funded participant and L1 inclusion. Availability concerns whether the action can happen.

The mechanism: keep each step's premises

A composition cannot strengthen an assumed step

Assumed, S4-R05. Each step cites its owner and preserves that rule's claim tag. An assumed step makes the composite assumed; an open step leaves the composition open there. A conditional proof lists the premises it consumes. Composition rule.

Assumed, C2-R03. Normal mode requires two distinct ZK leaves accepting the same journal hash. A leaf verifies a ZK proof system; the journal binds the execution claim and its inputs and outputs. A TEE does not count toward that threshold. Verifier rule.

Proven conditionally: if at least one required leaf is sound for the intended execution statement, a false journal cannot pass this normal proof check: it must pass that sound leaf. This uses the cryptographic and correct-guest premises. It proves neither that proofs arrive nor that every other landing check passes. S4-R06.

Check your understanding

A backend is sound but offline. Which premise failed?

Availability failed; soundness need not have failed. The available proof can be correct while the required threshold remains unmet.

Does passing the degradation timer permit a certificate-free sentinel landing with one proof?

No. C2-R11 keeps its normal threshold; C2-R12's degraded route needs a non-empty certificate. The outage remains L19's case.

Can proven local steps turn assumed availability into a proven whole-chain liveness guarantee?

No. S4-R05 retains the weakest step and its actor, data, funding and inclusion premises. Open composition stays open.
