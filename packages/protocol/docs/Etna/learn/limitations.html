The problem: a correct description can still describe a real risk

Approval has a scope

Assumed, C6-R12. A reviewer can approve a paragraph that accurately describes a remaining delay. That does not remove the delay or accept it for the user. A limitation is a residual harm the design does not prevent; its row records its source, owning rule, relevant numbers, disposition and acceptance sentence. Implementation gaps are tracked separately. Vocabulary; acceptance rule.

Proven conditionally: following that rule prevents a transcription approval from silently accepting the described harm. Explicit acceptance of the residual is separate; a request-changes verdict ratifies nothing.
