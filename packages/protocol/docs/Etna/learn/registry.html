The problem: joining now must not rewrite yesterday's rights

Admission without an operator

Assumed, design motivation. Replacing a whitelist requires more than a public registration button. Nodes checking a block before its L1 landing need the same answer about who was eligible at that time. A later registration, exit or key rotation must not silently change that answer. S1 separates the time a write reaches L1 from the time it changes a role. S1-R02.

Assumed, vocabulary. An owner is the address whose keys and bonded seats are recorded; it may be a contract. A seat is a fixed registry index with an owner and an active interval. A term is a period on the protocol's time grid. A seat in that period's domain is in the registered pool; that alone is not a promise of a sequencing turn or user confirmation. S1 vocabulary; holder selection boundary.

The mechanism: public entry, future effect, retained liability

Follow the seat through its lifecycle

Assumed, S1-R04/R05. An entrant proves control of its sequencer key and possession of its attester key, calls deposit to credit its nonreserve balance, moves the required landing reserve with fundReserve, and calls register(count). The contract checks the cap, key bindings, capital, absence of a pending exit and absence of the owner's class-A slash record. D93 also requires that the owner will not be suspended at the new seats' activeFrom, else OwnerSuspended; the boundary cases below explain that check. It assigns seat indices. No operator approves the address. A class-A-slash record remains attached to that owner; a fresh address enters as a new owner with its own capital and fresh keys. Registration; key checks.

Assumed, S1-R06/R07. The owner’s requestExit must satisfy the minimum tenure. D93 exempts exitSlashed and exitStale from that requirement; their registration delay and evidence wait still apply. It takes effect after the registration delay; the seat still counts as held after it leaves the domain. Only finalization after the evidence window releases that seat's share of the withdrawal floor and allows index reuse. Finalization creates no payout entitlement beyond the remaining ledger. A partial exit leaves the remaining seats' keys alone; a terminal exit closes the keys' validity intervals while retaining historical attribution. Tenure; exit and release; duration register.

Assumed, S1-R08/R09 as revised by D93. Anyone may request the specified exits for a class-A-slashed owner or an owner with an open suspension past its stale line. A closed, bounded strike suspension is not stale merely because it lasts longer than a week. Suspension remains a separate eligibility flag; it does not erase the seat from the domain or reshuffle ranks. Permissionless cleanup; the open-run rule below.

D93: check eligibility at entry and let abandoned exits finish

Funding does not override a suspension record

Assumed, accepted mechanism: register(count) checks suspended(owner, now + DELAY_REG), the new seats’ activation time. An open record, a bounded strike interval or a record closed by reactivation can all cause OwnerSuspended. A class-A-slashed owner instead fails OwnerSlashed. For an unslashed owner, funding and reactivate() can close an open record; registration may succeed before that end if its proposed activation is at or after it. S1-R04; D93. This prevents registering fresh seats directly into the existing suspension.

D91/D93: an open suspension has a stale line; paid parking still remains

Follow the open run, not the latest report

Assumed, accepted mechanism: {s, e, eMin, o} records the suspension start, end, earliest permitted end and open-run start. While the record is open, a merged open write sets o := min(o, newStart); a bounded write leaves o unchanged. exitStale requires now > o + STALE_EXIT. The exact equality still refuses the call. A later zero-debit self-report cannot buy another week. S1-R08; S1-R09 store rule; D91.

Assumed, closing a record: for an unslashed owner after a sufficient top-up, reactivate() closes it at max(eMin, now + DELAY_REG). The owner may remain suspended until that future end, but the closed record no longer qualifies for exitStale. A bounded strike suspension is also closed from creation. Its exemption, including long ladder rungs, remains part of the unsigned residual. S1-R09 reactivation; S1-R08. This closes the zero-debit renewal attack; it does not bound how long a funded owner can keep seats ineligible.

D91: early standalone pins earn no reward; late and partial landings differ

The first pin still matters

Assumed, accepted mechanism: a first pin from recordAssignment(t) uses PIN_REWARD_RA, whose ramp begins one LAND_WINDOW after landableFrom(t). A landing that settles part of the term keeps the earlier PIN_REWARD ramp. Either reward is skipped when the pinner is the holder. Thus an early holder self-landing is guaranteed to avoid the pin reward if it is the first landing to reach that term. S1-R14; standalone-pin formula; C2-R16 conditions; D91.

Worked logical schedule: relative to landableFrom(t), a third-party standalone pin at +140 seconds before a holder landing at +150 earns zero. A standalone pin at +2,700 instead earns 400 × 900 / 1,800 = 200 TAIKO. A third party landing the first block at +140 can still earn the earlier landing-pin reward and other landing rewards before the holder lands the rest. These are the distinct cases in C2-E07, not measured profitability.

Open: D91 leaves the pin policy and residual sniping of late self-landers awaiting user sign-off. A partial-landing front-run remains a disclosed proving race; the standalone grace does not eliminate it. C2-R16 residuals. The accepted mechanism is not a promise that every self-landing avoids reserve payments.

Why later writes do not change a past term

Read the version effective at the term's start

Assumed, S1-R02/R10. Entry, exit and key-rotation writes have future effective dates. The domain tree keeps immutable versions tagged by those dates. For a term, a node uses its prescribed older L1 reference and the version effective at the term's start; the inbox later computes from the same historical inputs. A subsequent write creates a later version instead of rewriting the earlier one. Snapshot rule; persistent versions.

Proven conditionally. Under S1's A-T1 reorg premise and its complete flag rules, the node and later landing agree on that term's role inputs. This claim is not “every write has the same delay”: S1-R09 has separate flag dates and a bounded prospective suspension-merge exception. S1-R09's proof, with D45's minimum record closing case (c2), prevents that merge from changing an instant a node has already evaluated. Snapshot argument; flag-store argument; named premises. We leave committee voting and user-confirmation guarantees to their own lessons.

Keep ordinary seat entry separate from optional sentinel bonding

An armed pocket is a different condition

Assumed, D45/D48 as applied in S1. Registering an ordinary seat does not require arming a sentinel pocket. The special bonded-signer paths read the liable owner's armed pocket at their L1 reference, not merely money at its signing key's address; a pending disarm counts as unavailable for new admission. That pocket has separate retention and class-A-debit rules. The no-committee designated holder path instead keeps holder plus non-exclusion admission and ordinary live collectible backing; it does not require that pocket. These conditions cannot be swapped. Bonded-signer definition; separate pocket; D48 holder-path scope.

Assumed, D88 opt-in: armSentinel(amount, selfSign) sets selfSignable only when an arming first raises the pocket to its required threshold and sets sentinelSince, with selfSign = true and a caller then code-less or holding only a 7702 delegation. Later top-ups do not change that bit. At that threshold-crossing arming, selfSign = false leaves the bit clear and adds no sentinel own-address code duty; registered-key duties still apply. Signing as its own liable owner requires the bit. An opted-in owner must avoid non-7702 code until release; the code-duty slash needs no evidence that it signed. S3-R04; S8; signed-term debit and recovery.

Open, constructor exception: a contract calling the threshold-crossing armSentinel(amount, true) from its constructor appears code-less, sets the bit, and becomes slashable once its non-7702 code is deployed. Calling with false leaves the bit clear. D88 explicitly leaves this risk unsigned by the user; accepting the mechanism did not sign it.

Open. This distinction does not complete the recovery or slashing specification. D52 recorded C2/C5/C6 reference-premise obligations and the joint S2/S3 evidence gate. D61 and D63 merge the hatch consumer synchronizations; D62 merges C7 with remaining mode, fork-choice and encoding obligations. These merges do not close the evidence gate or every reference premise. The holder's live backing can be depleted; do not advertise the bonded-path floor for every registered holder. D52 merge limits; S1-R18's explicit bound.

Check your understanding

Does a successful registration immediately let the new seat participate?

Assumed rule: no. Registration creates a pending seat; its role interval starts at its future activeFrom. Relevant keys also need to be valid at the term's start. Under D93, registration itself fails if the owner would still be suspended at that future activation time. Registration; key intervals.

The seat has reached activeUntil. Is its index free, and did the owner receive a transfer?

Assumed rule: neither follows. It is expired but held until the evidence wait and finalization. Finalization makes the index free and lowers the ledger floor; it transfers and mints nothing. D93 permits a third party to finalize qualifying slashed or stale owners’ seats after the same evidence wait. A partial exit leaves current keys in place for remaining seats. S1-R07.

Can an unarmed no-committee holder be described as having the bonded-signer pocket's floor?

Assumed scope: no. D48 keeps that holder path open without arming. Its backing is ordinary live collectible collateral, which may be depleted; the separate bonded-signer condition cannot be inferred from having a seat. S1-R18.
