Lesson 7: deadlines, replacement, and prover economics

Prerequisite: lesson 6. Design page: Propose-with-proof §5 to §7; the constants and their chain on the parameters page; the residual is limitation L20 on the limitations page.

Goal

Understand who pays whom for a landing, what happens when nobody lands, and why a global outage never slashes an honest party.

The idea

There is no prover role. The sequencer whose blocks they are is the reserve bidder: it can land its own blocks at the minimum price (net cost: L1 gas plus the attester share, about 6 TAIKO per full term, offset in expectation by its own attester income, since a self-landing skips the lander's debit and credit; round 5, R5S-4). If it does not, the reward ramps linearly over LAND_WINDOW = 30 minutes to a level that pays a third party's proving and landing even in expensive hours, debited from the holder's landing reserve, LAND_RESERVE = 14,400 TAIKO per seat kept above its seat stake at registration and reactivation (35,400 TAIKO of entry capital per seat in all), never from its seat stake and never touched by liveness penalties (round 4, R4H-3, R4-C4). A reward earned on someone else's term (as lander, attester or pinner) refills the earner's own reserve first, up to 14,400 per seat, so ordinary operation moves only the attester share (round 5, R5S-4). Nothing is voided by time alone. Only a landed replacement or forced batch can void certified blocks, and there is no penalty for a certified view that nobody lands: it is landable by anyone and paid from the reserve.

Round 6 sized the reserve against three adversary schedules. The owner itself: by landing its own late terms with rewardTo a seatless address it controls, it can move reserve money to a withdrawable balance, and no rule can tell that address from a genuine third party; so the guarantee is per seat and read at every draw: an owner whose reserve falls below one term of exposure per seat (seats × 7,161 TAIKO) lapses 22 minutes later (DELAY_S; a lapse, like every suspension, moves no rank, so voiding its own draws after a seed is fixed helps no cartel) for at least SUSPEND(3), 36 h, until it tops up to seats × 14,400 and calls reactivate(); it can still launder the part above the line, but it cannot keep its draws below it (round 6, R6S-2). After STALE_EXIT = 7 days lapsed without reactivating, anyone may exit its seats with exitStale, so parked seats stop diluting committees (round 6, R6-C1). An outage: nobody can land during one, so each ramp runs from the L1 time the term could first land, landableFrom(t) = max(termEnd(t), lastLanded.at), fixed in the term record by the first landing that reaches the term; the first landing after an outage builds on the old lastLanded, so the at most four terms it reaches pay the maximum and every other backlog term ramps from a later landing, whatever the backlog's length (round 6, R6H-2). Short L1 slots: at 2-s slots one L1 block carries only 3 blobs, and the lander picks the range boundaries, so the per-landing reward is counted in units of LANDING_UNIT = 254,000 bytes at every slot time (round 6, R6H-3; below). An owner gets one ReserveLow event per crossing below (seats + 1) × 7,161, one term of headroom above the lapse line (14,322 for one seat, 64,449 for eight), re-armed only when the reserve is back at seats × 14,400; the client tops up to that level automatically, at most seats × 7,239 TAIKO, a sequencer duty (invariant I2).

The rules

  • Reward ramp. Per block, r(τ) from R_BLK_MIN = 0.5 to R_BLK_MAX = 4 TAIKO (marginal proving); R_BLOB = 1 TAIKO per blob for at most 15 blobs of the term, counted in landing order (round 6 fix pass); and per landing r_land(τ) from 0 to R_LAND_MAX = 1,300 TAIKO (a 2 M-gas landing at 100 gwei), all over the 30 minutes after landableFrom(t) (round 6, R6H-2). A term's sequencer blocks are capped at TERM_BYTES_MAX = 5 × 254,000 = 1,270,000 bytes, about 9.8 blobs (V10, which does not count forced-inclusion blocks or forced batches; round 4, R4H-6; round 6, R6H-3), and landing k of a term earns r_land only if k ≤ ⌈bytes landed so far / LANDING_UNIT⌉ (round 4, R4H-7; round 6, R6H-3). Every record fits one blob (V9: at most 130,035 bytes), so a greedily packed landing that is not its term's last carries more than 390,132 − 130,044 = 260,088 bytes of the term even in the three blobs (LANDING_BYTES) one L1 block carries at 2-s slots, and at most about 12,500 bytes of a term go uncounted (framing, FI-block records, view changes), so after k greedy landings bytesLanded exceeds (k − 1) × 254,000 and every greedy landing is paid. A one-block landing earns at most one per-landing reward and a term at the cap at most five (five landings at 2-s slots, two six-blob landings at 12-s slots), so the break-even fee is about 100 gwei per landing whatever the term size and the slot time. The worst case per term is therefore 60 × 4 + 15 × 1 + 5 × 1,300 + 60 × 0.1 (the attester share) + 400 (PIN_MAX, the ramp paid to whoever first pins the term's committee on L1, round 5, R5H-2) = 7,161 TAIKO, a hard bound for every landing pattern since the blob cap (sixty one-block landings would otherwise earn 60 R_BLOB, two such terms 14,412 > 14,400); the reserve holds two of them per seat (14,322 ≤ 14,400 ≤ B_SEAT = 20,000). It is realised only when another owner lands the whole term at the maximum ramp, and that owner's reserve is refilled by it. At the happy-path lag of 150 s a term left to a third party costs its holder about 640 TAIKO in five landings, about 310 for a full term in two six-blob landings and about 200 in one, and a single seat survives one maximum-ramp third-party term (14,400 − 7,161 = 7,239, above its 7,161 line) and lapses at the second unless it tops up. A landing may span four consecutive views to amortize gas; splitting a range earns nothing; a sequencer that knows its prover is down shortcuts the ramp with a signed boost, capped at R_BLK_MAX.
  • Replaceable. From about term end + 35 to 65 minutes without a landing (A1, a predicate on inbox storage and the block timestamp: min(termEnd + 60 min, max(termEnd + 30 min, lastLanded.at + 10 min)) + 5 min), the current holder may fork a REPLACE view from lastLanded; attesters attest it only if the predicate holds at the signed L1 reference in their own L1 view. The REPLACE view change can be recorded on L1 only if its lock equals lastLanded and the window has opened at record time, and the record voids nothing: until the replacement or a forced batch lands, landing the original stays valid, whichever lands first wins, and the loser is invalid and free (round 4, R4H-5, R4-C3: round 3's record closed the original tail, a proof-free void). The record is the VC the fork's landing must match and priority evidence for the forced path, and it is dead once a landing passes its lock. The window did not move when the ramps moved to landability in round 6, so after an outage longer than its cap the backlog is replaceable at recovery (limitation L6, unchanged); the ramp change moves who is paid how much, never what can be voided (R6H-2). Anyone landing a backlog may first post a bonded announceLanding (ANNOUNCE_BOND = 2,000 TAIKO), which defers the window by LAND_CHAIN_GRACE = 10 minutes and is forfeited, 40 % to the eventual replacer or forcer and 60 % burned, if no covering landing follows; it is refused once the head forced-inclusion entry has been due at the T_floor of the first unlanded term for longer than LAND_WINDOW (30 minutes; round 3, C3). The announcement and the recorded REPLACE or RESUME VC share one such deferral per range, from whichever comes first, never two (round 5, R5-C3). A REPLACE or RESUME lock vote whose signed L1 reference names a block at which the predicate did not hold, or a hash that is not canonical, is a class-B offence (S3d).
  • No abandonment rule. Rounds 1 to 3 carried an offence S4a (2,000 TAIKO for a certified view not landed by a hard deadline, with an outage gate and a void waiver); round 4 removed it entirely (R4H-5, R4-C3). A certified view is landable by anyone and paid from the holder's reserve, so a holder cannot abandon a provable view; the only unlandable certified content is a guest-completeness bug, which is a false positive for any abandonment rule and is handled by replacement plus the client blacklist; and no rule may infer fault from the absence of a landing (invariant I5). Residual (limitation L20): no L1 rule can guarantee funds for a term drawn before the reward debits that emptied a reserve; only a deliberate owner reaches it, by directing its own terms' maximum-ramp rewards to a seatless address, which since round 6 costs it its draws 22 minutes after its reserve crosses one term per seat (R6S-2); the unfunded window is the terms drawn in those 22 minutes plus those still unlanded at the lapse, each landed by its holder at gas cost or replaced with no penalty.
  • Single-proof mode: provisional landings. If nothing has landed for DEGRADE_AFTER = 2 h, a landing with a non-empty certificate is accepted with one ZK leaf until the next landing; certificate-free landings (dead mode, no-committee and open-empty terms, forced batches) always need two leaves (limitation L19). Such a landing is provisional (after the #22188 review, 2026-10-01): lastLanded advances, rewards are paid and every deadline runs, but no checkpoint is written for it; its record (the start it extended, its end, journal hash, leaf and time) becomes final, its checkpoint written, when anyone presents a proof of the same journal from the other leaf (confirmProvisional) or DEGRADED_FINALITY = 24 h pass with no conflict (finalizeProvisional, callable by anyone and run by the next landing). Checkpoints are written in order: a two-leaf landing that follows an unfinalized record writes none of its own and is subsumed by the checkpoint written at that record's finalization, the start of the oldest record still unfinalized, at most one window later. A conflict, a valid proof from the other leaf over the same inputs with a different end state, freezes every landing path, the forced batch included, until a DAO upgrade names the sound leaf and rolls lastLanded back to that start, at most a day of landings, whose rewards and fees are not clawed back (limitation L-PF). Safety still rests on T11: the end hash must equal the certified hash, whose header commits the state root 22 attesters executed, so a false root needs one ZK bug, a colluding quorum and, in addition, no honest proof from the other leaf for a day. Elapsed time is not evidence of soundness, which is why the landing is provisional, not why it is safe. Users see the label "landed (provisional)", and bridge messages that need a checkpoint wait up to 24 h.

Self-check

Every prover in the world is down for six hours. Who is slashed?

Nobody. No replacement can land without a proof, so nothing is voided, and no rule penalizes an unlanded view. The backlog lands when proving returns if six hours is inside the landing horizon, as it is at 12-s slots (27 hours) but not at 2-s slots (4.55 hours, where the unlanded ranges are lost instead, still without a slash, below), and it does not drain the honest set: only the at most four terms the first landing after the outage reaches pay the maximum ramp, so an owner at its required level keeps its draws from four seats up, while one with three seats or fewer can still lapse if that first range reaches more of its terms than it has seats (round 6, R6H-2, R6S-2). Had one leaf still worked, landings after the second hour would have been accepted provisionally and checkpointed a day later. Only an outage longer than the landing horizon (the smaller of the 34-hour role horizon and the EIP-2935 window) loses unlanded ranges, without a slash; that horizon is the design's one time-based void of certified blocks (the four-hour forced-inclusion stall of lesson 10 voids queue entries, and the certified blocks that carried an unprovable entry are voided only by the landed replacement or forced batch that ends the stall).

Why are descendants of a replaced range void, and why is that acceptable?

They were built on a state L1 will never hold. It is unavoidable whenever the preconfirmed chain runs ahead of L1; Etna bounds when it happens (only at a landed replacement, or at a DAO resolution of a proof conflict after a provisional landing) and expects it a few times a year.

Why is a holder whose certified view is never landed not penalized?

Because the view is landable by anyone and the holder's reserve pays whoever lands it; a rule that penalized "not landed" would have to infer fault from the absence of a landing, which an outage, a guest bug or a competing replacement can cause without any fault of the holder. That is why the design has no such rule; the replacer earns only the forfeited share of an announcement bond, if any, so replacing is never more attractive than landing the backlog.

Challenge this

After a two-hour outage proving returns. The current holder can either land the backlog (earning the maximum ramp on the terms the first landing after the outage reaches and a fresh ramp on every other term, round 6, R6H-2) or fork a REPLACE view. Model both payoffs including its own upcoming terms, and decide whether a refundable REPLACE bond (listed as not built) is needed. Then price the one-leaf case, in which every landing is provisional for a day.
