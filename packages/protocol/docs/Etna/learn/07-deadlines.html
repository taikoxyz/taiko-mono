Lesson 7: deadlines, replacement, and prover economics

Prerequisite: lesson 6. Design page: Propose-with-proof §5 to §7.

Goal

Understand who pays whom for a landing, what happens when nobody lands, and why a global outage never slashes an honest party.

The idea

There is no prover role. The sequencer whose blocks they are is the reserve bidder: it can land its own blocks at the minimum price (net cost: gas). If it does not, the per-block reward ramps linearly over 30 minutes to a level that pays a third party's proving and landing even in expensive hours; the reward is debited from the sequencer's landing reserve (LAND_RESERVE = 9,200 TAIKO per seat kept above its seat stake at registration, non-withdrawable while any seat is active or pending, and never touched by liveness penalties; round 4, R4H-3, R4-C4), never from its seat stake. When the sequencer lands its own term, the lander's debit and credit are both skipped (only the attester share, about 6 TAIKO per full term, still leaves, and the owner's own attester income offsets it in expectation), and when it earns a reward on someone else's term (as lander, attester or pinner) the credit refills its own reserve first, up to 9,200 per seat, so ordinary operation does not lower the reserve beyond the attester share (round 5, R5S-4: round 4 had credited every reward to the withdrawable balance, and an honest single-seat owner landing its own terms 2 to 3 minutes after term end drained its reserve in about 24 own terms). An owner whose reserve falls below two terms of exposure (9,122 TAIKO) gets one ReserveLow event per crossing (re-armed only when the reserve is back at its required level, so the event carries information); below one term of exposure (4,561 TAIKO) it becomes ineligible two hours later (DELAY_REG, never the short suspension delay) for at least SUSPEND(3), until it tops up (invariant I2); the client tops up automatically, and keeping the reserve funded is listed as a sequencer duty. Nothing is voided by time alone. Only a landed replacement or forced batch can void certified blocks, and there is no penalty for a certified view that nobody lands: it is landable by anyone and paid from the reserve.

The rules

  • Reward ramp. per block, r(τ) from 0.5 to 4 TAIKO (marginal proving), plus 1 TAIKO per blob, plus per landing r_land(τ) from 0 to 1,300 TAIKO (a 2 M-gas landing at 100 gwei), all over the 30 minutes after term end; a term's sequencer blocks are capped at 15 blobs (V10, which does not count forced-inclusion blocks or forced batches; round 4, R4H-6), and landing k of a term earns r_land only if k ≤ ⌈bytes landed so far / (6 × 130,044)⌉, so a term owes at most three per-landing rewards and one-block landings earn at most one (round 4, R4H-7). The worst case per term is therefore 60 × 4 + 15 × 1 + 3 × 1,300 + 60 × 0.1 (the attester share) + 400 (the maximum pin reward to whoever first pins the term's committee on L1, a ramp like the landing reward, round 5, R5H-2, sized in the fix pass) = 4,561 TAIKO, and the reserve holds two of them per seat (9,122 ≤ 9,200); it is realised only when another owner lands the whole term at the maximum ramp, and that owner's reserve is refilled by the same amount. A landing may span four consecutive views to amortize gas; per block and per needed landing, so splitting a range earns nothing; a sequencer can shortcut the ramp with a signed boost.
  • Replaceable. From about term end + 35 to 65 minutes without a landing (a predicate on inbox storage and the block timestamp), the current holder may fork a REPLACE view from lastLanded; attesters attest it only if the predicate holds in their own L1 view. The REPLACE view change can be recorded on L1 only if its lock equals lastLanded and the replaceable window has opened at record time, and the record voids nothing: until the replacement (or a forced batch) lands, landing the original stays valid, whichever lands first wins, and the loser is invalid and free (round 4, R4H-5 and R4-C3: round 3 had made the record itself close the original tail, and one L1 transaction could then void fresh locked blocks). The record is the VC the fork's landing must match and priority evidence for the forced path. Anyone landing a backlog may first post a bonded announceLanding (bond 2,000 TAIKO), which defers the replaceable window by 10 minutes and is forfeited, 40 % to the eventual replacer or forcer and 60 % burned, if no covering landing follows; it is refused once the head forced-inclusion entry has been due for longer than 30 minutes. The announcement and the recorded REPLACE VC share one such deferral per range, from whichever comes first, never two (round 5, R5-C3: counted twice, the forced-inclusion bound came to ten minutes more than the published 112). A REPLACE lock vote naming an L1 block at which the predicate did not hold is a class-B offence (S3d).
  • No abandonment rule. Rounds 1 to 3 carried an offence S4a (2,000 TAIKO for a certified view not landed by a hard deadline, gated on other landings having happened, waived for views voided by someone else's landing); round 4 removed it entirely (R4H-5, R4-C3). A certified view is landable by anyone and paid from the holder's reserve, so a holder cannot abandon a provable view; the only unlandable certified content is a guest-completeness bug, which is a false positive for any abandonment rule and is handled by replacement plus the client blacklist; and no rule may infer fault from the absence of a landing (invariant I5). Residual: no L1 rule can guarantee funds for a term drawn before the reward debits that emptied a reserve; honest operation never empties it (round 5), an owner can only do so on purpose by directing its own terms' maximum-ramp rewards to a seatless address, and such a term is landed by its holder at gas cost or replaced with no penalty.
  • Single-proof mode. If nothing has landed for two hours, one ZK system suffices for certified ranges until the next landing. Safe because the end hash must still equal the certified hash, whose header commits the state root 22 attesters executed; a false root then needs one ZK bug and a colluding quorum.

Self-check

Every prover in the world is down for six hours. Who is slashed?

Nobody. No replacement can land without a proof, so nothing is voided (a recorded REPLACE VC voids nothing by itself), and no rule penalizes an unlanded view. The backlog lands when proving returns. Only an outage longer than the landing horizon (34 hours, or the EIP-2935 window if shorter) loses unlanded ranges, without a slash; that horizon is the design's one time-based void.

Why are descendants of a replaced range void, and why is that acceptable?

They were built on a state L1 will never hold. It is unavoidable whenever the preconfirmed chain runs ahead of L1; Etna bounds when it happens (only at a landed replacement) and expects it a few times a year.

Why is a holder whose certified view is never landed not penalized?

Because the view is landable by anyone and the holder's reserve pays whoever lands it; a rule that penalized "not landed" would have to infer fault from the absence of a landing, which an outage, a guest bug or a competing replacement can cause without any fault of the holder. That is why the design has no such rule; the replacer earns only the forfeited share of an announcement bond, if any, so replacing is never more attractive than landing the backlog.

Challenge this

After a two-hour outage proving returns. The current holder can either land the backlog (earning the maximum per block and per landing) or fork a REPLACE view. Model both payoffs including its own upcoming terms, and decide whether a refundable REPLACE bond (listed as not built) is needed.
