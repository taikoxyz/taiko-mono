Lesson 7: deadlines, replacement, and prover economics

Prerequisite: lesson 6. Design page: Propose-with-proof §5 to §7.

Goal

Understand who pays whom for a landing, what happens when nobody lands, and why a global outage never slashes an honest party.

The idea

There is no prover role. The sequencer whose blocks they are is the reserve bidder: it can land its own blocks at the minimum price (net cost: gas). If it does not, the per-block reward ramps linearly over 30 minutes to a level that pays a third party's proving and landing even in expensive hours; the reward is debited from the sequencer's bond. Nothing is voided by time alone. Only a landed replacement can void certified blocks, and the penalty for abandoning a certified view is payable only when other landings prove the chain was live.

The rules

  • Reward ramp. r(τ) from 0.5 TAIKO per block at term end to 4 TAIKO per block at term end + 30 min; per block, so splitting a range earns nothing; a sequencer can shortcut the ramp with a signed boost.
  • Replaceable. From about term end + 35 to 65 minutes without a landing (a predicate on inbox storage and the block timestamp), the current holder may fork a REPLACE view from lastLanded; attesters attest it only if the predicate holds in their own L1 view. Whichever lands first wins; the loser is invalid and free under the trailing-VERIFY shape.
  • Abandonment (S4a). A certified view not landed by term end + 60 min costs its holder 2,000 TAIKO (10 % of a seat; no strike), but only if some term landed within the hour before that deadline (the outage gate). Ten percent to the reporter, 40 % escrowed to the next ordinary lander, the rest burned.
  • Single-proof mode. If nothing has landed for two hours, one ZK system suffices for certified ranges until the next landing. Safe because the end hash must still equal the certified hash, whose header commits the state root 22 attesters executed; a false root then needs one ZK bug and a colluding quorum.

Self-check

Every prover in the world is down for six hours. Who is slashed?

Nobody. No replacement can land without a proof, so nothing is voided; the outage gate sees no landing in the hour before any deadline, so no abandonment can be reported. The backlog lands when proving returns.

Why are descendants of a replaced range void, and why is that acceptable?

They were built on a state L1 will never hold. It is unavoidable whenever the preconfirmed chain runs ahead of L1; Etna bounds when it happens (only at a landed replacement) and expects it a few times a year.

Why does the replacer get no share of the abandonment penalty?

A share would make replacing more attractive than landing the backlog after an outage. The escrowed 40 % goes to the next ordinary landing only.

Challenge this

After a two-hour outage proving returns. The current holder can either land the backlog (earning 4 TAIKO per block) or fork a REPLACE view. Model both payoffs including its own upcoming terms, and decide whether a refundable REPLACE bond (listed as not built) is needed.
