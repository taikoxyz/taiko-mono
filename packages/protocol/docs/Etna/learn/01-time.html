Lesson 1: time without slots

Prerequisite: lesson 0. Design page: Sequencing rights §1 to §3.

Goal

Understand the four time rules everything else rests on: terms, cycles, the snapshot, and future-dated effect. After this lesson you can answer "which L1 state decides who sequences at time t, and why do all nodes agree?"

The idea

Ethereum's slots and epochs are consensus-layer concepts that change length across forks and are invisible to contracts except through the beacon-root precompile. Etna uses only two clocks a contract can read: block.timestamp (seconds) and block.number (L1 blocks). It cuts wall time into terms of 60 seconds on a grid anchored at the activation timestamp T0. A term's holder is computed from L1 state that is already old enough to be final, so it never changes under a reorg, and every registry change is dated far enough into the future that the "old" state and the "current" state give the same answer.

The rules

  1. Term. termOf(τ) = (τ − T0) / 60; termStart(t) = T0 + 60t. The grid's phase relative to L1 slots is irrelevant.
  2. Cycle. 60 terms (one hour). One randomness seed is fixed per cycle.
  3. Snapshot S(t). The highest L1 block with timestamp ≤ termStart(t) − LOOKBACK, LOOKBACK = 1,200 s. At term start the inputs are 20 minutes old, beyond L1 finality at 12-second slots (12.8 min) and by a wider margin at shorter slots.
  4. Rule D (future-dated effect). A registry write executed at L1 time τ changes eligibility only for evaluation instants T ≥ τ + DELAY, with every DELAY ≥ LOOKBACK + 2·TERM = 1,320 s. A term evaluates eligibility at the single instant termStart(t). Two delays exist: DELAY_REG = 7,200 s for entry and exit, the only inputs that change E_t (the domain count: registered, active seats, suspended ones included) or a seat's rank, and for a reserve lapse; DELAY_S = 1,320 s for suspensions from class A or B evidence. A suspension of either kind changes no rank: the suspended owner's seats stay in the sortition domain, and a seat drawn while suspended is simply a void view (as a holder) or an empty committee slot. That is why the short delay is safe even though the owner can suspend itself (it can be its own challenger): a self-inflicted suspension costs a seat or the ledger and moves nobody's draw (round 4, R4-C2, restated in round 5, R5S-2: every input that changes E_t or a rank is DELAY_REG-dated; a suspension changes neither).

Why it works

Take a write in a block after S(t). Its timestamp τ exceeds termStart(t) − LOOKBACK, so τ + DELAY ≥ τ + LOOKBACK + 2·TERM > termStart(t) + TERM. It cannot touch term t. A node replaying events up to S(t) and the inbox at landing therefore evaluate term t on the same inputs, provided the inbox can still answer "which seats were eligible at termStart(t)" after later writes took effect: it keeps every version of a persistent tree over the domain, each entry or exit adding a version tagged with its effective time and leaving older versions untouched, and reads the version effective at termStart(t) (lesson 2; round 4, R4H-1, and the round-5 fix pass). No proofs are needed. This is the design's claim 2 on the sequencing page, tagged proven.

Self-check

A registrant sends register at 10:00:00. Its seat is eligible from what time, and which is the first term that can draw it?

DELAY_REG is 7,200 s, so eligibility starts at 12:00:00; the first term whose start is at or after 12:00:00 can draw it.

L1 reorgs 5 blocks deep at 12-second slots. Does any term's holder change?

No. Every input to a term is at least 1,200 s (100 blocks) old at its start. Only a reorg deeper than 20 minutes could change an assignment, which is outside the assumption T1.

Why is a suspension delayed by 1,320 s rather than applied immediately, and why may it be shorter than the 7,200 s of a registration?

An immediate suspension would change whether a term's drawn holder may serve, for a term nodes have already computed from S(t). The 1,320-s delay is the smallest that keeps the snapshot and the live state identical for every term. It may be shorter than DELAY_REG because a suspension changes no rank: E_t and every seat's rank are the same with or without it, so the seed of a cycle cannot be ground through it, whereas a registration or exit moves ranks and must be dated past the next cycle's seed instant (round 5, R5S-2).

Challenge this

Rule D makes every penalty bite 22 minutes late. Find a scenario where a misbehaving holder exploits those 22 minutes, and cost it. Then check lesson 5: withheld blocks are worthless within 5 seconds regardless of eligibility, so what exactly does the delay let an attacker keep?
