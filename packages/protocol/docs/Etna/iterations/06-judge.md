### Assumptions and checks actually performed

**Cryptographic and execution assumptions:** collision resistance, canonical signature validation, KZG binding, at least one correctly enforced complete relation in the required proof conjunction, correct common specification, faithful parser/accounting/engine implementation and intact legacy authenticated state. A shared relation error defeats both proof backends; the document does not claim cryptographic diversity eliminates it.

**Network and data assumptions:** Ethereum execution/consensus/finality; reorg-aware clients; selected data retrieval in 60 seconds; both proofs within 180 seconds for target workloads; valid funded inclusion within 120 seconds; measured execution/propagation compatible with soft issuance; stage retention covering 900 seconds plus retrieval; sufficient long-term state/DA archives. These are conditional liveness premises, not measured guarantees. Publication deadlines penalize objective failure and can penalize honest intent during censorship, partitions or fee spikes.

**Economic assumptions:** a funded participant can pay computation, both DA publications, gas and positive rent for target landing; bonded services can finance extra fragment calldata; reporters have a reason or subsidy to report; the capture theorem additionally needs a ready and willing independent competitor by D<900 and bounded net gain relative to rent. ETH/TAIKO conversion is never assumed automatic. With 100% basefee to a controlled coinbase, self-paid L2 basefee is an internal transfer.

**Upgrade/migration assumptions:** DAO performs only the authorized installation/upgrades and does not install malicious code; old storage/escrow/caches are sound; migration state and proof artifacts are authenticated; retained system account and actual activation witness fit the selected profile. Once installed and activated correctly, continued DAO activity is unnecessary. No claim covers arbitrary incompatible future Ethereum forks or malicious upgrades.

**Executed checks:** read all normative inputs and both fixtures; recomputed file byte counts/line counts/SHA-256 from the pinned Git object; traced state/commitment dependency direction, authenticating call boundaries and deadline order; inspected the source EIP-4396 formula and directly verified the refreshed 100% fee constant. ### Deduplicated finding J06-01 — zero-rent capture and economic concentration

- **Sources:** reviewer `06-C-1` (`06-c.md:17–31`), judge IC-01, and the same surviving limits independently recorded by A (`06-a.md:129–131,150,173`) and B (`06-b.md:67,126,129,148`). These are one economic issue, not four findings.
- **Original severity:** Medium (C; judge IC-01).
- **Adjudicated severity:** **Medium**. It is a feasible concentration/participation risk, not a proof, custody, mandatory-FIFO or permissionless-admission exploit.
- **Requirements:** primarily R6; R3/R4 service economics and R7 affordable cadence are affected. R1's objective access rule is not disproved.
- **Disposition:** **accepted-with-rationale for the bounded design claim; economic launch validation remains open**. It is neither fixed nor rejected as infeasible.
- **Exact snapshot evidence:** `design/index.html:158–161` ([rent](../design/index.html#rent)); `design/roles.html:275–282` ([competition](../design/roles.html#competition)); `design/arguments.html:25–27` ([assumptions](../design/arguments.html#assumptions)) and `design/arguments.html:118–121` ([l4](../design/arguments.html#l4)). All at `34f4e8a1cb47e34451b66fa518d5819693833fc0`.

**Feasibility adjudication.** C's schedule survives the existing rules. For an unchanged head accepted at T, choose a real origin near T+360, publish a bounded consistent context at T+420, mature it at T+780, and land at T+900. At landing, origin age is 540 seconds, stage age is 480, and rent is zero. The block timestamp can be chosen consistently with the selected origin and parent; all mandatory parent-frozen requests are still processed. The actor can prepare this schedule again for each accepted parent. It need not carry an origin or stage from T to expiry. If an outsider cannot afford a positive quote, a possible L1 ordering lets the actor win the zero-price race and make the outsider's distinct staged proof stale. This establishes possibility, not a guarantee that the actor wins every race. Real proof, publication, gas, builder and forced-execution costs remain; profitability requires sufficient external value. The illustrative timestamps/rent/freshness inequalities were independently recomputed in memory during adjudication.

**Why Medium is retained.** A-FUND for some participant can hold while no independent competitor is willing or able to pay before the floor. Normal cryptography, Ethereum validity and DA can all hold. The zero-rent fallback proves that an unchanged head has an open recovery path; it does not guarantee that a small entrant wins against a continuing incumbent. Faster repeated capture is also rational when external consolidated gain exceeds the rent. Fee, order-flow and service concentration can therefore persist, with stale-work costs for rivals and reduced affordable throughput. Calling the theorem conditional does not remove those effects, so C's material economic risk is retained.

**Why the current design R6 gate nevertheless passes.** The hard R6 table requires objective penalties and *concrete anti-monopoly economics*, including mechanism rationale; it does not require every possible owner concentration to be impossible or every canonical win to incur strictly positive protocol rent. This snapshot supplies: (1) a mandatory owner-independent charge on every early winner; (2) a once-per-accepted-head reset and nonrecoverable sink; (3) the explicit bound `Σcost ≥ Σq(Dᵢ)` when a ready funded rival imposes D<900; (4) the consolidated gain threshold for negative profit; (5) a separately specified public FIFO escape; and (6) explicit cost/entry tradeoffs against perpetual positive fees, exclusive leases, lotteries and Sybil-sensitive caps (`roles.html:275–289`, [competition](../design/roles.html#competition)). Those are testable mechanisms and algebra, not merely disclaimers or voluntary collateral that an ordinary winner can avoid. The positive-cost theorem does not derive its rival from permissionlessness, and no such derivation is accepted here.

I accept the zero floor as a deliberate eventual-entry tradeoff for this design deliverable. I **do not** accept a claim of measured market diversity, affordable continuous cadence, an unconditional positive capture cost, or a sustainable equilibrium. Before launch, a complete cost/revenue model must demonstrate the claimed entrant schedule and funding, measure D with header acquisition included, and compare relevant net capture value with q(D); otherwise the positive deterrence claim has no demonstrated application to that launch environment. If the intended R6 were instead strengthened to require positive additional cost even without a ready early rival, this zero-floor design would fail that stronger gate and need a reviewed economic change. That is a boundary of this adjudication, not a silent rewrite of C's finding.
