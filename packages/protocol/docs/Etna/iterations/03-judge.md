## 3. Confirmed and downgraded findings: trace, gap, required revision

Grouped by the mechanism they touch, so one revision closes each group.

### Group A: the slashing rules accept honest signatures made under design-mandated behaviour (R3-S1, R3-S2, R3-S4, R3-S5)

These four falsify the slashing page's "proven" claim that no false accusation exists (`slashing.html:52` "Why false accusations are impossible", `:161` claim 1) and the certificate page's TH8 line "the accused always had a way out" (`preconf.html:127`). R6 and P6 are hard gates.

**R3-S1 (High). S3d verifies a reference the accused never signed.** `preconf.html:26` defines the timeout preimage exhaustively as keccak(DOMAIN_TIMEOUT ‖ chainId ‖ t ‖ v ‖ vcHash ‖ kind ‖ lockHeight ‖ lockPhHash) and says a REPLACE timeout "additionally names the L1 reference (number, hash)", i.e. outside the signed bytes; `interfaces.html:37` has `struct TimeoutMsg { ...; Replace l1Ref; uint8 attesterIdx; bytes sig; }` with l1Ref as a free field, and `ViewChange` (`interfaces.html:22`, `landing.html:105`) carries no reference at all. S3d (`slashing.html:45`; `slashFalseReplacement(TimeoutMsg _to, bytes _pubkey)` at `slashing.html:86`) verifies one signature over the reference-free preimage, then "recompute[s] replaceableFrom as of that L1 block" at the caller-supplied reference. Trace: after any genuine replacement (a stall past replaceableFrom, then the holder's REPLACE view), an outsider takes the 22+ honest REPLACE timeouts from the views topic (`ViewsByTerm` returns them, `interfaces.html:165`), rewrites l1Ref to a canonical block with timestamp ≤ replaceableFrom, and calls S3d per key: class B, B_SEAT = 20,000 TAIKO per key, three strikes (36-h suspension), 2,000 TAIKO to the challenger per key, dedup keyed on (rule, key, term, height) so one hit per key per event. 22 keys: 440,000 TAIKO lost by the committee that performed the design's own replacement, for about 22 × 200k gas. Gap: an "L1 fact" rule whose L1 input is not bound to the accused's signature is an accusation object. Severity High, not Critical: the precondition (a real replacement event) is not attacker-triggerable at will and the loss is bond griefing, not a safety failure; within the precondition it is a complete, cheap, repeatable wipe of the honest supermajority. Revision: sign l1RefNumber ‖ l1RefHash inside the REPLACE and RESUME preimage (and the forced marker for RESUME); add the reference to `ViewChange` so L9 and recordViewChange see what was signed; require the reference to be at least ANCHOR_MIN_AGE old and canonical (blockhash / EIP-2935) at attestation and at S3d; S3d reverts on a non-canonical hash. Update `preconf.html:26`, `interfaces.html:22/37`, `landing.html:105`, `slashing.html:45`.

**R3-S2 (High). The lock reset turns the next mandatory timeout into a lock lie.** `preconf.html:90` (§6 "Lock reset"): after a forced or replacement landing "every certificate above lastLanded is void for it and every attester's lock resets to lastLanded". The attester machine (`preconf.html:98-100`) still says "ATTESTING(v) --timer > TIMEOUT or now ≥ termEnd+END_GRACE--> TIMING_OUT(v): sign TO_i", kind TIMEOUT within 5 s (no new certificate can restart the timer because every certificate above lastLanded is void) or the mandatory TERM_END at term end (`preconf.html:79`). The attester is still in view (t_c, 0, O); no RESET state exists; so it signs a timeout under O with lock = lastLanded. S3b (`slashing.html:44`) accepts "a timeout or VC signature by key i of kind TIMEOUT, TERM_END or FALLBACK with lock L, plus an attestation by i under the same opening object (equal t, v, vcHash) on a header whose carried certificate is above L", verified by "opening objects equal; kind not REPLACE or RESUME; two single verifications", with no forced-ring or replacement-head check (the void waiver exists only on S4a). Every attester that attested the stalled chain under O has such an attestation. Trace preconditions are cheap and attacker-timed: a 0.001 ETH calldata FI entry, a stall to replaceableFrom (A1 gives about 2,220 s after the last landing), a one-block forced batch with two proofs landed mid-term. Each `slashLockLie` is class A (whole ledger, seats ineligible, exit only), 2,000 TAIKO to the challenger per owner. The honest attester has no way out: refusing TIMEOUT still leaves TERM_END; signing the pre-reset lock instead closes the term on a void, unlandable parent. The round-2 fix (R2-F3) exempted only the REPLACE and RESUME kinds, so this is a residual, not a duplicate. High rather than Critical: no bridged asset or state root is at risk and the chain does not halt permanently; the harm is honest bonds and eligibility per stall. Revision: after a reset the only timeout kind signable under the old opening object is RESUME (also at term end, where the RESUME VC doubles as the handoff); S3b ignores timeouts whose (lockHeight, lockPhHash) equals a forced-ring entry or a replacement landing's head; the attester machine gets an explicit RESET state. Update `preconf.html:79/90/98-100`, `slashing.html:44`.

**R3-S4 (High). A late original landing voids a certified REPLACE fork without the void waiver.** Landing A2 (`landing.html:82`) lets the current holder reopen its view with a REPLACE VC whenever A1 holds; attesters attest it; fork choice (`preconf.html:57`, highest (t, v, height)) makes every later honest term extend the fork; replaceableFrom stays passed (the first unlanded term is unchanged) and the TH13 row (`landing.html:180`) tells holders that above the break-even fee "landing waits or the holder pays, and the outage gate ensures the wait costs no bond". A3 (`landing.html:83`) explicitly keeps the original landable: "Until a replacement lands, landing the original stays valid and pays the ramp ... If the original lands after a REPLACE view was certified, that view is unlandable and the holder resumes ... with a RESUME VC". The original landing has replaced = false and writes no forced-ring entry, so it is neither a "forced or replacement landing". After the RESUME chain lands terms above the fork, every fork term whose hard deadline is still ahead satisfies all four S4a conjuncts (`landing.html:84`, `slashing.html:47`): a certificate of (t_f, v) above what landed for t_f (nothing), DEADLINE_HARD passed, bit clear, outage gate passes (a strictly later term landed within the hour before the hard deadline). The void waiver is keyed only on "voided by a forced or replacement landing"; the only concrete waiver mechanism is the forced-ring check (`forced-inclusion.html` §7, `isSupersededByForced`), and the inbox has no record of the A3 void at all. Judge's arithmetic: with the fork REPLACE at E + 2,100 and the RESUME landing at about E + 9,360, fork terms k with 96 ≤ 37 + k ≤ 156 pass the gate, about 60 terms at 2,000 TAIKO each; the reporter clause "superseded by an ordinary landing of a later term" is affirmatively satisfied, so 10 % goes to the reporter and 40 % to the next ordinary lander, which the reporter can be. The fork's originating REPLACE view is not reportable (the original's landed height for that term is higher), so only the honest followers are hit. Not a duplicate: R2-S3's fix covers the forced/REPLACE-wins branch and leaves this mirror branch uncovered; the FI page's own intent "a resumer whose fresh certified chain lost the race is not penalized" (`forced-inclusion.html` §7) is not implemented for this race. Caveats: the fork reorg itself is inside accepted L6/L14 (every fork block is labelled "replaceable"), so the new harm is the penalty and its diversion; losses scale with fork length. Even without a malicious lander any REPLACE-vs-original race the original wins penalizes every honest fork holder, so the false positive is structural. Revision: waive S4a for any view whose blocks became unlandable through a landing not by its own holder (record on L1 which opening object each ordinary landing supersedes, or waive for any view whose opening-object lineage descends from a REPLACE/RESUME VC recorded on L1 and whose certified heights were voided by another party's landing); or end the original's landability ABANDON_GRACE after a REPLACE VC is recorded on L1 (which is what "strict priority of a late original", `landing.html:155`, was meant to bound). Restate the "A chain-wide outage slashes nobody" and "No false accusation exists" claims (`slashing.html:161/169`, `landing.html:169`) and the TH13 "wait costs no bond" line.

**R3-S5 (Medium). The VC-formation sentence contradicts S3c.** `preconf.html:82`: attester j signs a VC iff, among other things, "it signed no other lock for (t, v)", and in the same paragraph "A higher honest lock arrives as a timeout and the proposer re-proposes; with ≥ Q honest online attesters the VC forms at the honest maximum in one extra round trip". The VC is "Q timeouts aggregated over one lock" (`glossary.html`, view change row), the TimeoutMsg carries the lock, and S3c (`slashing.html:46`) slashes class A any key with two timeouts for one (t, v, vcHash, kind) at different locks. Re-proposal at the honest maximum needs the lower-lock signers to sign a second lock, which S3c forbids. The split is reachable without any colluding attester: a leader that stays silent for about 4.6 s and then releases block n makes the 5-s timers straddle C(n)'s assembly (about 0.3 s of gossip spread plus up to 1 s of CLOCK_SKEW), so a split such as 15 at lock n−1 and 17 at lock n is ordinary and neither side reaches Q = 22. Under the re-sign reading about 15 honest keys are class-A slashable (same shape as R1-F8 / R2-F3); under the no-re-sign reading no TIMEOUT VC can form for the rest of the term (all attesters are TIMING_OUT and "attest nothing in v"), and the term closes only by a fresh TERM_END-kind VC at termEnd + END_GRACE (kind is in the S3c key, so that one is signable) or the next committee's FALLBACK. Corrections to the attacker's trace: the leader's cost is 0 (MISS applies only to a landed TIMEOUT-kind VC), the harm is confined to the attacker's own term, and the same straddle arises from an honest 5-s hiccup, so the liveness sentence is false as written regardless of intent. Medium (bounded griefing / per-term degraded service; not High because it is confined to one term and the theft branch depends on an implementer choosing the sentence that contradicts S3c). Revision: either sign the VC vote in a domain distinct from the silence timeout (a timeout carries no lock; the VC message carries lock*; S3c applies to VC messages only; S3b compares the VC lock with attestations), or define a strictly higher lock under the same opening object as a permitted monotone upgrade (S3c = two timeouts whose locks are not ancestor-ordered, or two locks at equal height with different phHash). Update `preconf.html:82`, `slashing.html:46`, the W1 argument (`preconf.html:106`, `arguments.html:20`).

### Group B: the replacement mechanism's L1 record (R3-S6)

**R3-S6 (Medium). recordViewChange collides on (termId, view).** `landing.html:118` and `interfaces.html:94`: "recordViewChange(ViewChange) // anyone; first per (termId, view) wins"; `ViewChange` (`interfaces.html:22`) has no opening-object field. Landing A2 (`landing.html:82`) has the holder "reopen its current view (same term and view number)" with a REPLACE VC, and L4 (`landing.html:33`) requires every VC in a landed chain to be "equal to the view change first recorded on L1 for that (t, v) if one exists". So the REPLACE (or RESUME) VC, the fork's later TERM_END/TIMEOUT VC for the same (t, v), and any VC of the original tail for that (t, v) fight for one slot. The attacker's trace holds (record the fork's TERM_END VC first; the fork's opening landing then fails L4 with ViewChangeMismatch), and it is worse without an attacker: the certificate page (`preconf.html:87`) has anyone record VCs "within seconds", so the REPLACE VC is normally recorded first and the fork's own TERM_END VC for that (t, v) then cannot match, so the term following any replacement cannot be opened by an ordinary VC chain; the only exits are the original tail (A3, if provable) or the sentinel/dead-mode path after 75 minutes, whose segments open with keccak("ETNA_DEAD" ‖ lastLanded.blockHash ‖ t) and need no VC chain. Not a duplicate: R1-F8/R2-S1 put vcHash into Attestation, TimeoutMsg and Certificate, not into ViewChange or the record key. Medium: pure liveness, gas-only, bounded by dead mode, no theft; but it defeats the replacement mechanism itself and is repeatable per stall. Revision: add the opening object to `ViewChange` (the vcHash of the view being closed, or for REPLACE/RESUME the object it opens) and key the record and the L4 match on (termId, view, openingObject) (or at least (termId, view, kind)); state that REPLACE and RESUME VCs are recorded under the object they open. Update `preconf.html:87`, `landing.html:33/105/118`, `interfaces.html:22/94`.

### Group C: forced inclusion and landing economics (R3H-1, R3H-2, R3H-3)

**R3H-1 (High). FI_SKIP bounds one entry, not the stream.** `forced-inclusion.html` §2 (line 13 of the text extract; source `forced-inclusion.html:~40`): skipped(i, T) holds for the head entry iff it is due at T and T − lastLanded.anchorTipTimestamp ≥ FI_SKIP = 14,400 s; every landing of every kind (ordinary L8, forced batch rule 4, dead mode obeying L8, T_floor = max(lastLanded.anchorTipTimestamp, termStart − ANCHOR_MAX_AGE − 1)) must consume a due head entry, and every landing resets lastLanded.anchorTipTimestamp. Under the design's own stated residual (a decodable entry no guest can prove within FI_ZK_GAS_LIMIT: `forced-inclusion.html:152` "Unprovable head entry (round 1, F4): bounded to four hours ... cost to the attacker 0.001 ETH plus state gas per four hours of stall, if such a guest bug exists"), the attacker saves P1; within about 35 minutes every block must carry FI(P1) (V8) and nothing containing it can land; at four hours the skip landing voids P1 and resets the anchor-tip timestamp to X; the attacker saves P2 at X, due X + 300; only the few minutes of blocks anchored before X + 300 can land, then a fresh four-hour clock runs. Nothing prices repetition: the fee is FI_BASE_FEE × (50 + pending)/50 with pending ≈ 0 each cycle; "requester bonds" and "a bonded forced-inclusion lander role" are in the FI page's rejected list and the limitations page's not-built list; the void entry's fee goes to the includer, who can be the attacker landing the skip batch; no skip-history state exists (FiEntry, FiQueue, ForcedRecord). Each cycle also voids about four hours of certified/locked blocks above the FI block (a locked-block reorg per cycle). So finality and L2→L1 bridging stay halted at about 0.02 ETH/day until a DAO image rotation, contradicting `forced-inclusion.html` §13 "DAO. Never needed", the roles page DAO row (`roles.html:93`), index §3 and L16's "bounded to four hours", and R1's "The chain stays live if the DAO never acts again". Not a duplicate: R1-F4 is listed as fixed and its fix is what fails (the same builds-on status the round-2 judge gave R2-F5); the FI page's own phrase "per four hours of stall" half-admits repetition but nowhere states the aggregate is unbounded. High, consistent with the round-1 ruling on the identical precondition ("the design already treats such bugs as realistic enough to design around"): the fix converted a one-shot halt-until-DAO into a re-armable halt-until-DAO at trivial cost with a periodic reorg of locked blocks. Revision: a refundable per-entry bond forfeited (burned) when the entry is skipped, sized above the cost of one four-hour stall; an FI fee floor that escalates exponentially with skips in the last seven days; and restate L16, P5 and the index as a per-entry bound. Revisit the rejected "requester bonds" item for the skip case only.

**R3H-2 (High). No consensus bound on per-term data; sequential landing stalls behind an unprofitable term.** `landing.html:26`: "Ranges are lander-chosen, so no per-term cap exists on-chain; the caps are per landing: 6 blobs ... and 256 blocks", and the 32 KiB/s figure is "client policy". Attesters attest iff V1-V6 (plus V9) pass (`preconf.html:35/60`); V9 (`preconf.html:44`) bounds one block to 390,132 B. A holder registered at exactly 105 % of its floor (reward-type debits "stop at the buffer", `slashing.html:24`, `landing.html:60`) signs 60 blocks of about 385 KB of incompressible calldata (about 6 M L2 gas each, under the L2 block gas limit): all certified and locked; 180 blobs = 30 landings of about 1.5 M gas each (two ZK leaves plus the rest, `landing.html:37`) = about 45 M gas plus 180 blobs (about two minutes of the whole L1 blob budget) against a maximum reward of 60 × 24 + 180 × 1 = 1,620 TAIKO ≈ 0.24 ETH, so break-even is about 5 gwei, and 0 if the holder sits at its buffer.
