# Round 6 STEAL report

Model: Claude Fable 5.1 (claude-fable-5-1), as reported by the session environment; fable was requested.

Design under test: the design site as committed after the round-5 revision and its two fix passes (commit 9a208ed, "Etna round-5 second fix pass").

## Method

1. Converted every page of `packages/protocol/docs/Etna/design/` to text (HTML parser, scratchpad only) and read index, roles, sequencing, preconf, landing, slashing, forced-inclusion, bridge-migration, interfaces, parameters, arguments, l1-dependencies, frame-transactions, limitations and glossary in full; then `01-threat-model.md`, `README.md`, `iterations/05-round.md`, `05-attack-steal.md`, `05-judge.md` and `04-round.md` so that no fixed finding is repeated.
2. Attacked the round-6 focus list in order: the persistent domain tree (versioning, monotonicity, version-log search, walk gas, pins and PIN_REWARD, seat recycling against old versions); receipt-time staleness and the redraw; the timeout rule on the attester's own attestations; the FALLBACK gates and the recording duty; suspended seats in the rank domain; reserve refill, ReserveLow hysteresis and self-landing accounting; S3b without the landed-head exemption and the RESET/RESUME/REPLACE votes; FI_ANCHOR_LAG and the 112-minute bound.
3. For every candidate, wrote the trace against the design AS WRITTEN (quoted sentence, actors, bonds, message sequence with times, rule satisfied or evaded at each step), computed cost and gain, and discarded anything that needs a cryptographic break, a malicious DAO, L1 finality failure or more malicious attesters than T11 allows unless the design's own bound is wrong.
4. Findings are appended below as each is complete; the closing section lists what was tried and which rule stopped it.

## R6S-1 [High] The L20 drain window is a holder-chosen, repeatable, forced-batch-guaranteed reorg of every locked block: the published 39-ETH "locked" credit bound and L6's "unprofitable to the forcer" are false (R6, R7; TH5/W1, TH8; invariant I4 as a claim, limitation L6/L20 as stated)

**Actors.** Attacker A: one owner, CAP = 8 seats at the 72-seat launch registry (bond 8 × 21,000 + 73,600 reserve = 241,600 TAIKO, about 36 ETH, all of it A's own and none of it slashed below). No committee collusion (T11 irrelevant). Counterparty X credits deposits at the "locked" level (the published level, credit bound 13 × B = 39 ETH). Honest holders H1, H2, ... of the terms after A's.

**Trace (L1 seconds; D = the L1 time at which A's reserve first crosses below 4,561).**
1. D − 1 day to D: A runs the drain the design itself describes (landing §5 residual, L20): A lands its own terms at +150 s with rewardTo = a seatless address it controls; the lander's debit is not skipped because rewardTo does not resolve to an owner with seats, so each landing moves about 250 TAIKO (60 × 0.79 + 6 + 108 + 33 pin) from reserveGwei to an immediately withdrawable balance. No third party competes (250 TAIKO < 2 M gas at any fee above 20 gwei). About 280 own terms (35 h at 8/72) bring reserveGwei from 73,600 to 4,561; one more landing crosses the line at D. Cost so far: 0 (the gas A pays to self-land anyway).
2. D: ReserveLapsed is written with effect [D + 7,200, D + 7,200 + 129,600) (landing §5: "makes the owner ineligible with a DELAY_REG-dated effect"). A keeps draining its next two or three terms to reserveGwei = 0 (each landing takes min(reward, balance)).
3. D + 1,500 to D + 7,200: A is drawn about 10 more times (8/72 × 95 terms). Each term t' A sequences normally: honest committee(t') attests, blocks reach "locked" at 1.7 s; one block carries A's deposit of X_dep to counterparty X through a contract that executes only while the L2 SignalService's newest L1 checkpoint number is below N (true in this chain, false in any replay anchored later). X credits at locked (the level table: 39-ETH bound). A never lands t'. Nobody else is paid to: the lander reward is "min(x, balance)" with balance 0, so reward = 0 against about 2 M gas plus proving.
4. termEnd(t') + 150 s: H1 (term t' + 1) cannot land its own certified blocks (L1: firstHeight == lastLanded.height + 1 revert NotNext). Landing of the whole chain stalls behind A's unfunded term; every later holder's locked blocks pile up above lastLanded.
5. termEnd(t') + 0: A saves one calldata forced-inclusion entry (0.05 ETH bond + 0.001 ETH fee); due 300 s later at every anchor; never consumed on L1 because nothing lands.
6. replaceableFrom = min(termEnd(t') + 3,600, max(termEnd(t') + 1,800, lastLanded.at + 600)) + 300 = termEnd(t') + 2,100 s (lastLanded.at ≈ termEnd(t' − 1) + 150). One honest announceLanding defers it once to + 2,700 s and forfeits 2,000 TAIKO (800 to A) unless the announcer lands A's term at its own cost.
7. replaceableFrom + 1 L1 block: A lands a forced batch (view 254, m = 1, two ZK proofs of one FI block prepared in advance, about 1.2 M gas, no certificate, no rights check). Accepted: "(1) block.timestamp > replaceableFrom() ... (4) m = min(due entries at T_A, 64) ≥ 1". Effect: lastLanded jumps to the forced head; "every certificate above lastLanded is void" (preconf §6 lock reset): A's term t' and every honest term sequenced since (35 to 45 minutes of locked blocks) are void, no key slashable, no MISS (A's blocks were certified, not silent), no rule reads the missing landing (I5). The RESUME view re-includes the void transactions, but A's deposit now reverts (checkpoint ≥ N); X already paid out X_dep.
8. The next A term still inside [D, D + 7,200) repeats steps 3 to 7 once more (two reorgs per window). At D + 7,200 A is suspended 36 h; it tops up 73,600 (its own freed capital) and reactivate()s; the cycle restarts after the next 35-h drain.

**Design text exploited.** Landing §5: "No L1 rule can guarantee funds for a term drawn before the debits that emptied the reserve, and the owner can still trigger those debits deliberately ... such a term is landed by its holder at gas cost, or replaced under A1 with no penalty to anyone, and the drain costs the owner one SUSPEND(3) (36 h) of lost revenue per cycle." Forced-inclusion §6: "A landing through the same land with the sentinel view FORCED = 254 ... No rights check, no certificate, no absence penalty". Limitations L6: "Residual: one reorg of the backlog per stall, bounded by the grace period, unprofitable to the forcer beyond its own transaction's ordering, with no penalty to anyone." Slashing §4: "This is the published credit bound for 'locked', and it holds at every lock".

**Cost / gain.** Per 2-h window: two chain-wide stalls of 35 to 45 min and two reorgs of 35 to 45 min of every holder's locked blocks; A's cost is 0.1 ETH of forced-batch gas, the FI fee, and 36 h of its own revenue (about 240 terms × 0.01 ETH ≈ 2.4 ETH); the 241,600-TAIKO bond is never touched. Gain: whatever X credits at "locked" in each of A's terms, twice per cycle, against a published bound of 39 ETH per locked window and a real deterrent of about 1.2 ETH per reorg. Variant without the drain: during any L1 fee spike above the ~100-gwei break-even nobody lands anyone's term (landing §5), so any bonded party with a 0.05-ETH FI entry voids the whole backlog at replaceableFrom with the same forced batch; L6 calls this outage case "unprofitable to the forcer", which is false for a forcer holding a credited deposit in the backlog.

**Why the stated residuals do not cover it.** L20 costs the drain at "one SUSPEND(3) per cycle" and describes the unfunded term's fate as "landed at gas cost or replaced with no penalty"; L6 describes the backlog reorg as an outage event; neither states that an unfunded term stalls every later holder's landing (sequential L1), that the holder can guarantee the void itself with a forced batch, or that the locked credit bound is 0 for blocks in a drained owner's terms. This builds on R5S-4 (fixed for the honest path) and states explicitly that the L20 residual the fix left is a usable attack, not a cost to the owner.

**Fix.** (a) Pay no lander reward to a rewardTo that is not a registered owner with seats, or escrow such credits for EVIDENCE_WINDOW behind the same non-withdrawable rule as the reserve (closes the drain; a genuine third-party lander registers one seat or waits a week). (b) Make an unfunded term unsequenceable rather than unlandable: a holder is eligible for a draw only if reserveGwei ≥ one term of exposure at S(t) (the flag already exists; apply it at the draw, not DELAY_REG later). (c) Refuse a forced batch, and a REPLACE, while the first unlanded term's reserve could still pay a landing, and publish beside "locked" that the credit bound is 0 for any block in a term whose holder's reserveOf() is below one term of exposure.

## R6S-2 [Medium] The landing reserve is non-withdrawable per owner only down to 4,561 TAIKO, not 9,200 per seat: a CAP owner unlocks 94 % of its reserve at zero cost and the published sizing (16 terms, P(> 16) < 0.1 %) is false for any owner that chooses to (R7, R1; invariant I2 sizing; L20 as stated)

**Actors.** One owner A with 8 seats; no counterparties, no collusion.

**Trace.** As R6S-1 step 1, stopping at reserveGwei = 4,562: A lands each of its own terms at +150 s with rewardTo = a seatless address it controls (about 250 TAIKO per term; no third party competes below the 20-gwei break-even for 250 TAIKO), about 275 terms, 35 h at 8/72. The seatless address's credits are "withdrawable immediately for addresses with no seats and no key history" (slashing §1). A never crosses the lapse line, so ReserveLapsed never fires, no SUSPEND(3) is paid, and A stays eligible with 8 seats backed by 4,562 TAIKO instead of 73,600. A withdrew 69,038 TAIKO (about 10.4 ETH) of capital the design calls non-withdrawable.

**Design text exploited.** Landing §5: "Non-withdrawable. The reserve cannot be withdrawn while the owner has any active or pending seat; it leaves the ledger only through requestExit + EVIDENCE_WINDOW, like seat stake." Landing §5: "An owner with CAP = 8 seats holds 73,600 TAIKO of reserve, about 16 terms of worst-case exposure ... the probability that more than 16 of such an owner's terms fall in one window is below 0.1 %". Landing §5 residual: "the drain costs the owner one SUSPEND(3) (36 h) of lost revenue per cycle" (the cycle is unnecessary; the drain stops above the lapse line).

**Consequence.** After the free drain, one third-party landing at the maximum ramp (4,561) takes the reserve to about 0 and every further term drawn inside the next DELAY_REG is unfunded (R6S-1's window), so the effective reserve of any owner is one term, whatever its seat count, and the per-seat requirement protects only owners that do not know the trick. The 1.05 × floor seat stake is untouched, so no slashing deterrent changes; the harm is to the funded-landing guarantee (R7) and to the published entry condition (R1).

**Cost / gain.** Cost 0 beyond the self-landing gas A pays anyway; gain 69,038 TAIKO of capital freed per CAP owner (about 10 ETH), repeatable for every Sybil owner.

**Fix.** Same as R6S-1 (a): rewards to a seatless rewardTo are escrowed behind the reserve rule or refused; or require reserveGwei ≥ seats × LAND_RESERVE at S(t) for a seat to be drawn at all, so a drained owner loses its draws two hours after the drain instead of keeping them.

## R6S-3 [Medium] The FALLBACK gates protect committee(t)'s locked blocks only if committee(t) reaches Q at term end; a cartel that withholds its TERM_END votes needs no same-block race, at any slot time (R6; P2, L5(b) as stated; TH5/W1)

**Stated claim attacked.** Preconf §5: "what protects committee(t)'s locked blocks is L1, not slashing: the honest TERM_END VC (formed at termEnd + 2 s, recorded at + 2.5 s) is live before the earliest FALLBACK is acceptable (+ 12 s), and a FALLBACK recorded first is superseded by it until consumed. The residual is a FALLBACK consumed by a landing before the honest view change ... reached L1 (a same-block race at 12-s slots, needing Q of committee(t + 1) and a proof ready within seconds)". Limitations L5: "the same-block race it also needs (a proof ready within one L1 slot) multiplies it down further"; at 2-s slots the honest VC is on L1 within one slot, so the design's residual there is 0.

**Actors.** Cartel C with one third of the seats across many owners (one BLS key per owner). Term t with c ≥ 11 cartel slots in committee(t) (the L2 blank-term condition; about half of all terms at one third, by the design's own hypergeometric accounting) and ≥ 22 cartel slots in committee(t + 1) (4·10⁻⁵ per term, the design's figure); both committees are public 20 minutes ahead, so C acts only on such a term. Honest leader(t, v_last) and honest leader(t + 1, 0). Counterparty X credits at "locked".

**Trace (seconds from termEnd(t)).**
1. −60 to −1: leader(t, v_last) produces blocks; the 32 − c honest attesters plus exactly c − 10 "dirty" cartel keys attest, so every block up to h is certified and locked (22 attestations). The remaining cartel keys in committee(t) attest nothing above h' = h − 40. C's accomplice deposits to X in block h − 30; X credits at locked at about −28 s.
2. +2: TERM_END timeouts: the 32 − c ≤ 21 honest attesters sign TERM_END at lock h; C's c ≥ 11 slots sign nothing (silence is unslashable). No TERM_END VC forms (21 < Q = 22). Nothing of committee(t) can ever be recorded for (t, v_last, closes).
3. +12: gate (i) opens for committee(t + 1). C's 22 clean keys sign LockVote(kind FALLBACK, lock h', signerTerm t + 1) for (t, v_last, closes); none of these keys attested anything above h' under that opening object, so S3b has no pair; S3c needs two votes by one key (none). The 10 honest members of committee(t + 1) vote FALLBACK at h (their lock) and cannot reach Q. C records the FALLBACK; gate (ii) is met with the public certificate C(h'); gate (iii) has nothing to supersede it, now or later.
4. +13: honest leader(t + 1, 0) holds a valid VC closing term t at h' (V2 checks signatures against committee(t + 1)) and builds on h'; committee(t + 1) certifies; blocks h' + 1 .. h are orphaned. X's credit is gone from the canonical chain; no key is slashable; no landing, no proof and no L1 ordering race were needed.

**Why it is not the stated residual.** L5(b) and preconf §5 assume committee(t)'s own TERM_END VC exists and race it to L1; here the same cartel prevents it from existing, which T11's liveness clause already treats as reachable (the blank-term table). The stated per-term bound (4·10⁻⁵, at most one view above the FALLBACK lock) still holds (2·10⁻⁵ per term with the c ≥ 11 factor, about ten terms a year at one third of the seats); what is false is the claim that a proof-ready same-block race is needed, and therefore the implicit claim that the path closes at short slot times. The three gates close R5S-5's live-term case and leave this one open.

**Cost / gain.** Cost: 0 bonds; C forgoes the attester income of one term. Gain: everything X credited at locked in h' + 1 .. h, against a published bound of 39 ETH.

**Fix.** Let a FALLBACK VC for (t, v) be recorded and landed only at a lock that is ≥ the highest lock any recorded or presented certificate of committee(t) proves for that view (the landing already verifies certificates; store the highest certified height per (t, v) at record time, or require the FALLBACK lock to equal the highest certificate the FALLBACK's own signers present), so a FALLBACK can close a view but never move its lock below a block committee(t) certified; and extend S3b's attestation half to any key that signs FALLBACK while holding a certificate above its lock (a signed Status or lock-vote re-vote is not needed: the FALLBACK vote itself can carry the highest certificate the signer holds, making a lower lock a lie).

## R6S-4 [Low] Internal contradictions that change the domain tree's ordering, a published gas figure or a slot-robustness claim (R5, R7; documentation)

1. **Exit effective time.** Slashing §9 owner machine: "ACTIVE --requestExit(seats)--> EXITING (per seat, activeUntil = max(τ + DELAY_REG, activeFrom + MIN_TENURE))". Sequencing §2, §5, §10, the parameters page and the glossary: requestExit "is accepted for a seat only once τ + DELAY_REG ≥ activeFrom + MIN_TENURE ... and sets activeUntil = τ + DELAY_REG, so ... every domain write takes effect at τ + DELAY_REG, monotone in transaction order". Under the slashing-page formula a version tagged activeFrom + MIN_TENURE is written ahead of later versions with smaller effective times, the log is unsorted and "the latest version with E ≤ termStart(t) (a binary search of the log)" returns a wrong version: the inbox and the nodes then disagree on E_t and every rank of every term inside the window, so honest landings revert NotHolder until the terms are replaced. An owner reaches it by calling requestExit before day 7 − 2 h of a seat. The sequencing page is normative, so this is a documentation defect, but it is the one contradiction that would break invariant I1 if implemented from the wrong page.
2. **Registration gas.** Sequencing §11: "Registration gas | ≈ 2 × 97,920 state + ~60k exec"; sequencing §5, parameters §1, interfaces `register`: each seat "writes a new domain-tree version (about 16 fresh nodes ... ≈ 1.6 M gas of state under EIP-8037, paid by the writer)". A CAP registration is about 13.6 M gas, not 0.26 M, and sits just under the 16.8 M EIP-7825 cap; the entry-cost figure the roles page relies on ("about two fresh storage slots per first seat") is wrong by 50×.
3. **FI_ANCHOR_LAG unit.** Forced-inclusion §15: "the attester-policy lag is a block count (4 min to 40 s)"; forced-inclusion §8, §11 and the parameters page: 288 s, "stated in seconds so the policy is slot-independent ... (round 5 fix pass)". A client built from §15 recreates the round-5 B3 defect at 2-s slots (a 40-s lag below the 48-s minimum anchor age: honest attesters withhold from every honest block while an entry is due).
4. **Committee-root key hashes are undated.** Sequencing §8 pins "committeeRoot[t] = keccak of the 32 attester key hashes" and interfaces §1 keeps one `attKeyHash` per Operator, while `rotateKey` is "effective τ + DELAY_REG" and `keyOwnerAt(keyId, term)` exists. Which hash the walk reads for a term pinned after a rotation took effect (up to 34 h later) is unstated; if it is the current hash, committee(t)'s certificates no longer verify against the root, term t is unlandable and replaced, and any committee member can bring that about for every unpinned term by one rotation at gas cost. State that the root uses the key valid at termStart(t) (a per-key validity interval, as keyOwnerAt implies).

**Fix.** One owner per statement: delete the max() formula on the slashing page; replace the registration-gas row with the per-seat tree cost; replace §15's block-count phrase; add the key-validity rule to sequencing §8 and interfaces §1.

## What I tried and could not break

1. **Domain-tree ordering and the version-log search.** Tried to make the inbox read a different version than the nodes: every domain write (register, requestExit, exitSlashed) is tagged τ + DELAY_REG with one constant, requestExit is refused until τ + DELAY_REG ≥ activeFrom + MIN_TENURE, so effective times are monotone in transaction order, equal effective times keep write order, and every version with E ≤ termStart(t) is written at least DELAY_REG − LOOKBACK = 6,000 s before S(t). Rule D plus "latest version with E ≤ termStart(t)" gives the same answer at S(t) and at landing (invariant I1). Only the slashing-page max() formula breaks it (R6S-4 item 1).
2. **Seat recycling against old versions.** Tried to land or evidence an old term with a recycled seat's new owner: a hole is reassigned only after activeUntil + RECYCLE_GRACE (129,600 s) ≤ now, and every term inside ROLE_HORIZON (122,880 s) started after that activeUntil, so the recycled seat is absent from the version every landable term reads; suspensions and reserve lapses are flags, not tree writes, so they cannot reorder anything.
3. **Walk gas against the EIP-7825 cap.** Recomputed the pin: 576 descents of 16 levels touch about 4,000 distinct cold nodes at 65,535 seats (about 9 M gas), independent of writes after the term started because a version is immutable; a landing that must also pin at the maximum registry is 11.5 M, over LANDING_GAS_BUDGET and a 2-s-slot block but under the 16.8 M cap, and the design already routes large-registry pins to a separate recordAssignment. A register(8) at about 13.6 M also fits. No unpinnable term was found.
4. **PIN_REWARD extraction.** Tried to farm the 400-TAIKO pin: the reward is 0 at term end and 33 TAIKO at the +150-s happy-path landing, the holder's own landing or recordAssignment pins for gas only, so a third party is paid only for terms unlanded for about 12 minutes at 30 gwei; a blank term can cost its holder up to 400 TAIKO unless it pins itself, which is bounded and self-defended.
5. **Receipt-time staleness and the redraw.** Tried to revive a private or late block through a redrawn committee: a cartel member may lie about its receipt time, but attestation still needs Q of the redrawn committee (L3 unchanged); a leader can split a committee by publishing at the 3-s edge (the stated 1(d) residual); a future-stamped block is not attested until its timestamp, so it never satisfies condition (2) and cannot hold a view; the leader cannot sign a different block at the same height under a new redraw count because S1's key omits the redraw count.
6. **The timeout rule on the attester's own attestations.** Tried to keep a view with a block only part of the committee attested without the 1(d) split: a block attested by Q is certified and the clock restarts on the certificate; a block attested by fewer than Q but ≥ 11 − c is exactly 1(d); a cartel of 17 can delay a dead leader's takeover by at most REDRAW_MAX × TIMEOUT = 10 s because timeouts are keyed per redraw count.
7. **S3b and the RESET/RESUME/REPLACE votes.** Tried to find an honest TIMEOUT, TERM_END or FALLBACK vote below a certificate the key attested: an attester's lock is at least the carried certificate it verified at V3, votes are monotone, TERM_END after a HOLD sits at the carried lock (not above it), RESUME is the only kind signable after a reset and S3b never reads it, and a RESUME or REPLACE record is accepted only at lastLanded after replaceableFrom with the predicate re-evaluated, so S3d cannot slash an honest signer.
8. **Suspended seats in the rank domain.** Tried to use self-suspension to move a rank, shrink a committee below K_MIN_CERT, or sit in the committee of a term the owner holds: a suspension empties only the owner's own slots, the primary is excluded by owner whether void or not, no-committee mode needs 25 of the 32 drawn slots to be the attacker's suspended seats, and the flag at termStart(t) is fixed by S(t) because clearing is DELAY_REG-dated.
9. **Reserve refill and ReserveLow.** Tried to lapse an honest owner through third-party landings or attester shares: a landing at +7 min at 30 gwei moves about 680 TAIKO and costs the lander about 400, the holder lands first at +150 s, attester credits refill the owner's reserve first, and the hysteresis fires once per crossing; the only drains are the owner's own (R6S-1, R6S-2).
10. **FI_ANCHOR_LAG and the 112-minute bound.** Tried to extend censorship with a landing whose first segment lies in an earlier term (T_floor read from the first segment's term, so D = 0 for one landing covering the first obliged term): the landing's anchor tip then exceeds s + FI_DELAY, the next landing must consume, and LAND_WINDOW_MAX caps the chain of late landings at termEnd(t') + 3,900 s, so the entry still lands by s + 6,720 s or the forced path opens; the honest-attester case is bounded by V8 at attestation and the 1(d) one-term delay.
11. **Proof copying, journal bindings and the forced batch as theft.** rewardTo is bound into every journal, the forced journal is domain-separated, blob hashes come from blobhash and the parent root from storage; a forced batch can void (R6S-1) but never finalize a false root or redirect fees.
12. **Challenger loops, announcement bonds, FI fee farming.** Each debit pays at most min(10 %, 2,000), duplicates are keyed; a forfeited announcement pays the forcer 800 TAIKO against 1.2 M gas and a 35-minute stall it cannot create on its own (it can, with R6S-1, but the bond is incidental); FI fees go to the segment's sequencer and bonds burn on skip.
