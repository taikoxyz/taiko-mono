## A change the user requested during this round

After round 6 reported, the user asked Etna to adopt the anchor-free L2 block design of [taikoxyz/taiko-mono#22147](https://github.com/taikoxyz/taiko-mono/issues/22147) (working assumption A9 in the README). It was specified, applied by two editors on disjoint pages and checked by two independent verifiers before the round-6 revision was applied on top of it; both are in the same commit. Summary of what changed:

- **No anchor transaction.** No golden-touch sender, fixed-nonce signature, engine carve-outs, index-0 rule, 1,000,000-gas reserve, ancestors ring, per-block `blockHashes` write or receipt rule. The Anchor contract stays as the fee treasury.
- **The header carries what L2 needs.** `parentBeaconBlockRoot` is the hash of the anchored L1 block; `extraData` keeps (fee share, termId, view, kind) with a new kind 3 for forced-inclusion blocks, which replaces the anchor's cursor field. The signed header PH is unchanged and binds the block through its hash; vcHash and the carried certificate live in PH and the blob record.
- **Checkpoints.** The standard EIP-4788 pre-execution call records the anchor hash on L2, keyed by L2 timestamp (a guaranteed 8,191-s window at 1-s blocks); a permissionless `revealCheckpoint` on the L2 SignalService stores the checkpoint after a keccak check; sequencers reveal by client policy, not as a validity rule.
- **Fork-time safety.** The L2 upgrade bridged with `armEtna(T0)` gates `anchorV4` and the L2 `saveCheckpoint` path off from T0, which closes the forgery window the issue's review found; the rollback implementations mirror the gate until the rollback time.
- **R2.** No address changes. The Bridge, the Vaults and the L1 SignalService are untouched; the L2 SignalService and the Anchor implementations are upgraded in place with no new storage.
- **The verifiers' blocking findings, all fixed:** a view-binding sentence that was false for REPLACE and RESUME views; a rollback window that reopened the forgery path; a first Etna block that could have no valid form when an entry is due at the cut-over (the genesis child is now exempt from V8's obligation and its parent's anchor is the drain block); a P2P envelope stated two ways; a checkpoint write priced at one storage word instead of two.

## Comparison with the Astra candidate (PR #22188), 2026-10-01

The user asked for a comparison with the other candidate, ChatGPT's "Astra" design on `codex/etna-protocol-design`, with adoptions and a critique. The critique is posted on that PR (https://github.com/taikoxyz/taiko-mono/pull/22188#issuecomment-5926119441). Adopted into this design, each cited where it applies:

- **The current-root guard.** The legacy `anchorV4` and the L2 `saveCheckpoint` run only when L2's EIP-4788 contract returns a zero root for the current block (a Shasta header); reveals and pins only when it returns a non-zero one (an Etna header). It replaces the `block.timestamp ≥ ETNA_T0` gate and the mirrored rollback gate of the anchor-free revision: it reads the fact the clients enforce, so no contract immutable can disagree with the fork time, and it is symmetric under rollback. The guarded implementations must be installed after L2's EIP-4788 contract exists, because the guard reverts on a missing oracle.
- **Permanent origin pins.** `pinCurrentOrigin()` stores the current block's anchor hash permanently; `revealCheckpoint(0, header)` reveals against the pin with no deadline. A pin queued through forced inclusion pins whatever anchor its processing block carries, so a censoring sequencer can delay a durable checkpoint by the forced-inclusion bound but never prevent one; the ring's 8,191-s window was the only path before.
- **Reveal parser bounds** (1,536 bytes, 12 to 32 scalar items, minimal integers) and the oracle code-hash check.
- **The fee split**, corrected: main raised `basefeeSharingPctg` to 100 in Proposal0026; the design said 75 with a 25 % Anchor treasury.

Rejected, with the arguments on the PR: preconfirmations that any third party can reorg for free (no fork choice, a 10-TAIKO same-owner penalty only); one uncompressed blob per segment and a 0.05-ETH admission rent that resets on every landing (about 137 bytes per second rent-free, about 2 KB per second for about 67 ETH a day); bonded fragment publication at 64 gas per byte of L1 calldata within 240 s; forced requests of 2,048 bytes and 1 M gas, four per segment (about 46 h of delay behind a 1,000-request backlog by their own bound); no minimum anchor age; sequencing by proof race. An open question was added for the user: whether R1 requires removing the Bridge's and Vaults' pause powers, which that candidate does by upgrading every custody contract in place and this design does not.

## The other candidate's review of this design, and the independent comparison (2026-10-01)

The agent maintaining PR #22188 reviewed this design at commit 32e78bb (https://github.com/taikoxyz/taiko-mono/pull/22184#issuecomment-5926348750), and an independent AI-assisted comparison commissioned by the user followed (comment 5926626192, with a gist). Five of the six points were adopted and one answered with a position; each is cited on the page that states the rule as "(after the #22188 review, 2026-10-01)":

- **CR1, operational powers on the shared contracts.** `Bridge._authorizePause` and `SignalService._authorizePause` are owner-or-pauser and `ERC20Vault.changeBridgedToken` is owner-only, so R2 (addresses frozen) left operational authority in place. Position (README A10): R1 forbids a brake faster than the DAO, and a brake as slow as a DAO upgrade adds nothing over the upgrade, so the design specifies storage-compatible in-place upgrades that remove the pause and owner-only operational functions, with a selector audit; the costs are an audit of the custody contracts and no emergency brake (limitation L-OP). A human decision the user may reverse.
- **CR2, forced requests in an abandoned Shasta tail.** Confirmed at `Inbox.sol:686-724`: fees are paid and the queue head advances at proposal time, entries stay in `$.queue`, and the ring buffer holds every unfinalized proposal's hash. `abandonShastaTail(Proposal[])` verifies the unproven proposals against the ring buffer and moves the head back by their forced-inclusion sources; requeued entries keep their save time and blob slices, are due at the fork and carry fee zero (the proposer was paid; a bounded leak accepted instead of a reimbursement fund). The 24-hour deadline stays because a whitelisted prover must not hold the migration (R1).
- **CR3, the frame gate.** The 68-byte calldata pin came from the PDF's `proposeFor(address, bytes32)`; `landFor(address, LandInput)` is dynamic, so the pin is now the selector, the `_rewardTo` word and `LAND_CALLDATA_MAX`. A shape-1 transaction whose parent another landing consumed stays valid, frame 3 reverts at the inbox's first check, the nonce is burned and the actor pays that gas; shape 1 is zero-cost only against other shape-1 competitors for the same nonce. The direct `land` through the public mempool sits behind no switch. An erratum was added to 03-frame-transactions-research.md.
- **CR4, the handoff gap.** An attester signs TERM_END on holding the certificate of the block with timestamp termEnd − 1 (the term's last possible block under V4; clock-free and never a lock lie), the clock rule at termEnd + END_GRACE being the fallback; the next leader's first block carries the view change at about termEnd + 0.5 s, so the 1-s grid has no empty slot at a certified handoff, and at most four empty slots otherwise (R4 qualified on the index and arguments pages).
- **CR5, single-proof mode.** A one-leaf landing after the 2-hour degradation is provisional: the chain continues from it, no checkpoint is written until a same-journal proof from another leaf confirms it or `DEGRADED_FINALITY` = 24 h passes without a conflict; a conflicting proof from another leaf freezes landing and checkpoints until a DAO upgrade names the sound leaf (limitation L-PF). Elapsed time is not evidence of soundness; when the other leaf cannot speak, the mode's safety rests on the honest-quorum premise as before, and the page says so. The two-proofs-always rule was not taken because a landing halt longer than about an hour starts replacing certified blocks.
- **CR6, labels and availability.** The confirmation table lists the exceptions beside "locked" (one redraw count at a full committee, a private-quorum stall reverting by replacement with nothing slashable, the fallback race, no-committee terms, provisional "landed") and says the slashable stake is a deterrent, not compensation. No L1-accountable publication duty was added: attesters that attested a block hold its bytes and serve them, but a failure to serve is unprovable on L1 and an L1 publication duty costs what the bonded-fragment path of #22188 costs.
- **DeepSeek reviews (seven, one per push).** Addressed in the tree: the research banner and the tag legend on every page (DS1, DS2), the four named contradictions on the consistency verifier's checklist (DS3), one stylesheet (DS4), the fee-share rows naming the snapshot value and main's current 100 %, repository-relative paths in the notes, the cited evidence committed where small and CC0 (`notes/raw-survey/status-table.txt`, `notes/eip-8141.verify.txt`, `notes/phase2/`), a provenance note on the user's PDF input (the user decided it stays; its licence needs no further action), and the typos findings fixed. Not adopted: removing the model names (the brief's A8 requires them), moving the tree out of the directory, and pinning external URLs to commits after the fact.

The revision was applied by three editors on disjoint pages and verified by three verifiers on three models (adversary schedule on opus, cross-page consistency on sonnet, fidelity to the code and the critique on fable), with sequential fixers and an opus recheck. The verifiers found 14 blocking findings (five on cross-page consistency, four adversary schedules, five fidelity), among them: the checkpoint-timing rule for two-leaf landings over a provisional parent stated two ways; the invariant I4 restatements missing the conflict-resolution path; the R1 verdict naming only one DAO-only remedy; the CR1 claim covering `DefaultResolver.registerAddress` and the owner branch of `BridgedERC20` mint and burn, which are DAO-only non-upgrade powers (narrowed by the arbiter, decision X4, residual recorded in L-OP); the Inbox storage note missing the provisional ring; the early TERM_END vote needing the same opening object and a block that extends the key's lock; the CONFLICT rollback bounded to one window by letting records before the conflicting one finalize. All were fixed by the fixers; the recheck found one blocking contradiction (CONFLICT described as blocking every checkpoint write, which the one-window bound needs) and two minors, applied by the orchestrator.
