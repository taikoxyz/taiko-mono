## 3. Confirmed findings: trace, gap, required revision

### F1 (High) Committee smaller than Q cannot certify; no rule covers 4 ≤ committee size < 22

Trace, as re-checked. Q is a constant: "Quorum Q = ⌊2K/3⌋ + 1 = 22" (preconf.html §1) and landing L5 requires "popcount ≥ Q = 22" (landing.html:35) for every sortition-view landing; timeouts, view changes and the FALLBACK VC also need Q signatures (preconf §5). committee(t) is "the first K = 32 distinct eligible seats of a second walk ... skipping seats whose owner is in the term's holder list" (sequencing.html §8), so its size is min(32, eligible − holder-owned). The only certificate-free live regime is no-committee mode, "while fewer than K_MIN = 4 attester seats are eligible" (sequencing.html:72; parameters K/Q/K_MIN row). The parameters dependency chain permits activation with "K_MIN + V_MAX + 1 = 9 ≤ registered seats" and only "recommend[s] 37" (parameters.html:30); the 37-seat figure is a pre-T0 checklist item (bridge-migration.html:94) whose own "else the no-committee landing regime applies" is false for 9 to 21 seats under the K_MIN = 4 rule. With, say, 20 registered seats and 5 to 8 holder-owned seats, the committee has 12 to 15 members, no certificate or VC can ever reach 22 signatures, the leader holds after two uncertified blocks, nothing lands, and the only exits are forced batches (view 254) and dead mode after "termOf(now) − lastLandedTerm > DEAD_TERMS = 75" (sequencing.html §6). A dead-mode landing updates lastLandedTerm (landing L10), so dead mode closes and the cycle repeats at roughly one landing per 75 minutes. The word "widen" occurs once in the entire design site, in the sentence that defers to the rule: "The certificate page provides the widening rule" (sequencing.html:203); the certificate page has no such rule. The same state is reachable later by attrition: with 22 to 27 eligible non-holder seats a single offline attester fails the term, and preconf §11's "P(no quorum) ≈ 1.7·10⁻⁴" assumes K = 32. Under F3 an adversary drives the eligible set into this band.

Design gap. A regime the design permits (registry between K_MIN and roughly Q + holders + offline slack) has no certification rule, no landing rule and no stated recovery other than the dead-mode floor; the design text refers to a rule that does not exist, and its liveness ladder ("Committee silent → next committee signs a FALLBACK", arguments.html §3) assumes the next committee can reach Q, which it cannot in this band.

Required revision (any of these closes it; the first two are the minimal set):

1. Define quorum as a function of actual committee size, Q(m) = ⌊2m/3⌋ + 1 for m = |committee(t)|, and state the 2Q − K slashable-intersection bound per m; or treat any term whose committee has fewer than K_MIN_CERT members (a stated constant, at least 2Q − K + 1 of them being needed for a meaningful lock) as no-committee mode (certificate-free landing under the sentinel rules).
2. Make the no-committee predicate the actual committee size of the term, not the count of eligible attester seats, and reconcile parameters.html:30, sequencing.html:72, bridge-migration.html:94 and preconf.html:247 to one rule.
3. Either write the widening rule on the certificate page or delete the sentence at sequencing.html:203 and rewrite "the next primary starts at term end", which contradicts V2 (a term's first block must carry a VC closing the previous view).
4. If activation is to require a seat floor, enforce it in initEtna rather than a checklist.

### F3 (High) The strike ladder is a removal lever for an 11-of-32 minority or a stakeless DoS

Trace, as re-checked. Attesters sign a timeout when "no new certificate in (t, v) has appeared for TIMEOUT = 5 s" (preconf §5); nothing conditions the timeout on non-receipt of the leader's blocks. With 11 of 32 committee seats silently refusing to attest, the honest 21 cannot form a certificate, honestly time out, and a true VC forms; the landed VC applies "MISS_PENALTY = 50 TAIKO, 100 % burned ... one strike per owner per 600 s" (sequencing.html:92; slashing.html:83) and "three strikes in a week → suspension from τ + 22 min, doubling per strike" up to 64 h (sequencing §7). The design states the mechanism itself: "With ≥ K − Q + 1 = 11 of 32 committee slots it can refuse to attest an entrant's blocks; the honest 21 then time out honestly, a true VC forms, and the entrant loses 50 TAIKO and a strike. P[Bin(32, c) ≥ 11] = 0.04 / 0.36 / 0.79 at cartel seat share c = 0.2 / 0.3 / 0.4" (slashing.html:84), but concludes "The entrant's seat is never removed; strikes decay after seven clean days" and analyses only the 50-TAIKO money. Suspension removes eligibility (sequencing §2: eligible(i, T) := ... ∧ ¬suspended(owner, T)). Because every view of an affected term times out, all five holders of the term take a strike; with the 600-s coalescing cap an owner can accrue up to six strikes an hour, so at c ≈ 0.33 a one-seat honest owner reaches three strikes within about an hour and a multi-seat owner within about 30 minutes; even at c = 0.2 (which limitation L9 treats as the comfortable assumption) an eight-seat honest operator is suspended within a few hours. Each further strike doubles the suspension; suspended seats leave the eligible set, so c rises toward 1 and the honest set cascades out for up to 64 h at a time. The cartel's cost is forfeited 6-TAIKO attester shares; its bond is never at risk ("Silence is never slashed"), and 400k TAIKO of seats at c = 0.33 is withdrawable after exit. Once the cartel holds whole committees it can perform the zero-cost premature-VC reorgs of attested blocks that limitation L4 accepts as a one-view nuisance. The stakeless variant: the assignment is public 20-80 minutes ahead (limitation L1, "Mitigation is replacement within 5 s"); a 6-second flood at three term starts 600 s apart is three strikes, and each later burst after reactivation doubles the suspension, so roughly ten bursts keep an operator out indefinitely. Limitation L2 and the open question on slashing.html §17 ("cap suspensions at 8 h ... so a 30 to 40 % cartel cannot walk an entrant into 64-h suspensions") acknowledge the direction, but not the 11-seat trigger, the sub-hour speed, the DoS variant, or the cascade.

Design gap. A performance ladder that is fed by an unslashable, minority-manufacturable event and whose output is loss of eligibility hands a non-DAO party a removal power (R1) and breaks the anti-monopoly cost curve (R6: sequencing §12 prices 50 % of terms at 800k TAIKO, but ~200-400k recoverable TAIKO yields near-total control after the cascade). This composes with F1: suspension shrinks the eligible set into the uncertifiable band.

Required revision:

1. Decouple suspension from timeout VCs, or raise the bar: strike only when the VC carries at least Q + (K − Q) = 32 − (allowed abstainers) timeouts, or only when the VC's signers include a supermajority that also attested nothing of that leader in the view (so 11 abstainers cannot both block the certificate and complete the VC); at minimum, treat a VC whose timeout bitmap includes seats that attested no block of the view as non-striking.
2. Cap suspension (the design's own 8 h proposal) with daily strike decay, and never suspend a seat when doing so would leave fewer than K + V_MAX + 1 eligible seats (a seat floor).
3. State TH6 honestly on the limitations page: each successful 6-s DoS is a strike; either randomize or delay the leader's network identity (the design's own VRF open question) or make the ladder DoS-insensitive per item 1.
4. Recompute the anti-monopoly section with suspension as the lever, not the 50 TAIKO.

### F4 (High, conditional) A decodable, non-void forced-inclusion entry that no guest can prove halts every landing; calldata entries never expire

Trace, as re-checked. FI block content (forced-inclusion §3) has two anchor-only escapes: an undecodable payload and a void entry. A payload that decodes, executes validly on every node, but that both ZK guests fail to prove (a zkVM completeness bug, a precompile edge case, or a cycle/memory cliff inside FI_GAS_LIMIT = 5 M) has neither. Every ordinary landing must consume it: L8/F5 requires c ≥ min(D, ⌈2n/3⌉) with T_floor monotone in real time, so within ~35 minutes of the save D ≥ 1 and any landing of n ≥ 1 blocks must consume the head entry, which the guest must prove ("the forced-inclusion blocks are exactly the consumed queue entries in order, derived from their own blobs", landing §4). REPLACE views start at lastLanded with cursor = fiQueue.head and obey V8, so the landing page's poison-content answer ("Head range unprovable (guest bug): after replaceableFrom the holder forks a REPLACE view", landing §5) does not apply; the forced batch must contain "exactly m blocks, all FI blocks" verified "with ZK_K = 2 always" (forced-inclusion §6), so escalation is harder, not easier; dead mode (view 255) obeys the same L8 count; single-proof mode helps only if one leaf still proves the entry. "Calldata entries never void" and "No owner call replaces today's queue reset" (forced-inclusion.html:79), and the page's own failure analysis says "DAO. Never needed" (forced-inclusion.html:158). The exit is therefore a DAO guest/inbox upgrade. Cost: the ~0.001 ETH fee (refunded to whoever consumes it) plus ~196k state gas; no bond, no slashing rule.

Design gap. The design keeps guest completeness bugs as a realistic residual for ordinary blocks (SLASH_ABANDON rationale: "false positives exist (guest bug)"; landing §5 "Head range unprovable (guest bug)") and handles them by replacement, but the FI path is mandatory, replacement-immune and, for calldata, time-immune. When the precondition is realized, landing, finality and bridging halt until the DAO acts, contradicting R1 ("The chain stays live if the DAO never acts again") and the FI page's claim.

Severity note. High per the scale (prolonged halt of landing, not permanent: the DAO can upgrade out of it; L2 preconfs continue; no A1-A3 loss). It is conditional on the attacker finding a latent bug or resource cliff shared by both guests; the design already treats such bugs as realistic enough to design around, so the condition does not lower the severity of the design gap.

Required revision:

1. A time-based skip for the head entry of both kinds: if the head entry has been due for longer than FORCE_SKIP (hours, not days) and no landing has consumed it, every landing path may treat it as void (anchor-only block, fee to the includer). This reintroduces one clock-based rule, so state it on the arguments page and in limitations.
2. Bound FI content by a proving-cost budget the guest can enforce cheaply (cycle count or a restricted opcode/precompile set for FI blocks), not by EVM gas alone.
3. Optionally, a bounded "manifest unprovable under budget" witness the guest can emit in place of full execution, so the entry derives to an anchor-only block without a DAO rotation.
4. Correct the FI page's "DAO. Never needed" and the roles page's DAO row to reflect whichever mechanism is adopted.

### F5 (Medium) S7 slashes honest attesters when a certified ordinary block is unprovable

Trace, as re-checked. S7 is answered "only [by] a landing covering the certified height within DEMAND_WINDOW (30 min)" (preconf §6); a landing needs a valid proof over block h (L11). demandData is callable by anyone for 0.05 ETH, "refunded on default". The gate is "outage gate as S4a" (slashing.html:50), i.e. some term landed within the hour before the deadline; landing is sequential and stuck exactly at h, so the landing of h − 1 minutes earlier opens the gate. Every bitmap member and the leader lose one seat each (class B, 23 × 20,000 = 460,000 TAIKO), take three strikes, and a single-seat owner falls below the floor into open-ended suspension. DEMAND_WINDOW (1,800 s) expires before replaceableFrom (≥ termEnd + 2,100 s), so S7 fires before the milder replacement path can run. The preconf FP table lists the case verbatim ("all Q+1 honest holders unable to land one batch in 30 min while other landings happen", preconf.html:204) but never connects it to the poison-content residual, which the landing page prices at SLASH_ABANDON only. The only repetition mitigation is a non-consensus client blacklist a variant transaction evades.

Design gap. S7 is meant to punish unavailable data; it cannot distinguish "unavailable" from "available but unprovable by any guest", so under the same precondition as F4 it becomes a near-free, repeatable bond-griefing tool against honest committees (TH8). Same precondition as F4; F5's own contribution is the S7 amplification.

Required revision: accept an availability response that is not a full landing (for example, posting the range's canonical blobs with the certificate to L1, which the inbox can bind by blobhash without a proof) as a valid answer to demandData; or suspend S7 while the demanded height lies inside a range that is under replacement or that a lander has attempted; and make re-inclusion of void-range transactions a client duty with a quarantine list (Q-signed on the REPLACE VC) rather than an unconditional duty.

### F6 (Medium) The lander reward cap does not cover landing cost above roughly 20 gwei; the "100-gwei hour" rationale is false

Trace, as re-checked. R_BLK_MAX = 4 TAIKO per block, TERM = 60 s at 1-s blocks, 1 TAIKO ≈ 1.5·10⁻⁴ ETH, landing gas 1.5 to 2.0 M (landing §3). Maximum reward per term is 240 TAIKO ≈ 0.036 ETH; a landing at 100 gwei costs 0.15 to 0.20 ETH before proving. The rationale "maximum exceeds proving plus landing in a 100-gwei hour" (landing.html:157; parameters.html:66; arguments.html:54, tagged assumed) is therefore off by a factor of about five. The design's own 10-gwei arithmetic pins the third-party break-even near 18 to 24 gwei; at the assumed V_term = 0.01 ETH one landing per term is loss-making for the holder above about 6.7 gwei. The abandonment gate ("some term landed within OUTAGE_WINDOW", landing.html:83) means that if nobody lands during a spike nobody is penalized, so waiting is an equilibrium; after 35 to 65 minutes the backlog becomes replaceable and after 2 h unrecorded terms become unlandable (F7).

Design gap. TH13's "the ramp pays more when gas is dear" is not true above ~20 gwei with the stated constants; minute-level landing (R7) fails organically at fee levels mainnet sees routinely, and the halt is unforced but rational. Medium because any single party who values finality breaks the equilibrium, landed state is untouched, and every TAIKO amount is already flagged assumed/open (L15).

Required revision: index the reward to block.basefee (the landing page's own open question) with a cap derived from the per-term bond lock; publish the break-even fee beside R_BLK_MAX; consider allowing one landing to cover several consecutive views to amortize the fixed gas; correct the three "100-gwei" sentences.

### F7 (Medium) Two clock-based voids exist while "nothing voids by clock" is tagged proven

Trace, as re-checked. Landing L4: "A term older than ROLE_HORIZON (2 h) is checked against its recorded assignment; without a record it is unlandable and therefore replaceable" (landing.html:34); recordAssignment is callable only "while now ≤ termStart + ROLE_HORIZON" (sequencing §10, interfaces). Landing is sequential (L1), so one unrecorded term at the front of a backlog makes the whole backlog unlandable; the only way forward is a REPLACE, dead-mode or forced landing that voids every certified and locked block above lastLanded, unslashed because the outage gate is closed. Limitation L14 admits it ("a time-based void for very long outages"); landing §18 asks whether it is acceptable. DEGRADE_AFTER = ROLE_HORIZON = 7,200 s, so the single-proof degradation path that exists for a one-backend outage lands exactly on the record deadline of the first unlanded term. Separately, landing L7 refuses an anchor tip older than the EIP-2935 window and the anchor page states "A batch whose landing slips past the window (an outage longer than 4.55 h at 2-s slots) is unlandable and replaced" (bridge-migration.html:61; also :184). Both contradict "Nothing voids by clock ... proven" (arguments.html:55), landing §16's "a global proving outage voids and penalizes nothing" (proven) and index §3.

Design gap. A stall longer than 2 h (or 4.55 h at 2-s slots) turns into a certain, unslashed reorg of hours of "locked" blocks; P2's stated exceptions do not include it; users acting at the locked level during an outage are not warned by the level table.

Required revision: make assignment recording implicit in every landing and poke (the landing path already loads the keys), or keep roles resolvable for as long as certificates are landable (the 2,048-term ring exists); retag the three "proven" claims as conditional and add the EIP-2935 case to the limitations table with the slot-time dependence stated; consider DEGRADE_AFTER < ROLE_HORIZON.

### F8 (Medium) REPLACE/RESUME dedup key contradicts the S1/S3a evidence keys; the REPLACE view number is undefined

Trace, as corrected by the verifier and re-checked. Preconf §6 keys attestation dedup on "(term, view, opening object, height), so a REPLACE fork by the same holder is not equivocation" (preconf.html:88), and landing A2 says "Attestations are indexed by (t, v, height), so this is not equivocation" (landing.html:81), which is itself a different key. S1 is "equal (chainId, term, view, height), different phHash" (slashing.html:41), S3a single is "equal (t, v, height), different phHash", the fork-choice rule stores two blocks at equal (t, v, height) as equivocation evidence, and gossip mutes a signer after a second message per (signer, t, v, height, type); none of them carries the opening object. No page assigns a view number to a REPLACE or RESUME view: openings are defined only for TIMEOUT (leader(t, v+1)) and TERM_END/FALLBACK (leader(t+1, 0)); A5 says a view opens with exactly one opening object; L4 requires isHolder(t, v, sequencer). The attacker's claim that the fork reuses heights under the old (t', v') is wrong (A4 binds termId to the block timestamp, and replaceableFrom is ≥ 35 min past termEnd(t')), so the collision needs the same holder's same current view to contain both original-tail and fork blocks at one height. That is exactly the design's primary replacement use case (a short unprovable head range, the holder forking mid-view), so it is reachable, not merely theoretical; when it occurs, a design-following holder and up to 32 attesters are class-A slashable by anyone for a 2,000-TAIKO challenger reward, or clients honouring the (t, v, height, phHash) slashing-protection record refuse to sign and replacement fails until a forced batch or dead mode.

Design gap. TH8's "no honest party can be slashed by accusation" is violated by the design's own replacement procedure; the specification is incomplete (view numbering for REPLACE/RESUME) and self-contradictory (three different dedup keys).

Required revision: include the opening-object hash (or the VC hash) in the S1, S3a single, S3a pair, S3c keys, in the fork-choice equivocation record, in the gossip dedup key and in the client slashing-protection record; define the REPLACE/RESUME view number (for example v_replace = a fresh view index above V_MAX reserved for replacement, checked by isHolder against the current holder) and reconcile preconf §6 with landing A2.

### F9 (Medium) FALLBACK is one committee deep; two consecutive sub-Q committees wedge the term chain until dead mode

Trace, as re-checked. Preconf §5 authorizes exactly one committee to close a term without its own TERM_END: "committee(t+1) signs a FALLBACK VC for the highest (v, lock) it holds" (preconf.html:83). V2 requires a term's first block to carry a VC closing the previous view; the VIEW machine leaves ACTIVE(t, v) only on a VC for (t, v); landing L4 requires a contiguous VC chain from lastVCHash. If committee(t) and committee(t+1) both have ≥ 11 non-signers (persistent abstention, or a correlated outage), term t is never closed, leader(t+1, 0) and leader(t+2, 0) cannot produce a V2-valid first block, and no rule lets committee(t+2) sign for t; signerTerm exists in the VC struct (interfaces.html, landing §9) but no rule says which signer terms are accepted beyond t and t+1. Sequencing §13's "the next primary starts at term end" contradicts V2. Abstention is unslashable and unstruck. At c = 0.2 to 0.33 the adjacent-pair probability per term is ~0.2 % to ~13 % (design's own table). A transient outage self-heals when committee(t+1) returns (FALLBACK has no deadline); only persistent abstention wedges to dead mode after 75 terms; any certified prefix stays landable without a VC (landing §2), so only the uncertified tail is lost.

Design gap. The liveness ladder's "Committee silent → next committee signs a FALLBACK" rung is one deep; the failure analysis (preconf §13) treats only a single dark committee.

Required revision: allow any later committee within ROLE_HORIZON to sign a FALLBACK for an open predecessor term, with signerTerm checked at landing against the recorded or computable committee; state the rule in preconf §5 and landing L4; fix sequencing §13.

### F10 (Low) Cross-page constant drift

Confirmed drift (five sub-claims): TERM_RING is 2,048 terms at landing.html:164 and slashing.html:169 but 21,600 (aliased Shasta ring) with COMMITTEE_RING = 2,048 at parameters.html:73 and bridge-migration.html:73; the anchor figure says "≤ 2048 headers" (bridge-migration.html:53) while the normative rows set MAX_L1_HEADERS_PER_LANDING = 4,096 (bridge-migration.html:159, parameters.html:82) from a 2,880-header worst case; the FI queue walk is "≤ 50k" at landing.html:43 and :161 but "≤ 270k" at parameters.html:70 and forced-inclusion.html:127; rights are "≤ 100k" at landing.html:43 but isHolder is "≤ 290k worst case" at sequencing.html:219; the appended Inbox slot count/gap is 23/20 at interfaces.html:195 but 22/21 at bridge-migration.html:73, and Holders.mode has three values at sequencing.html:121 and four at interfaces.html:32. Refuted sub-claims: the README's Frame-Transaction baseline explicitly asks the design to mark what degrades if EIP-8141 slips, which l1-dependencies and frame-transactions do; the roles page's "termEnd + LAND_WINDOW (30 min)" duty is the economic deadline and matches landing §6's two-deadline definition. No exploitable trace: the stated header cap is 4,096 with the 2,880 worst case under it, and the worst-case gas sum (~2.4 M) stays inside the 10 M budget. Low.

Required revision: make the parameters page the single source and delete or link the duplicated numbers; derive the header cap in one place from ANCHOR_MAX_AGE + TERM + LAND_WINDOW_MAX + ABANDON_GRACE over the shortest slot time; align the slot count and the Holders.mode enum.

### F2 residual (refuted finding; wording fix still required)

The attack needs `register([65534])` while ~40 seats exist. Sequencing §2 defines the registry as "an append-only array" whose registration is an append scheduling a new length (LengthScheduled), and equates index count with seat count ("filling it costs 1.31 billion TAIKO"), so a single seat cannot set L_t = 65,535. But `register(uint16[] _seats)` plus "every named index free" is loose enough that an implementer could allow sparse indices. Required wording: a named index must be either a FREE recycled hole (EXPIRED + RECYCLE_GRACE) or equal to the scheduled length; and the committee walk (sequencing §8) needs a stated try bound and a stated result when fewer than 32 eligible non-holder seats exist (which F1 also needs).

## 4. Refuted findings

- F2 (halt, claimed High): refuted by sequencing.html §2 and §5, which define the seat array as append-only with the length scheduled per registration (LengthScheduled(len, effectiveAt)) and price a full array at 65,535 × B_SEAT, so one bonded seat cannot occupy index 65,534 and dilute density; dilution by churn costs a full B_SEAT locked ~9 days per hole and self-heals because entrants prefer cheap overwrites. Residual (Low) recorded in §3 as a wording fix.
- F10 sub-claims on the Frame-Transaction baseline and on the 30-versus-60-minute deadline: refuted by README "marks which parts degrade gracefully if they slip" and by landing §6's explicit economic/evidence deadline pair.

## 7. What the attackers could not break (consolidated)

From the halt report, the censor report, and my own re-reading; each item names the rule that stopped it.

1. Handoff ambush and withholding W1-W5: V2/V3 (a term's first block carries the recorded VC and extends the lock), recordViewChange on L1 within seconds, and S3c requiring 2Q − K = 12 double-signers; a private lock needs Q + 1 colluders and is bounded by S7 (30 min, 23 seats). Certified-but-withheld state is at most two blocks and dies within TIMEOUT.
2. Seed grinding and entry/exit timing: Rule D (DELAY_REG = 7,200 s > CYCLE + LOOKBACK − TERM = 4,740 s) with the freshness window; the parameter dependency chain (DELAY_S, ROLE_HORIZON, SUSPEND(3), DEAD_TERMS × TERM > LAND_WINDOW_MAX + ABANDON_GRACE, anchor windows inside EIP-2935) re-computed and consistent.
3. Sybil monopolization by registration: linear seat cost with the super-linear share curve H·X/(1 − X), CAP = 8 per address, future-dated writes from a 20-minute-old snapshot, no roster vote; exclusion by a full array costs more than the token supply. (Bounded and priced, as the design states; F3 shows the ladder, not registration, is the cheap lever.)
4. Censoring or front-running a registration: a plain future-dated L1 write, FOCIL-eligible; only T1 dependence.
5. Making a landing revert through the FI queue: anchored-time due-ness (T_floor from storage plus the chain's own term id), pull-based fees, bounded walk; the censor's stale-anchor loop is bounded by V8's MAX_FI_RUN and the 102-minute argument.
6. Forging a checkpoint through a false anchor (TH17): guest header chain to the tip, blockhash/EIP-2935 tip check on L1, monotone A3; a fake root can be attested only by ≥ Q attesters skipping V5 and can never land.
7. Opening dead mode on a slow but live chain: 75 terms exceed LAND_WINDOW_MAX + ABANDON_GRACE.
8. Burning the shared nonce or the landing slot with Frame Transactions: two-shape gate; without EIP-8141 losers pay ~25k gas plus a blob fee only.
9. ePBS Empty slots and 2-s slots: every deadline in seconds; an Empty slot delays inclusion by one slot.
10. DAO inaction on any liveness path: no owner-gated function found (F4's guest-image completeness is the one exception this round).
11. Key theft and rogue-key BLS framing: proof of possession at registration; the capped challenger share (2,000 TAIKO) keeps self-report and theft unprofitable.
12. Double-signing griefing (S3a/S3c): evidence requires the accused keys' own conflicting signatures; the initiating cartel loses its own ledger; no way to make an honest key sign twice short of a cryptographic break.
13. Steal-goal surface (from my re-reading, since the steal report was truncated): no rule pays a lander or attester without a landed proof and a valid end certificate; the journal names no lander so proof copying steals nothing; rewards are same-transaction debits from the holder's locked bond; challenger payouts are capped and require the accused's signatures or an L1 fact. The only bond-loss paths against honest parties found this round are F3, F5 and F8, all liveness/griefing rather than theft.
