## R4H-1 [High] The eligible list is positional and history-free, so the inbox cannot recompute holders for any term whose start precedes a mutation that took effect before its landing; honest terms become unlandable on ordinary churn
Requirement: R1, R7, P3/P4, P7 (locality claim 2); TH12/TH15. Builds on R3H-5: the round-3 fix (sortition over a swap-and-pop list) broke the property that made the seat-array walk history-free.

Why: the old walk read Seat{activeFrom, activeUntil} per index, so `eligible(i, termStart(t))` was position-independent and evaluable from current storage at any later time. The new walk maps `idx(t,k) mod E_t` to a *list position*, and swap-and-pop moves elements on every removal. The contract keeps one live list ("This is what lets the contract keep no history"), so once any mutation with effective time E in (termStart(t), landingTime] has been applied, the position->seat map the inbox evaluates for term t differs from the one every node evaluated at termStart(t).

Trace (12-s slots, honest network, attacker A with one spare seat; any honest registrant does the same for free):
1. 10:00:00 A calls register(1) (bond 21,000 + 6,200 TAIKO, returned after exit + 7 d). Mutation "enter list" effective E = 12:00:00 (DELAY_REG). Rule D: known to every node.
2. Term t has termStart(t) = 11:59:30. Nodes compute holders/committee from the list at 11:59:30 (A not yet in it). H = holder(t,0) is honest and produces certified blocks 11:59:30..12:00:29.
3. 12:00:05 H's first landing of term t (blocks up to 11:59:50) is submitted. L4: "applied lazily by the first land, registry call or poke after the effective time" -> the landing applies A's entry at position E_t (append) before evaluating isHolder. An append does not move existing positions, so this one passes. But at 12:00:06 an exit or suspension whose activeUntil/suspension start is 12:00:00 is applied (or A's own requestExit at 10:00:01 -> effective 12:00:01): swap-and-pop moves the last seat into the hole.
4. 12:00:25 H lands blocks 11:59:51..12:00:20 of term t: the inbox walks idx(t,k) over the *current* list; position idx(t,0) now maps to a different seat -> isHolder(t,0,H) false -> revert NotHolder. No lander can ever satisfy L4 for term t; recordAssignment(t) computes the same wrong pin; "eligibleAt(_time)" has no history to answer with.
5. Sequential landing (L1 "NotNext") stalls every later term behind t. Nothing lands until A1 opens at ~12:36-13:05; the current holder forks a REPLACE view from lastLanded and voids ~35-65 min of honest locked blocks (void waiver: nobody penalised, nobody paid). If a further mutation takes effect inside the replacing term's window, the replacement reverts too; after 75 unlanded terms only dead mode (view 255, no isHolder) lands.

Design text: "maintained by the contract with future-dated swap-and-pop (a seat enters the list at its activeFrom and leaves it at its activeUntil or suspension start, applied lazily by the first land, registry call or poke after the effective time, in effective-time order; Rule D makes the list at termStart(t) a function of S(t)). Seat identity stays the append-only array index; the walk maps a list position to a seat id." and "Term t evaluates eligibility at the single instant termStart(t). This is what lets the contract keep no history and need no proofs."

Cost/gain: zero beyond gas for a natural exit, suspension, reserve dip or registration; an attacker sustaining one removal per 30 min needs ~2.8 M TAIKO revolving (returned). Gain: a 35-65-min landing halt plus a locked-block reorg per mutation, repeatable; with mutations every few minutes the chain lands only through dead mode.
Fix: make the sortition domain history-free again: keep the list append-only with tombstones plus a per-term eligible count (Fenwick/prefix tree over `eligible(i, termStart(t))`), or version the list per term (store the swap log so the inbox can undo mutations later than termStart(t)), or pin every term's digest atomically at the first mixing transaction at or after termStart(t) *before* applying any mutation with E > termStart(t) and make L4 read only pins. State the tie-break for equal effective times.

## R4H-2 [High] FI_SKIP is evaluated against lastLanded, not per entry, so one poison entry skips the whole queue behind it, burns every honest requester's bond, and the 24-h skip counter then prices forced inclusion out of reach
Requirement: R1, P4/P5 (TH7); builds on R3H-1: the round-3 fix (FI_BOND burned on skip, fee floor x4 per skip) is inadequate because the skip predicate and the escalation counter are global, so the victims, not the poster, pay and escalate.

Trace (calldata entries; L1 time in s after the last ordinary landing L0 with anchorTipTimestamp = A0):
1. 0 s: attacker P saves calldata entry E0 whose transactions are decodable but exceed FI_ZK_GAS_LIMIT on both guests (the design's own residual, L16). Cost 0.05 ETH bond + 0.001 ETH fee. Due at A0+300.
2. 348 s onward every valid block must include E0 (V8); every landing path must consume the head (L8, forced batch step 4, dead mode obeys A1): nothing lands. Users who are now censored post honest FI entries E1..E20 during the stall (each 0.05 ETH bond + rising fee; that is the design's escape hatch).
3. 14,400 s: skipped(E0, T) holds. Anyone builds the forced batch: "m = min(due entries at T_A, 64) ... the batch has exactly m blocks, all FI blocks", derived from lastLanded. Block 0 derives E0 to an anchor-only block (skip). Block j derives E_j: the predicate is "the head entry ... is due at T and T − lastLanded.anchorTipTimestamp ≥ FI_SKIP"; lastLanded is the state *before* this batch (it is updated only in step 7 of the forced landing), so T − A0 ≥ 14,400 still holds and E1..E20 are skipped too. Bonds and fees of 21 entries burned (~1.05 ETH of honest money), skip counter = 21.
4. The required fee for any new entry is now FI_BASE_FEE × 4^21 ≈ 4.4·10^9 ETH for 24 h: forced inclusion is unusable, and the censored users have lost their bonds and their place. Even under the narrowest reading (only E0 skipped), the same batch still burns nothing else but the counter is 1 and every subsequent natural 4-h outage (both provers, L1 fees above the 100-gwei break-even for 4 h) skips the honest head entry and quadruples the floor for everyone.
5. 24 h later the counter window empties; P repeats for another 0.051 ETH. Steady state: ~4 h of full stall plus ~20 h of FI blackout per day, and the L16 "geometric price" is paid by victims.

Design text: "void(i, T) := ... ∨ skipped(i, T), where skipped(i, T) holds for the head entry of either kind iff it is due at T and T − lastLanded.anchorTipTimestamp ≥ FI_SKIP = 14,400 s" and "msg.value ≥ FI_BOND + FI_BASE_FEE × (FI_FEE_THRESHOLD + pending) / FI_FEE_THRESHOLD × 4^skips, where skips is the number of head entries skipped in the last 24 h" and "the n-th such entry within 24 h costs its poster 0.05 + 0.001 × 4^(n−1) ETH".

Cost/gain: 0.051 ETH per day (the poster's first skip is the cheap one; escalation is charged to later posters). Gain: forced inclusion (the only censorship-resistance path and the bridge-exit path) disabled for the whole day, honest bonds burned, and a locked-block reorg per cycle.
Fix: key skips to the entry (skipped only if this entry has been the head across a landing gap of FI_SKIP measured from *its own* first due-ness, i.e. store headSince), skip exactly one entry per forced/replacement landing (then lastLanded.anchorTipTimestamp resets and later entries get a fresh 4 h), and make the escalation per poster (fee floor keyed on msg.sender's skips) or refund honest entries' bonds when the skipping entry was not theirs.

## R4H-3 [High] The landing reserve is withdrawable at any time and the assignment is public 20 minutes ahead, so a holder empties its reserve after the snapshot and certifies a term nobody is paid to land (R3H-2 fix inadequate)
Requirement: R7, P4 (TH2, TH13). The round-3 fix requires LAND_RESERVE at *registration* and future-dates reserve-ineligibility by DELAY_S, but withdrawal of everything above 1.05 × floor stays permitted at any time; Rule D then guarantees the holder keeps every term already snapshotted.

Trace (attacker A, one seat, registry of ~72 seats):
1. A registers with 1.05 × 20,000 + 6,200 TAIKO. Eligible from τ+7,200.
2. S(t) = termStart(t) − 1,200 s. At S(t)+1 every node (and A) knows A = holder(t, 0). A calls withdraw(6,200) — "Partial withdrawal of any excess over 105 % of the floor at any time" / "withdraw ... limited to the excess over 1.05 × floor". Balance is now exactly 1.05 × floor; the reserve is 0.
3. The reserve rule fires: "A reserve below the per-term exposure makes the owner ineligible, future-dated by DELAY_S, until topped up" → effective at S(t)+1+1,320 = termStart(t)+121 > termEnd(t). Rule D: term t (and any other term whose S is already past) still belongs to A. A tops up 6,200 at termStart(t)+60 (before the ineligibility instant; the roles machine says SUSPENDED_OPEN needs top-up + reactivate at τ+1,320, so at worst A loses 22 min of eligibility, and it can withdraw again for its next draw since the reserve rule reads a debit, not a withdrawal).
4. termStart(t)..termEnd(t): A produces 5 blocks of 390,123 bytes of incompressible calldata (V9 allows it; 75 % of the base fee returns to A's coinbase), total 1,950,615 B ≤ TERM_BYTES_MAX; then 55 near-empty blocks. Attesters check V1-V6, V8-V10 and certify; the term needs three 6-blob landings (~2 M gas each).
5. termEnd(t) onward: the ramp reaches 4/block + 1,300/landing at +30 min, but "A debit of x is min(x, balance)" and reward debits stop at 1.05 × floor: every lander is credited 0. A does not land. Landing is sequential: every honest term after t waits.
6. replaceableFrom = termEnd(t) + 35..65 min: the current holder forks a REPLACE view, voiding term t and every honest locked block since. S4a on A: the outage gate needs "a term strictly later than t landed within OUTAGE_WINDOW before the hard deadline", impossible while landing was stuck behind t; the void waiver covers the record. A pays nothing.
7. Repeat at every draw (1/72 of terms ≈ 20 terms/day).

Design text: "Reward-type debits (ramps, blob reward, boost, attester share) are drawn from the holder's landing reserve, a ledger amount of LAND_RESERVE = 6,200 TAIKO that registration and reactivation require on top of 1.05 × the seat floor ... A reserve below the per-term exposure makes the owner ineligible, future-dated by DELAY_S, until topped up; so every certified term has a funded lander reward by construction" versus "Withdrawal. While seats exist: the excess over 105 % of the floor."

Cost/gain: gas plus ~0.01 ETH of L2 fees per draw, no bond at risk. Gain: a 35-65-min landing halt and a reorg of the honest window per draw; bridging stalls for that window each time. Related: even honest owners are 39 TAIKO (≈ 7 held terms of the 6-TAIKO attester share, which is debited on self-landing too) from the exposure threshold, contradicting "a fresh owner is never one debit from suspension".
Fix: lock the reserve like the seat stake (withdrawable only through requestExit + EVIDENCE_WINDOW, or with a DELAY_REG-dated withdrawal request), require reserve ≥ exposure at S(t) for eligibility (evaluated with the same future-dated rule as seats), and keep a margin of at least one term's exposure above the threshold.

## R4H-4 [High] The "timeout only on leader silence" fix (C1) is defeated by V3: a live leader can sign at most two uncertified blocks, so an abstaining 11-seat minority still makes the honest majority time out the primary, take its term and bleed it
Requirement: R6 (anti-monopoly cost curve), P3, TH8/TH9/TH10. Builds on C1: the round-3 fix assumed the leader keeps producing attestable blocks while certification is withheld; the validity predicate forbids that.

Trace (full committee m = 32, cartel C holds 11 committee seats and the view-1 backup; honest primary H; honest attesters A1..A21; times in s after termStart):
1. 0.0 H signs PH(h+1) carrying the opening VC; 0.3 A1..A21 attest (21 < Q = 22: no certificate). 1.0 H signs PH(h+2) carrying the VC ("The first two blocks of a view carry the VC instead"); attested by 21, no certificate.
2. 2.0 H cannot sign a valid PH(h+3): V3 requires "carriedCertHeight ∈ {n−1, n−2}" and no C(h+1) or C(h+2) exists; its client is in HOLD ("PRODUCING --height − highest own certified > CERT_LAG_MAX+1--> HOLD"). Any block it signed anyway is dropped by every honest attester (V3 fails; "failure is silence").
3. 6.0..7.0 each honest attester's condition holds: no new certificate for TIMEOUT and "has itself attested no block of (t, v) in that window" (its last attestation was at 1.3 s, the window is the last 5 s). A1..A21 sign TO(t, 0, TIMEOUT); C's 11 sign as well (they attested nothing).
4. 7.4 anyone aggregates 32 timeouts, proposes VC(t, 0, lock = h) with C(h) (the previous lock); all 32 cast lock votes; the VC forms. C's backup, holder(t, 1), starts at h+1 and C's 11 now attest, so the term proceeds under C.
5. The landing of view 1 carries the TIMEOUT VC: "Each TIMEOUT-kind VC applies the absence penalty to the timed-out leader": H is debited 50 TAIKO, burned. If view 1 is honest, the same 11 seats repeat steps 1-4 and the backup is bled too; after V_MAX the term idles.

Every step follows the rules as written; no key signs anything slashable (silence is unslashable). Hence, contrary to the slashing page's table, a cartel with ≥ 11 committee seats captures every term in which it also holds a backup view, and blanks (and bleeds 50 TAIKO per view from) every other one. The round-3 simulation figures ("Cartel ≥ 11 of the committee (blank term)": 62 % at 33 % of seats) therefore describe *takeovers*, not blank terms, and the "term share = seat share" claim, the linear cost curve and the "absence bleed is no longer reachable by a minority" sentence all fall back to the round-3 C1 state (63-87 % capture at a third of the seats). Liveness consequence: an honest owner drawn as primary in such terms loses its term and 50 TAIKO per draw; at the table's 62 % rate a single-seat owner reaches the 50 % hard floor (200 bleeds) in ~2 weeks at 40 seats and is suspended.

Design text: "It is signed for kind TIMEOUT only when the attester has seen no new certificate for TIMEOUT seconds and has itself attested no block of (t, v) in that window (round 3, C1: attesters that attested a live leader never time it out, so an abstaining minority cannot manufacture a takeover)" and "carriedCertHeight ∈ {n−1, n−2} (CERT_LAG_MAX = 1) ... The first two blocks of a view carry the VC instead" and "the honest 21 keep attesting, no view change forms, the primary keeps its term at the sequenced level and nobody is penalized".

Cost/gain: 11 committee seats (probability per the hypergeometric table; 0 % at the launch condition with single-seat cartels, 62 % at 32 of 96 seats), no slashing. Gain: term capture plus a 50-TAIKO bleed of every honest holder per view.
Fix: either let a leader keep producing *valid* blocks without a certificate (drop the V3 cert requirement while no certificate exists in the view and bound the unattested tail differently), or define leader silence as "no valid signed header received in the window" (attesters that *received and validated* a block do not time out even if they cannot attest above their lock), so that abstention alone never produces a VC; and re-derive the slashing page §5 table with takeovers counted.

## R4H-5 [High] A REPLACE view change recorded after the original backlog has already landed closes the live view on L1 and voids fresh locked blocks without a landing, a deadline or a slash
Requirement: P2 (locked blocks revert only by 12 slashings, a landed replacement after the deadline machinery, or an L1 reorg), R6; builds on R3-S4: the recorded-REPLACE close was specified without a freshness check.

Trace (current holder H honest, committee honest, lander L honest, griefer G = anyone with gas; L1 12-s slots):
1. R: replaceableFrom passes after a proving outage; lastLanded = (t0, v0, h0).
2. R+50 H (following A2) forms a REPLACE VC for its view (t, v) with l1Ref = block N at R+2 (canonical, 48 s old; A1 holds there). Attesters check A1 at N in their view and cast REPLACE lock votes at lock h0; VC_R = {t, v, closes: orig, REPLACE, lock h0, l1Ref N} exists at R+51 and is gossiped.
3. R+52 L's landing of the original backlog h0+1..h0+900 (a proof finished during the outage) is included first; per A3 "Until a REPLACE VC is recorded on L1 ... landing the original stays valid and pays the ramp". lastLanded = h0+900, lastLanded.at = R+52, replaceableFrom moves ~35 min ahead. H's recordViewChange(VC_R) in the same slot lands after L's landing (or H drops it). H continues the original view: blocks h0+901.. certified and locked over the next minutes.
4. R+150 G calls recordViewChange(VC_R): "may be called by anyone at any time and stores the first VC per (t, v, closes)". VC_R is a valid Q-signed object; S3d does not apply (A1 held at N). Nothing in the rule compares VC_R.lock (h0) with lastLanded (h0+900) or re-evaluates A1 at record time.
5. Effects as written: "From that record on, the inbox refuses original blocks above lastLanded (the record names the closed opening object and the lock)" — H's blocks h0+901..h0+1,000 (locked) can never land; every attester that observes "a recorded REPLACE VC" enters RESET ("every certificate above lastLanded is void for it, every attester's lock resets to lastLanded") and may sign only RESUME under the old object. H must reopen with a RESUME view from h0+900 and re-include the transactions. ~100 s of locked blocks reverted; no deadline had passed (lastLanded.at is fresh), no replacement landed, nobody is slashable. S4a: the void waiver protects H, so the reorg is free for everyone.
6. G can repeat whenever an outage ends with a REPLACE VC that lost the race, and H itself (or the cartel's backup) can hold such a VC for later use as long as its original object stays open.

Design text: "recordViewChange(vc) may be called by anyone at any time and stores the first VC per (t, v, closes); later landings must match. A recorded REPLACE VC also closes the original tail on L1: from that record on, the inbox refuses to land original blocks above lastLanded" and "Lock reset. When a node observes a landing flagged as a replacement, a forced landing, or a recorded REPLACE VC, every certificate above lastLanded is void for it, every attester's lock resets to lastLanded".

Cost/gain: one L1 transaction (~150k gas). Gain: an unpunishable revert of locked blocks (the level table promises 12 slashings) and a RESUME round for the committee; repeatable per outage.
Fix: recordViewChange for kind REPLACE (and RESUME) must require vc.lockHeight == lastLanded.height, vc.lockPhHash == lastLanded.phHash and block.timestamp > replaceableFrom at record time (A1 re-evaluated), and the attester RESET must key on a record whose lock equals the node's own view of lastLanded; otherwise the record is refused (or stored as inert).

## R4H-6 [Medium] V10 counts forced-inclusion blocks against the term budget: a blob-shaped FI backlog exhausts a term at second ~23, after which neither an FI block nor a sequencer block is valid; the honest primary and every backup are timed out and bled, and the forced batch is unprovable above 15 blob entries if the guest applies V10 to it
Requirement: P3, P4/P5 (TH7 stuffing bounds, "two entries per three seconds" claim), R6 (TH8 bleed).

Trace (attacker S, blob-shaped entries; honest primary H, backups B1..B4, honest committee):
1. S saves N = 100 blob entries of 130,044 bytes each (bond 0.05 ETH each, refunded on consumption; quadratic fee 0.001 × (100 + 4,950) ≈ 5 ETH; ~196k state gas each). Due at +300 s.
2. Term t begins after the anchor passes due-ness. V8 forces FI, FI, seq, FI, FI, seq, ... Each FI block's canonical record is ≈ 130,100 bytes (the blob bytes are the txListBytes). After 15 FI blocks (~23 s) the term's sum is ≥ 1,951,500 B > TERM_BYTES_MAX = 1,950,660.
3. Block 24 must be an FI block (V8: the last two blocks ending at the parent are seq, FI, not both FI) but fails V10; a sequencer block fails V8. No valid block exists for the rest of the term. H holds; honest attesters have attested nothing for 5 s and sign TIMEOUT; VC(t, 0) forms; B1 faces the identical wall and is timed out; B2..B4 likewise; after V_MAX the term idles.
4. The landing carrying the four TIMEOUT VCs debits 50 TAIKO from H and from each of B1..B4 ("Each TIMEOUT-kind VC applies the absence penalty to the timed-out leader once per (t, v)"): 250 TAIKO burned per term from honest owners who could not act. Users get ~8 s of sequencer blocks per minute, not "one block in three".
5. The backlog drains at 15 entries per term, not 40: N = 100 buys ~7 dead terms; the L8 "one hour of delay ≈ 60 ETH" figure becomes ~22 ETH per hour, and the design's throughput floor is 2.7× too high.
6. If the guest asserts V10 on forced batches (the FI page says the guest checks V10 "as well" and forced blocks carry no term binding), a forced batch with m = min(due, 64) ≥ 16 blob entries exceeds the budget and "exactly m blocks" makes it unprovable: 16 blob entries (~0.9 ETH in refundable bonds and fees) disable the forced path during a stall until 15 are consumed some other way.

Design text: "V10 term budget: the sum of canonical record bytes over the blocks of one term is at most TERM_BYTES_MAX = 15 × 130,044 = 1,950,660 bytes" and "A backlog drains at two blocks in three while the sequencer keeps one block in three" and "Throughput floor: two entries per three seconds under any chain that lands at all" and "m = min(due entries at T_A, 64) ≥ 1 and the batch has exactly m blocks, all FI blocks".

Cost/gain: ~5 ETH plus gas per 100 entries (bonds refunded); gain: 7 terms with 37 s of dead time each, 250 TAIKO of honest bleed per term, a 2.7× slower FI drain, and (under the strict reading) a disabled forced path. The same wall is reachable by the primary itself: five 390-KB blocks fill the budget at second 5 and every honest backup is bled 50 TAIKO for a term nobody can serve (zero cost to a primary that keeps 75 % of its own base fees).
Fix: exclude FI blocks from V10 (they are already bounded by one blob each and MAX_FI_PER_LANDING) or size TERM_BYTES_MAX for 40 FI blocks; exempt forced batches from V10 explicitly; never apply the absence penalty to a view whose VC follows a V10-exhausted term (or make V10 a per-view budget so a backup starts fresh); restate the stuffing arithmetic.

## R4H-7 [Medium] The per-landing reward is paid per landing, not per landing a term needs, so single-block landings during a prover outage drain the holder's reserve in five transactions, leave the rest of the term unfunded and make the holder ineligible
Requirement: R7, P4; TH2/TH8 (bond griefing through rewards).

Trace (holder H honest, its prover down; lander G):
1. Term t: 60 certified blocks, 3 blobs total. H's prover dies; H signs no boost. At termEnd + 30 min r = 4/block, r_land = 1,300/landing.
2. G lands block h+1 alone (one blob): credited 4 + 1 + 1,300 = 1,305 TAIKO, debited from H's reserve (6,200). Nothing caps the count: "r_land ... is paid once per landing whose first segment belongs to the term". Landing 2..4 (blocks h+2, h+3, h+4) each earn 1,305. After landing 4 the reserve holds 980; landing 5 takes the rest ("A debit of x is min(x, balance)"). Five landings × ~2 M gas ≈ 10 M gas ≈ 0.5 ETH at 50 gwei for 6,200 TAIKO ≈ 0.93 ETH: profitable in its own right.
3. H's reserve is now 0 < per-term exposure → H is ineligible from τ + 1,320 until it tops up; blocks h+6..h+60 (55 blocks, still 3 blobs) have no funded reward: "so every certified term has a funded lander reward by construction" is false for them. Nobody but H lands them; if H's prover is still down, sequential landing stalls behind them until H tops up 6,200 or A1 opens and the current holder forks a REPLACE view (voiding the honest backlog; S4a on H reverts under the outage gate only if no later term landed, which here is guaranteed since landing was stuck).
4. G can do this to every term whose holder is slow after term end, and to a holder whose prover is merely late (the ramp starts at termEnd with r_land = 0, but r_land reaches 100 TAIKO after 138 s, already above the ~50 TAIKO gas cost of a landing at low fees).

Design text: "plus one per-landing component r_land(τ) for each landing a term needs ... r_land ramps from 0 to R_LAND_MAX (1,300 TAIKO ...) over the same window and is paid once per landing whose first segment belongs to the term. V10 caps a term at 15 blobs, so at most three landings are ever owed" (the cap is an inference; no rule limits payments to ⌈blobs/6⌉).

Cost/gain: 5 landings ≈ 10 M gas; gain up to 6,200 TAIKO from an honest reserve, the owner's ineligibility for ≥ 22 min, and a landing stall on the unfunded tail.
Fix: pay r_land at most ⌈(blobs of the term landed so far)/MAX_BLOBS⌉ times per term (track per-term landings in TermRecord), or pro-rate r_land by the fraction of the term's V10 bytes the landing carries; keep the reserve threshold above one term's worst case plus one landing.

## R4H-8 [Low] Internal contradictions between pages and learn/ after the round-3 pass (by consequence)
Requirement: documentation consistency (an implementer reading the wrong page builds a different rule).

1. The slashable REPLACE object. Roles §2, sequencing §7, landing §12, limitations L6 and preconf §8 still say "a REPLACE timeout whose L1 reference did not satisfy the replacement predicate" / "a REPLACE timeout with a false L1 reference is class B"; the slashing page and interfaces say timeouts carry no reference and S3d takes a LockVote (`slashFalseReplacement(LockVote ...)`). The slashing page's own interface sketch still declares `slashLockLie(TimeoutMsg calldata _to, ...)` while S3b's evidence is "a lock vote"; the interfaces page has `slashLockLie(LockVote ...)`. Consequence: an implementer of the roles/sequencing text slashes on an object that carries nothing to verify.
2. Glossary "Timeout | ... carrying its lock" contradicts the glossary's own "View change | Q lock votes ... (timeouts carry no lock)" and preconf §1.
3. Learn lesson 2: "Activation needs six distinct owners and 44 seats for full committees" versus the design's published launch condition of 8 owners and 72 seats (Anchor §5, sequencing §8, slashing §5); lesson 2 also opens with "a hash walk over the seat array" before describing the eligible list.
4. Learn lesson 5: "the first recorded per (term, view) wins" versus records keyed on (term, view, closes) (R3-S6 fix).
5. Landing §12 TH13: "up to about 100 gwei per term landed (400 gwei with four-term landings)" versus §5 "≈ 100 gwei per landing whatever the term's size" and the rule that r_land is paid "once per landing whose first segment belongs to the term" (a four-term landing collects one r_land, so 400 gwei is wrong; if the intent is one r_land per term covered, the rule text is wrong and R4H-7's drain is larger).
6. ## What I tried and could not break
1. REPLACE VC against an announced backlog (defeating announceLanding's priority with an earlier L1 reference): announceLanding is only callable "while replaceableFrom has not passed", so no block before the announcement can satisfy A1 (timestamp > the pre-announcement replaceableFrom never held); S3d slashes any other reference.
2. Leader-side "blank my own term" by gossiping at 4.9 s so that half the committee times out and half attests: the timeout window slides ("attested no block ... in that window"), so attesters that attested at 4.9 s sign TIMEOUT at 9.9 s and the VC forms 5 s late; no stuck view. Without a certificate the leader is anyway limited to two blocks (which is what R4H-4 exploits in the other direction).
3. Partition of the committee into two halves below Q: no certificates and no VC on either side; the term is closed by a later committee's FALLBACK VC (any depth); fail-closed with no penalty (MISS needs a VC, S4a needs the outage gate).
4. Sustained landing stall through an L1 fee spike above the 100-gwei break-even: the outage gate closes S4a, nothing voids before A1; the residual (one backlog reorg per stall by the holder or dead mode after 75 min) is limitation L6 and was not repeated.
