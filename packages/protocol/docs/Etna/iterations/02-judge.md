## 3. Confirmed and downgraded findings: trace, gap, required revision

Grouped by the design mechanism they touch, so that one revision closes each group.

### Group A: the opening object is not in the signed objects (S1, F3, S9); the certificate-pair rule has no conflict check (S1)

**S1 (High), re-checked.** The S3a pair row (slashing.html:43) states the offence as "two valid certificates at one (t, v, vcHash, height)" and the verification as "root check; popcount ≥ Q both; two aggregate verifications; every key in both bitmaps (≥ 2Q − K = 12 by pigeonhole) is slashed class A". The adjacent S1 and S3a-single rows require "different phHash"; the pair row does not, and `slashCertificatePair(Certificate a, Certificate b, CommitteeProof cp)` takes nothing else. Two valid certificates over one honest header are ordinary: "anyone holding Q attestations builds it" (preconf.html:25), and with 30 of 32 attestations on the public topic any two ≥ Q subsets are both valid (the carried certificate in the next block and the lander's endCert in LandInput are two such objects, both public on L1). So a seatless submitter burns the intersection (12 to 29 honest whole ledgers) for ≈ 600k gas and earns the capped 2,000-TAIKO challenger share; the §15 claim "each rule's accept set is two signatures by one key over conflicting messages" is intent, not the rule. Second part: `Attestation{termId, view, height, phHash, attesterIdx, sig}`, `TimeoutMsg{...}`, `Certificate{...}` (interfaces.html:33-37) carry no vcHash, and A_i is signed over `(DOMAIN_ATTEST ‖ chainId ‖ t ‖ v ‖ height ‖ phHash)` (preconf.html:24), so the F8 keys "(t, v, vcHash, height)" are not enforceable by the contract for attester evidence: vcHash is inside phHash only through the header preimage, which S3a evidence does not supply.

**F3 (Medium), re-checked.** The S3b row (slashing.html:44): "a timeout or VC signature by key i with lock L, plus an attestation by i on a header of the same term at a view ≤ v whose carried certificate is above L". A REPLACE VC per landing A2 has lock = lastLanded = ℓ (landing L4: "each VC's lock equal to the previous segment's last block"); the same honest attester earlier attested ℓ+40 carrying C(ℓ+39) > ℓ in the same (t, v). The offence holds verbatim on the accused's own two signatures; REPLACE/RESUME are exempted only from MISS (landing L4), not from S3b. For headers, PH.vcHash is "non-zero only on a view's first block" (bridge-migration.html:26), so every later block of the original tail and of the REPLACE fork carries vcHash = 0 and the S1 key collapses to (t, v, height): header ℓ+2 versus ℓ+2' is a valid S1 pair. Clients key their slashing-protection record on (t, v, vcHash, height) (preconf.html:48) and will therefore sign both sides believing it safe. Rational attesters never sign a REPLACE; replacement as specified is dead, and the fallback is dead mode at 75 minutes rather than ~35.

**S9 (Medium), re-checked.** Landing L4 (landing.html:34) accepts view 255 when "the term must be open-empty, in no-committee mode, or the chain in dead mode, and the sequencer bonded"; no isHolder check, although sequencing.html:72 says the no-committee holder "is still chosen by sortition". A bonded outsider lands its own one-block view-255 chain from lastLanded and voids the sortition holder's sequenced minute for gas plus two proofs. The loser cannot reopen: A5 (bridge-migration.html:36) says "(termId, view) changes iff vcHash ≠ 0 ... within a view none", the "dead-mode marker" is named on three pages and defined on none, and re-signing height h+2 on the winner's parent yields an S1 pair (t, 255, 0, h+2) with different phHash. Confirmed by the censor attacker's F1 fourth point independently (37 % of terms become a landing race with only four cartel seats beside five 8-seat honest operators).

Design gap. Round 1's F8 required every equivocation key to include the opening object; the revision changed the key text and the client record but not one signed message format, not the S3b rule, and not the sentinel views. TH8's "the accused must have had a way to avoid it" is violated by the design's own replacement procedure and by ordinary certification (S1).

Required revision:

1. S3a pair and the S6 certificate form: require `phHash_a ≠ phHash_b`; add the design's own proposed test "two valid certificates over one header slash nobody".
2. Put the view's vcHash into the attestation and timeout signing domains and into the Attestation, TimeoutMsg and Certificate structs, and carry vcHash in every signed header (not only the first block of a view), or require the PH preimage as evidence in every attester rule so the contract can read the opening object.
3. S3b: compare locks only within one opening object; additionally exempt kinds REPLACE and RESUME from S3b and make a REPLACE timeout slashable iff A1 did not hold at its L1 reference (an L1 fact), which is the objective form of "false replacement".
4. Define the dead-mode and no-committee opening object (for example keccak("ETNA_DEAD" ‖ lastLanded.blockHash ‖ t)), permit it in A5 as a fresh opening object after any landing, and require isHolder(t, 255-holder) for no-committee terms until the sortition holder's economic deadline, after which the open race applies.
5. Also fix the client slashing-protection record on slashing.html:138, which still reads "(t, v, height, phHash)".

### Group B: the round-1 F5 fix, both directions (S4 = F2, H2)

**S4 / F2 (Medium), re-checked.** preconf.html:89: a demand is answered by "a data post answerDemand(certHash, blobs): the canonical blobs of the range ... whose hashes the inbox records against the demand (no proof needed ...)"; interfaces.html:96 sketches `answerDemand(bytes32 _certHash, uint64 _firstHeight)` with no check; the S7 row needs only that "neither a landing nor a canonical-blob data post" exists. The certificate commits to phHash (keccak-type); the post is KZG versioned hashes; no rule links them, no rule voids or slashes a non-canonical post, and the design's own rejected list says why: "availability response as a DA post (the inbox cannot check derivation)" (preconf.html:173). So Q+1 colluders answer any demand with six arbitrary blobs, the certificate is never voided, the FALLBACK at a non-voided lock never triggers, and recovery waits for replacement (≥ 35-65 min) at a cost of at most SLASH_ABANDON. The collusion-table row "certify a block only they hold, or stall ≤ 30 min ... 23 seats = 460k TAIKO", limitation L3's price, and the glossary's "answered only by a landing" are all false as written. Users' transactions were ordered blind for a full term (TH6), contrary to "nobody transacted on private state".

**H2 (Medium), re-checked.** The post is "the canonical blobs of the range from lastLanded + 1 to the certified height, attached to an L1 transaction" (singular), and a transaction carries 6 blobs (3 per block at 2-s slots). At the client target of 32 KiB/s the range above lastLanded exceeds 6 blobs within ≈ 25 s. Whenever landing stalls for 30 minutes while the last landing L0 is inside the hour (any proving outage, fee spike above the break-even, or H1/poison content), a demand filed at L0 + 1 min matures with the outage gate open, and the honest committee holding all the data cannot answer. Harm: class B for every bitmap member and the leader (up to 460k TAIKO), plus three strikes and a floor check each, i.e. suspension of honest owners; duplicates are keyed per certificate, so one demand per certificate of the term hits the same committee repeatedly. The verifier's note is right that under S4 as written an honest committee can post any single blob and be safe; H2 becomes live the moment S4 is fixed by making posts checkable. The two must be fixed together.

Design gap. S7 tries to be an availability rule verified by an object the inbox cannot verify. Round 1 accepted the DA post to close the honest-but-unprovable false positive and thereby removed the rule's deterrent entirely.

Required revision (one coherent rule, not two patches): either (a) make the post checkable and unbounded in size: bind a per-range `blobHashesRoot` into a Q-signed object (for example a field of the certificate or of every header's PH), accept posts only from bonded bitmap members, let posts accumulate a prefix across several transactions within DEMAND_WINDOW, clear the demand when the accumulated prefix reaches the certified height, and slash a post whose hashes are not the committed ones as class B (the poster's own signed lie); or (b) drop the post: S7 is answered only by a proof-carrying landing, and a demand is suspended (fee refunded, no slash) whenever `replaceableFrom` has passed or the outage gate fails, since the unprovable-honest case is then handled by replacement and the quarantine list. Under either option, restore L3's price and the collusion-table row from the chosen rule, and fix the glossary.

### Group C: the outage gate and the forced/replacement void (S2, S3, H1)

**S2 (Medium), re-checked.** A4 (landing.html:83): the gate passes iff "some term landed within OUTAGE_WINDOW (60 min) before the hard deadline"; nothing constrains `_landedTermRef` relative to the reported term (slashing.html:46, :123; interfaces.html:109). Under the design's own lag ("attested ≈ s + 6 min, landed ≈ 2 to 3 min later", forced-inclusion.html:86) term t'−1 lands after termEnd(t'), so the last pre-outage landing sits inside the gate window of t' and t'+1. At DEADLINE_HARD anyone files reportAbandoned(t', v, cert, cp, t'−1) and debits an honest holder that had seconds before the outage began. This contradicts the proven claim "A chain-wide outage slashes nobody" (slashing.html:226) and the landing page's "a proving outage shorter than the horizon voids and penalizes nothing". Bounded (≈ two holders × 2,000 TAIKO per outage longer than an hour) but repeatable, permissionless and profitable (200 TAIKO reporter share plus 800 escrow to the next lander). The same gate is inherited by S7.

**S3 (High), re-checked.** forced-inclusion.html:70 lets anyone land a forced batch once `block.timestamp > replaceableFrom()` with one entry due at a lander-chosen anchor: "No rights check, no certificate, no absence penalty"; §7: "A forced landing voids exactly what a replacement voids". Given a ≥ 35-min landing stall with proving still possible (a fee spike above the published break-even, at which the design says landing "waits"; all landers offline; a head guest bug; H1), an outsider with no seat saves a calldata FI entry, waits for replaceableFrom = E + 2,100 s, and lands a one-block forced batch for 0.001 ETH plus ≈ 1.2 M gas. Every certified and locked block above lastLanded is void; the forced block executes on the pre-stall state, so a transaction that spent funds inside the voided backlog (credited by a counterparty at "locked") is double-spent; re-inclusion is a client duty (landing.html:81) and V8 orders due FI entries before sequencer blocks, so even an honest RESUME runs the forced transaction first. Then: "Forced landings never write the fields the outage gate reads" (forced-inclusion.html:74) is true, but the RESUME ordinary landing that follows lands within [termEnd, DEADLINE_HARD] of every stalled term, and the waiver on the same line keeps "an abandoner, whose deadline passed before the forced landing" reportable: at E + 2,350 s that is every term whose economic deadline had passed (≈ five to nine honest holders × 2,000 TAIKO), reportable by anyone. L6 accepts holder-driven replacement griefing because "landing the backlog pays the maximum per block and is more lucrative" and "the replacer gets no penalty share" (landing.html:182); neither binds an outsider using the attester-free forced path, and the double-spend makes the reorg profitable. The level table does name "batch replacement at the landing deadline" as a locked-revert route, but limitations.html:32 labels confirmations "outage" only after DEGRADE_AFTER (2 h) while the zero-slash revert opens at 35 min, and the published 39 ETH "locked" bound is the slashing-route bound only.

Why High: an unpunishable reorg of locked blocks at the attacker's chosen moment, a double-spend, and inverted accountability (honest holders slashed, the forcer unslashable), at negligible cost. The precondition is exogenous and rare (a few landing stalls per year), which bounds frequency, not impact; and the design's own acceptance rationale for L6 (unprofitable, holder-only) is what fails.

**H1 (downgraded to Medium), re-checked.** No V-rule bounds a block's canonical record; MAX_ENVELOPE = 524,288 bytes is the only per-block byte bound and is justified as "≈ 2.3 × the per-second blob budget" (preconf.html:167); zk-gas charges no per-byte cost beyond TX_INTRINSIC_ZK_GAS (`docs/zk_gas_spec.md:71`), and 400 KB of calldata is well under the 45 M block gas limit (`docs/Derivation.md:374`), so honest attesters certify and lock it. A landing's blobs travel in one transaction (L6), landing is sequential (L1), and the design's own slot table gives 3 blobs per L1 block at 2-s slots: a block whose record exceeds 3 × 130,044 bytes is unlandable by anyone and everything above it waits. The design's "blobs per block 10/7/3 so landers shrink ranges" (landing.html:205) cannot shrink below one block. Downstream: replacement at replaceableFrom, ≈ 35 honest terms voided and S4a-reportable (the L6 residual), the attacker paying one 2,000-TAIKO liveness debit per event. Why Medium rather than High: the halt is not permanent (the replacing holder may drop the oversized transaction; re-inclusion is a client duty, no validity or slashing rule sanctions the drop, and the design lists poison content as "handled by replacement plus a client-side blacklist"); the absolute form exists only at the extrapolated 2-s-slot parameters; the 12-s form (a 60 × 512 KiB term ≈ 236 blobs ≈ 40 landings against a 60-block reward) is economic griefing in F6's family, bounded per event and refusable by honest landers at ≈ 0.4 ETH per attacker term. But R5 requires the design to work at 2-s slots, so the fix is mandatory, not optional.

Design gap. Three separate defects share one root: the outage gate is a timestamp test that does not distinguish "the chain was live past t" from "the chain was live before t"; a forced or replacement landing is allowed to void a landable backlog with no accountability for the voider and no protection for the voided; and no rule ties a block's size to the smallest L1 block that must carry it.

Required revision:

1. S2: require `_landedTermRef > _termId` for S4a and S7 (a landing of a strictly later term proves the chain was live past t; it exists only after replacement or after the range itself landed); harmonise the glossary (:46) and the slashing rows to that wording.
2. S3, accountability: the S4a waiver must exempt every view whose certified blocks were voided by a forced or replacement landing, unless the outage gate (as fixed above) was already open before that landing; a reporter share is paid only when the view was voided by its own holder's REPLACE or by an ordinary landing of a later term. With (1) this closes the farm without weakening S4a against a genuine abandoner while others land.
3. S3, the reorg itself: give a landable backlog priority over the forced and REPLACE paths through a bonded, permissionless landing intent recorded on L1 (`announceLanding(firstHeight, endHeight, bond ≥ SLASH_ABANDON)`), which defers `replaceableFrom` by LAND_CHAIN_GRACE once per range and is forfeited (40 % to the eventual forced or replacement lander) if no covering landing follows; a fake intent therefore costs its poster the same as an abandonment and cannot censor the forced path for long. Alternatively, or additionally, order the RESUME's re-inclusion of voided transactions before serving due FI entries for the first MAX_FI_RUN-equivalent stretch after a void (a V8 exception keyed on the RESUME opening object), so a forced transaction cannot front-run the voided backlog's state.
4. S3, disclosure: label confirmations "replaceable" at `replaceableFrom`, not "outage" at DEGRADE_AFTER; publish beside "locked" that its 39 ETH credit bound holds only while landing keeps up within LAND_WINDOW, and the unpunished-revert window otherwise. Re-examine limitation L6 in that light: it is not an accepted residual while an outsider can trigger and profit from it.
5. H1: a validity rule V9: a block's canonical record (headerCore ‖ seqSig ‖ VC bytes ‖ txListBytes) must fit the per-L1-block blob capacity at the minimum supported slot time (≤ 3 blobs ≈ 390,132 bytes), enforced at attestation and asserted by the guest; MAX_ENVELOPE derived from it; the lander paid per blob as well as per block (so a legitimately large term is still landable at a profit); and an objective quarantine path for provable-but-oversized transactions so a replacer may drop them under a rule rather than at its discretion. With (2), voided holders whose first unlanded term belongs to another owner are no longer slashed.

### Group D: bond accounting and the lander reward (S5, S6)

**S5 (Medium), re-checked.** slashing.html:24: "Liveness debits (absence, abandonment, reward payments) never trigger that check; ... a hard floor at 50 % is the backstop"; §6: the lander reward is "debited from the responsible holder's ledger and credited to rewardTo in the same transaction"; §1: "Credits to addresses with no seats and no key history are withdrawable immediately". An owner therefore lets a seatless alter ego land each of its terms at the ramp maximum (1,440 TAIKO per term, or at once through a RewardBoost whose `minRewardPerBlockGwei` has no stated cap) until its ledger sits at 0.5 B per seat, keeping every seat eligible. Class A then takes "100 % of the owner's ledger" = 0.5 B: a 13-slot locked reorg costs 130,000 TAIKO, not the published 260,000 (≈ 39 ETH); "Sybil invariance ... loses ≥ m × B whatever the seat split" (slashing.html:62) is false for a self-drained owner, and the anti-monopoly and liveness sizing (B ≥ 100 × V_term) are halved. The per-term exposure row bounds one term, not cumulative self-dealing.

**S6 (Medium), re-checked.** landing.html:54: leaf input = `hashPublicInputs(journalHash, leaf, address(0), chainId)`, matching today's `LibPublicInput.sol:18-36` where the signer slot "for ZK ... must have value address(0)"; the journal fields (:46-53) name no lander; L1-L11 depend on inbox state and calldata only. A copied LandInput with `rewardTo` swapped passes identically; the loser is NotNext, "invalid and free" under frames. Both submission shapes expose the payload (direct-to-builder: the builder is the copier; without EIP-8141: a public type-3 transaction). The design asserts "copying a mempool proof steals nothing" (landing.html:59) while binding rewardTo for forced batches precisely so that "a mempool copy of the proof cannot redirect the fees" (forced-inclusion.html:71) and listing the trade-off as an open question (:206). The threat model lists "front-run other provers' submissions" as a prover capability (01-threat-model.md §2). Medium: an incentive break of the third-party fallback for TH2 and of R7's "third-party landing is paid in full", not a safety break; self-landing holders are unaffected.

Required revision: S5: reward-type debits (lander ramp, RewardBoost, attester share) stop at the buffer (1.05 × floor); below it the holder must top up or land itself; cap `minRewardPerBlockGwei` at R_BLK_MAX; or, if the design prefers to keep the floor rule, publish every class-A credit bound as m × HARD_FLOOR × B and re-derive B_SEAT. S6: bind rewardTo into the journal (or the unused signer slot of hashPublicInputs) for ordinary landings as for forced ones, and state that proofs are per-lander (the P2P proofs topic then carries the lander address); if fungibility across landers is wanted, the alternative is a prover signature over (journalHash, rewardTo) verified on L1, which keeps the proof fungible and the reward assigned.

### Group E: the committee walk (F1)

**F1 (High), re-checked and re-simulated.** sequencing.html §5 stops the holder walk at five distinct owners; §8 builds the committee "skipping seats whose owner is in the term's holder list H". With the design's own launch figure of 40 honest seats (slashing.html §5) held as five 8-seat operators, the five holders are the five honest owners in nearly every term and all 40 honest seats are excluded. ### Group F: forced-inclusion skip under a backlog (F5)

**F5 (Medium), re-checked.** forced-inclusion.html §2 defines `due(i, T) := i < tail(T) ∧ savedAt_i + FI_DELAY ≤ T` (independent of head position) and `skipped(i, T)` "for the head entry of either kind when it has been due for FI_SKIP = 14,400 s of anchored time without being consumed"; the only per-entry state is FiEntry{dataHash, savedAt, feeWei, kind, offset}, so due-age can only be computed from savedAt; no text resets the clock when an entry reaches the head. Under a backlog an entry with ≈ 9,800+ entries ahead at save time (drain rate two per three seconds, MAX_FI_RUN = 2) reaches the head at an anchored time ≥ savedAt + 14,700 s and is voided (anchor-only block, fee to the includer), while the chain landed continuously. The proven claim "FI_SKIP can fire only after four hours without any landing, because every landing path must consume a due head entry; so it never skips an entry a live chain could have included" (:189) is a non sequitur: landings consume a due head entry, not entry i. Priced (≈ 1,000 ETH to build the backlog, ≈ 480 ETH/h to hold it, partly recouped by a cartel sequencer's consumed fees), comparable to the delay the design already accepts under L8, but the outcome changes from bounded delay to deletion of every forced transaction saved in the window, so P5 fails while funded. The stall-resumption variant (every entry due > 4 h at the resuming anchor voids in turn) also follows from the text and is a false positive against honest requesters after any four-hour landing stall.

Required revision: age the skip from the moment the entry became both head and due (store `headSince` when the head advances), or gate `skipped` on `T − lastLanded.anchorTipTimestamp ≥ FI_SKIP` so the rule literally means "no landing of any kind for four hours", which is what the round-1 F4 fix intended; restate the §16 claim from the chosen rule.

## 7. What the attackers could not break (consolidated)

From the three reports and my own re-reading; each item names the rule that stopped it.

1. False finalization through single-proof mode (steal 1; arguments §2): L5 requires the journal's end block hash to equal a certified header whose state root Q attesters executed, so a forged leaf needs a colluding quorum as well (T11); TEE never counts.
2. Forging a checkpoint through a false anchor (steal 9; TH17): the guest chains every anchored header to the tip, the inbox checks the tip against blockhash or EIP-2935, A3 is monotone; a fake root can be attested only by ≥ Q attesters skipping V5 and can never land.
3. Handoff ambush and withholding W1-W5 under the revised keys (halt 1, censor 3): landing withheld blocks above the recorded TERM_END lock needs a second VC for the same (t, v, kind), i.e. 2Q(m) − m S3c double-signers, and the honest VC reaches L1 within seconds; the seal is the committee's VC, so nothing sequencer-signed exists to withhold. (F3 weakens the keys against honest replacement, not in an attacker's favour.)
4. Wedging the term chain with consecutive dark committees (halt 2): the F9 fix lets any later committee within the horizon sign a FALLBACK with priority by proximity; abstention delays 10 s per dark committee, never wedges.
5. Making a proven landing revert, or reorging in-flight blocks, through the forced-inclusion queue (steal 3, halt 3, censor 8): anchored-time due-ness (T_floor from storage plus the chain's own term id), pull fees, bounded walk; an entry becoming due is a non-event for blocks whose parents anchor older L1 blocks.
6. Skipping a specific due entry through FI_SKIP or a chosen forced-batch anchor without a real four-hour stall (steal 4, censor 8): every landing path must consume the head, and a four-hour stall cannot be manufactured because the requester itself can land a forced batch at replaceableFrom. (F5 is the backlog case where the stall is not needed.)
7. Deferring a forced inclusion with four-view landings (censor 4): T_floor from the landing's term shifts mandatory consumption by at most three terms, inside the 102-minute argument.
8. Opening dead mode on a slow but live chain, or the sentinel-255 race under a full committee (halt 4, censor 6): DEAD_TERMS × TERM = 4,500 s > LAND_WINDOW_MAX + ABANDON_GRACE = 3,900 s; view 255 is refused unless the term is open-empty, no-committee or dead. (The race exists only in no-committee terms: F1, S9.)
9. Seed grinding, entry and exit timing (halt 6, censor 2): Rule D (DELAY_REG = 7,200 s > CYCLE + LOOKBACK − TERM) and RND3 freshness; a stale seed needs 41 minutes with no landing and no poke(), which anyone can call; the lever is then a few bits of seat placement.
10. Excluding an entrant by front-running its registration (censor 1): each front-run locks a fresh 20,000 TAIKO for 2 h plus the 7-day exit lock; the entrant retries for gas only, through a private relay or FOCIL. (A wording fix removes the race: §3.)
11. Suspending honest operators with timeouts, the round-1 F3 surface (halt 7, censor 5): the normative rule makes a timeout strike-free and floor-free; with a dispersed honest set the veto probability at c = 0.2 is the binomial 4 % the design states and the bleed to the floor takes months. (S7/F4 is the stale text; F1 is the concentrated-honest-set case.)
12. Using S4a or S7 against a holder or committee the attacker censored (censor 7): S4a needs a quorum object above what landed, the hard deadline and the outage gate; S7 needs a certificate the victims signed; a cartel can only spend its own seats at 22:1 against itself. (S2, S3 and H2 are the gate's own defects, not accusation objects.)
13. Premature view change with fewer than Q colluders (censor 9): a VC needs Q(m) signatures; below Q colluders can only abstain; above Q it is the accepted L4.
14. Redirecting forced-batch fees by copying a mempool proof (steal 2): rewardTo is bound into the forced journal. (S6 shows ordinary landings lack the same binding.)
15. Griefing S1 by replaying a valid signed header (steal 5): S1 needs the accused key's own two signatures; committee views carry vcHash in the first header. (S1 second part, F3 and S9 are the cases where the key is not in the signed object.)
16. Stealing a bond by exiting before evidence (steal 6): every evidence window ends by termStart + 7 d and exit waits 7 d past activeUntil and past key retirement; the S3a-pair 34-h ring is shorter than the lock.
17. Rogue-key framing (steal 8): proof of possession at registration. (Residual: PoP not bound to the owner, §3.)
18. Claiming the challenger share by self-reporting (steal 10): the min(10 %, 2,000) cap against class A/B debits is a net loss to the offender.
19. Draining the attester reward by 1-block landings (steal 7): stopped only by the gas floor (6 TAIKO per landing against ≈ 1.3-1.5 M gas); a latent lever below ≈ 0.5 gwei, recorded as a wording fix in §3.
20. Absence bleed as an exclusion lever below the T11 boundary with a dispersed honest set (censor 5): 200 draws at the binomial rate is months, and the victim can top up.
