## Independent verdict

I found no demonstrated way to forge an accepted root, bypass the mature actual-data gate, acquire an exclusive canonical right, or indefinitely skip a fixed due forced request while valid successors continue. One **Medium** economic finding remains: the mandatory rent does not impose a positive capture cost in the no-ready-competitor case, and the scenario supplying a positive bound assumes a funded competitor already reaches L1 before the zero-rent point. This is a disclosed, bounded-policy gap, not an exploitable proof or custody invariant failure. The design's core safety and open-entry arguments are conditional; R2 deployment compatibility and R4/R7 throughput are unverified launch obligations.

## Findings

### 06-C-1 — Medium — Zero-rent repeat capture has no endogenous counterpressure

**Snapshot evidence.** The rent is reset on each accepted head and decays to zero after 900 seconds (`design/index.html:158-161`, [rent](../design/index.html#rent); `design/roles.html:239-267`, [rent economics](../design/roles.html#rent)). The positive capture-cost proof explicitly requires A-COMPETITION(D): an independent, funded, ready, willing entrant whose valid action is included by D<900 (`design/roles.html:274-281`, [competition](../design/roles.html#competition); `design/arguments.html:25-27`, [assumptions](../design/arguments.html#assumptions)). The design calculates 0.046666666666666667 ETH per head at a 60-second cadence and about 67.2 ETH/day of rent, and says honest affordability is open (`design/roles.html:279-288`, [competition](../design/roles.html#competition)). An unchanged head offers zero rent only to an entrant that can keep it unchanged through fresh staging, proof and inclusion (`design/arguments.html:118-120`, [capture argument](../design/arguments.html#l4)). Forced FIFO remains mandatory (`design/index.html:183-186`, [forced inclusion](../design/index.html#forced)).

**Preconditions.** One actor has a viable proving pipeline and enough fee/MEV/private-order-flow value to operate it; independent small entrants can afford eventual zero-rent submission but cannot fund the contemporaneous positive quote, duplicate DA and proof risk. This deliberately does **not** satisfy A-COMPETITION(D). L1 includes the actor's valid transactions and the actor obeys all proof, stage, origin and forced-prefix rules.

**Concrete trace.** 1. The actor stages complete valid candidate data and produces a proof for accepted head H, so it can submit after the 360-second public-age gate. 2. It waits until H is 900 seconds old, refreshes origin/context and staging as needed, then lands a valid successor at `q=0`; the acceptance resets the rent reference. 3. An independent participant unwilling or unable to pay a positive quote must wait for the new head's zero point while building a fresh eligible context. The actor can repeat step 2 at that point, making the participant's proof or stage stale. 4. Each actor-produced successor still processes its frozen due FIFO prefix; the actor retains discretion over other transaction ordering and soft-branch promotion. This can repeat without buying bonds, a ticket or a positive protocol rent.

**Cost and gain.** Protocol rent is **0 ETH per win** on this schedule. The attacker still pays real staging/republication, proof and L1 costs, plus any forced-processing burden; no profitable attack is asserted without revenue exceeding those costs. It can keep discretionary order, fee/MEV opportunities and customer flow. Other provers lose paid staging/proving work when stale, and ordinary users may face concentrated ordering or soft rollback. No claim is made that this trace defeats forced transactions, forges roots, or violates a signed duty.

**Invariant and requirements.** It falsifies an *unconditional* interpretation of “sustained capture has a mandatory positive identity-independent cost” or “zero-rent access ensures a small entrant can break a live incumbent.” The written theorem itself survives because its A-COMPETITION(D) premise is absent. R6 remains **open as an effectiveness/economic-calibration question**, with R3/R4 participation economics affected; R1's objective entry rule is not falsified.

**Exploitability and named assumptions.** This is a rational equilibrium/censorship-risk counterexample, not a presently exploitable contract invariant. A-CRYPTO, A-L1, A-DA and A-IMPLEMENTATION can all hold. A-FUND for some participant can hold, but A-COMPETITION(D) fails specifically at the positive price. A-INCLUDE for otherwise-valid affordable transactions does not supply ETH to the entrant, and A-SERVICE does not guarantee independent owners. The design acknowledges both the zero-rent and above-rent-MEV limits (`design/roles.html:279-282`, [competition](../design/roles.html#competition); `design/arguments.html:164-167`, [known limits](../design/arguments.html#limits)).

**Minimal resolution.** Keep the conditional theorem scoped as written, and require a measured market model showing that an independent entrant can finance a valid race at a specified D and that relevant capture value is below `q(D)` after all L1/proving/DA costs. If the hard R6 goal requires positive deterrence even without that entrant, revise the admission economics or state that this stronger goal is unavailable under a zero price floor; evaluate the honest-throughput cost of any revision. This finding proposes no code edit.

## Rejected attempted attacks

1. **First disclose a private valid branch in `submitSegment`.** A matching actual-data stage must be 360–900 seconds old and the action republishes complete data; the proof binds the same context/body. A hash-only private stage or changed order cannot pass (`design/staging.html:89-93`, [registry](../design/staging.html#registry); `design/staging.html:117-121`, [acceptance](../design/staging.html#acceptance); `design/codec.html:20-44`, [body](../design/codec.html#body)). This still allows a mature competing branch to win.
2. **Use Sybil buckets, jobs or address rotation to reserve canonical production.** None grants a canonical ticket; anyone may submit with an eligible stage, valid proof, producer self-authorization and current rent (`design/roles.html:14-18`, [role boundary](../design/roles.html#boundary); `design/roles.html:94-107`, [jobs](../design/roles.html#jobs)). Sybils can evade *same-owner* conflict attribution, but cannot create an exclusive right or avoid the global rent on an early accepted head (`design/roles.html:84-90`, [registry](../design/roles.html#registry)).
3. **Append forced requests while a proof runs or grind empty successors forever to skip a fixed due request.** The parent freezes cut/time, and each successful child processes up to four oldest due records before discretion. Freshness forces snapshot renewal as accepted history advances (`design/index.html:179-186`, [forced inclusion](../design/index.html#forced); `design/codec.html:127-132`, [forced bytes](../design/codec.html#forced)). A paid backlog and prover outage can still make delay long; the design states no uniform short SLA (`design/arguments.html:84-95`, [FIFO argument](../design/arguments.html#l2)).
4. **Frame an honest losing publisher as the canonical conflict signer.** The acceptance receipt authenticates the producer; slash evidence requires same collateral owner, immediate base, height and different proved block hash. Another owner's winning branch is insufficient (`design/staging.html:154-181`, [authorization and block tree](../design/staging.html#authorization); `design/accountability.html:39-53`, [conflict evidence](../design/accountability.html#canonical-conflict)). Cross-owner and pipelined-base false negatives are explicit limits, not proof of forged attribution.
5. **Late publication cures a signed fragment default or pays the responder.** Every version-2 fragment has its own absolute `issuedAt+240` deadline, with immutable first receipt, direct default proof, and a response deadline capped by publishBy (`design/accountability.html:16-34`, [fragment publication](../design/accountability.html#fragment-publication); `design/codec.html:134-176`, [manifest](../design/codec.html#manifests)).
6. **Bootstrap with an attacker-selected zero origin or erase queued migration requests.** H0 is certificate-derived; only its exact parent hash receives the sentinel exception. The first real origin proves ACTIVE, the first segment has an empty mandatory prefix, and its successor stores the preserved queue's actual cut (`design/migration.html:269-330`, [bootstrap](../design/migration.html#bootstrap); `design/codec-vectors.md:73-87`). This is conditional on the migration proof and implementation, not an authenticated live deployment check.
