## 3. CONFIRMED findings: trace, design gap, required revision

### R4H-1 [High] The eligible list is positional and history-free; ordinary churn makes in-flight terms unlandable

**Trace (summarized).** Any registry mutation (entry `activeFrom`, exit `activeUntil`, suspension start, reserve-lapse ineligibility) with effective time E in (termStart(t), landing time of t] is "applied lazily by the first land, registry call or poke after the effective time" (sequencing §5). A removal is a swap-and-pop that moves the last seat into the hole. The next landing of term t computes `isHolder(t, v, sequencer)` by walking `idx(t, k) = keccak(seed ‖ 0x01 ‖ t ‖ k) mod E_t` over the *current* list (landing L4 reads pins only for terms older than ROLE_HORIZON); the position now maps to a different seat, so L4 reverts `NotHolder` for every lander, forever. `recordAssignment(t)` after the mutation pins the same wrong walk; `eligibleAt(_time)` has no history to answer with. Sequential landing (L1 `NotNext`) stalls every later term until `replaceableFrom` (35-65 min), when a REPLACE or forced landing voids the certified window. With 3-4 terms in flight at a 2-3-minute landing lag, every honest mutation whose effective instant falls inside that window breaks a term at zero cost; an attacker schedules one `register`/`requestExit` pair per few minutes to make it continuous. Dead mode (sentinel 255, no `isHolder`) eventually restores landing, which is why this is High and not Critical.

**Design gap.** Sequencing §2 "Term t evaluates eligibility at the single instant termStart(t). This is what lets the contract keep no history and need no proofs" was true of the old per-index `Seat{activeFrom, activeUntil}` walk and is false of a position-indexed list. Rule D only guarantees that writes effective *after* termEnd(t) are invisible at landing; it says nothing about writes effective inside [termStart(t), landing]. The round-3 judge's revision asked for "swap-and-pop with the index history needed for terms inside ROLE_HORIZON, or a Fenwick tree" (03-judge.md:92); the applied fix kept the swap-and-pop and dropped the history. The page's own rejected-alternatives list records the hazard ("swap-and-pop seat arrays (index reuse changes computed terms)", §17). Claims falsified: sequencing claim 2 ("Node snapshot equals landing-time state"), locality P7, R7 minute-level landing.

**Required revision.** Make the sortition domain history-free again, by one of: (a) an append-only list with tombstones plus a per-term count of `eligible(i, termStart(t))` (prefix/Fenwick structure) so `idx mod E_t` indexes eligible seats at termStart(t) from current storage; (b) a versioned list (store the swap log with effective times; the inbox undoes mutations later than termStart(t) when evaluating term t, bounded by ROLE_HORIZON); or (c) mandatory per-term pins taken *before* any mutation with E > termStart(t) is applied (every land/registry call/poke first pins every term whose start has passed and is unpinned, then applies mutations), with L4 reading only pins for every term. In all cases specify the tie-break for mutations with equal effective time and whether a call applies pending mutations before or after computing rights. Re-state sequencing §2, §5, claims 1-2, P7, and the "no history" sentence.

### R4H-3 [High] The landing reserve is withdrawable at any time; a holder certifies a term nobody is paid to land

**Trace (summarized).** Owner A registers with 1.05 × floor + 6,200 TAIKO. At S(t)+1 the assignment is public and A = holder(t, 0). A calls `withdraw` for the whole excess over 1.05 × floor (slashing §1 "While seats exist: the excess over 105 % of the floor"; roles §1; interfaces `withdraw ... limited to the excess over 1.05 × floor`; bridge-migration §4 drops today's `LibBonds` withdrawal delay, `contracts/layer1/core/libs/LibBonds.sol:57-88`, in favour of "seat-floor withdrawal replaces the flat delay"). The reserve rule fires future-dated: "A reserve below the per-term exposure makes the owner ineligible, future-dated by DELAY_S" (landing §5), i.e. at termStart(t) + 121 s > termEnd(t); Rule D leaves term t with A. A certifies five 390,123-byte blocks (V9, V10 admit them: 1,950,615 B, three 6-blob landings). After termEnd the ramp reaches 4/block + 1,300/landing, but every debit is `min(x, balance)` above the buffer, so every third-party lander is credited 0; landers have "no duties enforced, economic pull only". Sequential landing stalls behind t until `replaceableFrom`, a REPLACE fork voids the honest window, S4a cannot fire (outage gate needs a strictly later landed term, impossible under `NotNext`; the void waiver covers the record), no timeout applies (A produced certified blocks). A tops up before its own ineligibility instant and repeats at every draw.

**Design gap.** The R3H-2 fix guarantees a funded reward only at registration; nothing locks the reserve afterwards, and the eligibility consequence is dated so that every term already snapshotted stays with the withdrawer. Landing §5 "so every certified term has a funded lander reward by construction" is false. Same class as R3H-2 (repeatable 35-65-minute landing and bridging halt plus an unpenalized reorg of the locked window).

**Required revision.** (i) The reserve is not withdrawable while any seat is active or pending; it leaves only through `requestExit` + EVIDENCE_WINDOW (like seat stake) or through a DELAY_REG-dated withdrawal request. (ii) Reserve lapse (which can then only happen through debits) is a DELAY_REG-dated, not DELAY_S-dated, eligibility write (see R4-C2). (iii) Size the reserve for the concurrent terms an owner can hold inside one landing window (see R4-C4) so that a lapse is rare and never one term deep. (iv) State plainly that no L1 rule can guarantee funds at landing time for a term drawn before the debits that emptied the reserve, and bound that residual.

### R4H-4 [High] The "timeout only on leader silence" fix is defeated by V3

**Trace (summarized).** Full committee m = 32, Q = 22; cartel holds 11 committee seats and the view-1 backup; honest primary H. H signs PH(h+1) and PH(h+2) (the first two blocks of a view carry the VC); 21 honest attesters attest, 21 < Q, no certificate. PH(h+3) cannot be valid: V3 requires `carriedCertHeight ∈ {n−1, n−2}`, and the SEQUENCER machine says "PRODUCING --height − highest own certified > CERT_LAG_MAX+1--> HOLD" (preconf §10). Any header H signs anyway is dropped (V3 fails; "failure is silence"). At ≈ 6.5 s every honest attester's condition holds as written: "no new certificate for TIMEOUT seconds and has itself attested no block of (t, v) *in that window*" (preconf §1; §10 "no own attestation in (t,v) within TIMEOUT"; roles §2 "only if it attested nothing in that window"): its last attestation was at ≈ 1.3 s, outside the 5-s window. 21 honest + 11 cartel timeouts ≥ Q; VC(t, 0, lock h) forms; holder(t, 1) (the cartel) starts and is certified by its own 11 plus the honest 21. Landing L4: "Each TIMEOUT-kind VC applies the absence penalty to the timed-out leader": H loses 50 TAIKO. Nothing is slashable (silence is not slashable; S3b needs an attestation above the signed lock; S3c needs two locks).

**Design gap.** Slashing §5 ("no timeout forms either (the honest 21 attested the live leader and therefore do not sign TIMEOUT)", "The absence bleed is no longer reachable by a minority at all"), sequencing §12 TH8/TH9 and limitation L2 all assume a *view-scoped* rule that no page states; the normative text is window-scoped, and V3 makes the window empty within 2 s. Slashing §13 even still says "≥ 11 colluders refuse to attest: 50 TAIKO". My re-run of the §5 walk under the rule as written: cartel takes over 30 % of all terms at 40×1/20×1, 40 % at 8×8/32×1, 45 % at 100×1/70×1 (0 % at the 8×8/8×1 launch condition). The round-3 C1 state is restored: term share ≠ seat share, the "proven" cost curve X·H/(1−X) falls, and honest primaries are bled 50 TAIKO per captured view.

**Required revision.** Scope the silence condition to the view: an attester that attested *any* valid block of (t, v) never signs TIMEOUT for (t, v) (it may sign TERM_END/FALLBACK/REPLACE/RESUME as before). State the consequence: a leader that produces two valid blocks and then goes dark, or is abstained against, keeps its view at the sequenced level until TERM_END; that is the blank-term residual of L2 and it must be priced there (see R4-C1). Do not relax V3 to let a HOLDing leader keep producing (the design rejected "a long provisional window of unattested progress" for good reason). Re-derive slashing §5, §13, sequencing §12, preconf §14 and L2 under the corrected rule; the slashing §5 table then describes blank terms, not takeovers, and must carry the revenue-share column (R4-C1).

### R4H-5 [High] A REPLACE VC recorded after the backlog landed closes the live view and voids fresh locked blocks

**Trace (summarized).** `replaceableFrom` passes after an outage; lastLanded = h0. Holder H forms a valid REPLACE VC_R (lock h0, l1Ref N canonical, A1 held at N) per A2. Lander L's backlog landing (proof finished during the outage) is included first, as A3 permits ("Until a REPLACE VC is recorded on L1 ... landing the original stays valid"); lastLanded = h0+900, `lastLanded.at` fresh, replaceableFrom ≈ 35 min ahead. H continues the original view and certifies h0+901... Later anyone (or H's own in-flight transaction) calls `recordViewChange(VC_R)`: "anyone; first per (termId, view, closes) wins; a REPLACE record closes the original tail" (landing §9, interfaces). Nothing compares VC_R.lock (h0) with lastLanded (h0+900) or re-evaluates A1 at record time; S3d cannot fire (A1 held at N). Effects as written: the inbox "refuses to land original blocks above lastLanded" and every attester observing "a recorded REPLACE VC" enters RESET with "every certificate above lastLanded ... void" (preconf §6, §10). The locked blocks h0+901.. can never land; because records are keyed on (t, v, closes), the stale VC_R also blocks any TERM_END/FALLBACK/RESUME VC closing the same object (L4 requires equality with the first recorded), so the open view cannot be closed by an ordinary chain and recovery runs through the forced/dead-mode path. S4a is waived by the design's own text.

**Design gap.** The R3-S4 fix gave an L1 record two effects (inbox refusal, attester RESET) unconditionally. P2 ("a locked block is reverted only by ≥ 12 provably slashable attesters, by a landed replacement after the deadline machinery, or by an L1 reorg", arguments §1) is broken by a single 150k-gas transaction. The precondition (a stall past replaceableFrom in which the REPLACE loses the race to the backlog) recurs per outage and is manufacturable by the stalled holder.

**Required revision.** `recordViewChange` for kinds REPLACE and RESUME must require `vc.lockHeight == lastLanded.height`, `vc.lockPhHash == lastLanded.phHash` and `block.timestamp > replaceableFrom` at record time (A1 re-evaluated on inbox state), else refuse (or store inert with no closing effect). Attester RESET must key on a record whose lock equals the node's own lastLanded. Keep the R3-S4 property (a late original cannot void a recorded fork) by having the record, once accepted, close the original tail *at that lock*. Restate A3, preconf §6 "Lock reset", the S4a waiver, and the R3-S4 row of the iteration log.

### R4-C1 [High] Blank terms convert about a third of seats into most productive revenue; the TH9 cost curve is wrong by 2x to 10x

**Trace (summarized).** Under the *intended* C1 rule (view-scoped, which R4H-4 says must be adopted), a cartel holding ≥ m − Q + 1 = 11 committee seats in a term with an honest primary simply does not attest: no certificate, no VC, the leader HOLDs after two blocks, the term is closed by FALLBACK at the previous lock and nothing of it lands (V7). preconf §13 and slashing §5 concede the mechanism ("Not defended: blank terms at the rates in the table above (limitations L2)"). Cartel-primary terms are certified normally. My re-run (20,000 terms per row, same walk as sequencing §5/§8): honest-primary blank rate / cartel share of productive revenue = 0.58 / 0.54 at 40×1 vs 20×1 (33 % of seats); 0.91 / 0.88 at 40×1 vs 27×1 (40 %); 0.69 / 0.62 at 8×8 vs 32×1; 0.87 / 0.84 at 100×1 vs 70×1; 0.81 / 0.78 at 200×1 vs 134×1; 0.00 / 0.11 at the launch condition 8×8 vs 8×1. The censor's figures reproduce; the effect scales with share, not registry size. Abstention costs the cartel only the 0.1 TAIKO/block attester share, which is not paid when nothing lands.

**Design gap.** Slashing §5/§15 ("share X costs X·H/(1−X) seats", "50 % of terms costs 0.8 M TAIKO and yields exactly 50 % of term revenue"), sequencing §12 TH9 ("50 % = 64 seats") and the R6 verdict ("linear in share") are stated as proven while the design's own accepted residual makes a 27-seat cartel (540k TAIKO, nothing at risk) collect ≈ 88 % of productive revenue against 40 honest seats, leaving honest seats ≈ 10 % of their expected income (an exclusion pressure the design says is "unbounded at exclusion"). The MISS_PENALTY sizing premise ("entry stays profitable when half of a party's draws are griefed", p = 0.5) is false. T11 is stated three ways (arguments §3 "fewer than 11 committee seats abstain"; arguments §7 "fewer than Q colluders"; threat model "fewer than the attester quorum threshold"), and only the Q form is in the threat model, yet liveness needs the 11 form. Users get certified progress only in cartel terms; P3 ("certified blocks within seconds") holds only there.

**Required revision.** (i) Republish the anti-monopoly section from simulation: term share, blank rate and *productive revenue share* per owner structure; delete the X·H/(1−X) curve as a proven claim or restate it as the price of *primary* share only, with the blank-term column beside it. (ii) Restate T11 for liveness as "fewer than m − Q(m) + 1 abstainers per committee" on the arguments page and in the threat model, and restate the MISS_PENALTY sizing premise. (iii) Evaluate a mitigation. The censor's STALL vote (floor(m/2)+1 attesters that attested but saw no certificate sign a redraw of the committee for the same leader; no backup, no MISS) raises the blanking threshold from 11 to 16 of 32 and appears safe against a cartel majority (a ≥ 17 cartel can already blank; redrawing its own leader's committee gains it nothing), but it is a new signed object and needs its own equivocation key, its own place in the collusion table and a deterministic redraw seed; the judge does not accept it unanalysed. (iv) Rewrite L2 to state the revenue consequence honestly.

### R4-C2 [High] The landing reserve is an owner-triggered eligibility write on the short delay: seed-known assignment grinding

**Trace (summarized).** k single-seat owners. seed(c) is public from cycleStart − 1,200 s. For each term starting ≥ τ + 1,320 s the attacker evaluates the holder walk under each subset of its owners being eligible: withdrawing the reserve (permitted, see R4H-3) makes an owner ineligible at τ + DELAY_S = 1,320 s; deposit + `reactivate()` restores it at τ + 1,320 s (sequencing §10 owner machine, no strike, no cooldown). Each subset changes E_t, hence the modulus of `idx(t, k)`, and the swap order changes which seat sits at which position, so each flip is a fresh draw. One L1 transaction per term at ≈ 0.002 ETH against ≈ 0.01 ETH of term revenue. Rule D holds (1,320 ≥ LOOKBACK + 2·TERM), so node and contract agree; what fails is RND3's freshness argument, which assumes every roster-affecting write is dated ≥ 4,740 s ("DELAY_REG > CYCLE + LOOKBACK − TERM = 7,200 s (no grinding when the seed is fresh)"; "freshFrom(c) = cycleStart(c) − 3660 s, the last instant at which a registry write can affect cycle c").

**Design gap.** The R3H-2 fix added a registry input (reserve ≥ exposure) dated with the suspension delay instead of the registration delay, and left it under owner control. The "proven" claims "No entry or exit grinding when the seed is fresh" and "Share bound w/A" (sequencing §16) are false. The attacker's probability figures are overstated (subsets of equal size share E_t; I estimate 2x to 4x for four to eight single-seat owners before position grinding, more with it) but the amplification is unbounded in repetition and has no penalty path. Secondary: the two-interval suspension store is sized for 36-h intervals (parameters §1); repeated one-term reserve lapses inside ROLE_HORIZON drop live intervals and make committee attribution and evidence views inexact.

**Required revision.** Reserve-driven ineligibility must not be a fast owner-triggered write: make the reserve non-withdrawable while any seat is active (R4H-3 fix), date any reserve lapse with DELAY_REG, and give a lapse a minimum SUSPEND-length interval (or count it as a structural event with the same store semantics). Restate the two-interval store invariant and RND3 for every eligibility input, and add a rule: *every* eligibility input is either DELAY_REG-dated or not owner-triggerable.

### R4H-6 [Medium] V10 counts forced-inclusion blocks: a blob-shaped FI backlog exhausts a term at ~23 s and bleeds every leader

**Trace (summarized).** V10 sums "canonical record bytes over the blocks of one term" and an FI block's record includes its txListBytes (the decoded blob's transactions, includer-independent, up to ≈ 130 KB); FI blocks are "signed like any block" with the sequencer's term/view (forced-inclusion §3), so nothing excludes them. With ≈ 15 pending blob entries and the V8 pattern FI, FI, seq, the fifteenth FI block (block ≈ 22) exceeds TERM_BYTES_MAX = 1,950,660; a sequencer block there fails V8 ("valid iff no entry is due at the parent's cursor at T_p, or the MAX_FI_RUN = 2 blocks ending at p are all FI blocks"). No valid block exists for the rest of the term; V10 is per term, so every backup view inherits the exhausted budget. Honest attesters see leader silence and sign TIMEOUT; each TIMEOUT VC applies MISS to the timed-out leader (landing L4): primary plus three backups (V_MAX = 4 takeovers) = 200 TAIKO per term burned from owners that had no valid block available. The backlog drains at 15 entries per term, not 40, so the stuffing arithmetic (60 ETH/hour, "users keep one block in three", L8) and the throughput floor ("two entries per three seconds") are wrong by ≈ 2.7x; users get ≈ 7 blocks per minute. Self-inflicted variant: a primary fills the budget with five V9-maximal blocks and bleeds three honest backups for one 50-TAIKO penalty of its own. Forced batch: the guest "asserts V10 as well" with no view-254 exemption stated, so under the strict reading an m ≥ 16 full-blob forced batch is unprovable.

**Design gap.** V10 was introduced for R3H-2 without checking its interaction with V8 or MISS; TH8's acceptance criterion ("the accused must have had a way to avoid it") and the slashing page's "the accused always had a way out" are violated. Fee correction: 100 entries cost ≈ 0.2 ETH by the design's own formula, so the attack is cheaper than the attacker claimed.

**Required revision.** Exclude FI blocks and forced batches from V10 (they are already bounded by one blob each and MAX_FI_PER_LANDING) or size TERM_BYTES_MAX for 40 FI blocks plus the sequencer's own budget; make V10 per view or waive MISS for any view whose term has less than one FI record of budget left; state the forced-batch exemption explicitly; restate L8, the stuffing arithmetic, the throughput floor and the per-term exposure (which then covers more blobs).

### R4H-7 [Medium] r_land is paid per landing, not per landing a term needs: one-block landings drain the reserve

**Trace (summarized).** Term t has 60 certified blocks in 3 blobs; the holder's prover is late. Lander G lands block h+1 alone at termEnd + 30 min: credited 4 + 1 + 1,300 = 1,305 TAIKO (landing §5: r_land "is paid once per landing whose first segment belongs to the term"; L1: `firstHeight == lastLanded.height + 1`, "Several landings may sit adjacent in one L1 block"; L6: "any single certified block is landable in one transaction"). Landings 2-5 repeat; after five the 6,200-TAIKO reserve is empty (`min(x, balance)`, reward debits stop only at the buffer). Per landing ≈ 0.2 ETH of TAIKO against ≈ 0.03 ETH of gas at 20 gwei. The owner is ineligible after DELAY_S; the remaining 55 blocks have an unfunded reward; sequential landing stalls behind them until top-up, self-landing or a replacement that voids the honest backlog.

**Design gap.** "V10 caps a term at 15 blobs, so at most three landings are ever owed" is an inference, not a rule; `TermRecord` carries no landing counter. The round-3 judge asked to "index R_BLOB and the ramp to the landings actually needed (ceil(blobs/6))" (03-judge.md:80); the fix adopted the words "for each landing a term needs" without the mechanism. The round-3 note that one-block landing multiplication is a loss (03-judge.md:149) predates r_land.

**Required revision.** Track landings per term in `TermRecord` and pay r_land only for landings up to ⌈bytes landed so far / (6 × 130,044)⌉, or pro-rate r_land by the fraction of TERM_BYTES_MAX (or of the term's certified bytes) the landing carries, so the per-term r_land exposure is exactly 3 × R_LAND_MAX as the exposure formula assumes. Keep the reserve above one term's worst case plus one landing.

### R4-C3 [Medium] A recorded REPLACE VC voids the whole certified backlog with no proof

**Trace (summarized).** A proving outage begins at X; A1 holds at ≈ X + 36 min. The current holder H reopens its view as a REPLACE view with a true, ≥ 48-s-old L1 reference; honest attesters check only A1 (landing A2), so Q sign; H calls `recordViewChange`. From that record "the inbox refuses original blocks above lastLanded" (A3): the ≈ 36 terms of locked backlog are dead even if a lander finishes a proof one minute later. ## 7. What the attackers could not break (consolidated)

From the halt report (10), the censor report (10) and the partial steal report (5), deduplicated:

1. **REPLACE against an announced backlog.** `announceLanding` is callable only while `replaceableFrom` has not passed, so no L1 reference before the announcement satisfies A1; S3d slashes any other reference. Priority holds for one grace.

4. **Sustained landing stall via an L1 fee spike above break-even.** The outage gate closes S4a; nothing voids before A1; the residual is L6 and was not re-reported.
5. **FI_SKIP gate refresh by a lander-chosen anchor tip, RESUME V8-exception loops, announceLanding chaining.** All bounded as in round 3 (once per lastLanded, keyed on the opening object, +10 min).

7. **ePBS Empty slots and no Frame Transactions.** A stale landing reverts for ≈ 25k gas plus a blob fee; a forced-batch loser pays no blob fee; no rule reads slots; the EIP-2935 window exceeds every on-time landing at 2-s slots.
