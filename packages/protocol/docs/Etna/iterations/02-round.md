## Findings and the revisions made

| Id | Sev. | Finding (one line) | Revision applied to the design |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | High | The S3a certificate-pair rule had no phHash inequality, so two valid certificates over one honest header slashed 12 to 29 whole ledgers; no signed attester object carried the opening object. | The pair rule requires `phHash_a ≠ phHash_b` ("two certificates over one header slash nobody"). `vcHash`, the view's opening object, is now a field of the Attestation, TimeoutMsg and Certificate structs, in both BLS signing domains, and in every signed header (not only the first block of a view). |
| S3 | High | An outsider with no seat could land a forced batch 35 minutes into a landing stall, reorg a locked backlog, order its own transaction first, and farm abandonment payouts from the voided holders. | Four rules together: the outage gate needs a strictly later term to have landed; an S4a **void waiver** exempts every view voided by a forced or replacement landing (reporter share only when the holder's own REPLACE or a later ordinary landing superseded it); a bonded permissionless `announceLanding(first, end)` (bond ≥ SLASH_ABANDON) defers `replaceableFrom` by LAND_CHAIN_GRACE once per range and is forfeited (40 % to the eventual forcer or replacer) if no covering landing follows; the RESUME view's first block re-includes voided transactions before serving any due forced entry (a V8 exception keyed on the opening object). Confirmations are labelled "replaceable" from `replaceableFrom`, and the "locked" credit bound is published as valid only while landing keeps up. Limitation L6 restated. |
| F1 | High | The committee walk excluded every holder by owner, so with the honest set held as five 8-seat operators every honest seat was excluded and a 20 % single-seat cartel vetoed 92 % of launch-sized terms; the collusion table was indexed by seat share only. | The walk excludes only the primary holder by owner (the party whose blocks the committee certifies) and the backups by their drawn seat only. The committee-size expectation is stated as a function of owner structure, `initEtna` requires MIN_OWNERS = 6 distinct owners and 44 seats (K + CAP + V_MAX), the collusion figures are restated (a 10-seat cartel reaches 11 slots in about 9 % of terms against five 8-seat honest owners), the "37 seats" recommendation and the "indexed by seat share" sentence are gone, and the impossible "honest 21 form a VC" sentence is corrected (the 11 abstainers decide between co-signing and a penalty-free fallback). |
| S2 | Medium | The outage gate accepted any landed term inside the window, including the last pre-outage landing, so a chain-wide outage slashed the lagged holders. | `reportAbandoned` requires `_landedTermRef > _termId`: only a landing of a strictly later term proves the chain was live past the reported term. Glossary and slashing rows harmonised. |
| S4 / F2 / H2 | Medium | The round-1 data-post answer to an availability demand could not be verified by the inbox (any blobs answered it), and a demand over a stuck-but-live chain slashed an honest committee because a post carries at most 6 blobs. | The availability escalation (S7, `demandData`, `answerDemand`, DEMAND_FEE, DEMAND_WINDOW, the voided-certificate check in L5) is **removed**. A private certificate is an unslashable stall until the replacement window opens; recorded as limitation L3 with the reason no L1 rule can distinguish unavailable from available-but-unprovable data. Class B now consists of S6 and the new S3d. |
| S5 | Medium | Reward-type debits could erode a holder's bond to the 50 % hard floor through a seatless alter-ego lander, halving every class-A credit bound. | Reward-type debits (lander ramp, blob reward, RewardBoost, attester share) stop at the buffer, 1.05 × floor; below it the holder lands itself; `minRewardPerBlockGwei` in a boost is capped at R_BLK_MAX. BUFFER_BPS rationale corrected (220 absence bleeds from buffer to hard floor, not "20 misses"). |
| S6 | Medium | `rewardTo` was not bound to the proof, so a copier or the builder took the lander reward. | `rewardTo` is bound into the journal for every landing kind; proofs are per lander and the proofs topic carries the lander address; the "proofs are fungible so copying steals nothing" claim is withdrawn (the blob bytes remain deterministic, so a prover can prove for any lander on request). |
| S7 / F4 | Medium | The round-1 rule "a timeout never strikes or floor-checks" was contradicted in eight places (figure caption, TH3, worked example, FI page, `MissPenalised(..., strikes, coalesced)`, the sequencing bond-floor sentence, README A7). | Every survivor rewritten; `MissPenalised` carries no strike field on any page; the bond-floor check is restricted to class A and B debits; README A7 restated. |
| S9 | Medium | A sentinel-view (255) sequencer that lost a landing race could not reopen without equivocating, and any bonded address could sequence a no-committee term and void the sortition holder's minute. | The sentinel opening object is defined as `keccak("ETNA_DEAD" ‖ lastLanded.blockHash ‖ t)`, accepted by Anchor rule A5 as fresh after any landing and included in every equivocation key. In a no-committee term the landing right is reserved to the sortition holder until the term's economic deadline; the open race applies only after it. |
| F3 | Medium | S3b compared locks across opening objects and did not exempt REPLACE and RESUME, so a design-following replacement signer was slashable. | S3b compares locks only under one opening object and exempts kinds REPLACE and RESUME. New objective rule **S3d, false replacement** (class B, one seat): a REPLACE timeout naming an L1 reference at which the replacement predicate did not hold, verified from the inbox's own term records. The client slashing-protection record includes `vcHash`. |
| H1 | Medium | No rule bounded a block's canonical record, so a 512-KiB block was certifiable but unlandable where an L1 block carries three blobs (2-s slots), freezing sequential landing. | Validity rule **V9**: the canonical record `headerCore ‖ seqSig ‖ VC bytes ‖ txListBytes` is at most 3 × 130,044 = 390,132 bytes, checked at attestation and asserted by the guest; MAX_ENVELOPE equals it; the lander is additionally paid R_BLOB = 1 TAIKO per blob so a legitimately large term stays landable at a profit; the per-term exposure is re-derived (3,626 TAIKO ≤ half a seat). |
| F5 | Medium | FI_SKIP aged an entry's due-ness, not its head position, so under a backlog every entry that waited four hours was voided while landings continued. | `skipped(i, T)` is gated on `T − lastLanded.anchorTipTimestamp ≥ FI_SKIP`: the rule literally means "no landing of any kind has anchored L1 time within four hours", which is what round 1 intended. |
| S8 | Low | The forced-inclusion queue was described both as "kept with the same struct" and as a fresh two-slot `FiEntry`; the refund rule was stated two ways. | The legacy struct stays at slots 255-256; `fiEntry()` normalises both layouts (legacy: `blobHashes[0]` as dataHash, `timestamp` as savedAt, `feeInGwei × 1e9` as feeWei, kind BLOB); new entries carry a version tag with kind and exact fee in a parallel mapping; one void rule everywhere (fee to the includer, no refund). |
| S10 / H3 / H4 | Low | Constant and signature drift: ROLE_HORIZON 7,200 s on the slashing page, 2Q(m) − m "2 at m = 8", `landFor(actor, packedInput)` with a batch id on the frame page, glossary S7, index "below four", roles and arguments pages at "4 TAIKO". | All corrected: ROLE_HORIZON 122,880 s everywhere with the parameters page as owner; 2Q(m) − m = 4 at m = 8 (K_MIN_CERT re-justified); the frame page's gate signature matches `landFor(address, LandInput)` with no batch id; the parameters page is normative and other pages link to it. |

Wording fixes carried from the could-not-break lists: `register(count)` with contract-assigned indices (a same-block append can no longer revert an entrant); proof of possession over (pubkey, owner, chainId) and key uniqueness; the attester reward is 0.1 TAIKO per landed block rather than 6 per landing (1-block landings earn nothing extra); T_floor uses the first segment's term.
