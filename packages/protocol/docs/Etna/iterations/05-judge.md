## 3. CONFIRMED and DOWNGRADED findings: trace, design gap, required revision

### R5S-1 [High] The S3b landed-head exemption makes a locked-block revert free

**Trace (summarized).** Cartel C holds Q = 22 of committee(t) (probability about 4·10⁻⁵ per term at one third of the seats by the certificate page's own table; committees are public 20 to 80 minutes ahead, so C picks its term). Honest leader H produces and C attests normally. Let L be a certified block of the view that is also a landed head: either a block h_a that C's own lander lands during the term (a one-block landing is legal, L1/L6, and puts h_a "into the landing ring"), or, race-free, the view's opening lock L0 (the previous view's last block, landed at the happy-path lag). A counterparty credits block n > L at the "locked" level. C's keys then sign LockVote(kind TIMEOUT, lock L) and form VC(t, v, TIMEOUT, L); L4 accepts a VC whose lock equals lastLanded when applied ("A view without certificates yields a VC at the previous lock"), leader(t, v+1) builds on L, and every block above L, including locked ones, is orphaned; MISS falls on H. Each cartel key also attested headers whose carried certificate is above L, which is exactly S3b's accept set, but the verification step "L is not a landed head in the landing ring" refuses the evidence for every signer. S3c needs two votes by one key (none); S6 needs an invalid preimage (none). Honest attesters (10 of 32) cannot form a competing VC; a FALLBACK by committee(t+1) fires only if no VC closes term t. The verifier's correction is right: landing a block certified 10 s earlier is outside the design's own 2-to-3-minute latency assumption, but L0 is landed under the happy path within the term, and for a fresh h_a the S3b check reads the ring at evidence time, so the cartel can race a challenger's `slashLockLie` with its own landing; the outcome rests on prover speed, not on a rule.

**Design gap.** Slashing §3 S3b: "L is not a landed head in the landing ring (a vote at a reset lock is mandated, never a lie; round 3, S2)"; interfaces §1 `slashLockLie` "lock not a landed head"; preconf §6 "S3b additionally ignores any lock vote whose lock equals a landed head in the inbox's landing ring"; bridge-migration §4 and interfaces §5 define the ring as "landed heads for the S3b reset check", distinct from the forced ring (ForcedRecord, ring of 8). The round-3 judge asked for the narrower rule ("S3b ignores timeouts whose (lockHeight, lockPhHash) equals a forced-ring entry or a replacement landing's head", 03-judge.md, R3-S2), and the glossary still says a lock is "reset only by a landed replacement or forced batch", but the normative text broadened the exemption to every landing. Since round 3 the RESET state already forbids TIMEOUT and TERM_END under the old object, so the exemption protects no honest signer and only the cartel case. Falsified: the "locked" row of the level table (preconf §4) and the collusion table row "revert a locked block, one redraw count: yes (S3b), 12 seats" (preconf §14); slashing §4 "Attested or locked reorg ... ≥ 13 × B = 260,000 TAIKO ≈ 39 ETH: the published credit bound for locked"; invariant I4 and P2 as stated on the arguments page. Not a duplicate: R3-S2 introduced the exemption; R4H-5 concerned recorded REPLACE VCs.

**Required revision.** Delete the landed-head exemption from S3b (RESET already makes every mandatory post-reset message a RESUME, which S3b never applies to), and delete the "landing ring" storage with it; or, if a belt is wanted, exempt only locks equal to a forced-ring entry or to the head of a landed replacement (a flag on `lastLanded`), never heads of ordinary landings. Check that no honest lock vote of kind TIMEOUT/TERM_END/FALLBACK can sit below a certificate the same key attested outside a reset (it cannot: an attester's lock is at least the carried certificate it verified at V3, and votes are monotone). Restate preconf §6, slashing §3 S3b, interfaces §1 and §5, bridge-migration §4, and, until fixed, the level table's "locked" bound as 0 for any lock equal to a landed head.

### R5S-2 [High] Evidence-driven suspension is owner-triggerable: a post-seed 22-minute grinding lever

**Trace (summarized).** The seed of cycle c is fixed at cycleStart − 1,200 s (RND2); RND3 freshness covers only DELAY_REG-dated writes (freshFrom = cycleStart − 3,660 s). An owner signs, with its own registered sequencer key, a private header for a term it held that names keccak(garbage) as its carried certificate, never gossips it, and submits it as S6 evidence against itself: S6 accepts "a signed or attested header whose carried-certificate or VC preimage fails", verified by "keccak(preimage) equals the named hash; signatures valid; preimage fails" (slashing §3), and slashing §1 states "The offender can always be its own challenger". Class B is "+3 strikes" (slashing §2) = STRIKE_THRESHOLD, and the owner machine dates the result SUSPENDED[τ + 1,320, τ + 1,320 + SUSPEND(n)) (sequencing §10) with τ chosen by the owner. `eligible(i, T)` is per owner, so one 20,000-TAIKO debit (2,000 recovered as challenger, 18,000 burned) removes up to CAP = 8 seats from E_t 22 minutes later, inside a cycle whose seed is known. A cartel with k sybil owners enumerates the 2^k subsets of self-suspensions against every term of the cycle off-chain for free and pays only on a hit. The verifier's enumeration (2^13 subsets x 58 terms at 150 seats, 50 cartel) puts a Q-committee hit at roughly 10 % per cycle at one third of the seats and about 1 % at 30 %, well below the attacker's 80 % (equal-size subsets share E_t and most seats) but orders of magnitude above the 4·10⁻⁵ per term the collusion table assumes; the paid cost per hit is 18,000 TAIKO per suspended owner plus 36 h of their revenue. A hit hands the cartel the Q-committee levers the design prices: an unslashable private-certificate stall (L3), a zero-cost attested-block revert, and R5S-1 or R5S-5.

**Design gap.** Sequencing §2 (eligibility-input rule): "suspensions from evidence against the owner (class A or B) are the only DELAY_S-dated input. No fast owner-triggered write exists, so RND3 holds for every input"; parameters §1: "which needs evidence against the owner and is not owner-triggerable"; sequencing §16 "proven No entry, exit or reserve grinding when the seed is fresh". All three rest on a premise the slashing page contradicts. Landing §5 already names a self-inflicted class-A slash as an owner action, which shows the self-evidence path was known and only its eligibility timing was overlooked. Rule D, invariant I1 and the two-interval store are untouched (the interval is at least 36 h). It is the defect class the round-4 judge rated High as R4-C2, reached through the route that fix's wording excluded by assertion. Not a duplicate: R4-C2 covered the reserve lapse only.

**Required revision.** Either (a) date every suspension, class A and B included, DELAY_REG (a class-A owner's ledger is already empty; its remaining draws inside DELAY_REG are the L20 residual), which retires DELAY_S as an eligibility input; or, better, (b) keep suspended seats in the rank domain so that no suspension changes E_t or any rank: a suspended seat drawn as a holder is a void view (skipped without timeout, as void views already are), and drawn as a committee slot it is an empty slot (m shrinks, the quorum scales), never a re-draw that shifts later ranks. Under (b) DELAY_S keeps its purpose (a slashed owner loses its own draws in 22 minutes) without a grinding surface, and E_t changes only through DELAY_REG-dated entry and exit. In both cases restate sequencing §2, §11 (DELAY_S row), §16, parameters §1 and §2 (DELAY_S row), the glossary's Rule D entry, and the collusion table's "per term" probabilities as "per term, given that no owner write changes a committee after the seed is fixed".

### R5S-5 [High] A later committee can record a FALLBACK VC for a live term: no L1 time gate, no slashable lock lie

**Trace (summarized).** Honest leader A = leader(t, 0) and honest committee(t) certify term t; block n is locked at about termEnd − 38 s and credited by a counterparty. Cartel C holds Q of committee(t+1) (same 4·10⁻⁵ per term at one third; committees are public 20 minutes ahead). C's keys pre-sign LockVote(kind FALLBACK, lock L_low) for (t, 0, closes = vcHash_t) with L_low a certified block below n (or, W1 variant, a private block h + m with a colluding leader(t+1, 0)). At termStart(t+1) + 0.5 s C calls `recordViewChange` with VC{t, 0, closes, FALLBACK, signerTerm = t+1, lock L_low}. The only gate on a FALLBACK VC is the signer's clock (preconf §5: "once termEnd(t) + END_GRACE + VC_FALLBACK · (t' − t) has passed"), and the design's principle is "Clocks decide only whether to sign"; `recordViewChange` (interfaces §1, landing §9) has time or lock conditions only for kinds REPLACE and RESUME, and "first live record per (termId, view, closes) wins". The honest TERM_END VC at lock h, formed at +2 s and submitted at +2.5 s, is refused. Landing L4 requires every VC to "equal the view change first recorded on L1 for that (t, v, closes)" and verifies a FALLBACK "against committee(signerTerm) (a FALLBACK VC may be signed by any later committee within the landing horizon)", so the honest chain above L_low, and every honest block of term t+1 that carries the honest TERM_END VC as its opening object, can never land; after replaceableFrom (35 to 65 minutes) a REPLACE from lastLanded voids them. Slashing: S3b needs an attestation by the same key under (t, 0, vcHash_t) above L_low, which committee(t+1) keys never produced; S3c is keyed on (t, v, vcHash, kind) and the two VCs differ in kind and signer set, so the 2Q − m intersection argument does not apply across committees; S6 needs an invalid preimage; silence is unslashable. W1 variant: landing L5 "Intermediate segments need no certificate of their own: each is ended by a view change whose lock certificate the VC's signers verified", so a FALLBACK lock at a private h + m needs no L1-verified certificate, and uncertified blocks land, breaking V7 with zero provable keys.

**Design gap.** The FALLBACK signer set was widened in round 1 (F9, "any later committee within the landing horizon") without an L1-checkable gate, and round 4's `recordViewChange` fix (R4H-5) added conditions for REPLACE and RESUME only. Falsified: P2 as stated ("A locked block is reverted only by ≥ 2Q(m) − m provably slashable attesters ... or by a landed replacement or forced batch after the deadline machinery"), since here the deadline machinery is forced by one unslashable record, the proof-free-void class round 4 closed for REPLACE records; preconf §16 "proven W1 requires two VCs for one (t, v), hence 12 S3c-slashable keys"; the collusion table rows "revert a locked block ... yes (S3b) 12 seats" and "handoff ambush (W1) 12 VC double-signers yes (S3c)". The published 39-ETH "locked" credit bound is 0 in any term whose next committee holds Q cartel seats. Not a duplicate: L5 and round-3 halt item 9 are the reverse direction (colluding committee(t) racing honest committee(t+1)'s fallback and invalidating about a minute of the uncertified fallback chain); this voids an honest term's locked blocks.

**Required revision.** (i) In `recordViewChange` and in L4, accept a FALLBACK VC for term t only if block.timestamp ≥ termEnd(t) + END_GRACE + VC_FALLBACK · (signerTerm − t) and no live VC for (t, v, closes) is recorded. (ii) Require the landing that consumes a FALLBACK VC to verify a certificate at the FALLBACK lock (or accept a FALLBACK lock only at lastLanded), so a FALLBACK can never land uncertified blocks (restoring V7 across committees). (iii) Close the residual same-block race the time gate leaves at 12-s slots (the honest TERM_END VC formed at +2.5 s may reach L1 in the same block as a FALLBACK signable at +12 s): give committee(t)'s own TIMEOUT or TERM_END VC precedence over a recorded FALLBACK for the same (t, v, closes) until a landing has consumed the FALLBACK, or refuse a FALLBACK while a TERM_END VC of committee(t) at a higher lock is presented in the same call; state which, and fold the remaining residual into L5 with its bound. (iv) Restate P2 (arguments §1, §2), preconf §5, §7 (W1), §14 and §16, slashing §4, and the level table for cross-committee VCs.

### R5S-4 [Medium] Routine self-landing drains the landing reserve: honest owners lapse in about 24 own terms

**Trace (summarized).** Landing §5, slashing §1 and §6 define every reward-type debit (ramp, R_BLOB, r_land, attester share) as drawn from the holder's `reserveGwei` and credited "to the rewardTo that the proof's journal binds, in the same transaction"; slashing §1 states of a self-directed landing that the funds move "from the reserve to a withdrawable balance". Nothing on any page routes a credit whose rewardTo is the holder's own owner back into `reserveGwei`; the only refill is `deposit()` ("tops up seat stake, buffer and reserve", interfaces §1), which the roles page never lists as a sequencer duty. The ramp starts at term end and the happy-path landing of a term's tail is 2 to 3 minutes after it (landing §5 timeline), so an honest self-lander at +150 s pays r ≈ 0.79/block (47.5), 15 for blobs, r_land ≈ 108 and 6 to other owners' attesters: about 177 TAIKO of reserve per own term (recomputed; 51 TAIKO even if the landing were included at term end exactly). A single-seat owner (8,400) crosses ReserveLow (8,322) on its first self-landing, so the warning carries no information, and the 4,161 lapse line after 24 own terms, roughly a day at 1 of 72 seats; an 8-seat owner (67,200) lasts about 358 own terms, about 54 h at 8 of 72. The lapse is DELAY_REG-dated and lasts at least SUSPEND(3) = 36 h (landing §5, slashing §9 RESERVE_LAPSED). No attacker is needed.

**Design gap.** Roles §1 "Self-landing costs only L1 gas" and slashing §6 "Self-landing nets zero" are true of the total balance and false of the sub-balance that governs eligibility; the reserve-sizing argument ("two terms of exposure", "P(more than 16 terms in one window) < 0.1 %") models late third-party landings only. Invariant I2's residual (L20) treats the self-directed drain as a deliberate maximum-ramp action costing the owner a SUSPEND(3); it is the outcome of honest happy-path operation. Corrections to the trace: nothing is stolen (the credit is the owner's own withdrawable balance); thresholds are per owner, so large owners lapse later, not at the same elapsed time. Not a duplicate: R4H-3, R4-C4 and L20 never considered the honest drain or the useless ReserveLow warning. Medium: no funds lost, a one-line contract rule fixes it, but honest small operators are silently suspended on a day timescale and the published sizing argument is wrong (R1 entrant burden, R7 funded landing).

**Required revision.** Credit any reward-type credit whose rewardTo resolves to an owner with seats into that owner's `reserveGwei` first, up to its required level, and only the excess into the withdrawable balance (this also refills the attester-share drain from committee income); equivalently, skip the debit and credit when lander and holder share an owner. Re-derive the reserve sizing with the honest drain included, make ReserveLow fire once per crossing with hysteresis, list "keep the reserve funded" as a sequencer duty with the client's automatic top-up, and restate roles §1, landing §5, slashing §1/§6/§9, L20 and I2.

### R5H-1 and R5-C1 [Medium, one defect] The leader-liveness signal is free and unconditional: header-gossip squatting and no takeover for a non-serving holder

**Trace (summarized).** The normative trigger (preconf §1, §5, §10; sequencing §7; glossary "Timeout"): an attester signs TIMEOUT for (t, v) only after TIMEOUT seconds with "no header of (t, v) with a valid leader signature (V1, V2) at a height above its lock, and no new certificate"; "Headers that fail V3 ... still count as liveness"; the ATTESTER machine restarts its timer "on each signed header of (t, v) above lock_i"; the SEQUENCER HOLD state says "keep gossiping signed provisional headers above the tip" with no payload, height or rate bound (preconf §18 lists that as an open question); the only pre-relay bound is height within [lock − 64, lock + 512] (§9). A holder O that never releases a certifiable block signs and gossips one header per 4.9 s at fresh heights lock+1, lock+2, ... (no S1 pair), with or without a body. No honest attester ever signs TIMEOUT; no VC forms; no backup takes over; the term is blank to termEnd. MISS is applied only "when a landed batch carries a timeout view change" (sequencing §7), so O is never debited, never strikes and never reaches the 200-draw hard floor. Summed over a tenure, O blanks its whole primary share w/A at zero protocol cost; a cartel does it on all its primaries.

**Design gap.** Sequencing §7 "Squatting is deterred by the bleed alone: a seat that never serves loses 50 TAIKO per draw and reaches the 50 % hard floor after 200 draws", sequencing §12 and slashing §11 "TH10 squatting: defended by bleed", README A7 "hard bound is the committee timeout (5 s)" (quoted in the index R4 verdict), sequencing §12 TH3 "Replacement within TIMEOUT per dark view", arguments §4 "withholding ... times out in 5 s", and the MIN_TENURE pricing ("drawn and bled for at least a week") are all false for a header-gossiping non-server. The design concedes the single-term case in preconf §14, sequencing §14 and slashing §13 ("keeps the view alive without certified progress, unrewarded, for at most the term") without carrying the consequence to the claims above. Severity: the harm is confined to the squatter's own primary terms (60 s blank instead of a 5-s takeover), no safety effect, forced inclusion and other holders' terms proceed, and the squatter idles B_SEAT per seat and forgoes its own revenue; TH10 is Medium in the threat model. R5-C1's additional claims are overstated: the "55-s free ordering option" and "TH6 window 5 s → 60 s" are not new (a holder emitting empty certified blocks and including everything at blocks 56-60 has the same option today; STALE_MAX and V4 still bound private state to at most 2 blocks), and Variant B shifts no revenue (the cartel blanks its own terms and earns less). Not a duplicate: R4H-4's fix created this surface, and round 4's self-blanking item was judged under the replaced attested-based rule. The halt attacker's proposed "produced-but-uncertified" debit is not adoptable: on L1 it is indistinguishable from an abstaining minority blanking an honest leader (the R4H-4 case), and a Q-signed availability vote is the rejected data-post class.

**Required revision.** Bound the liveness signal to the leader's own next attestable blocks: an attester restarts its timer only on a header of (t, v) at height in {lock + 1, lock + 2} with a valid leader signature and an available payload that passes V1, V2 and V6, with PH.timestamp ≤ now + CLOCK_SKEW (otherwise a lock+1 block stamped termEnd − 1 passes and is unattestable until 59 s). A leader held by an abstaining minority keeps re-gossiping its two real uncertified blocks and is still never timed out (R4H-4's property survives); a leader withholding payloads or bodies is timed out at 5 s and cannot later re-sign lock + 1 with new content (S1). Then restate A7, TH3, TH6, TH10, MIN_TENURE's rationale, preconf §14 (the "unrewarded, for at most the term" row becomes "timed out at 5 s"), sequencing §7/§12/§14, slashing §11/§13 and arguments §4; close preconf §18's open question.

### R5H-2 [Medium] The committee-walk gas bound omits the mutation-ring correction term M; under delayed pinning it compounds into unpinnable or unlandable terms

**Trace (summarized).** Sequencing §5 and §8 state the per-lookup cost of the corrected Fenwick descent as O(log MAX_SEATS + M) and the committee walk as up to 512 such lookups; the published worst case ("about 0.6 M gas at a 128-seat registry ... up to about 3 M at the 65,535-seat maximum", repeated on the parameters page) carries no M term. M has no aggregate cap: "M is priced" bounds each owner to 16 entry/exit mutations per week, but owners are unbounded, so N seats bought across N/8 addresses (300 seats ≈ 8.5 M TAIKO locked about 16 days) yield about 300 exit mutations that `requestExit` can cluster into a 2-h effective window, and honest churn at a large registry produces the same M with no attacker. The correction set for term t is "the ring entries with effective time in (termStart(t), now]", so M is small only if t is pinned promptly; pinning is voluntary and unrewarded ("landers pin ahead with recordAssignment(t)", gas "paid once per term by whoever pins it"), landings pin at most SETTLE_MAX = 4 terms each, and during a stall (proving outage, fees above the ~100 gwei break-even, L1 congestion) nobody has an obligation or incentive to pin. Under the stated per-lookup cost, N = 300 puts a standalone `recordAssignment` near the EIP-7825 cap (512 × ~316 reads) and far over LANDING_GAS_BUDGET when walked inside a landing; even an implementation that loads the ring once spends O(512 · M) per term, r_land (sized for a 2 M-gas landing) does not cover it, third-party landers stop, and catch-up of a backlog (up to 2,048 terms, each needing its own walk) becomes uneconomic or impossible exactly when R7 catch-up matters. TH13's defence ("≤ 2.5 M gas") silently assumes a pinned term.

**Design gap.** The gas bound is wrong as stated, the normative complexity is the pessimistic per-lookup form, and nothing makes the M ≈ 0 path the incentivised one. Why Medium rather than High: the halt is not indefinite (a term whose start is after the burst's window has M ≈ 0, so once replaceableFrom passes the holder forks a REPLACE view or anyone lands a forced batch and the chain resumes within about the burst span plus one landing; the stalled backlog, up to 34 h of locked blocks, is voided without penalty, an amplification of the accepted L6 residual); the attack needs a pre-existing pin lag of at least the burst span (MutationScheduled events give 2 h of notice for any watcher to pin), and adversarial congestion long enough to suppress 1-to-3-M-gas pins costs far more than the pins; the cheap version relies on an honest stall or large honest churn.

**Required revision.** Gather the correction set once per pin (sorted by seat id; O(M + 512 · log MAX_SEATS · log M)) and state that as the normative complexity with an M-inclusive gas bound; cap the mutations effective per unit time on-chain (queue a registry write whose effective slot already holds K mutations to the next slot) so the walk is provably O(512 · log + M_cap); pay or reward prompt `recordAssignment` from a small per-term pool (or let the first lander that pins a term keep a fixed share of r_land) so the M ≈ 0 path is incentivised under congestion; restate sequencing §5/§8, parameters (MAX_TRIES_COMMITTEE row), landing §3 gas and TH13.

## 7. What the attackers could not break (consolidated)

Merged from the three reports (steal 11, halt 7, censor 10 items), deduplicated and grouped; each holds against the design as written.

State safety and proofs (P1):
1. Forged state root through the journal bindings: parent root from storage, blob hashes from `blobhash`, anchor tip against blockhash or EIP-2935, FI queue from storage, end hash bound by an L1-verified certificate; only a ZK break (out of scope) works, and single-proof mode after 2 h still needs a quorum certificate (steal 8).
2. Copying another lander's proof to take its reward: the journal binds rewardTo for every landing kind, so a swapped address yields a different journal hash (steal 1).

Withholding, replacement and records (P2 outside the three Highs):
3. Profit from withholding or abandoning after S4a's removal: withheld blocks have no certificate and die in TIMEOUT; a certified view is landable by anyone; MISS is burned so a false VC pays no one (steal 3).
4. A recorded REPLACE or RESUME VC to void fresh locked blocks: accepted only with lock == lastLanded after replaceableFrom, dead once lastLanded moves, no closing effect (steal 5).
5. Chaining `announceLanding` to defer the forced path indefinitely: one deferral per lastLanded plus the C3 refusal; the residual is the one extra grace of R5-C3 (censor 8).
6. Announcement-bond theft: 40 % of 2,000 TAIKO against about 1.2 M gas, two proofs and a 35-minute stall the forcer cannot create; refused once the head FI entry is due longer than LAND_WINDOW; negative expected value (steal 4).
7. Cross-redraw locked-block revert with precomputed committees: at one third of the seats no term in 20,000 had ≥ 17 in draw 0 and ≥ 22 in a redraw; the design's 6·10⁻⁷ per view stands inside T11 (censor 4).

Sortition, randomness and registry:
8. Seed-known grinding through any DELAY_REG-dated owner input (reserve withdrawal, reactivate, exit, re-register): every such input is 7,200-s dated against a 3,660-s freshness window; the reserve is non-withdrawable while a seat is active (censor 1; the DELAY_S route is R5S-2).
9. Pinning a committee of one's choosing after a redraw: L4 derives the redrawn committee by the inbox's own walk keccak(seed ‖ 0x03 ‖ t ‖ n ‖ m), justified by 17 REDRAW signatures of committee n − 1; calldata key hashes are checked against the pin (steal 6).
10. Mutation-ring ordering and lazy application: entries at exactly termStart(t) are kept, later ones undone, `recordAssignment` reverts before termStart; no node/inbox divergence was found (steal 7).
11. Excluding an entrant at registration: indices are contract-assigned (FREE holes first, then appends); the array cap costs more than the supply; the BLS key is protected by the proof of possession (censor 2).
12. Migration term-record ring collision: `land` reverts until the Shasta ring is drained and M1 requires every proposal proven or abandoned, so no Etna record is written to the aliased ring while a Shasta proposal is live; firstEtnaTerm ≤ about 1,500 even at a 25-h drain (halt 5).
13. Launch padding then exit: MIN_TENURE binds fresh seats for 7 days ≥ MIGRATION_LEAD, so padded seats are drawn through T0; the residual is the L2 cartel case (censor 6; the bleed part is R5H-1).

Takeover, timeouts and committees:
14. Taking a producing leader's view with fewer than Q attesters, or via the redraw vote: a TIMEOUT needs Q signers each without a signed header for 5 s; REDRAW changes no holder, applies no penalty and is capped at 2; a cartel below 17 gains nothing from it (halt 2, halt 3, censor 3).
15. Partition halt or false takeover: a minority can only blank; on heal the node verifies the view change and stops; a majority partition is outside T11 (halt 2).
16. Bleeding an online owner through the hard floor or the reserve: MISS never touches the reserve and needs a Q-signed TIMEOUT VC; reward debits never touch seat stake (censor 10).
17. Reserve-lapse griefing through third-party landings: the lander pays about 2 M gas per landing, r_land starts at 0 at term end and is gated by bytes landed, the holder can self-land first, and ReserveLow fires ahead; a net loss to the griefer (steal 2, halt 4, censor 5; the honest-path drain is R5S-4).
18. Attester-reward skimming by bitmap choice or last-block-only signing: the whole pool is 6 TAIKO per term and a lander still needs Q valid attestations; immaterial (steal 9).

Forced inclusion and censorship:
19. FI stuffing, skipping or self-request to censor or farm fees: quadratic fee plus 196k state gas per entry bounds delay (L8); FI_SKIP needs four hours without any landing; a skipped entry's bond and fee burn and the floor quadruples; the T_floor count forces every landing to consume due entries; self-stuffing is zero-sum at best (steal 10, censor 7).
20. Turning V10 or the byte budget into a MISS or a V8 deadlock: FI blocks are exempt from V10 and a sequencer block is always the sequencer's choice (censor 9).

Liveness floors and L1 environment:
21. Prover-failure halt: an outage shorter than the horizon voids nothing and slashes nobody; poke() keeps the seed fresh; single-proof mode after 2 h; the reserve is ring-fenced from liveness debits (halt 1).
22. Dead-mode or no-committee unrecoverability: after DEAD_TERMS = 75 any address with B_SEAT sequences with sentinel view 255 and the first landing wins, no DAO action; armEtna failing only starts launch in no-committee mode (halt 7).
23. Short slots, ePBS Empty slots, 4788 gaps, no Frame Transactions: nothing counts slots, LOOKBACK and DELAY grow in blocks as slots shrink, 4788 is never read, and without EIP-8141 the primary path is a type-3 transaction with a deadline; the only short-slot pressure is the landing gas budget, which is R5H-2 (halt 6).
24. Challenger loops and self-reporting: each debit pays at most min(10 %, 2,000) and duplicates are keyed, so self-reporting always loses at least 90 % of the debit (steal 11; the eligibility effect of self-reporting is R5S-2, a grinding lever, not a profit).
