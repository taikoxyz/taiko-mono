## 2. Roles

### S4-R01. The role set and the rule that no role has a gate

**[assumed: proposed rule]** Etna has seven roles, as the roles page counts them: two bonded roles that share one registration (the sequencer, which is the holder and leader of a view, and the attester); three unbonded roles that need no registration (the lander and prover, the challenger, the forced-inclusion requester); the passive node operator; and the DAO. Owner, seat, holder and leader name facts of the bonded roles (§1). The hatch lander is not counted as a role of its own: a hatch is a landing through `land` with view 254 (C3-R07), open to anyone because HATCH_BOND = 0 (D51(3), which supersedes D39's "bonded"; C3-R07 check 2 reads `rewardTo`'s ledger balance against HATCH_BOND and locks nothing), so landing one is the lander role's action. The RPC node and the archive are duties and holdings of data that the pages place under the node operator (roles page §6) and under C2-R18, not roles of their own; the C1 caller of a pin or reveal and the bridge relayer are voluntary actions, not roles (C1 §5; roles page §6). No role is admitted, removed or paused by anyone in day-to-day operation. Entry to a bonded role is a registration with a bond and a code-less key, with no admission decision (S1-R04, S1-R05); an owner address with a class-A `slashedAt` cannot register again (`OwnerSlashed`, D38), an objective rule on a recorded slash rather than a decision, a new address being a new owner; entry to every other role is a plain L1 transaction or nothing (C2-R01, C3-R01, S3-R08). Exit is the owner's own (S1-R07) or permissionless after a class A slash or a stale suspension (S1-R08). **Adversary schedule:** an admission or removal decision, a pauser key, an owner-gated function on a liveness path, a treasury or discretionary recipient, or the DAO used as an operator; none exists on an Etna contract, and C4-R10 (merged, D32) removes the pause and every owner-only operational power of the shared contracts, D26's three DAO-only powers included (S4-R04). Source: roles page, introduction and §9 first claim; rounds 4 (R4H-5, R4-C2) and 6 (R6-C1); after the #22188 review for the shared contracts; D26, D30, D38.
