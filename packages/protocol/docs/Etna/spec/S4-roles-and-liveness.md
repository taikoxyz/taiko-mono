## 2. Roles

### S4-R01. The role set and the rule that no role has a gate

**[assumed: proposed rule]** Etna has seven roles, as the roles page counts them: two bonded roles that share one registration (the sequencer, which is the holder and leader of a view, and the attester); three unbonded roles that need no registration (the lander and prover, the challenger, the forced-inclusion requester); the passive node operator; and the DAO. Owner, seat, holder and leader name facts of the bonded roles (§1). The RPC node and the archive are duties and holdings of data that the pages place under the node operator (roles page §6) and under C2-R18, not roles of their own; the C1 caller of a pin or reveal and the bridge relayer are voluntary actions, not roles (C1 §5; roles page §6). No role is admitted, removed or paused by anyone in day-to-day operation. Entry to a bonded role is a registration with a bond and a code-less key, with no admission decision (S1-R04, S1-R05); entry to every other role is a plain L1 transaction or nothing (C2-R01, C3-R01, S3-R08). Exit is the owner's own (S1-R07) or permissionless after a class A slash or a stale suspension (S1-R08). **Adversary schedule:** an admission or removal decision, a pauser key, an owner-gated function on a liveness path, a treasury or discretionary recipient, or the DAO used as an operator; none exists on an Etna contract, and C4 removes the pause and owner-only operational powers of the shared contracts (S4-R04). Source: roles page, introduction and §9 first claim; rounds 4 (R4H-5, R4-C2) and 6 (R6-C1); after the #22188 review for the shared contracts.

**[proven: no admission, removal or pause gate on any Etna contract, by enumeration; conditional on C4 for the shared contracts]** Registry writes are the owner's own or permissionless (S1-R03, S1-R04, S1-R06 to S1-R08, S1 §13 second proven paragraph); every suspension flag is set by evidence or ledger arithmetic, never by a decision (S1-R09, S3-R05); landing, evidence, forced-inclusion and queue functions are callable by anyone (C2-R01, S3-R08, C3-R01, C3-R11); no accusation object exists (S3-R09). The shared contracts' selector audit is C4's and is open (§9).
