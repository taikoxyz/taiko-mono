### C6-R12. How a limitation enters and leaves the register

**[assumed: proposed rule]** A residual enters as a row of C6-R08 with a new id (the next free L number; page ids are kept) when a section, a red-team round or a review names it; it is **proposed** until a decision-log entry, or B's and J's reviews of this section each stating that they accept the row as a residual, ratify it, then **recorded** (§1's single test). A section that states a limitation inside another's text (C3's stall inside L16) cites the id assigned here.
