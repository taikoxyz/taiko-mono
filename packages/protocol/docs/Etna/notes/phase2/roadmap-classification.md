# L1 roadmap survey — Etna classification column (Glamsterdam + Hegotá)

Date of all fetches: **2026-09-30**. Inputs: `roadmap-glamsterdam.md`, `roadmap-hegota.md` (same directory). Every EIP classified below was **re-fetched at run time** from `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ethereum/EIPs/master/EIPS/eip-NNNN.md` (local copies in `raw-classify/`); the Gloas consensus specs were fetched from `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/master/specs/gloas/{beacon-chain,fork-choice,validator,builder,p2p-interface}.md`; client configs from `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/eth-clients/{mainnet,sepolia,hoodi}/main/metadata/config.yaml`. Quotes are verbatim from those files. Claims are tagged **VERIFIED** (primary source fetched, URL given) or **UNVERIFIED**.

Etna hard requirements used as the yardstick (from the task brief): **R1** fully permissionless (no whitelist); **R2** reuse existing SignalService/Bridge/Vault addresses; **R4** 1-second L2 preconfirmed blocks; **R5** no dependence on the CL proposer lookahead and no coupling to L1 slot time (12 s / 6 s / 4 s / 2 s must all work; may consume EIP-4788 roots); **R6** permissionless slashing verifiable on L1; **R7** propose-with-proof in one L1 action (batch data + ZK proof), blobs for DA. Baseline: Frame Transactions (EIP-8141) assumed live before Etna launches.

Stance vocabulary:
- **REQUIRES** — Etna cannot meet R1–R7 without it; a fallback design or "cannot launch without it" is given.
- **OPTIONAL** — Etna works without it, but adopting it improves cost, latency, safety or simplicity.
- **IGNORES** — no dependency and no meaningful benefit. **IGNORES (tolerate)** = no dependency, but the change alters Etna's environment (gas, header format, timing, mempool) and the design must be robust to it; the "tolerate" note says how.

Run-time fork status (VERIFIED from EIP-7773 and EIP-8081 on `master`, 2026-09-30): Glamsterdam SFI = 2780, 7688, 7708, 7732, 7778, 7843, 7928, 7954, 7976, 7981, 7997, 8024, 8037, 8038, 8045, 8061, 8246, 8282 (+ networking 7975, 8070, 8136, 8159, 8189; informational 7904, 8261); Sepolia activation epoch 353024 = 2026-10-06 13:53:36 UTC, Hoodi/mainnet rows empty. Hegotá SFI = 7805, 8141; CFI = 3298, 7668, 7906, 7979, 8015, 8131, 8163, 8250, 8253, 8272, 8279; PFI = 27 EIPs; DFI = 18 EIPs (lists reproduced in `roadmap-hegota.md`). Mainnet slot time `SLOT_DURATION_MS: 12000`, `BLOB_SCHEDULE` max 21 since epoch 419072, no `GAS_LIMIT_SCHEDULE` on mainnet; Sepolia `GAS_LIMIT_SCHEDULE: 200000000 @ 353024`.

### 1d. Direct answers to the "pay special attention" questions

1. **ePBS — execution vs payment.** Builder is charged at beacon-block processing (`process_execution_payload_bid`, "the committed value is deducted from the builder's beacon chain balance"); the L2 tx is executed only after the payload is revealed (≤50 % of slot) and "not widely validated until the proposer of slot `N+1` releases their beacon block on top of block `N`". Payment to the proposer is settled in the child block (`apply_parent_execution_payload` → `settle_builder_payment`) and is unconditional even for an Empty slot. So "builder paid" ≠ "Etna tx executed". VERIFIED (G(beacon-chain), R(7732)).
2. **ePBS — same-slot races.** The builder has a per-slot monopoly ("no possible *unbundling* of the builder's payload in the same slot"); competing Etna proposals are ordered by that builder; payload content is committed via `bid.block_hash` before the beacon block, so submission must reach the builder during slot N−1/early N. Losers should be cheap or mempool-invalid. VERIFIED.
3. **ePBS — blob availability.** Blob columns are broadcast by the builder with the payload (≥ t=50 %), PTC attests availability by t=75 %, and `on_execution_payload_envelope` requires `is_data_available`. L2 derivation from blobs starts ~half a slot later than today; preconfirmations (R4) cover the gap. VERIFIED (G(validator), G(fork-choice)).
4. **Block-level access lists — propose-with-proof gas.** No per-tx gas change; single-tx verifier cost is unaffected by parallelism; indirect benefit through the 200 M gas limit. VERIFIED (R(7928), C(sepolia)).
5. **FOCIL.** Gives 1-of-N-honest, fork-choice-enforced inclusion of *public-mempool, non-blob, still-valid* txs within ~1–2 slots; does not guarantee ordering, inclusion of blob txs, inclusion when invalidated by a race, or inclusion in a full block. Etna's forced inclusion can use it as the L1 leg; the L2 leg (deadline + permissionless proposal) is Etna's. VERIFIED (R(7805), R(8369)).
6. **Slot time.** See §3. Etna assumptions that would break if written in blocks/slots: forced-inclusion deadlines, proving windows, bond/challenge periods, "N L2 blocks per L1 block", 4788/2935/BLOCKHASH lookback windows, per-block blob capacity. VERIFIED (R(7782), R(8198), R(4788), R(7709)).
7. **Blob count / PeerDAS.** Mainnet max 21 (BPO2) with no change scheduled; per-block blob capacity halves/thirds under shorter slots (per-second constant); reserve price 8 192 × base fee per blob (7918); senders compute cell proofs (7594). VERIFIED.
8. **Frame Transactions.** Enable: sponsored/contract-sender blob-carrying proposals, atomic multi-frame propose+prove+bond, mempool-enforced timestamp expiry, validation-controlled nonce increment (nonce-as-lock). Degrade gracefully to type-3 txs from EOA/7702 EOAs with on-chain reverts instead of mempool invalidation. VERIFIED (R(8141)).
9. **EIP-4788.** Optional; randomness available via `PREVRANDAO` without it; window is 8191 roots (wall-clock shrinks with slot time); ePBS Empty slots leave root(N−1) unrecorded — fallback via `state.block_roots` under root(N). VERIFIED (R(4788), G(fork-choice)).
10. **Gas repricing — proof verification.** Glamsterdam reprices state access/creation, calldata floor, intrinsic gas; it does **not** reprice precompiles (7904 is informational, 8200 EVMification is DFI). Verifier cost ≈ unchanged; bookkeeping SSTOREs ×3.6 (write) and new slots ~98 k state gas. VERIFIED (R(8038), R(8037), R(7773), R(8081)).
11. **Nonce semantics.** Only 8250 (frame senders; keeps one-pending-per-sender in the public mempool) and 8253 (28 legacy accounts). VERIFIED.

**Nothing in either fork is a REQUIRES for Etna**; therefore no "cannot launch without it" item exists. Etna can launch on today's Fusaka mainnet and remains valid through Glamsterdam and Hegotá provided the tolerate notes above are honoured.
