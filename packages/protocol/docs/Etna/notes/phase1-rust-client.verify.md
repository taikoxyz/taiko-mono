## Overall

The summary is safe to rely on for the gates, timing constants, invariants and interface signatures it lists: 81 of 83 spot-checked claims match the code line-for-line, including every access gate (gossip signer whitelist, node self-whitelist, JWT surface, ingress readiness, staleness, proposer epoch-operator check, anchor-tx authenticity), every timing constant checked (384 s refresh, 24 s submit timeout, 6 s probe, 12 s reorg reset, 144 s shutdown window, 12×128 / 12×512 s offsets, 10 s parent-request cooldown) and the engine/derivation ordering. Two claims are imprecise rather than wrong: C14 says "distinct" hashes per height but the tracker does not dedupe hashes, and C47 omits the branch where a lower L2 RPC head beats `head_l1_origin` as the resume point. Readers should re-check three things themselves: (1) the per-height gossip caps count messages, not unique blocks, so an equivocating operator's budget is slightly different from what §9/§10 imply; (2) the "best-effort" materialized probe in §4.5 actually propagates RPC errors; (3) FCU-INVALID never aborts derivation (only newPayload-INVALID does), which matters when reasoning about liveness under a malicious proposal. Everything about epoch/slot coupling (hard-coded 12 s slot, 32-slot epoch, no `getOperatorForCurrentEpoch` in the whitelist driver, membership-only signer check) is confirmed and is the most important design input for a permissionless successor.
