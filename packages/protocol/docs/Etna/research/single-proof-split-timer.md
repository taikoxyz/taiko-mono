# Single-proof throughput: research note on a split timer

**Labels.** [proven] means an argument written out on this page or in the cited spec text, not machine verification. [assumed] means a premise, a proposed rule, or a figure nobody has measured. [open] means unresolved; where possible the note says what would close it. Figures use C2 §12's values at `982aac6` unless stated: DEGRADE_AFTER 7,200 s, DEGRADED_FINALITY 86,400 s, PROVISIONAL_RING 12, RETAIN_SECONDS 7,200 s, MAX_VIEWS 4, MAX_BLOCKS 256, MAX_BLOBS 6, LANDING_BYTES 390,132 bytes, TERM 60 blocks of 1 s, ROLE_HORIZON 122,880 s, and C7-R13's TERM_BYTES_MAX 1,270,000 bytes.

## 1. Purpose and status

This is a research note. It is not a specification and it changes no rule. It records the design round of 2026-10-03 on finding landing-02 with cross-section-01 (C2-R12's single-proof throughput, the proposed limitation L-SPT), run after B's review of `982aac6` (#22236, comment 5972414158), which asked for a design round before user escalation: "separate the outage-entry timer (last ≥ZK_K landing) from degraded admission spacing, and resize the provisional ring/retention/reward exposure together". B also stated that "a timer-only change retaining 12 records is unsafe" and that B sees "no demonstrated impossibility forcing L-SPT acceptance".

**Outcome [assumed: review record].** The candidate below was attacked twice. Both attacks found it unsound as proposed. Their amendments fix several problems, but not every problem clearly: the throughput claim fails under byte load (§4, P1), and the fixes for the ring restart, the held money and the mode's stickiness each add new rules that no attack round has examined. So the spec keeps the L-SPT disclosure ([open: user sign-off], not signed; label unified 2026-10-03 with the README and C8-traceability, earlier "[open: user decision]"; C2-R12, §16's R1 row) with its premises corrected under B-W22-A05, and this note holds the candidate as **[open: research]**. Nothing here is user-signed, and nothing here is adopted.

## 2. The defect [proven: C2-R12 at `982aac6`]

C2-R12 uses one clock, `lastLanded.at`, for two jobs: deciding that an outage has started (no landing that updates `lastLanded.at` for DEGRADE_AFTER), and spacing one-leaf landings, which is what bounds the provisional ring (C2-R13, PROVISIONAL_RING = 12 = DEGRADED_FINALITY / DEGRADE_AFTER). Every one-leaf landing resets that clock, so the mode admits one landing per DEGRADE_AFTER, each of at most 240 blocks (four occupied terms, not necessarily consecutive; 240 s of chain only under full production, B-W22-A05). A timer-only change that keeps 12 records is unsafe: with a spacing `S` below 7,200 s, more than 12 records are unfinalized inside one DEGRADED_FINALITY, and slot `k % 12` would be overwritten while live [proven: arithmetic].

## 3. The candidate [assumed: candidate rule, not adopted]

1. **Split timer.** A new word `lastStrongAt` (`uint48`) is set by every accepted landing that updates `lastLanded.at` and whose L11 verification accepted at least ZK_K distinct leaves. The inbox is in SINGLE-PROOF iff `block.timestamp − lastStrongAt > DEGRADE_AFTER` and not in CONFLICT. Inside the mode a certified landing gets `kRequired = 1` iff also `block.timestamp − lastLanded.at ≥ DEGRADED_SPACING`; every other landing needs ZK_K. A hatch preserves both clocks.
2. **Ring and pass.** PROVISIONAL_RING ≥ DEGRADED_FINALITY / DEGRADED_SPACING; a finalization pass handles at most FINALIZE_MAX records; a one-leaf append reverts `ProvisionalRingFull` while its slot holds a live record. The 24 h boundary is explicit: a conflict or confirmation is accepted iff `block.timestamp < landedAt + DEGRADED_FINALITY`, and a record is time-finalizable iff `block.timestamp ≥ landedAt + DEGRADED_FINALITY`.
3. **Retention.** RETAIN_SECONDS rises to cover the backlog age in the mode (14,400 s was proposed against a floor of 4,500 + 7,200 + 120 = 11,820 s).
4. **Held money.** A one-leaf landing makes its reserve debits at landing but holds its reward credits, attester shares and C3-R06 step 5 money under `heldHash`, released by `releaseProvisional(k, H)` once record `k` is FINAL.
5. **Proposed defaults.** DEGRADED_SPACING 120 s, PROVISIONAL_RING 1,024, FINALIZE_MAX 16.

What survived both attacks [proven, as the attacks checked it]: the superset property (every one-leaf landing admitted at `982aac6` is still admitted), the spacing property, the derived ring floor DF / S = 720 at S = 120 s, the liveness arithmetic 1,024 × 120 = 122,880 ≥ 86,400, the 11,820 s retention floor, the 13,620 s anchor-age bound (a0 + 7,320 + 1,800 at a0 = 4,500), and the slot-278 packing of `lastStrongAt`.

## 4. Problems found, with the amendment each attack proposed

| # | Problem | Status after amendment |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | **Bytes, not blocks, bind [proven: arithmetic].** A landing carries at most LANDING_BYTES = 390,132 bytes, while V10 allows up to TERM_BYTES_MAX = 1,270,000 bytes per 60-s term. At S = 120 s the one-leaf capacity is about 0.154× production at V10's cap; it reaches 1× only while terms average at most about 195,066 bytes. Under load the backlog grows and the horizon and retention losses return when the chain is busiest. | **[open]** Either keep S = 120 s with an explicit byte premise and move byte-loaded terms into the L-SPT residual, or lower S to about 18 s (390,132 / (1,270,000 / 60)) and re-derive the ring at 86,400 / S (at least 4,800), the pass cap, the drain count and the record gas. Neither was attacked. |
| P2 | **Restart and a large ring.** C2-R14's restart clears the ring: 1,024 records of 8 words is about 8,192 zeroing stores, about 41M gas; clearing only the cursor leaves stale PROVISIONAL records that make `ProvisionalRingFull` revert for good. | **[open]** Keep the cursor monotonic, store the voided range, and treat a voided slot as free (a C4-R12 obligation on B). Not attacked. |
| P3 | **Held money stranded.** The append checks FINAL, not released, so a FINAL unreleased record can be overwritten, destroying `heldHash` and leaving debited TAIKO with no ledger owner (S3-R20's conservation). `H` was never emitted, so third parties cannot rebuild it. | Fixable as stated: require FINAL and released (or voided), emit `ProvisionalHeld(k, H)`, keep `releaseProvisional` callable in CONFLICT for FINAL records before `r`, and add held TAIKO as a ledger line. **[assumed]** |
| P4 | **Stickiness is a security change [proven: schedule].** An F-only lander moving `lastLanded` every 120 s makes every stale ZK_K proof fail `NotNext`, and rational landers prefer one proof. The mode can then stay open while both leaves work, so every record's safety rests on one leaf, a quorum and a watcher's counterproof within 24 h, and checkpoints lag 24 h. At `982aac6` the mode closed itself after one record. | **[open]** Candidate exit: a successful `confirmProvisional` of the newest record sets `lastStrongAt`; or a cap MODE_MAX. Either breaks the invariant `lastStrongAt ≤ lastLanded.at` and needs a fresh superset proof. Not attacked. |
| P5 | **CONFLICT front-run.** A conflict raised on a later, deliberately inconsistent record lets an earlier false record become time-finalizable inside CONFLICT, and the proposed drain obligation would finalize it. At `982aac6` records were at least 7,200 s apart. | **[open]** Store `conflictAt`; finalize in CONFLICT only records with `landedAt + DF ≤ conflictAt`; keep accepting conflicts on earlier records. Not attacked. |
| P6 | **Requester refunds are not held.** `withdrawFiRefund` lets a requester colluding with a faulty leaf withdraw FI_BOND under a false "executed" bit before the window ends, so "zero money moves before finalization" is false. | **[open]** Gate the refund on the record's finality (needs a per-id record pointer), or state an unheld leak of at most 64 FI_BOND per landing. |
| P7 | **Honest loss after CONFLICT.** Held credits of sound one-leaf records from `r` onward are never paid under the recommended disposition, a new uncompensated loss to honest landers and signers (not a slash). The hold also delays refill credits, which can push an honest owner across S3-R23's lapse line where `982aac6` would not. | **[open: user decision]** Change the disposition (release records the DAO-named sound leaf accepts), and count held refill credits toward the lapse check, or disclose. |
| P8 | **Economic premise.** At 120 s lag `r_land` is about 86.7 TAIKO, so sustained third-party landing breaks even only near 6.7 gwei on `r_land` alone (plus the block ramp). Above that the spacing in practice is set by fees. | **[assumed]** State the figure and name holder self-landing or RewardBoost as the path at higher fees. |
| P9 | **Definitions and vectors.** The strong-landing count needs L11 to verify every presented proof and to revert on an extra failing one; vector E20(i) of the candidate was unreachable; capacity must be stated in blocks and bytes (B-W22-A05). | Fixable as stated. |

## 5. Why the spec keeps the disclosure [assumed: judgment of this round]

P1 alone means the candidate does not deliver its purpose under load at S = 120 s, and the remedy that does (S ≈ 18 s) changes every derived size. P2, P4 and P5 each need a new rule that no attack round has examined. So the amendments do not clearly fix every problem, and the task's rule keeps the disclosure. The next round should start from S derived from bytes, a monotonic ring cursor with a voided range, a `confirmProvisional` exit, and `conflictAt`, and attack them together.

## 6. Obligations a later adoption would place on B [open: B]

S2's "outage" label would key on `lastStrongAt`; C4-R07 and C4-R12 would initialize it; C4-R12 would keep the ring cursor monotonic, bound its drain by `conflictAt` and dispose of held amounts. None of S2, C1 or C4 is edited by this note.
