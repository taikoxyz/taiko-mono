# PR #22262 — reply to review comment 6011885154 (reviewed revision `1c03e4b`)

I re-checked all eight findings against the rules: six valid, two partly valid with the stated mechanism wrong.

**1. VALID.** Four equal validators: A locks X on a round-0 PoLC of three prevotes while B and C miss it and never lock; in round 1 B, C and D sign a valid >2/3 certificate for Y, which A can receive before the round-1 prevotes. Safety needs 1/3+ honest locked power at the commit round; one locked validator is not an assumption failure, and no conflicting commit exists. CONS-04(2) line 158, CONS-05:187–189 and CONS-15(2) mandate a sticky halt. Change: CONS-04(1)(a) gains a third release — a valid certificate at H accepted under CONS-05 when no conflicting certificate or finalized block is held — and CONS-04(2), CONS-05 and CONS-15(2) narrow the halt to conflicting finalized decisions. `02-consensus.html`; F1 re-review; no fixed decision touched.

**2. PARTLY VALID.** CONS-01(iii) has no recovery carve-out — at H = resumeHeight the "parent finalized at H−1" is retired; L1-05 row 6 still derives firstBlockHeight = previousHeight + 1 while L1-06 requires max(lastLandedHeight + 1, resumeHeight); the journal's prevHeight/firstHeight pair is undefined after a recovery; and at an epoch boundary PRF-05(ii)/CONS-10(6) demand an epoch_anchor from B_anchor, the discarded block at h_last(e−1). Wrong: proof continuity is checkpoint-anchored — PRF-02 binds prevBlockHash/prevStateRoot to the checkpoint at 100, L1-06 starts the batch at 111, and a header at 111 with parent 100 is provable; what is missing is consensus and execution semantics. Change: a recovery-transition rule (new REC-04, or carve-outs) in `02-consensus.html` CONS-01(iii)/CONS-05(iii)/CONS-10(6), `05-proof-statement.html` PRF-02/PRF-05(ii), `04-l1-integration.html` L1-05 row 6/L1-06. Escalated: it completes D2/D-7, but changes a signed-input surface.

**3. VALID.** The endpoint is an unauthenticated invoker claim, not merely underivable: MIG-02 slot 270 stores `recoveryClaimedResumeHeight`, "the invocation's claim ... from which the completion transition writes resumeHeight". REC-02's "No discretion" row says the invoker chooses nothing; a low claim leaves discarded heights landable, a high one retires certified heights, and resumeHeight is monotone. The cutover is open: completion binds only at Ethereum-final depth, yet L1-06 reads resumeHeight at inclusion. Change: completion derives resumeHeight from the discarded branch's head certificate verified under CONS-05 against the L1-committed set root, bounded by D_MAX, plus one cutover rule. Files: REC-02, L1-06, PRF-01/PRF-02, MIG-02 slot 270, `09-parameters.html`. Escalated: B_REC_KEEP ≥ value_at_risk(D_MAX) presupposes an authenticated endpoint.

**4. VALID.** PRF-06 line 306 requires each block's fee-vault balance delta to equal the fees its execution collects. ECON-02(7)(b) requires a permissionless sweep moving the vault's ETH through the preserved Bridge in an ordinary L2 transaction, so the delta is F − V; every nonzero sweep makes its block, hence the batch, unprovable; the D-8 path cannot settle. Change: PRF-06 verifies gross fee credits and reconciles the delta — delta + swept = F, with swept derived in-guest from the sweep's canonical message (ECON-02(7)(b) already fixes selector, destOwner, to, nonce); CONS-01(viii) and ECON-04 apply the same predicate. `05-proof-statement.html`, `07-economics-slashing.html`, `02-consensus.html`; no fixed-decision conflict.

**5. VALID.** PRF-07(0) makes the payload length-prefixed transaction bodies only; PRF-02 publishes the head hash, predecessor, range-end timestamps and first block's feeRecipient; DA-01/DA-04 make the accepting transaction canonical and exclude witnesses. A middle block's TIMESTAMP or COINBASE is authenticated by the terminal state root but never published, so no L1-only node can execute the batch. Shasta comparison checks out: `taiko-client/bindings/manifest/manifest.go` BlockManifest carries Timestamp, Coinbase, AnchorBlockNumber, GasLimit and Transactions. Change: extend PRF-07(0)'s frame with a per-height manifest of non-derivable execution fields (or the full header), committed by dataCommitment and decoded by PRF-06; DA-01/DA-05 and L1-05 row 8 follow. Escalated as a tradeoff: more published bytes and blobs per batch, feeding S1/S4; D5 unaffected.

**6. VALID.** ECON-02(5)(d)/(e) compute Alloc(e) and ProvingShare(e) from pool_before(e), the raw balance; frozen-but-unclaimed Alloc is not a liability there. 100 arrives, split 50/50; claims are delayed; 50 more arrives and the next freeze sees 100 cash, promising another 50 and paying out another 50 — every per-epoch check passes with 50 against 100 of claims. Change: define pool_before(e) as balance minus outstanding frozen-but-unclaimed Alloc, reserve the liability at the (5)(d) freeze, and add the solvency invariant to ECON-01/ECON-02(5)(d) with the R3-CE-03 view and fuzz assertion. `07-economics-slashing.html`; D-8/D-9 preserved.

**7. VALID.** ECON-03(6) caps aggregate inactivity at CHURN_LIMIT·TotalVP(e); MEM-05(2) makes the queue strictly FIFO and never overtaken. An entry whose effStake exceeds the cap can never become effective, blocks everyone behind it, and MEM-05(3) then makes withdrawal permanently impossible — "deferral is never a denial" is false. Change: partial exit — make effective the remaining churn budget for the head-of-queue entry, keeping its position across versions; MEM-06 exposure and MEM-02 snapshot semantics account for partially exited stake. `07-economics-slashing.html`, `03-membership-staking.html`. The per-entry-ceiling alternative is a product tradeoff; escalated.

**8. PARTLY VALID.** Already registered: ECON-07(1) says no stored value carries the epoch's production or landing time, that the commit-anchored close can open the release gate before evidence is admissible, and that it must be closed before launch; ECON-07(5) uses that close for release and ECON-02(5)(d) freezes P(e)/PS(e) at the same close — the consistency is already there. New and valid: an epoch retired by REC-02 never gets a landing marker, and neither candidate anchor covers it. Change: fix and store t_observed(e), add "or retirement by REC-02" to the candidates, and route evidenceClose(e), the ECON-02(5)(d) freeze and MEM-05(3)(b) through it. Escalated: the anchor is already a pre-launch decision under ECON-07; add the retirement case to it.

**Readiness bullets.** Accepted as gates, not rule changes: the first and third go into `05-phase-b-plan.md`; the measurement bullet is already the spec's status; migration rehearsal and independent blob review are already required by REC-03/Q-A3/MIG; reconstruction is claim 5, blocked until the execution fields publish.
