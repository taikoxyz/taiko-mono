## Verification of the claims charged to this round (all confirmed)

1. **The guarded predicate is identical wherever it appears.** `MEM-13`(3): "if and only if `lastHeartbeatSeq(v) > 0` **and** `lastHeartbeatAt(v) ≥ I*(e) − HEARTBEAT_WINDOW`" with the low-height note. `MEM-09`(1): the roster filter states the same two conjuncts and notes that an entry with no accepted heartbeat is ineligible regardless of the arithmetic. Register: the heartbeat row now reads "an active entry is eligible only if `lastHeartbeatSeq(v) > 0` and its last accepted heartbeat named that window or the one immediately before it; an entry whose heartbeat named neither, **or which has no accepted heartbeat at all**, is excluded" (round-3 F1 closed); the `HEARTBEAT_WINDOW` row ("requires `lastHeartbeatSeq(v) > 0` and the last accepted heartbeat …") and the `lastHeartbeatSeq` row (the acceptance guard, "never reset by a key rotation") are consistent. `spec/01` (system model) states the guard, the shift and the cost. The index's MEM-13 row states the guard and the shift. The delta (definition and both restatements) is guarded. `DEFERRED.md` and `DECISIONS.md` carry the R3-LT-01 addenda. The course states it as an imperative ("a validator **must have posted** a heartbeat … at least once in every window"), which excludes a never-attested entry. **No page states the unguarded formula**, and no surface admits a never-attested entry at a low height.
2. **The guard cannot be gamed, and does not exclude an honest launch attester.** Replay is rejected by `(2a)`(b) (the named window must be the current one), `(2a)`(c) (the sequence must strictly advance) and `(2a)`(d) (the named window's start block must strictly advance), with monotone records (`(2b)`). A retired key's signature is "invalid whenever it was produced", retirement is permanent and un-rebindable, and the non-zero-key rule closes `ecrecover`'s `address(0)` wildcard; `hbSeq ≥ 1` is bound in the payload; a sequence advance without a window advance is rejected by `(2a)`(d) and a window advance without a sequence advance by `(2a)`(c); a key rotation never resets the records; and `v` names exactly one live entry. On the honest side, an entry that attests in the counting window records `lastHeartbeatAt = (q+1)·W = I*(e) − HEARTBEAT_WINDOW` exactly, so the predicate holds with equality, and any later accepted window also qualifies — the guard (seq > 0) is satisfied by the acceptance itself. The guard remains load-bearing after the launch: `I*(e_0+3) = floor((L1_0 + EPOCH_LEN_L1)/W)·W` can still be 0 when the reference block is inside the first window, so an unset record would satisfy a bare inequality there.
3. **The two-window launch fix is real and closes the capture in every launch order.** With `q = floor(L1_0/W)`, the shifted instant is `I* = (q+2)·W` and eligibility is `lastHeartbeatAt ≥ (q+1)·W`; the counting window `[(q+1)W, (q+2)W)` begins at the first window boundary strictly after `L1_0` (so it is entirely post-activation) and has full length `W` wherever in its window `L1_0` falls, which removes the round-2/3 short-remainder case. The gate is a condition on the call — no append before that window closes — identical for every appender and naming no entry or caller, and the lowest-missing rule prevents a caller from targeting another epoch to bypass it. Therefore at the earliest legal append every entry that attested during the counting window is eligible **regardless of ordering**: being first to attest or first to append cannot compose the roster; an attestation in the window containing `L1_0` (window q) does not qualify, so a prepared adversary's earlier heartbeat is worthless; and an entry that never attests fails both the guard and the inequality. `e_0+3` resumes the unshifted definition, whose threshold is at or below `(q+1)W` (since `L1_0 + EPOCH_LEN_L1 < (q+2)W`), i.e. "a lower later threshold admitting, never excluding". The duty is stated in `CONS-14`(1) and names the counting window; `CONS-13`(3) re-bases the launch append opening and requires the entries final before their epoch is entered; `CONS-14`(3) carries the first-boundary halt and its clearance by the lowest-missing append. The cost is disclosed consistently: "up to two heartbeat windows" of append delay and up to one full window added to the `CONS-13`(5) halt, total up to two windows in blocks and unbounded in wall clock because `HEARTBEAT_WINDOW` is bounded only from below (MEM-13(3), CONS-13(3), spec/01, the index, 09's `HEARTBEAT_WINDOW` row, and 08:134 "delayed by up to two HEARTBEAT_WINDOWs — one full window more than the round-2 transition disclosed"). No deadlock: the gate opens at `(q+2)W`, and the halt clears once the appended entries are final.
4. **The pending-change ordering influence remains bounded.** `MEM-13`(6): "while a change is pending, an append caller's ordering relative to it selects whether the version is evaluated under `W_old` or `W_new`; this is binary, it is bounded to the pending change, it cannot name an arbitrary instant, and it needs the ordering control of F8's class (a block producer's power over L1 transaction order) rather than a new capability"; the exclusion run is sized as `(W_old − W') / EPOCH_LEN_L1` contiguous versions, "bounded by the backlog actually drained before the incumbent's re-attestation"; the index row and the 09 `HEARTBEAT_WINDOW` row carry both, and the register row's "cannot permanently empty" retains its caveat. A window change in flight during the launch transition is an instance of the same disclosed Open, not a new capability.
5. **Nothing outside the increment moved.** REC-01's normative text is intact (annotations only); the exit (MEM-15, L1-13) is untouched and reads no membership record; `MEM-13`(4) states "D-7, D-8 and D-9 are untouched"; D-11 is untouched; exclusion removes no weight and the decay tombstones stay withdrawn; and the regression scan found no live-context read of `T_ROTATE`, `T_ROTATE_DELAY`, `CONS-16`, `T_INACTIVE`, `D_LAPSE_MAX` or `lastObserved` (the only hits are the index's parameter-map row stating that the rotation names "MUST NOT be used by any rule, client, parameter or migration text").
