## 1. What the previous Etna design actually was

1. **Sequencing model: a bonded 32-seat committee, not L1 sequencing and not stake-weighted PoS.** A term is 60 s (P/spec/S1-seats-and-sortition.md#L89, S1-R01); a term has up to V_MAX + 1 views, "View v is open from the moment a node holds its opening object" (P/spec/S2-certificates-and-handoff.md#L62, S2-R01). There is no propose-then-prove: land(LandInput) "carries a range of certified blocks as blobs attached in range order plus one proof, and is the only L1 action that advances lastLanded. There is no propose step and no sequencer seal: the batch commitment is the digest every party computes from the certified chain" (P/spec/C2-landing.md#L83, C2-R01).
2. **Who proposes: the "holder"/leader drawn by sortition, per term and view.** idx(t,k) = keccak(seed(c) || 0x01 || t || k) mod E_t; the walk skips visited/excluded seats and stops at K selections or WALK_MAX = 8K draws (P/spec/S1-seats-and-sortition.md#L176-L182, S1-R12/R13). The whole committee walk incl. gas is a single definition in S1-R13.
3. **Who attests: committee(t), up to K = 32 seats, quorum Q(m) = floor(2m/3) + 1** (P/spec/S2-certificates-and-handoff.md#L66, S2-R02); Q(32) = 22. A certificate is an aggregate (one pairing verified on L1) over a bitmap; locks and lock votes record the locked block (P/spec/S2-certificates-and-handoff.md#L106-L160).
4. **Who proves: any permissionless lander.** land takes rewardTo in calldata; landFor is gated by the frame gate G_LAND (C5-R12/R14) and pays a bound recipient. The proof-system set is "two distinct ZK leaves of N, TEE never counts" (C2-R03); certificate-free regimes (dead mode, no-committee, open-empty, hatch) "always need two ZK systems" (L19, P/spec/C6-threat-model-and-limitations.md#L245). There is no prover allowlist (T5/T6/T7 "Removed", C6-R03).
5. **What the L1 Inbox accepts: C2-R05's ordered checks L1-L11** (P/spec/C2-landing.md#L172): L1 sequence (firstHeight == lastLanded.height + 1, <= MAX_BLOCKS); L2 deadline (degraded shape only); L3 parent link from storage; L4 up to MAX_VIEWS segments + the VC chain from lastVCHash; L5 end certificate (popcount(bitmap) >= Q(m), aggregate sig); L6 blobhash(i) equality and blobHashesRoot; L7 anchor tip freshness; L8 forced inclusion (C3-R06 governs); L9 replacement (isReplace, L4-derived); L10 effects; L11 proof (verifier.verify(journalHash, proof, kRequired), then checkpoint or provisional record). Reverts: NotNext, RangeTooLarge, BadCertificate, AnchorTooOld, BadProof, etc. (C8 lists the error family; P/spec/C8-traceability.md counted 81 error names, 64 with a logical case, 16 open gaps, 1 reserved LegacyFiProfileUnsupported).
6. **Anchor-free L2.** No anchor transaction (issue #22147); standard EIP-4788 and EIP-2935 run as system operations before ordinary txs (P/spec/C1-anchor-free-l2.md#L56-L62, C1-R02/R03); every block has one C2-authenticated origin O_b and sets header.parentBeaconBlockRoot = O_b.blockHash (P/spec/C1-anchor-free-l2.md#L48, C1-R01); the L2 SignalService has a permissionless revealCheckpoint parser (C1-R08) plus, under D80, an inline-header signal proof C1-R11 carrying l1HeaderRlp inside proveSignalReceived's _proof (format tag ETNA_SIGNAL_PROOF_V1).
7. **"Finality"/confirmation labels (S2-R18, P/spec/S2-certificates-and-handoff.md#L363): sequenced (0.3 s, revertible without slashing) -> attested C(n) (0.7-1.0 s) -> locked (1.7-2.0 s; displacing it inside its own opening slashes 2Q(m_b) - m_b positions; 12 at m=32) -> fully backed (D29/R-C's minimum over the closing chain) -> landed (checkpoint written by C2-R13/R15) -> landed (provisional) (one-leaf/degraded) -> L1-final (provisional) (D98: provisional receipt in a finalized L1 block, no covering checkpoint) -> final (covering checkpoint + authenticated canonical L1 tx in a beacon-finalized block; no universal wall-clock bound). Each label ships "what locked is not" exceptions (I4).
8. **Degraded / single-proof mode.** If block.timestamp - lastLanded.at > DEGRADE_AFTER (7,200 s) a landing with kRequired = 1 is accepted and is provisional (writes no checkpoint, stores a ProvisionalRecord in a ring, C2-R12/R13); a later confirmProvisional or a 24 h no-conflict rule finalizes. Throughput bound: one one-leaf landing per DEGRADE_AFTER, <= MAX_VIEWS = 4 segments (one per occupied term), so <= 240 blocks (L48/L-SPT; "MAX_BLOCKS = 256 does not bind", P/spec/C2-landing.md#L245).
9. **Forced inclusion is a minimal escape hatch (D39/D41), not a per-block duty.** FIFO requests (saveForcedInclusion blob / saveForcedInclusionCalldata, FI_BOND + FI_FEE), a schedule computed from (head, base) with open_i = max(s_i + HATCH_DELAY, b + HATCH_GRACE), close_i = open_i + HATCH_WINDOW (P/spec/C3-forced-inclusion.md#L77-L124); once the window is open the chain advances only by a landing that consumes the request; anyone may land a hatch: view FORCED = 254, empty certificate, ZK_K leaves, empty replay list, HATCH_BOND = 0 (P/spec/C3-forced-inclusion.md#L271, C3-R07; D51(3)).
10. **Slashing and economics are single-ledger and TAIKO-denominated.** "The existing TAIKO gwei bond ledger in the Inbox proxy: per address a balance, two sub-balances, reserveGwei and the sentinel pocket sentinelGwei, the announcement pocket announceLockedGwei ... no L2 ledger, no ETH bonds" (P/spec/S3-slashing-and-economics.md#L53-L82); three offence classes A (safety, 100 % of the ledger), B (structural, B_SEAT per lying key), F/MISS; catalogue ids S1, S3a-S3e, S6, S8, MISS with objective evidence only (S3-R08, P/spec/S3-slashing-and-economics.md#L136).
11. **The validity predicate is one deterministic statement.** C7-R01 composes C1 + H1-H3 + V1-V6, V8, V9, V10 with three results: unknown context / invalid / valid; two named predicates VALID_GOSSIP and VALID_LANDED (P/spec/C7-block-validity.md#L59); validity reads no wall clock and "V4 permits one block per second and forbids nothing about cadence" (P/spec/C7-block-validity.md#L434).
12. **Retained surfaces and migration.** R2 requires reusing the existing SignalService/Bridge/Vault addresses; C4 defines an in-place upgrade path (freeze, drain, authenticated abandonment after DRAIN_DEADLINE = 86,400 s, a "complete retained-surface change table" C4-R10, P/spec/C4-migration.md#L189), and D26 removes three DAO-only powers: Anchor.withdraw, DefaultResolver.registerAddress, the owner branch of BridgedERC20 mint/burn.
13. **L1 dependencies (C5).** Required set: EIP-4844 blobs/BLOBHASH + KZG, blockhash/EIP-2935 history reads, EIP-7825 gas cap (P/spec/C5-l1-dependencies-and-frames.md#L64); EIP-8141 Frame Transactions is a gate for zero-cost landing races only (degradation = losers pay gas, C5-R18); FOCIL "never protects a landing (blob transactions are not candidates)" (L29).
14. **Readiness was never met.** D44's seven-item bar; at the accepted head: "D44 item 5 resets to 0 of 2" after the full-tree round found one High + 12 Mediums + 21 Lows (D97, P/DECISIONS.md); no machine-checked model and no implementation exist (L34); "every 'proven' is argued on a page" (P/spec/C6-threat-model-and-limitations.md#L262); no measurement procedure has run (D69/README item 4).
15. **Two independent designs were converged.** Candidate A (Claude; committee/certificates, decided) vs candidate B (Codex; open staging + proof race + Dutch rent, dropped), with agent J (DeepSeek) as third-voice judge (P/00-decomposition.md; D7, D3). Dropped for cost/mechanism: the Dutch admission rent, the one-blob staging segment, the mandatory public-data staging gate, B's accountability registry, bonded fragment publication, the anchor transaction (P/00-decomposition.md#L36-L41).

## 3. Accepted limitations and unmeasured items

Source registers: P/spec/C6-threat-model-and-limitations.md C6-R08 register rows L1-L51 (#L221 onward), C6-R09 ledger of Mediums (#L292), C6-R10 whole-design residuals (#L325), C6-R11 out of scope (#L337), S4-R15 exceptions E1-E10 (P/spec/S4-roles-and-liveness.md#L227), each section's "Limits and rejected alternatives", plus the C6 measurement plan and unmeasured register.

### Cryptographic
- **L7** "A common-mode bug in the shared guest code defeats the two-system rule" (accepted; only diverse guest implementations resolve it). **T3**: at least two distinct ZK leaves sound and independent; "a TEE never counts"; at N >= 4 leaves every permitted acceptance pair must contain a sound leaf.
- **C1 limits:** "No 'pin means available/final/confirmed' label - Proven: a hash preimage can be withheld, and its containing state can reorganize." **L21**: "Anchor-free blocks: the permanent map of past L2 block hashes shrinks to EIP-2935's window of 8,191 L2 blocks (about 2.3 h at 1-s blocks); getBlockState().anchorBlockNumber and the Anchored event disappear. **Open** whether any L2 application relies on the permanent lookup."
- **L36** witness reuse: blobs of one range are identical up to the sequencer witness and the quorum witness bytes (D16/D21/D23 keep the quorum witness outside every hash). **L38**: "Unpublished certificate preimage: ... a landed block whose preimage was never published is unslashable."
- **C6-R11 out of scope:** "cryptographic breaks of secp256k1, BLS12-381, KZG or the ZK systems' assumptions, guest bugs being in scope only through the two-leaf rule."

### Economic
- **L15** "Every TAIKO amount is a formula on assumed inputs (term revenue, exchange rate)." **L9** "An entity's seat share is only priced, never bounded." **L2** cartel/blank-term behaviour; **L20** no L1 rule guarantees funds for a term drawn before the debits that emptied the holder's reserve (LAND_RESERVE 14,400 vs 7,161 per-term exposure). **L10** key theft nets at most CHALLENGER_CAP = 2,000 TAIKO. **L16** small registries: 2Q(m) - m = 12 at 32, 6 at 16, 4 at 8, none below 8. **L24** the D1 "locked at ~2 s" scope (equivocation route only, inside the block's own opening). **L50** strike-coalescing false positives. **S3 section 14** rejected alternatives list (50/50 split, challenger bonds, victim compensation, etc.).
- **Unmeasured economic inputs:** V_term ~ 0.01 ETH per term and P ~ 1.5e-4 ETH per TAIKO (A-REV, "**unmeasured**"); R_BLK_MIN/R_BLK_MAX 0.5/4 TAIKO per block "assumed, unmeasured"; the cartel table is "assumed (simulated, script not in the tree)".

### Performance
- **R4/S4-R15 E1 (verbatim):** "The cadence, certification (about 0.7 s) and locking (1.7 to 2.0 s) are A-T9 projections, and S2 section 5 shows the 450-ms attestation figure does not follow from A-T9's stated 300 + 200 ms. D1's two seconds is therefore a projection."
- **L34** "No machine-checked model of the view-change and landing state machines and no implementation exist; every 'proven' is argued on a page."
- **Unmeasured numbers** (C6 measurement plan, "no procedure has run"): MP-05 worst-case landing gas fit (**open**), MP-07 race-loser gas, MP-19 predicate evaluation cost, MP-21 poke() gas, MP-24 committee walk gas, MP-31 rung-1 takeover gap (7 s default), MP-32 rung-6 proving interval (240 s default), MP-33 landed latency, MP-34a evidence gas, MP-34b per-term recording cost, MP-34c END_GRACE, MP-35 level timings (gated defaults 0.5 s sequenced / 2.0 s locked, attested reported only).
- **S4 section 5:** "Landed latency, proving | 2 to 3 min happy path from a range's last block; at most TERM + 180 s = 240 s from any block (MP-06 (b)) | **unmeasured**."

### Liveness
- **L1** assignment is public 20-80 minutes ahead. **L3** Q + 1 silent colluders certify data only they hold -> unslashable stall. **L14** the landing horizon is the one time-based void (ROLE_HORIZON 34.1 h; EIP-2935 window 27.3 h at 12-s slots, 4.55 h at 2-s slots). **L25** retention gap: duty lapses at RETAIN_SECONDS = 7,200 s while landability lasts to the horizon; "a failure to serve is unprovable on L1". **L2/L4** minority blanking/premature view change. **L11** L1 reorg deeper than ANCHOR_MIN_AGE (48 s). **L12/L13/L29** L1 censorship, builder boycott, FOCIL never protects a landing. **L28** "No builder is known to accept shape 2 privately." **L31** "The predicate cannot express provability." **L32** challenger outage windows. **L35, L44, L45, L46, L51** seed freshness, REPLACE l1Ref split, two-colluder TERM_END stall, forced FALLBACK handoff, maturity latency/post-maturity divergence. **L17** migration pauses L2 production, "about an hour in the nominal case ... with no upper bound on the pause". **L47** legacy expiry/changed LEGACY_BLOB terms. **L48** L-SPT (<= 240 blocks per DEGRADE_AFTER). **L49** LS1-2 sentinel prefix (<= 4 occupied terms).

### Governance
- **R1 is conditional:** "100 % permissionless; DAO upgrades only" - proven conditionally for operational authority, conditional on C4-R10's table and its A-MANIFEST audit; L19 and L-PF are user-signed exceptions, "with the DAO as last resort". **C6-R10 (b)**: "Exactly two states wait for the DAO: CONFLICT after a provisional landing (L-PF) and a certificate-free landing ... during a one-leaf outage (L19)." **T4/A-GOV**: the DAO is assumed honest; capture is out of scope (C6-R11). **Rule G** and S3-R08 clause (iii-a) merged as **unadopted proposals** (D85).

### Migration
- **L17** (pause), **L41** "Stuck assets at the anchor: with Anchor.withdraw ... removed, Ether or tokens sent to the anchor contract stay there unless a DAO upgrade recovers them." **L42** fixed resolver table + bridged-token corrections need an upgrade. **C4-R04** "the legacy proof-backed phase can still be gated by SGX admission" (a whitelist on liveness). **C4 sections 8/9** requeue-vs-drain, DRAIN_DEADLINE = 86,400 s. **L37** custody effects survive a CONFLICT rollback (Bridge received-signal cache bits / effects already executed are not undone). **C1 limits:** "No global latest-checkpoint pointer"; "No per-record administrator repair or pin pruning" (assumed).

### Whole-design residual statements (C6-R10, #L325)
