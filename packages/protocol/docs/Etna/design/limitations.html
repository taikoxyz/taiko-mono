Known limitations and what we did not build

Every per-block "not defended" paragraph, collected in one place with the reason it is accepted, followed by the mechanisms considered and left out. Red-team findings that survived verification and were accepted rather than fixed are added here by the iteration reports.

1. Known limitations

#LimitationOwner pageWhy accepted
L1The assignment is public 20 to 80 minutes ahead (TH6): a targeted DoS or front-running preparation window against a known holder.SequencingMitigation is replacement within 5 s, not secrecy; secret leader election costs about 100k gas per landing and is listed below as not built.
L2Eleven abstaining committee seats can make the honest majority time out a live primary (TH8); non-receipt is unprovable. Loss: one view and 50 TAIKO, never eligibility. Above the T11 boundary a cartel sitting in most committees can bleed an honest owner to the 50 % hard floor in about a week; a stakeless network flood at a public term start costs its target 50 TAIKO per burst.Sequencing, SlashingRound 1 (F3) showed that any eligibility consequence of a timeout is a removal lever for an unslashable minority, so timeouts now carry only the burned bleed; the bleed is bounded by the victim's own draw rate, costs the cartel its attester income, and the victim may top up. A pause of the bleed for demonstrably online owners is an open question.
L3Q + 1 silent colluders (leader plus a quorum) can certify data only they hold: a stall of at most 30 minutes before the availability escalation slashes 23 seats.CertificatesOutside the stated collusion bound T11; bounded harm; priced at 460,000 TAIKO.
L4A premature view change by Q colluders orphans at most two sequenced blocks at zero provable cost.CertificatesTime is local and unprovable; harm is one view; rotation and strikes bound repetition.
L5Fallback race: a colluding committee that abstains all term and records its own term-end view change just before the next committee's fallback can invalidate up to about a minute of the honest fallback chain.CertificatesNeeds ≥ Q colluders in one committee; first-recorded-wins keeps the outcome deterministic.
L6Replacement griefing after a global outage: once replacement is permitted and proving returns, the current holder may fork and race the backlog; if it lands first the outage's certified blocks are void and their sequencers pay the abandonment penalty.LandingBounded to one reorg of the backlog per outage; landing the backlog pays more than replacing it; a refundable REPLACE bond is listed as not built.
L7A common-mode bug in the shared guest code defeats the two-system rule.LandingOnly diverse guest implementations resolve it; a third ZK system is the stated target.
L8Forced-inclusion stuffing with a quadratic fee buys a bounded delay; an attacker buying most of L1's calldata can keep two of every three L2 blocks an FI block for a few ETH per hour.Forced inclusionOrdinary users keep one block in three; the drain rate is a tunable constant.
L9An entity's seat share is only priced, never bounded: no owner-keyed rule can distinguish one entity's many owners from many entities.SlashingProven impossibility; the cost curve is linear in share and unbounded at exclusion; the collusion tables assume p ≤ 0.2 as an economic assumption.
L10Key theft: a thief can destroy the victim's bond (equivocate and self-report), netting at most 2,000 TAIKO.SlashingThe capped challenger share bounds the thief's gain; custody is the operator's problem.
L11An L1 reorg deeper than 48 s makes an honest holder's certified range unlandable and it pays the abandonment penalty for an event it did not cause.AnchorNeeds a > 20 %-stake payload reorg; the penalty is nuisance-level; a reduced rate was a needless case split.
L12L1-level censorship of a registration or of a blob-carrying request is outside the design (T1); FOCIL protects only calldata legs after Hegotá.Slashing, Forced inclusionAn L1 property; the calldata request shape exists for exactly this case.
L13Landing needs an L1 builder to include a blob transaction; a total builder boycott stalls landing (but voids nothing).LandingT1; the public-mempool gate removes the builder dependency once EIP-8141 is Final.
L14The landing horizon is the one time-based void. A range whose first term is older than 34 h (ROLE_HORIZON) and was never pinned, or whose anchor tip has left the EIP-2935 window (27 h at 12-s slots, 4.55 h at 2-s slots), can no longer be landed and is replaceable; certified and locked blocks in it are lost without a slash. The level table's "locked" promise therefore holds only while the chain is within the horizon; nodes label confirmations "outage" once no landing has happened for DEGRADE_AFTER.Landing, AnchorRound 1 (F7) found the original "nothing voids by clock" claim overstated. Unbounded role history would cost state on every term, and the EIP-2935 window is an L1 fact; the horizon is stated everywhere the claim was made.
L15Every TAIKO amount is a formula instantiated with assumed inputs (term revenue, exchange rate).ParametersMeasurement is a listed prerequisite; ratios, not amounts, are the design intent.
L16Small registries certify with small committees: the quorum scales with the committee size, so the number of provably slashable double-signers behind a "locked" block is 12 at 32 seats but only 2 at 8, and below 8 no certificates exist (no-committee mode); nodes publish the committee size beside the confirmation level. A FI head entry that no guest can prove stalls every landing path for up to four hours (FI_SKIP) before it is voided. No-committee operation gives users the sequenced level only; the 1-s block guarantee is then a rewarded duty bounded by the 5-s timeout, not a slashable rule (README assumption A7 revised accordingly).CertificatesSilence is deliberately unslashable because a colluding committee could manufacture it.
L17The migration pauses L2 block production for about an hour, at most a day if the outgoing whitelisted provers stall.AnchorSequencing from an unfinalized tail would need a drain-time exception to the parent rule.
L18The ordinary MEV of a single sequencer within its public, attested block stream (including sandwiching a forced-inclusion block whose payload is public five minutes ahead).allOut of scope (01 §8); Etna removes only the private-state amplification.

2. What we did not build

MechanismWhy left outWhere it would go
Secret leader election (VRF over the attester key)About 100k gas per landing and a new key-usage class to close a 20 to 80-minute predictability window that replacement already bounds.Sequencing §4; would replace the public hash walk.
TEE fast lane at the sequenced levelAssumption A3: TEE is never sufficient for finality; a fast-lane signal changes nothing above it.A client-side confirmation hint only.
ETH reward pool from the Anchor's 25 % sharePuts a bridge hop, a relayer and an exchange rate near liveness economics; landers and attesters are paid in TAIKO from the responsible holder's ledger.A storage-compatible sweep with a non-zero gas limit and an L1 receiver.
Term auctions (posted price or sealed bid)An L1 transaction per bidder per term, dead exactly when L1 is congested; prices monopoly instead of bounding it.A sealed-bid auction remains a candidate for pricing B_SEAT.
Treasury or DAO share of slashed fundsA discretionary recipient is an operational role (R1) and an incentive to slash.—
View-change verification inside the ZK guestSaves about 115k gas per view change but adds circuit surface; the withholding defence must not rest on ZK soundness.Landing §3 L4, if landing gas at 2-s slots proves tight.
Refundable REPLACE bondOnly justified if replacement griefing after outages is observed on an adversarial testnet.Landing §6.
Calldata DA fallback for ordinary landingsSix times dearer under the EIP-7976 floor and not FOCIL-protected either.—
A bonded forced-inclusion lander roleA content-deterministic path needs no accountability.—
Per-block attester payments on L2Needs per-block bitmaps the guest cannot verify cheaply, or unverified bitmaps a leader fills with its own seats.—
Prover assignment or ahead-of-time proving auctionsAny assignment is a liveness dependency on the assignee.—
Multi-hop bridging, bridge quota changes, relayer economicsR2 freezes the shared contracts; day-to-day bridging already needs no admin.—
