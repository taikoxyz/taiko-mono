Known limitations and what we did not build

Every per-block "not defended" paragraph, collected in one place with the reason it is accepted, followed by the mechanisms considered and left out. Red-team findings that survived verification and were accepted rather than fixed are added here by the iteration reports.

1. Known limitations

#LimitationOwner pageWhy accepted
L1The assignment is public 20 to 80 minutes ahead (TH6): a targeted DoS or front-running preparation window against a known holder.SequencingMitigation is replacement within 5 s, not secrecy; secret leader election costs about 100k gas per landing and is listed below as not built.
L2An abstaining minority of m − Q(m) + 1 committee seats (11 of 32) in the first draw, and a blocking minority in each of up to two redraws (a redraw needs ⌊m/2⌋ + 1 = 17 votes from attesters that attested but saw no certificate), can withhold certification from a term (a blank term at the sequenced level); non-receipt is unprovable. They cannot time out, bleed or take a producing leader's view (round 4, R4H-4; since the round-5 fix pass the rule reads the attester's own state: an attester that attested the leader's blocks, which the abstainers keep uncertified, never signs a timeout), and a stakeless network flood at a public term start costs its target one view and 50 TAIKO per burst. A cartel majority of ⌊m/2⌋ + 1 (17 of 32) can also redraw a committee that has already certified, since the REDRAW precondition is not verifiable on L1; what it buys is a re-roll toward Q (L16). The revenue consequence, from simulation (slashing §5; round 4, R4-C1; rows for the concentrated honest set added in round 5, R5-C2): below about 25 % of the seats a cartel gains nothing; at the T11 boundary (one third) it gains about 5 points of productive revenue against a dispersed honest set (40 single seats: 38 %) and about 11 points against the eight 8-seat honest owners the launch condition produces (44 %, whether the cartel holds 32 single seats, 4 × 8 or 2 × 8 + 16 × 1), because excluding an 8-seat primary by owner (and up to four backup seats) leaves a pool of about 84 to 88 seats in which the 32 cartel seats are about 36 to 38 %; a single-seat entrant against such a cartel is excluded only by its own seat (the cartel stays at 33 to 35 % of the pool), has about 16 % of its primary terms blanked with two redraws and earns about 84 % of its fair revenue; above one third the gain is superlinear (40 % of seats yields about 70 to 74 %, 50 % yields 97 %) and the design does not defend it.Sequencing, SlashingT11 bounds malicious seats below one third; the launch condition (8 owners, 72 seats) is a heuristic that keeps blanking rare for small cartels at launch (a single 8-seat cartel blanks 0 % there) and nothing enforces it; inside T11 the loss is the term's revenue, never eligibility, the term or the leader's stake.
L3Q + 1 silent colluders (leader plus a quorum) can certify data only they hold: an unslashable stall until the replacement window opens (35 to 65 minutes after the last landing, up to 75 with a landing announcement), after which the holder reopens from the last landed block; the private blocks land only if the colluders publish them before that.CertificatesOutside the stated collusion bound T11. Rounds 1 and 2 showed no L1 rule can distinguish unavailable from available-but-unprovable data without slashing honest committees, so the earlier availability-escalation rule was removed; the cost is a bounded stall, not a safety loss.
L4A premature view change by Q colluders orphans at most two sequenced blocks at zero provable cost.CertificatesTime is local and unprovable; harm is one view; rotation bounds repetition (a timeout never strikes).
L5Fallback race, both directions. (a) A colluding committee(t) that abstains all term and records its own term-end view change just before committee(t+1)'s fallback can invalidate up to about a minute of the honest fallback chain. (b) A Q cartel in committee(t+1) can sign a FALLBACK VC for an honest term t at a lock below its honest TERM_END lock; since round 5 (R5S-5) L1 records and lands such a VC only from termEnd(t) + END_GRACE + VC_FALLBACK (12 s), only while no VC of committee(t) is recorded for the view, only with a certificate at its lock (so it never lands uncertified blocks), and committee(t)'s own VC at a higher lock supersedes it until a landing consumes it. What remains is the same-block race at 12-s slots: the honest TERM_END VC forms at about +2.5 s and the FALLBACK becomes recordable at +12 s, so a FALLBACK consumed by a landing in the block before the honest VC reaches L1 voids at most one term of locked blocks above the FALLBACK lock, with no slashable key.CertificatesBoth need ≥ Q colluders in one committee, outside T11: about 4·10−5 per term at one third of the seats, 6·10−3 at 45 % (certificate page §14); first-recorded-wins keeps the outcome deterministic, and a landing that consumes a FALLBACK is itself a landing, so the harm is one term and never a proof-free void.
L6Replacement or forced-batch reorg of a landable backlog: once the replaceable window opens and proving returns, the current holder may fork and race the backlog, and any bonded party may land a forced batch; if either lands first the outage's certified blocks are void.LandingRevised after round 2 (S3) and round 4 (R4H-5, R4-C3). Nobody pays anything: there is no abandonment rule (S4a and its void waiver were removed in round 4); a recorded REPLACE VC voids nothing by itself (it is accepted only with lock = lastLanded after replaceableFrom, and serves as the VC the fork's landing must match and as priority evidence for the forced path), so until the replacement lands, landing the original stays valid and whichever lands first wins; a landable backlog gets bonded priority through announceLanding, and the announcement and the record share one LAND_CHAIN_GRACE deferral per range, from whichever comes first, never two (round 5, R5-C3: two separate deferrals had put the forced-inclusion bound ten minutes above the published 112); a REPLACE lock vote with a false L1 reference is class B (S3d); confirmations are labelled "replaceable" from replaceableFrom. Residual: one reorg of the backlog per stall, bounded by the grace period, unprofitable to the forcer beyond its own transaction's ordering, with no penalty to anyone.
L7A common-mode bug in the shared guest code defeats the two-system rule.LandingOnly diverse guest implementations resolve it; a third ZK system is the stated target.
L8Forced-inclusion stuffing with a quadratic fee buys a bounded delay; an attacker buying most of L1's calldata can keep two of every three L2 blocks an FI block for a few ETH per hour. FI blocks and forced batches are exempt from the per-term byte budget V10 (which counts sequencer blocks only; round 4, R4H-6), so a blob-shaped backlog never exhausts a term or bleeds its leader, and 40 FI blocks per term still drain.Forced inclusionOrdinary users keep one block in three; the drain rate is a tunable constant.
L9An entity's seat share is only priced, never bounded: no owner-keyed rule can distinguish one entity's many owners from many entities.SlashingProven impossibility; the price of primary share is linear in seats and unbounded at exclusion, but productive revenue is not linear: blank terms give a cartel above one third of the seats a superlinear revenue share (L2; round 4, R4-C1); the collusion bound is T11 in its single form (fewer than one third of the seats malicious; fewer than m − Q(m) + 1 abstainers in a committee and its redraws; fewer than Q colluders), an assumption, not a rule.
L10Key theft: a thief can destroy the victim's bond (equivocate and self-report), netting at most 2,000 TAIKO.SlashingThe capped challenger share bounds the thief's gain; custody is the operator's problem.
L11An L1 reorg deeper than 48 s makes an honest holder's certified range unlandable; the range is replaced after the deadline machinery and the holder loses its revenue, with no penalty, for an event it did not cause.AnchorNeeds a > 20 %-stake payload reorg; the loss is one range's revenue and nothing from the bond.
L12L1-level censorship of a registration or of a blob-carrying request is outside the design (T1); FOCIL protects only calldata legs after Hegotá.Slashing, Forced inclusionAn L1 property; the calldata request shape exists for exactly this case.
L13Landing needs an L1 builder to include a blob transaction; a total builder boycott stalls landing (but voids nothing).LandingT1; the public-mempool gate removes the builder dependency once EIP-8141 is Final.
L14The landing horizon is the one time-based void. A range whose first term is older than 34 h (ROLE_HORIZON) and was never pinned, or whose anchor tip has left the EIP-2935 window (27 h at 12-s slots, 4.55 h at 2-s slots), can no longer be landed and is replaceable; certified and locked blocks in it are lost without a slash. The level table's "locked" promise therefore holds only while the chain is within the horizon; nodes label confirmations "replaceable" from replaceableFrom (the moment a forced or replacement landing may void them; round 2, S3) and "outage" once no landing has happened for DEGRADE_AFTER, and publish beside "locked" that its 39-ETH credit bound holds only while landing keeps up within LAND_WINDOW.Landing, AnchorRound 1 (F7) found the original "nothing voids by clock" claim overstated. Unbounded role history would cost state on every term, and the EIP-2935 window is an L1 fact; the horizon is stated everywhere the claim was made.
L15Every TAIKO amount is a formula instantiated with assumed inputs (term revenue, exchange rate).ParametersMeasurement is a listed prerequisite; ratios, not amounts, are the design intent.
L16Small registries certify with small committees: the quorum scales with the committee size, so the number of provably slashable double-signers behind a "locked" block is 12 at 32 seats but only 4 at 8, and below 8 no certificates exist (no-committee mode); nodes publish the committee size beside the confirmation level. That bound holds within one redraw count: across a committee redraw (⌊m/2⌋ + 1 REDRAW signers of the certifying committee, then Q of the redrawn one, up to twice) a locked block can be reverted with only the keys sitting in both committees slashable, a path with probability about 6·10−7 per view at one third of the seats and 2·10−3 at 45 % (certificate page §14; round 4 fix). S3b spans redraw counts within an opening object, so a key in both committees that locks below a certificate it attested is still slashed, and it exempts no lock (round 5, R5S-1). The entry at fiQueue.head that no guest can prove stalls every landing path for up to four hours (FI_SKIP) before it is voided (a landing skips at most that one entry), and a stream of such entries is bounded only by the poster's budget (the fee floor quadruples per skip, so a two-day stall costs thousands of ETH) and voids the certified blocks above each entry once per cycle. No-committee operation gives users the sequenced level only; the 1-s block guarantee is then a rewarded duty bounded by the 5-s timeout, not a slashable rule (README assumption A7 revised accordingly).CertificatesSilence is deliberately unslashable because a colluding committee could manufacture it.
L17The migration pauses L2 block production for about an hour, at most a day if the outgoing whitelisted provers stall.AnchorSequencing from an unfinalized tail would need a drain-time exception to the parent rule.
L19Certificate-free landings (dead mode, no-committee terms, open-empty terms, forced batches) always need two distinct ZK systems: single-proof mode applies only where a quorum executed the state. One ZK back-end outage during such a regime stalls it until the DAO rotates images or adds a leaf.Landing, RolesDeliberate: without an attester-executed state root, one proof system would carry P1 alone. R1's "DAO never needed for liveness" therefore holds for certified operation and fails in the compound case of an uncertified regime plus a back-end outage (round 3, H4).
L20No L1 rule can guarantee funds at landing time for a term drawn before the reward debits that emptied its holder's reserve. The reserve (9,200 TAIKO per seat, two terms of the 4,561-TAIKO worst-case exposure per seat, pin reward included; about 16 terms for an 8-seat owner against an expectation of about 7 inside one LAND_WINDOW_MAX at the launch registry, with more than 16 below 0.1 % probability) is non-withdrawable and never touched by liveness debits; since round 5 (R5S-4) it is drained only by rewards paid to other owners' landers, attesters and pinners, a self-landing skips the lander's debit and credit, and every reward-type credit to an owner with seats refills that owner's reserve before its withdrawable balance, so the honest path (self-landing 2 to 3 minutes after term end, which round 4's sizing had left out and which drained a single-seat owner in about 24 own terms) moves only the attester share, offset in expectation by the owner's own attester income; ReserveLow fires once per crossing below two terms of exposure, with hysteresis, and the client tops up on it; a lapse makes the owner ineligible with DELAY_REG effect and, like every suspension, changes no rank. The residual window is the terms drawn inside DELAY_REG after the lapse plus the terms still unlanded at it. The owner can still bring the lapse about at will, by letting its own terms ramp and landing them with rewardTo an address that owns no seats (up to 4,561 TAIKO per term moves from the reserve to a withdrawable balance; the refill rule cannot tell such an address from a genuine third party), or by a self-inflicted class-A slash that takes the whole ledger and, since round 5 (R5S-2), changes no draw; invariant I2 is therefore stated with those two exceptions.Landing, SlashingRound 4 (R4H-3, R4-C4), re-sized in round 5 with the honest drain included. Such a term is landed by its holder at gas cost or replaced under A1 with no penalty; the self-directed drain costs the owner one SUSPEND(3) (36 h) of lost revenue per cycle, and the exposure is realised only at the maximum ramp.
L18The ordinary MEV of a single sequencer within its public, attested block stream (including sandwiching a forced-inclusion block whose payload is public five minutes ahead).allOut of scope (01 §8); Etna removes only the private-state amplification.
