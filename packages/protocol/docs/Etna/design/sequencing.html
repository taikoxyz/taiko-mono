Sequencing rights

Time is cut into 60-second terms. Within a term, each takeover opens a new view (view 0 is the primary holder, view v the v-th backup). The holder of a term is a pure function of the registry as it stood 20 minutes before the term and of a randomness seed fixed one hour ahead. Every node computes it identically and in advance; the inbox verifies the same function at landing. No slot, epoch, beacon root, lookahead or blockhash is read anywhere in this block.

What today does and why it fails

One PreconfWhitelist operator per 384-second epoch, chosen by beaconRoot % operatorCount from an EIP-4788 root probed in 12-second steps; roster edited by the owner or ejecters; an offline operator loses the whole epoch with no replacement and no penalty; clients read the roster at "latest" so nodes disagree near boundaries (00 §3.1, §9.2, §9.4). This fails R1 (TH19), R5 (TH15), R6 (TH9, TH10), and the locality property P7 (TH12).

1. Time

TERM = 60 s, termOf(τ) = (τ − T0) / TERM, T0 = Etna activation timestamp. CYCLE_TERMS = 60, so one seed per hour. Every quantity is a second count, an L1 block timestamp or a count.

2. Registry: seats and future-dated writes

Storage lives in the upgraded Inbox proxy after its 43-slot gap; logic in a linked library so the landing path reads it without an external call and the existing TAIKO bond ledger is reused (R2: no new address). proven the syncer address stays the Inbox proxy.

  • Seat: one unit of bond B_SEAT and one storage slot Seat{owner, activeFrom, activeUntil} in an append-only array of at most 65,535 (a 16-bit index; filling it costs 1.31 billion TAIKO, above the supply). An owner holds at most CAP = 8 seats. A slot is written fresh once (97,920 state gas under EIP-8037, paid by the entrant) and only overwritten afterwards.
  • Operator record: {seatCount, strikes, lastStrikeAt, exitAt, two suspension intervals}.
  • Rule D (future-dated effect): a registry write at L1 time τ changes eligibility only for instants T ≥ τ + DELAY, with DELAY_REG = 7200 s for entry and exit and DELAY_S = 1320 s for suspensions, both ≥ LOOKBACK + 2·TERM. Term t evaluates eligibility at the single instant termStart(t). This is what lets the contract keep no history and need no proofs.
  • register(count) requires bond ≥ (seatCount + count)·B_SEAT, a registered code-less sequencer key and a BLS attester key with proof of possession over (pubkey, owner, chainId), unique across the registry (see Slashing; the owner itself may be a contract), and no pending exit. Indices are contract-assigned: FREE recycled holes first (expired plus RECYCLE_GRACE), then appends at the scheduled length, so a same-block append by an incumbent never reverts an entrant's transaction (round 2). The array is dense, so its length always equals the number of seats ever claimed; requestExit is all-or-nothing; finalizeExit after activeUntil + EVIDENCE_WINDOW (7 days); withdrawal is limited to the excess over seatCount·B_SEAT.
  • eligible(i, T) := activeFrom ≤ T < activeUntil ∧ ¬suspended(owner, T).

3. Snapshot rule

S(t) = the highest L1 block with timestamp ≤ termStart(t) − LOOKBACK, LOOKBACK = 1200 s. A node replays registry events and the mix log up to S(t); no archive state is needed. Agreement with the contract: any write in a block after S(t) has τ + DELAY > termStart(t) + TERM, so both views evaluate term t without it. proven (claim 2). Under a total L1 halt the schedule stays defined.

4. Randomness

Compared: an EIP-4788 root (slot-keyed probe, fork-dependent gindices, Empty-slot gaps, 4.6-h window at 2-s slots: rejected); an EIP-2935 header proof of a timestamp-defined block's prevrandao (fork-dependent header RLP, a per-cycle transaction: kept as an upgrade path); round-robin with no randomness (predictable forever, terms sellable: rejected). Chosen: a prevrandao accumulator.

  • RND1. land, every registry mutation and the public no-op poke() execute, at most once per L1 block, seedAcc = keccak(seedAcc ‖ block.prevrandao). Each emits an event, so nodes replay the mix log in block order.
  • RND2. seed(c) = keccak(DOMAIN ‖ chainId ‖ seedAcc_at(T_seed(c)) ‖ c) with T_seed(c) = cycleStart(c) − LOOKBACK. The first mixing call at or after T_seed(c) fixes it on-chain; nodes compute the same value from the log without waiting.
  • RND3 (freshness). fresh(c) iff a mix occurred after freshFrom(c) = cycleStart(c) − 3660 s, the last instant at which a registry write can affect cycle c. When fresh, no registrant could know the seed when it last had a chance to change roster inputs. proven (claim 5). When not fresh (no landing and no poke for 41 minutes, itself a total-censorship event), the residual lever is a few bits of seat placement.

L1-proposer bias: the proposers of the last m blocks before T_seed(c) hold two bits each (reveal or skip; include or exclude a mixing transaction), so m colluding proposers choose among at most 4m seeds for one 60-term cycle. Same bound as every RANDAO consumer, including today's 4788 path. assumed RANDAO model.

5. Selection: weighted random by seats, hash walk, views

Compared: term auctions (an L1 transaction per term per bidder, dead exactly when L1 is congested, capital buys everything, still needs a random fallback: rejected); round-robin (rejected as above); weighted random with a cap (chosen).

  • idx(t, k) = keccak(seed(c) ‖ 0x01 ‖ t ‖ k) mod L_t, where L_t is the seat-array length effective at termStart(t).
  • Walk. For k = 0..63: skip visited or ineligible seats; append the seat's owner to the holder list H if not yet present; stop at 5 distinct owners.
  • Views. holder(t, v) = H[v] for v < |H|; beyond that, view v is the owner of idx(t, v) if eligible, else void and skipped without any timeout. At most V_MAX = 4 takeovers happen in one term; after that the term idles to its end.
  • Share. An owner with w of A eligible seats is primary with probability w/A; CAP bounds one address to 8/A; Sybil cost is linear in seats; exclusive control needs every eligible seat and cannot be held against an honest entrant whose append is a plain L1 transaction live two hours later. proven (claims 6, 7).
  • No cooldown, no weight halving. A consecutive-term cooldown is defeated by two addresses and couples term t to t−1; fractional weights cannot be expressed by a seat walk. Both are replaced by binary suspension with strikes.

6. Open terms and dead mode

A term is open-empty if the walk finds no eligible seat, and the chain is in dead mode if termOf(now) − lastLandedTerm > DEAD_TERMS = 75. In either, any address with bond ≥ B_SEAT may sequence with the sentinel view 255 and the first landing on L1 wins. (Forced batches use the sentinel view 254 and are the forced-inclusion page's concern.) A third open regime, no-committee mode, applies to any term whose committee walk finds fewer than K_MIN_CERT = 8 eligible non-holder seats (for example at launch): the holder is still chosen by sortition, no certificates exist, landings are accepted with an empty certificate, and users get the sequenced level only. Unlike dead mode, the landing right of a no-committee term is reserved to its sortition holder until the term's economic deadline (termEnd + LAND_WINDOW); only after that may any bonded address land from lastLanded (round 2, S9: an open race from the start let any bonded outsider void the holder's minute for gas). Every sentinel-view chain opens with the object keccak("ETNA_DEAD" ‖ lastLanded.blockHash ‖ t), which the Anchor accepts as a fresh opening object after any landing (Anchor A5) and which enters every equivocation key. Between 8 and 32 seats the committee is smaller and the quorum scales with it (§8). Dead mode needs no certificate, no attester and no registration delay: a chain whose whole registry and committee vanished is recovered by one honest bonded party within about 75 minutes plus proving. It is the one rule that is not a pure function of S(t): it depends on the node's current view of lastLandedTerm, only ever adds authorized sequencers, and the landing contract arbitrates. proven bounded disagreement, confined to recovery.

7. Absent holder: takeover, penalty, suspension

  • Holder of view v is replaced by the next non-void view when a view change VC(t, v, lock) exists: ≥ Q(m) of the term's m committee signatures (22 of 32 at full size) stating that no certified block above the lock was seen for TIMEOUT = 5 s. The rightful holder is the highest view with a VC closing every non-void view below it; a holder's first block carries the VC, so a partitioned node verifies signatures instead of trusting its clock. The new holder starts at the last certified block; uncertified blocks are unextendable and unlandable, so withheld blocks are worthless.
  • Absence penalty, applied by the inbox when a landed batch carries a timeout view change (no separate accusation transaction exists): MISS_PENALTY = 50 TAIKO, 100 % burned so a backup allied with attesters gains nothing from a false view change; one debit per (term, view). A timeout never changes eligibility: it adds no strike and triggers no floor check, because a timeout can be manufactured by 11 abstaining committee seats without any slashable signature (red-team round 1, F3). Squatting is deterred by the bleed alone: a seat that never serves loses 50 TAIKO per draw and reaches the 50 % hard floor after 200 draws (about five days at 40 seats), at which point it is suspended until topped up.
  • Evidence slash (from Slashing): a safety offence takes the whole ledger; a structural one takes one seat and adds three strikes.
  • Suspension at three strikes, where strikes come only from structural offences (class B on the slashing page: a signed object naming an invalid certificate or view change, or a REPLACE timeout whose L1 reference did not satisfy the replacement predicate): [τ + DELAY_S, τ + DELAY_S + SUSPEND(3)·2^min(n−3, 4)) with SUSPEND(3) = 129,600 s (36 h) so that roles stay exact through the 34-hour landing horizon, stored as two intervals; the dropped interval is provably dead for every still-landable term. proven (claim 3).
  • Bond floor: after a class A or class B evidence debit, a bond below seatCount·B_SEAT means open-ended suspension until top-up plus reactivate(). Liveness debits (absence, abandonment) and reward debits never trigger that check; reward debits stop at 1.05 × floor, and only the 50 % hard floor suspends for liveness bleed (Slashing §6), so a chain-wide proving outage never suspends the honest set.

8. Committee hook

committeeOf(t): the first K = 32 distinct eligible seats of a second walk keccak(seed ‖ 0x02 ‖ t ‖ m) over at most MAX_TRIES_COMMITTEE = 512 tries, skipping every seat of the term's primary holder (view 0, excluded by owner: the committee must not certify its own blocks) and the specific seats of the backups (excluded by seat only, so a backup's other seats may attest; a backup that takes over is then certified by a committee it may sit in, which the collusion count already includes since it counts the leader). Owner repeats among the rest are allowed, so the committee is stake-weighted. The expected committee size is therefore min(32, A − seats(primary) − |H| + 1) and full committees need A ≥ K + CAP + V_MAX = 44 eligible seats; initEtna requires MIN_OWNERS = 6 distinct owners and 44 seats (round 2, F1: excluding every holder by owner emptied the committee of the honest set whenever the honest set was five large owners). If fewer than 32 are found, the committee is the m seats found: the quorum is a function of the actual size, Q(m) = ⌊2m/3⌋ + 1, and a term with m < K_MIN_CERT = 8 is in no-committee mode (certificate-free landings, sequenced level only). The certificate page owns the certificate formats, the timeout and the security statement per m (the number of provably slashable double-signers behind a locked block is 2Q(m) − m: 12 at m = 32, 6 at m = 16, 4 at m = 8).

10. Interface sketch

/// @custom:security-contact security@taiko.xyz
/// L1: storage in the Inbox proxy after __gap; logic in linked library LibSequencerRegistry.
interface ISequencerRegistry {
    struct Seat     { address owner; uint32 activeFrom; uint32 activeUntil; }
    struct Operator { uint16 seatCount; uint8 strikes; uint32 lastStrikeAt; uint32 exitAt;
                      uint32 s1; uint32 e1; uint32 s2; uint32 e2; }
    struct Holders  { address[5] owners; uint16[5] seats; uint8 count; uint8 mode; } // 0 SORTITION, 1 OPEN_EMPTY, 2 DEAD

    // permissionless mutations; every eligibility effect is future-dated (Rule D)
    function register(uint16 _count) external returns (uint16[] memory seats_);   // indices contract-assigned
    function requestExit(uint16[] calldata _seats) external;
    function finalizeExit(uint16[] calldata _seats) external;
    function reactivate() external;
    function poke() external;                       // mixes prevrandao; emits Poked

    // "who may sequence at time t" (nodes)
    function termOf(uint48 _time) external pure returns (uint32 term_);
    function sequencerAt(uint48 _time, uint8 _view)
        external view returns (uint32 term_, address holder_, uint16 seat_, uint8 mode_);
    function holderOf(uint32 _term, uint8 _view) external view returns (address holder_, uint16 seat_, bool void_);
    function holdersOf(uint32 _term) external view returns (Holders memory h_);
    function committeeOf(uint32 _term) external view returns (uint16[32] memory seats_, address[32] memory owners_, bytes32[32] memory attKeyHashes_);
    function seedOf(uint32 _cycle) external view returns (bytes32 seed_, bool fresh_, bool fixed_);
    function isOpenTerm(uint32 _term) external view returns (bool openEmpty_, bool deadMode_, bool noCommittee_);
    function recordAssignment(uint32 _term) external;      // anyone, while now ≤ termStart + ROLE_HORIZON; pins digest + committee root
    function seedAccumulator() external view returns (bytes32 acc_, uint48 lastMixBlock_, uint48 lastMixTime_);
    function assignmentDigest(uint32 _term) external view returns (bytes32); // keccak(term, seed, L_t, holders)

    // Inbox-internal hooks used by land() and by slashing
    function isHolder(uint32 _term, uint8 _view, address _who) external view returns (bool);
    function applyMissPenalty(address _holder, uint32 _term, uint8 _view) external;
    function applyEvidenceSlash(address _owner, uint64 _gwei, uint8 _kind) external;

    event SeatClaimed(uint16 indexed seat, address indexed owner, uint32 activeFrom);
    event LengthScheduled(uint16 len, uint32 effectiveAt);
    event ExitRequested(address indexed owner, uint32 activeUntil);
    event Suspended(address indexed owner, uint32 from, uint32 until, uint8 strikes);
    event Reactivated(address indexed owner, uint32 activeAgainAt);
    event SeedFixed(uint32 indexed cycle, bytes32 seed, bool fresh);
    event Poked(uint64 l1BlockNumber);
    event MissPenalised(address indexed holder, uint32 indexed term, uint8 view, uint64 gwei);
}

The block's term, view and seat travel in the anchor calldata (see Anchor) and extraData keeps its 7-byte engine shape pctg(1) | termId(4) | view(1) | kind(1); the P2P envelope carries assignmentDigest, so a node whose own digest differs knows its L1 view is stale before it rejects the block. Roles stay computable on-chain for ROLE_HORIZON = 34 h after a term's start (seat recycling and suspension intervals are sized for it), which is the landing horizon: every landing and every poke() also pins the assignment digest and committee root of each term it settles, and anyone may pin a term with recordAssignment(t). A term older than the horizon whose digest was never pinned is unlandable and therefore replaceable; this is the one time-based void in the design, stated on the limitations page. Seat and owner state machines:

Seat:  FREE --register--> PENDING(activeFrom = τ+7200) --time--> ACTIVE --requestExit--> EXITING(activeUntil = τ+7200)
       --time--> EXPIRED --+RECYCLE_GRACE--> FREE
Owner: NORMAL --3 strikes | evidence slash--> SUSPENDED[τ+1320, τ+1320+SUSPEND(n)) --time--> NORMAL
       any --debit below seatCount·B_SEAT--> SUSPENDED_OPEN --top-up + reactivate--> SUSPENDED until τ+1320 --time--> NORMAL

12. Attacks defended and not defended

  • TH3 absent holder: defended. Replacement within TIMEOUT per dark view, void views free; 50 TAIKO burned per event, nothing else.
  • TH9 monopolization: bounded and priced, not prevented. Share X against H honest seats costs H·X/(1−X) seats (with H = 40: 50 % = 40 seats = 800k TAIKO, 90 % = 360, 99 % = 3,960); marginal cost H/(1−X)² is super-linear. A majority of seats yields a majority of terms; slashing and forced inclusion bound what it can do with them.
  • TH10 squatting: defended by bleed. A seat that never serves loses 50 TAIKO per draw (about 1,800 TAIKO per day at 40 seats) and is suspended at the 50 % hard floor after 200 draws; it earns no attester reward meanwhile.
  • Grinding: defended when fresh, bounded otherwise. Requires 41 minutes of total L1 censorship of Etna transactions, after which the lever is a few bits.
  • Seed bias by L1 proposers: bounded. ≤ 2 bits per proposer of the last m blocks; best of 4m seeds moves a term count by a few terms per cycle.
  • TH12 reorg: defended to finality.
  • TH8 false view changes: bounded, not prevented. Eleven abstaining committee seats can make the honest majority time out a live primary; non-receipt is unprovable. The loss is one view and 50 TAIKO burned, never eligibility (no strike). A cartel above the T11 boundary that sits in most committees can bleed an honest owner to the hard floor in about a week (200 of its terms), at the cost of its own attester income and with the victim free to top up; below the boundary the rate is orders of magnitude lower (limitations L2); the committee walk of §8 keeps the honest set's seats in the committee even when the honest set is a few large owners (round 2, F1). There is no accusation object anywhere in the block.
  • TH6 targeted preparation: not defended. The assignment is public 20-80 minutes ahead. Mitigation is replacement within TIMEOUT; secret leader election is out of scope.
  • TH18 EIP-8151: defended by code-less EOAs at registration and a two-branch evidence rule (if the key acquired code, slash for that L1 fact; else verify the signature pair).

13. Failure analysis

  • All seat holders offline. Every term dies after at most 4 timeouts (V_MAX); each certified absence bleeds 50 TAIKO, so within days dark owners hit the hard floor and terms become open-empty. If the committee is dark too, terms end unlanded until DEAD_TERMS pass; then dead mode lets one honest bonded party sequence and land without certificates. Liveness within about 75 minutes plus proving, no DAO action.
  • All seat holders malicious. They cannot finalize invalid state, skip due forced inclusions, block an honest registration, or hide equivocation. Withholding becomes absence. An honest entrant gets its proportional share two hours later.
  • Committee dead (fewer than Q(m) live seats). No view changes, so takeovers stop and an absent primary loses its term for everyone; the term is closed by a FALLBACK view change that any later committee within the landing horizon may sign (certificate page §5), and the next primary's first block carries it. A committee that is small because the registry is small has a proportionally small quorum (§8), so a small honest registry certifies normally with weaker collusion bounds that are published per term.
  • Provers dead. Batches are abandoned by the landing page's rule with a debit but no strike; mixing continues via poke().
  • Partition. A holder cut off for TIMEOUT is replaced; on reconnection it verifies the view change and stops. A node cut off from L1 keeps a correct schedule for 22 minutes and labels later confirmations "L1-stale".

15. Robustness

6/4/2-s slots: nothing counts slots; LOOKBACK spans 200/300/600 blocks while finality shrinks, so the margin grows; isHolder is ≈ 7k gas typical, ≤ 290k worst case, fitting a 10 M-gas block. Without Frame Transactions: zero dependency; no registry call is a race. ePBS Empty slots and 4788 gaps: 4788 is never read; the next block's prevrandao reflects every intervening reveal; one-slot-later confirmation is absorbed by the 1320-s minimum delay. EIP-8037: fresh slots only at registration.

  2. proven Node snapshot equals landing-time state (Rule D arithmetic).
  3. proven The two-interval suspension store never drops a live interval.
  4. proven Reorg safety under T1 at every slot time.
  5. proven No entry or exit grinding when the seed is fresh.
  6. proven Share bound w/A, cap 8/A, exclusive control needs every seat.
  7. assumed Exclusive control cannot be sustained (TAIKO liquid; a registration includable on L1 within hours).
  8. proven for dead mode, assumed for the certificate path: liveness with one honest funded party.
  9. proven Objective punishment: every debit needs a view change, a signature pair, or an L1 fact.

17. Alternatives rejected

  • Round-robin (predictable forever, O(A) position scans); term auctions (per-term L1 transactions, capital buys everything); Merkle sum tree with sortition proofs and maintenance drains (nine proofs per landing); a 448-seat bitmap with non-evictable incumbents; EIP-2935 header proofs for the seed (kept as upgrade); EIP-4788 seeds; consecutive-term cooldown; weight halving; 24-h exclusion on the first miss; 10 % liveness slash (too costly under TC griefing); BLS registration keys (150-200k gas per verification; revisit if EIP-8151 is scheduled); linear-scan fallback; swap-and-pop seat arrays (index reuse changes computed terms); TERM = 120 s.

18. Open questions

  1. Should the absence bleed scale with the owner's seat count so multi-seat squatters reach the floor as fast as single-seat ones?
  2. "MISS_PENALTY burned, never paid to the taking-over holder" is adopted; paying it would create an incentive for false view changes.
  3. Is the P8 super-linear-cost requirement satisfied by linear seat cost plus CAP plus per-incident slashing, or is an identity-bearing primitive wanted?
  4. Is a VRF-based secret leader election worth about 100k gas per landing to close the 20-80-minute TH6 window?
  5. Should the DAO be able to raise CAP by constant upgrade only (MAX_SEATS is fixed by the index type)?
  6. Adopted from the slashing page: the owner is separate from the signing keys (one extra slot per operator); the owner may be a contract.
