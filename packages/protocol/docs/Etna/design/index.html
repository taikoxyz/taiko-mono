Etna: a fully permissionless based rollup

Etna replaces the whitelisted operator and prover with bonded seats anyone can buy, replaces 384-second epochs with 60-second terms computed from L1 state, certifies every 1-second block with a bonded attester committee, and lands data and a ZK proof in one permissionless L1 action that finalizes immediately. The DAO keeps upgradeability and nothing else. This site is the design; the research behind it is in the repository documents. Status: draft under red-team review; see the iteration log and the readiness verdict in the README.

How to read claims

Every claim is tagged proven (an argument is given from stated rules), assumed (the assumption is named), or open (what would resolve it is named). Gas figures are estimates; TAIKO amounts are formulas instantiated with assumed inputs.

3. The architect's decisions

The three independent designs per building block disagreed on these points; the consistency critic listed 30 contradictions, 27 missing interfaces and 25 parameter conflicts. Decisions, with the losing option and the reason:

  1. Committee of up to 32 seats with a size-scaled quorum ⌊2m/3⌋ + 1 and BLS attester keys (over 16 seats with ECDSA lists, and over a constant quorum: round 1, F1). Twelve provable double-signers behind every locked block at full size, published per term; one pairing verifies any size; no ecrecover on the attester path under EIP-8151. Cost: proof-of-possession registration and about 115k gas per certificate.
  2. The committee's view change is the batch seal (over a sequencer-signed or quorum-attested seal). Nothing exists for a sequencer to withhold; landers land any certified prefix inside one view.
  3. A term-end view change closes every term (over "no object at the boundary"). The recorded lock is what makes the handoff ambush cost twelve slashed seats; the cost is one aggregate object per minute.
  4. Up to four consecutive views per landing (revised after round 1, F6: one view per landing made the fixed landing gas unamortizable at high fees). Each view is a segment bounded by view changes; the end certificate binds the last.
  5. A landed replacement is the only void; the clock does not void within a 34-hour landing horizon; no abandonment rule. Nothing voids a locked block except a landed replacement or forced batch after the deadline machinery, or 2Q(m) − m slashings (invariant I4); a proving outage shorter than the horizon voids and penalizes nothing. Round 5 closed the two objects that had been left wider than I4 allows: the lock-lie rule S3b exempts no lock (round 3 had exempted every lock that was a landed block, which let a Q cartel revert locked blocks with a timeout at a landed lock; the RESET state already makes every mandatory post-reset vote a RESUME, which S3b never covers; R5S-1), and a FALLBACK VC by a later committee is recorded and landed only from termEnd(t) + END_GRACE + VC_FALLBACK × (signerTerm − t) of L1 time, only while no VC of the term's own committee is recorded, only with a certificate at its lock, and is superseded by the own committee's VC at a higher lock until consumed (R5S-5). The abandonment offence of rounds 1 to 3 (S4a, with its outage gate and void waiver) was removed in round 4 (R4H-5, R4-C3): a certified view is landable by anyone and paid from the holder's reserve, so a holder cannot abandon a provable view, and the only unlandable certified content is a guest-completeness bug, a false positive for any abandonment rule, handled by replacement plus the client blacklist. The horizon and the four-hour forced-inclusion skip are the two clock rules, added or made explicit after round 1 (F4, F7).
  6. Anchored L1 time is the forced-inclusion clock (over term start or landing time). No third party can make a proven landing revert; an entry becoming due never reorgs in-flight blocks.
  7. Absence penalty 50 TAIKO, burned, with no eligibility consequence; challenger share 10 % capped (over 200 TAIKO with strikes and 50/50 splits). Round 1 (F3) showed that a timeout must never drive suspension; strikes come from structural offences only. Since round 4 (R4H-4) the bleed applies only to a leader silent to the whole committee, so an honest online leader is never bled, and it is paid from the 5 % buffer above the floor and then from seat stake, never from the landing reserve (R4-C4); since round 5 (R5H-1 and the fix pass) "silent" means that for 5 s no certificate appeared and no honest attester holds an attested block of the view that is still uncertified, so a holder that gossips headers without payloads is bled like a dead one.
  8. Registry index of 65,535 seats (over 4,096). Exclusion by a full array becomes unpriceable (above the token supply).
  9. Landing windows 30 min economic, 60 min hard; dead mode after 75 terms (over 35 min hard and 40 terms). Fewer honest false positives; the forced-inclusion bound becomes 112 minutes (102 plus the one LAND_CHAIN_GRACE per range that a landing announcement and an accepted REPLACE record share, whichever comes first; round 5, R5-C3) instead of 77.
  10. The owner is separate from its keys and may be a contract; only the sequencer key must be code-less.
  11. Sentinel views 254 (forced) and 255 (open-empty, dead, no-committee); at most four takeovers per term.
  12. No-committee mode replaces a separate committee-degraded mode: below K_MIN_CERT = 8 eligible committee seats, sortition still picks holders and landings are certificate-free, reserved to the holder until its economic deadline.
  13. Roles stay resolvable for 34 hours and every landing pins the terms it settles; a term older than the horizon that was never pinned is unlandable and replaceable (revised after round 1, F7).
  14. The public-mempool Frame-Transaction gate is not deployed until EIP-8141 is Final; the direct trailing-VERIFY shape is the primary path and plain transactions the fallback.
  15. No availability-escalation rule (revised after round 2, S4/H2: the earlier demandData game could not be verified by the inbox and either slashed honest committees or priced nothing). A private certificate is an unslashable stall until the replacement window opens (limitation L3); the opening object of every view is now in every signed attester message, so replacement and resumption never create equivocation.
  16. A landable backlog has bonded priority over the forced and replacement paths (round 2, S3): announceLanding (bond ANNOUNCE_BOND = 2,000 TAIKO, forfeited 40 % to the eventual forced or replacement lander and 60 % burned if no covering landing follows; refused once the head forced-inclusion entry has been due for longer than LAND_WINDOW, round 3, C3) defers the replaceable window once per range, and a recorded REPLACE VC is priority evidence and nothing more (round 4, R4H-5) that shares that one deferral: one LAND_CHAIN_GRACE per range, from whichever of the announcement and the record comes first, never two (round 5, R5-C3).
  17. The committee excludes only the primary holder by owner (round 2, F1: excluding every holder by owner emptied committees of the honest set); the published launch condition (eight owners, 72 seats) is a heuristic over a Sybil-able, revocable count that no rule enforces or protects, chosen so that a small cartel blanks few terms at launch; the DAO arms T0 with a second upgrade-class call armEtna(T0) after reading the domain count, and launch may begin in no-committee mode if the condition fails (round 4, R4-C5). The sequencing page owns every selection rule (other pages defer to it).
  18. A timeout is signed only on leader silence, and the lock travels in a separate monotone lock vote (round 3, C1 and S5: with "no certificate for 5 s" as the trigger, 11 abstaining seats made the honest majority hand a live primary's term to a cartel backup, breaking the cost curve; and re-voting at a higher lock was itself equivocation). Round 4 (R4H-4) fixed the silence signal itself and round 5 (R5H-1, R5-C1) bounded it; after the round-5 verification it reads the attester's own state rather than message arrival: an attester signs TIMEOUT for (t, v) only when no new certificate of the view has appeared for TIMEOUT seconds and it holds no block of the view that it attested and that is still uncertified. What that covers: a leader held by an abstaining minority has its two real blocks attested by the honest attesters, who then never time it out; a dead leader is timed out; a leader that withholds payloads or gossips bodiless, invalid or future-stamped headers gets nothing attested, is timed out at 5 s and bled, and cannot later re-sign lock + 1 with other content (S1). A redrawn committee attests the held block the redraw vote names, with the staleness bound waived (found in the fix pass: without the waiver the redraw could certify nothing). An abstaining minority can only make a term blank (invariant I3), and a bounded committee redraw vote (⌊m/2⌋ + 1 attesters that attested but saw no certificate, REDRAW_MAX = 2) raises the blanking threshold from 11 to 16 of 32.
  19. A REPLACE or RESUME VC is recorded only if its lock equals lastLanded and replaceableFrom has passed at record time, and a record has no closing effect: until a replacement or forced batch lands, landing the original stays valid and whichever lands first wins (round 4, R4H-5 and R4-C3, replacing round 3's rule under which a recorded REPLACE VC closed the original tail and one L1 transaction could void fresh locked blocks). The L1 reference is inside the signed bytes (for RESUME, the L1 block that contains the forced or replacement landing being resumed from; the certificate page owns the definition, round 5, R5S-3) and a reset attester may sign only RESUME (round 3, S1, S2, S6); the lock reset triggers on a landed replacement only, and because the reset makes RESUME the only signable kind, S3b needs no exemption for reset locks (round 5, R5S-1).
  20. Sortition is a rank among the seats eligible at termStart(t), computed at any time inside ROLE_HORIZON from the append-only seat array through a Fenwick prefix-count tree over "eligible now" corrected by a mutation ring of future-dated (effectiveTime, seatId, ±1) entries (invariant I1; round 4, R4H-1: round 3's dense eligible list with swap-and-pop, adopted after H5 so that phantom seats would not dilute the walk, moved positions under in-flight terms and made them unlandable). MIN_TENURE = 7 days keeps every seat drawn and bled for at least a week, which prices the mutations; MUT_PER_SLOT = 2 per 60-s effective slot caps them in time (M ≤ 4,096 over the horizon, so the stalest pin, about 9.2 M gas, fits one transaction) and the first party that pins a term is paid PIN_REWARD (a ramp to PIN_MAX = 400 TAIKO) from the holder's reserve, so the M ≈ 0 walk is the paid path (round 5, R5H-2). Entry and exit are the only inputs that change E_t or a rank; a suspension of any kind, class A or B evidence included, changes neither: a suspended seat drawn as a holder is a void view and drawn as a committee slot an empty slot, so no owner write, self-submitted evidence included, changes a committee after the seed is fixed (round 5, R5S-2; round 4's premise that an evidence-driven suspension could not be owner-triggered was false, since the offender can be its own challenger).
  21. A per-term byte budget (V10), a per-landing reward and a landing reserve at registration (round 3, H2: a per-block price could not cover a term that needs three landings, and a holder at its buffer could certify a term nobody was paid to land). Revised in round 4: V10 counts sequencer blocks only (R4H-6); the per-landing reward is paid only to the landings a term needs, at most three (R4H-7); the reserve is 9,200 TAIKO per seat (two terms of the 4,561-TAIKO per-term exposure, pin reward included), non-withdrawable while any seat is active or pending, ring-fenced from liveness debits, and a lapse is a DELAY_REG-dated suspension flag that, like every suspension, changes no rank (invariant I2; R4H-3, R4-C2, R4-C4; round 5, R5S-2). Round 5 (R5S-4) made the sizing true of the honest path: a self-landing skips the lander's reserve debit and credit (the attester share still leaves, offset in expectation), and every reward-type credit to an owner with seats refills that owner's reserve before its withdrawable balance, so only rewards paid to seatless addresses leave the reserve system; ReserveLow fires once per crossing below two terms of exposure, is re-armed only when the reserve is back at its required level, and the client tops up on it.
  22. Forced-inclusion entries carry a burned-on-skip bond and a skip-escalating fee floor (round 3, H1: a poison stream was re-armable for 0.02 ETH a day).
  23. Certificate-free landings never degrade to one proof, stated as limitation L19 (round 3, H4) rather than hidden behind a "proven" DAO-independence claim.
