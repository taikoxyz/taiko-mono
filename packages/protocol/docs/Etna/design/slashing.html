Slashing, bonds, rewards and anti-monopoly

Every offence provable on L1 has an evidence format a contract verifies from the evidence alone, submittable by anyone, with no challenger bond. False accusations are impossible rather than merely unprofitable: no rule accepts an unsigned claim. Bonds are sized to the value of a term and to the harm of a certified reorg. The price of primary share is linear in seats and unbounded at exclusion; the share of productive revenue follows it only below about a quarter of the seats (§5). Invariant I5: no accusation object exists; every rule's accept set is signatures by the accused over conflicting messages, a signed object with an invalid preimage, a signed L1 reference that is false, or code on the key, and no rule infers fault from the absence of a landing. Source: judge synthesis of the three B5/B6 designs, aligned with the certificate and landing pages.

What today does and why it fails

One bond rule exists, the late-proof liveness settlement, and it is dormant: mainnet minBond = livenessBond = 0, settlement is skipped while the prover whitelist is on, and half of any debit stays ownerless (00 §2.5). No contract ever sees a preconfirmation signature; both clients accept ten conflicting hashes per height and record nothing; the only sanction is roster removal by an admin. Provers are unpaid on-chain. This fails R6 and R1 and leaves TH4, TH5, TH8, TH9, TH10 and TH18 open.

1. Identity and ledger

  • Owner and keys. The slashable identity is the owner, the registry's operator record. At registration it binds a sequencer key (secp256k1 address, no code, TH18) and an attester key (BLS12-381, G1 public key, G2 signature, domain-separated) with a proof of possession verified by one pairing; without it the certificate-pair rule would be a framing vector. Keys are rotatable with the two-hour registry delay, and each key of an owner carries a validity interval (validFrom, validUntil): the committee walk, committeeRoot and every holder check read the key valid at termStart(t), so a rotation that takes effect after a term starts, up to 34 h before its landing, changes nothing for that term (round 6, R6S-4); a retired key stays bound to its owner for the evidence window. The sequencer key proves control at registration by signing keccak(DOMAIN_KEYREG ‖ chainId ‖ owner), so a front-run of a victim's registerKeys with the victim's address fails and nobody can bind an address whose key it does not hold (round 6, judge's note on the halt report). The owner address itself may be a contract (a treasury or multisig); only the sequencer key must be code-less.
  • Ledger. Today's TAIKO gwei ledger in the Inbox proxy; no L2 ledger, no ETH bonds. A debit of x is min(x, balance); the shortfall goes into the event; debits never revert.
  • Payout. Every debit splits into a challenger share min(10 %, 2,000 TAIKO) credited to the submitter's ledger entry, an affected-party share where a rule names one, and a burn accrued in pendingBurnGwei, swept by anyone to the dead address (the token has no burn function; a token pause can never block a slash). The offender can always be its own challenger, so the deterrent of a rule is the debit minus the challenger share; all sizing uses that number. Self-submitted evidence has no eligibility lever either (round 5, R5S-2): the suspension it produces changes no E_t and no rank, because suspended seats stay in the rank domain and are void views or empty committee slots when drawn (sequencing page §2), so an owner that reports itself loses a seat or its ledger and changes no draw of any term.
  • Floor, buffer and reserve. floor = seatCount × B_SEAT; registration and reactivation require 105 % of it plus the landing reserve LAND_RESERVE = 14,400 TAIKO per seat (two terms of the 7,161-TAIKO reward exposure, round 6, R6H-3; landing page §5), 35,400 TAIKO of entry capital per seat. A safety or structural debit below the floor triggers open-ended suspension until top-up plus reactivate(), whose eligibility effect is DELAY_REG-dated. MISS, the only liveness debit, never triggers that check: it is taken from the 5 % buffer above the floor, then from seat stake down to the hard floor at 50 %, the backstop, and it only emits an event before that; the hard floor itself suspends open-endedly with a DELAY_REG-dated effect (the crossing MISS is applied at a landing whose timing is choosable, so it is dated like an owner write; sequencing page §2) and returns through the same top-up plus reactivate() at τ' + DELAY_REG. Reward-type debits come from the reserve only: the lander ramp, the blob reward, the per-landing reward, a reward boost and the attester share are debited from the reserve and never from seat stake or the buffer, so an owner cannot drain its own class-A stake through self-directed rewards (round 2, S5); the reserve is ring-fenced from MISS (round 4, R4-C4), so a timeout can never empty it. A reward boost never exceeds R_BLK_MAX. Reward credits refill reserves first (round 5, R5S-4). Any reward-type credit (ramp, blob reward, per-landing reward, attester share, pin reward) whose rewardTo resolves to an owner with seats is credited to that owner's reserveGwei up to its required level (seatCount × LAND_RESERVE) and only the excess to its withdrawable balance; when the lander and the holder are the same owner the lander's debit and credit are both skipped; the attester share (0.1 TAIKO per landed block to the end certificate's signers, which never include the holder's own seats) is still debited, about 6 TAIKO per full term, and is offset in expectation by the attester income the owner's own seats earn, which refills its reserve first. Invariant I2, restated: no owner action short of a class-A slash of itself and of the self-directed reward drain costed in limitation L20, and no liveness event, can remove the funds that pay a drawn term's landers and attesters, and no owner keeps its draws with less than one term of exposure per seat in reserve (round 6, R6S-2, the per-seat line below); the reserve is drained only by reward debits to other owners' landers, attesters and pinners, and every such payment to an owner with seats refills that owner's reserve. Residual (limitations L20): an owner can still move the part of its reserve above that line to a withdrawable balance, by letting its terms ramp and landing them with rewardTo a seatless address it controls (at most 7,161 TAIKO per term); once the reserve is below seatCount × 7,161 the owner lapses 22 minutes later (DELAY_S) for at least 36 h, so laundering past the line costs it its draws, and the draws it still holds until then are backed by about one term of exposure per seat.
  • Withdrawal. While any seat is active or pending: the excess over 1.05 × floor + reserve; the reserve leaves the ledger only through requestExit plus the evidence window, like seat stake (round 4, R4H-3). After exit: everything, once the evidence window (7 days) has passed since the last seat's activeUntil and since the last key's retirement. Credits to addresses with no seats and no key history are withdrawable immediately.
  • Reserve lapse: a per-seat line, read at the draw (round 6, R6S-2). An owner whose reserve falls below seatCount × 7,161 TAIKO, one term of exposure per seat (7,161 for one seat, 57,288 for eight), lapses: the lapse takes effect at τ + DELAY_S (22 minutes after the crossing debit), is stored in the same two-interval store as structural suspensions with its minimum length SUSPEND(3) (36 h) kept, because the store's argument needs every interval that long (round 4, R4-C2), and ends only after a top-up to the required level (seatCount × LAND_RESERVE) plus reactivate(), whose effect is DELAY_REG-dated. At the draw a lapsed owner's views are void and its committee slots empty, like every suspension's, and the lapse changes no rank and no E_t (round 5, R5S-2). DELAY_S rather than DELAY_REG is safe for that reason: voiding one's own draws or emptying one's own committee slots after the seed is fixed never adds a cartel member to any committee and never moves another owner's rank, and DELAY_S = LOOKBACK + 2·TERM still makes the flag of every term a function of L1 state at S(t), so nodes and the inbox agree (invariant I1). The schedules this covers: an owner that launders its reserve through a seatless rewardTo still moves its own money above the line, but past it loses its draws 22 minutes after the crossing for at least 36 h, and every draw it still holds until then is backed by about one term per seat; a third party that lands a negligent owner's late terms can choose which debit crosses the line, and so, among terms whose seed is already known, when the lapse starts, but the only effect is that the owner's own views pass to their drawn backups, and it needs the owner to have ignored a warning one full term of debits earlier; a line per owner, as in round 5, let an eight-seat owner keep every draw on one term of reserve. ReserveLow fires once per crossing below (seatCount + 1) × 7,161, one term of headroom above the line (14,322 for one seat, 64,449 for eight), and re-arms only at the required level, seatCount × 14,400 (hysteresis, round 5, R5S-4); between the warning and the line lies a full term of maximum-ramp debits, and the maximum-ramp terms of one owner are at least one LAND_WINDOW apart unless one range reaches both, because each term ramps from the landing below it (landableFrom, §6; round 6, R6H-2), so the client's automatic top-up has about half an hour. A single seat (14,400) survives one maximum-ramp third-party term (14,400 − 7,161 = 7,239, above its 7,161 line) and lapses at the second unless it tops up. Sizing with the honest path included (round 5, R5S-4): a self-landed term costs the reserve only the attester share (the lander's debit and credit are skipped), so the honest drain is only the terms an owner leaves to third parties: at the happy-path lag of 150 s after landableFrom, a term at the V10 cap (1,270,000 bytes, about 9.8 blobs) costs about 640 TAIKO if it is landed in five landings, the most that are paid (60 × 0.79 + up to 15 + 5 × 108 + 6 + 33), about 530 in the four it usually needs at 2-s slots, about 310 in the two it needs at 12-s slots, and a term that fits one landing about 200 (round 6, R6H-3; §6), against 7,161 at the maximum ramp, offset by the lander, attester and pin income the owner's own seats earn, which is credited to its reserve first. A chain-wide outage of any length inside the landing horizon costs an owner at most the terms of its own that the first range after recovery reaches (usually the head term alone), each at most one term of exposure, because every later term ramps from the landing below it (round 6, R6H-2); an owner at its required level, two terms per seat, therefore keeps its draws from four seats up, since a range of at most four views reaches at most four of its terms, and an owner with three seats or fewer can still lapse if the first post-recovery range reaches more of its terms than it has seats. Keeping the reserve funded is a sequencer duty (roles page §1) that the client performs with an automatic top-up to the required level when ReserveLow fires, at worst about one term of exposure per seat. Residual, stated plainly: no L1 rule can guarantee funds for a term drawn before the debits that emptied the reserve; the window is at most the terms drawn between the crossing and its DELAY_S effect plus the terms still unlanded at it, and such a term is landed by its holder at gas cost or replaced under A1 with no penalty.
  • Committee attribution. The first landing that settles term t walks committeeOf(t) on-chain in the domain-tree version effective at termStart(t), reading each drawn owner's attester key valid at termStart(t) (sequencing page §8; never from calldata; round 6, R6S-4), and writes committeeRoot[t] (one overwritten slot); unsettled terms are settled by later landings (≤ SETTLE_MAX = 4 walks per landing) or by the permissionless recordAssignment(t) of the interfaces page, and the first party that pins a term is paid PIN_REWARD (a ramp to PIN_MAX = 400 TAIKO) from the term holder's reserve (round 5, R5H-2; §6), callable from termStart(t) until termStart + ROLE_HORIZON (34 h), inside which the registry's views are exact. Committee-dependent evidence supplies the key hashes and the public keys of the bits it verifies, checked against the pinned root.

Why "invalid takeover without preimage" and "parent lacks a certificate" have no standalone rule. Both are non-existence claims. Their provable forms are S6 (the header names an object; a supplied preimage that fails is the sequencer's own signed lie, and every attester of that header lied about V3). Their unprovable forms are harmless: honest nodes reject at V2/V3 before execution, nothing carrying such a header is landable, and the only way it becomes state is Q colluders certifying it, which never lands and is replaced.

Why false accusations are impossible. S1, S3a to S3c, S6 and S8 succeed only on the accused key's own signatures or code, over conflicting messages (a pair rule checks the inequality explicitly). S3d needs the accused's own signed L1 reference (inside the lock-vote preimage) plus an L1 fact about that reference. MISS needs a VC the landing verified. No rule infers fault from the absence of a landing: the abandonment rule of rounds 2 and 3 (S4a, with its outage gate and void waiver) was removed in round 4 (R4H-5, R4-C3) because a certified view is landable by anyone and paid from the holder's reserve, so a holder cannot abandon a provable view, and the only unlandable certified content is a guest-completeness bug, a false positive for any abandonment rule, handled by replacement plus the client blacklist. No rule accepts an unsigned claim; hence no challenger bond and no counter-accusation game. The challenger's only cost is gas: with Frame Transactions a trailing status VERIFY makes a losing or stale submission invalid and free; without them it reverts on AlreadySlashed for about 30k. proven

4. Bond sizing

Let V_term be one term's revenue (the whole base fee since Proposal0026 + tips + MEV, assumed ≈ 0.01 ETH; measurement is open item G8), P the TAIKO/ETH rate (assumed 1.5·10−4), H(X) the largest value users irrevocably act on within X seconds at a confirmation level.

  1. Attested or locked reorg. The cheapest provable attack on a locked block is a second certificate or view change: at a full committee ≥ 12 attester keys plus the leader, all class A: ≥ 13 × B = 260,000 TAIKO ≈ 39 ETH at the assumed rate. This is the published credit bound for "locked", and it holds at every lock, already-landed locks included (round 5, R5S-1); it is a deterrent, not compensation, since reorg victims are not identifiable on L1 and nobody is paid from the burn (§16), and it holds within one redraw count, because across a redraw the two committees' intersection can be empty (claim 3; certificate page §14), so a client shows these exceptions beside the label, with m, the private-quorum stall (L3), the fallback residual (L5) and the absence of the label in no-committee terms (certificate page §4; after the #22188 review, 2026-10-01); raise B (linear, no liveness cost) before raising Q (which lowers the stall threshold). It is conditional on the committee's size, and hence on the suspended share of the domain (round 6, R6-C1): for a committee of m non-empty slots the bound is (2Q(m) − m + 1) × B, a suspended or lapsed owner's drawn seats are empty slots, and with a share s of the domain suspended the expected m is about 32(1 − s); nodes publish m beside the level, and seats suspended or lapsed for more than STALE_EXIT = 7 days can be exited by anyone (sequencing page §5). A FALLBACK VC by a later committee is not a provable attack on committee(t)'s locked blocks and is not priced here: L1 refuses it while a live VC of committee(t) is recorded, before termEnd(t) + 12 s and below the highest certificate of committee(t) recorded for the view, which committee(t)'s leader and the member whose bit is the lowest set bit of the highest certificate's bitmap of the view record when no TERM_END VC has formed by termEnd + 2.5 s, the other members one L1 block later if neither did (certificate page §5), so the gate holds even when a blocking minority of committee(t) withholds TERM_END (round 6, R6S-3); it lands a FALLBACK only with a certificate at its lock and lets committee(t)'s own VC at a higher lock supersede it until consumed (certificate page §5, round 5, R5S-5). The carve-out's residual is limitation L5: a FALLBACK consumed in the same L1 block as, or before, the honest record, which needs Q of a later committee and L1 ordering (outside T1), slashes nobody and orphans every descendant of the FALLBACK lock until that landing, so no credit bound covers it.
  2. Sequenced-level reorg. One uncertified tip block (S1): B − 2,000 ≥ H(1 s).
  3. Liveness. B ≥ 100 × V_term so a seat outweighs 100 terms of income; MISS_PENALTY = 50 TAIKO is a nuisance, not ruin, and since the bleed applies only to a leader silent to the whole committee an honest online leader is never bled (round 4, R4-C1), so no griefing rate enters the sizing; per-term reward exposure 7,161 TAIKO (240 + 15 + 6,500: R_BLOB is paid for at most 15 blobs per term in landing order, the five paid landings of a cap term at three blobs each, and five paid landings are the most a term at the V10 cap of 5 × LANDING_UNIT = 1,270,000 bytes earns at any slot time, round 6, R6H-3, so the sum is a hard bound for every landing pattern: sixty one-block landings would otherwise earn 60 R_BLOB, 7,206 per term, and 2 × 7,206 = 14,412 > 14,400, round 6 fix pass; + 6 + the 400-TAIKO maximum pin reward, round 5, R5H-2) and LAND_RESERVE = 14,400 per seat covers two terms of it (14,322) and is at most B, so the entry capital is 1.05 × B + 14,400 = 35,400 TAIKO per seat; the honest path does not drain it, because a self-landing skips the lander's debit, the attester share it still pays is offset in expectation by its own attester income, and every reward paid to an owner with seats refills that owner's reserve first (round 5, R5S-4).
  4. Sybil invariance. Class A takes the whole ledger and the floor is seatCount × B, so an owner with m committee slots loses ≥ m × B whatever the seat split.

B_SEAT = 20,000 TAIKO = 300 × V_term satisfies all four at the assumed inputs. The same seats make an owner sequencer and attester: a second bond would only lower the per-role Sybil price. assumed inputs; every TAIKO figure is a formula instantiated with them.

5. Anti-monopoly

  • Share. Sortition is uniform over domain seats, those registered and inside [activeFrom, activeUntil) at termStart(t), suspended ones included: a suspended or lapsed owner's draw is a void view or an empty committee slot and moves no other owner's rank (sequencing page §5; round 5, R5S-2). An entity with k of n domain seats is drawn primary with probability exactly k/n per term, history-free, whatever the split across addresses; committee slots k·K/n in expectation.
  • Why 100 % is unreachable. Registration has no gate but bond and a code-less key; its effect is future-dated from a 20-minute-old snapshot, so nothing an incumbent does after the entrant's transaction changes its draws; no rule limits how many registry writes take effect per minute, so an incumbent cannot delay an entrant's activeFrom either (the round-5 fix pass removed a per-slot cap that allowed it); no roster edit, vote or sponsor exists. The one arithmetic hole is a full seat array: MAX_SEATS = 65,535 (the registry's index type), and filling it costs 1.31 billion TAIKO of seat stake alone (65,535 × 20,000), 2.3 billion with the buffer and the landing reserve (65,535 × 35,400), above the supply. proven given a registration is includable on L1 (T1; FOCIL-eligible).
  • Seat cap. CAP = 8 bounds an address, not an entity. Impossibility: no owner-keyed rule (cooldown, consecutive-term burn, per-owner decay) costs a Sybil-capable adversary more than the per-seat price, because the registry cannot distinguish m owners of one entity from m entities; such rules tax only honest large operators. Rejected accordingly. proven by indistinguishability.
  • Performance decay is the absence bleed and the hard floor only: 50 TAIKO per timed-out view, suspension when the ledger falls to half the floor. Strikes and the suspension ladder are reserved for structural offences (class B), which need the offender's own signed lie.
  • What a cartel can still do. With ≥ m − Q(m) + 1 slots (11 of 32) in a committee it can refuse to attest an honest primary's blocks; no certificate forms, no timeout forms either (the honest attesters attested the leader's two blocks, which stay uncertified, and an attester holding an attested block that is still uncertified never signs TIMEOUT; round 4 R4H-4, round 5 R5H-1), the primary keeps its term at the sequenced level, holds after two uncertified blocks, and loses that term's revenue; nothing is penalized and the term is not transferred. Attesters that attested but saw no certificate may redraw the committee twice (certificate page §5, REDRAW_MAX = 2), so blanking a term needs a blocking minority in the first draw and in each redraw (16 of 32 in the first draw). A blank term shifts revenue without shifting primaries: the cartel's own terms certify normally, so its share of productive revenue exceeds its seat share once it blanks a material fraction of honest terms. The committee is drawn without replacement from the pool left after excluding the primary by owner and the backups by seat, so the blocking probability is hypergeometric and depends on the owner structure. Revenue model (round 6, R6-C2): the last column counts certified terms, which is exact when the fees of a delayed second carry over to later blocks (queued transactions are served later, so delay costs users latency, not the honest holders revenue). A cartel with a blocking minority also delays every handoff out of a committee it blocks by 12 to 22 s, by withholding its TERM_END lock votes (certificate page §1), and each term whose TERM_END it withholds costs an honest member about 165k gas per record to record the view's highest certificate when the term is already pinned, plus the committee walk (about 1 M gas at 128 seats, 5.4 M at 4,096) when it is not, since recordCertificate pins the term first if needed, normally one or two records per withheld term, because the duty is staggered (certificate page §5; round 6, R6S-3 and the fix pass). If the revenue of those delayed seconds is lost instead, the shares weighted by seconds are 50.8 % rather than 44.0 % for 8 × 8 / 32 × 1, and 56.9 % rather than 48.9 % for six 8-seat honest owners against 24 single seats (the round-6 judge's re-run of the censor's simulation); those extra 7 to 8 points need the cartel to withhold TERM_END after the certified terms that precede honest terms as well, and fall to 2.4 to 3.5 points if it delays only the terms it blanks. The published launch condition is ≥ 8 owners and ≥ 72 seats in the domain (Anchor page §5), a heuristic read by the DAO before armEtna(T0) and enforced by no rule; at it a cartel of 8 single seats blanks nothing. The absence bleed is not reachable by a minority: a timeout view change needs Q signatures from attesters that saw no certificate for TIMEOUT and hold no attested block of the view that is still uncertified, so a cartel below Q cannot bleed a producing leader, and the bleed applies only to a leader that is silent to the whole committee. Eligibility is never removed by a timeout. Not defended: blank terms and the revenue shift at the rates in the table (limitation L2).

6. Rewards and accounting

  • Sequencer income (L2, ETH): unchanged: coinbase gets the whole base fee (BASEFEE_SHARING_PCTG = 100 since Proposal0026; corrected after reading PR #22188) plus tips and MEV; the Anchor's legacy balance is owner-withdrawable, never load-bearing.
  • Forced-inclusion fees and refunds (L1, ETH): fees pull-credited to the consuming sequencer at landing, to rewardTo for a forced batch (the includers' ETH fee ledger, one slot per lander, withdrawFiFees); the requester recorded with each new entry withdraws per entry, with withdrawFiRefund(id) against the status the consuming landing or forced batch wrote into the kind byte of the entry's own word (pending, consumed with content, voided and refundable, burned, claimed; no slot per requester is written): its bond when the entry is consumed, bond and fee when a stall voids it and it is not the stall's first void, and the escalation surcharge FI_FEE × (4e − 1), e being the exponent stored in that byte, when it is consumed with content, the includer then getting feeWei less that surcharge, so any overpayment above the required price is the includer's bounty, undiluted; the bond and fee of a stall's first void are burned (round 6, R6H-1 and the fix pass; forced-inclusion page §1 and §5 are normative). A legacy Shasta entry has no requester and no bond, so a stall that voids it refunds nothing: its fee is burned when it is the stall's first void and otherwise credited to the includer, as on expiry.
  • Lander reward (L1, TAIKO): the landing page's Dutch ramp per block, R_BLOB per blob and the per-landing reward r_land, debited from the responsible holder's landing reserve and credited to the rewardTo that the proof's journal binds, in the same transaction; a credit whose rewardTo resolves to an owner with seats goes to that owner's reserve first, up to seatCount × LAND_RESERVE, and only the excess to its withdrawable balance (round 5, R5S-4). Ramps from landability (round 6, R6H-2). The ramps r and r_land of term t, and its PIN_REWARD below, start at landableFrom(t) = max(termEnd(t), lastLanded.at), where lastLanded.at is the L1 time of the landing on which the first range reaching t builds; the value is stored in t's TermRecord by that landing, so every later landing of t ramps from the same instant and the break-even below holds for every landing of a term. The schedule this covers is a chain-wide outage: before recovery nothing can land, and ramps counted from term end would reach their maximum for the whole backlog; from landableFrom, only the terms that the first range after recovery reaches are paid from their term end, every later term ramps from the landing below it, holders self-land their backlog at no debit, and an absent holder's term is paid from a fresh ramp, so an outage costs each owner at most the head terms it holds. A1's replace window is unchanged (it already uses lastLanded.at, capped at termEnd + LAND_WINDOW_MAX), so after an outage longer than that cap the backlog is replaceable at recovery (limitation L6) and the change moves no safety boundary. TermRecord tracks bytesLanded; landing k of term t is paid r_land only if k ≤ ⌈bytesLanded_after / LANDING_UNIT⌉, with LANDING_UNIT = 254,000 bytes, the same constant at every slot time (round 6, R6H-3). The schedule this covers: the sequencer chooses its block sizes and the lander its range boundaries, and because every record fits one blob (the V9 bound, 130,035 bytes), a greedily packed landing that is not the term's last carries more than 390,132 − 130,044 = 260,088 bytes of payload even at 2-s slots, where one L1 block carries LANDING_BYTES = 390,132 bytes, and at most about 12,500 bytes of a term are not counted in bytesLanded, so bytesLanded after k greedy landings exceeds (k − 1) × 254,000 (landing page §5) and every landing a term's sequencer blocks need is paid however they pack (FI blocks add about 250 bytes each to a landing and never a blob; they are exempt from V10's byte cap and earn no lander reward of their own; round 6 fix pass); R_BLOB is paid for at most 15 blobs per term in landing order (round 6 fix pass); a term at the V10 cap (5 × LANDING_UNIT = 1,270,000 bytes) is paid at most five r_land whatever the split (four or five landings at 2-s slots, two at 12-s slots, or five deliberately split ones at any slot time), so the per-term r_land exposure is 5 × R_LAND_MAX, a one-block landing earns at most one r_land per term, and a third party breaks even at about 100 gwei per landing whatever the term size and the slot time. A self-landing (lander and holder one owner) skips the lander's debit and credit, so it costs L1 gas plus the attester share (about 6 TAIKO per full term), which the owner's own attester income offsets in expectation (round 5, R5S-4).
  • Attester reward (L1, TAIKO): ATT_REWARD_PER_BLOCK = 0.1 TAIKO per landed block (6 per full term), the only attester-reward constant, debited from each segment's holder's reserve, split equally among the end certificate's signers excluding the holder's own seats and credited to each signer's owner's reserve first (round 5, R5S-4); per block, so one-block landings earn nothing extra. Attesters that did not sign earn nothing, so refusing to attest costs income even though it is not slashable.
  • Pin reward (L1, TAIKO; round 5, R5H-2; sized in the fix pass): the first party that pins term t (by recordAssignment(t) or by the landing that settles it) is paid PIN_REWARD from the term holder's reserve, skipped when the pinner is the holder: 0 at landableFrom(t), rising linearly to PIN_MAX = 400 TAIKO at landableFrom(t) + LAND_WINDOW (round 6, R6H-2), which pays a pin at the launch registry (about 0.8 M gas) in full up to about 75 gwei and a 4,096-seat pin (about 5.4 M gas in the round-6 simulation) up to about 11 gwei; it is part of the per-term exposure (7,161) and pays whoever first walks the term's committee on L1, a landing or recordAssignment, at the fee of the moment. A recordAssignment pin is paid only for a term at or below the first unlanded term t′ of landing page A1, whose landableFrom is read from the current lastLanded.at; a later term has no landing below its range yet and earns no pin reward until it becomes t′ or a landing reaches it (round 6, R6H-2). This covers the outage schedule in which anyone pins every backlog term without a proof: that pays at most the head term's pin, not 400 TAIKO per backlog term.
  • Landing announcement bond: announceLanding posts ANNOUNCE_BOND = 2,000 TAIKO on the ledger, forfeited 40 % to the eventual forced or replacement lander and 60 % burned if no covering landing follows; an announcement is refused once the head FI entry has been due at T_floor of the first unlanded term for longer than LAND_WINDOW (round 3, C3).
  • No per-term protocol fee, no treasury: a discretionary recipient would be an operational role (R1) and an incentive to slash.
  • Invariant (checkable from events): TAIKO.balanceOf(Inbox) = Σ balance + pendingBurnGwei. Every credit is a same-transaction debit of another entry; nothing is minted; a shortfall lowers a credit, never raises it. ETH: address(Inbox).balance = Σ unconsumed FI fees + Σ unconsumed FI bonds + Σ claimable ETH, the claimable part being the includers' fee ledger plus the bond and refund of every entry whose status is refundable and not yet claimed (round 6, R6H-1 and the fix pass); the bond and fee a stall burns leave the inbox in the landing that burns them, sent to the dead address, a push that cannot revert and so cannot be used to make that landing fail. TAIKO announcement bonds and landing reserves are ledger entries inside Σ balance; the reserve is a sub-balance that only reward-type debits and exit can lower. proven by construction.

7. Worked example (wei; B_SEAT = 2·1022 wei)

Owner A holds 3 seats plus the 5 % buffer and 3 × 14,400 TAIKO of landing reserve: balance 63,000 + 43,200 = 106,200 TAIKO = 1.062·1023 wei. Its lapse line is 3 × 7,161 = 21,483 TAIKO and its ReserveLow line 4 × 7,161 = 28,644 (round 6, R6S-2). Term 81,230, view 0, height 4,873,412.

  • S1. A's split-brain pair signs headers h and h'. Node N holds both and submits: ≈ 95k gas ≈ 1.9·1015 wei at 20 gwei. Debit 1.062·1023 (class A takes the whole ledger, reserve included); N credited min(10,620, 2,000) TAIKO = 2·1021 wei; 1.042·1023 wei queued for burn; A's seats ineligible from τ + 1,320 s. Had A self-reported, its loss is still 1.042·1023 wei.
  • S3a pair. Certificates c1, c2 at the same (t, 0, height), 22 and 23 bits, intersection 13 slots held by owners B (4 slots), C (2), D (1), E (3) and A (3). If these five owners hold 18 seats in all, the total debited is 18 × (21,000 + 14,400) TAIKO = 6.372·1023 wei (whole ledgers, reserves included), 2·1021 to the challenger, the rest burned; the lower bound 13 × B = 2.6·1023 holds. The conflicting certificate could revert at most one 1-s block.
  • Late landing. Term 81,233 is certified (60 blocks whose records reach the V10 cap, 1,270,000 bytes, about 9.8 blobs) and A's prover is down. The term before it landed 150 s after its own end, so landableFrom(81,233) is 90 s after this term's end, and 40 minutes after term end every ramp is at its maximum. At 12-s slots a third party lands it in two landings of 6 and 4 blobs: 60 × 4 + 10 × 1 + 2 × 1,300 = 2,850 TAIKO to the landers, 6 TAIKO to the attesters and PIN_MAX = 400 TAIKO to the first of the two landings, which pins the term: 3.256·1021 wei debited from A's reserve (43,200 → 39,944 TAIKO). At 2-s slots one L1 block carries three blobs, so the same term needs four or five landings, each paid r_land (k ≤ ⌈1,270,000 / 254,000⌉ = 5, LANDING_UNIT; round 6, R6H-3); with five, the most that are paid, A pays at most 60 × 4 + 15 + 5 × 1,300 + 6 + 400 = 7,161 TAIKO, the full per-term exposure (43,200 → 36,039), and a lander may split the term into five landings at 12-s slots too; a sixth landing of the term would earn its ramp, R_BLOB only while the term's paid blob count is below 15, and no r_land, so sixty one-block landings would earn 60 ramps, 15 R_BLOB and 5 r_land, 7,161 again (round 6 fix pass). Either way A stays above its ReserveLow line, and each credit to an owner with seats tops up that owner's reserve before touching its withdrawable balance (round 5, R5S-4). Had A landed the term itself, only the 6-TAIKO attester share would have been debited. Nothing else changes; no rule fires for the delay.
  • MISS. Term 81,235: A is primary and dark; the committee's timeout VC is carried by the backup's landing; the inbox burns 5·1019 wei from A's buffer and nothing else changes. A's reserve is untouched and its balance stays above the floor.

11. Attacks defended and not defended

  • TH4 equivocation: defended. A holder that signs two headers at one height loses its whole ledger for at most one uncertified block; two certificates burn ≥ 13 seats ≈ 39 ETH at a full committee (fewer at a smaller m, §4) for one 1-s block of attested reorg.
  • TH5 withholding: defended by worthlessness and by "certified implies landable by anyone". The seal is the committee's view change; the holder holds nothing the committee lacks; a certified view is landable by any lander, paid from the holder's reserve, so there is nothing to abandon and no penalty is needed (round 4, R4H-5).
  • TH8 bond griefing: defended. No accusation object; duplicates revert; no challenger bond; dust deposits meet the buffer and floor rules; a timeout VC needs Q attesters that attested nothing of the view that is still uncertified, so a minority cannot bleed a producing leader, and MISS never touches the reserve or eligibility. Residual: Q colluders can time out an honest leader early for 50 TAIKO (outside T11).
  • TH9 monopolisation: priced below one third of the seats, not defended above it. A party that pays the linear price holds the primary share it paid for; a blocking minority of a committee and its redraws can blank honest terms and take a larger share of productive revenue (§5 table, limitation L2); censorship inside its terms is bounded by forced inclusion.
  • TH10 squatting: defended by the bleed. A holder that gets no block attested for 5 s is timed out whatever headers it gossips, outside the collusion and split cases of certificate page §1(d) (round 5, R5H-1, R5-C1), so a non-serving seat loses 50 TAIKO per drawn term (about 1,800 per day at 40 seats), reaches the 50 % hard floor after 200 draws and earns no attester income meanwhile.
  • TH18 EIP-8151: defended. No ecrecover on any attester path (BLS); the sequencer path has the two-branch rule and S8.
  • TH11 evidence races: defended with frames, degraded without. The cap was sized so one lost race per win leaves a challenger positive.
  • Key theft: partly defended. A thief can destroy the victim's bond but nets at most 2,000 TAIKO.
  • Not defended: blank terms by a cartel holding a blocking minority of a committee and of both redraws (≥ 16 of 32 in the first draw; unprovable, revenue effect in §5); value above the credit bounds (39 ETH per locked window; B − 2,000 per sequenced block); wallets must apply the level table with the exceptions shown beside each label (certificate page §4; after the #22188 review, 2026-10-01).

12. Failure analysis

  • Challengers all offline. Absence penalties, settlement, roots and rewards happen on the landing path, so liveness needs no challenger; equivocation deterrence is deferred (evidence valid 7 days; discovery is immediate in practice because ≥ Q bonded nodes hold every conflicting pair). All malicious: they can only submit valid evidence, race for the cap, or withhold it.
  • Attesters all offline. No certificates or VCs: S3/S6 evidence cannot exist, every term is unattributed; nobody is slashed for a committee-wide outage, by design. All malicious: conflicting certificates burn ≥ 13 seats each; certifying an invalid lineage burns a seat per slot; certifying private data stalls the chain until replacement at no provable cost (limitation L3); refusing to attest is unslashable liveness harm bounded by one term.
  • Holders all offline. A VC per view, 50 TAIKO per view until the hard floor, open-empty terms, then dead mode. All malicious: equivocation costs the ledger; withholding is worthless; coordinated non-landing pays the ramp from their reserves to any lander who lands their certified views, and once replaceableFrom passes a replacement or forced batch lands from lastLanded until dead mode.
  • Landers and provers all offline. Nothing is slashed, and only the terms that the first range after recovery reaches are paid from their term end; every later term ramps from the landing below it (landableFrom, round 6, R6H-2), so the outage costs each owner at most the head terms it holds (§1); once the replacement predicate opens, a replacement or forced batch may land from lastLanded and the unlanded backlog is voided with no penalty to anyone (limitation L6); a landed replacement, never a record, is what voids it (round 4, R4H-5).
  • The token. Transfers reverting (pause or upgrade) block deposits, withdrawals and burn sweeps; every slash, reward and settlement is ledger-internal and unaffected.
  • DAO inactive. Parameters frozen; nothing else changes.

14. Robustness

6/4/2-s slots: every window is in seconds or terms; only S3d reads blockhash or EIP-2935 (for its signed L1 reference; a reference older than the window makes the evidence revert), and no rule reads either EIP-4788 contract (L1's beacon roots or L2's, which holds the anchored L1 execution block hashes); worst evidence ≈ 600k gas = 6 % of a 10 M-gas block. Without Frame Transactions: losing evidence races pay ≈ 30k; no evidence path uses a shared nonce or blobs; this is the block least affected by EIP-8141 slipping. ePBS Empty slots and L1 EIP-4788 gaps: not consumed; an Empty slot delays an evidence transaction by one slot against 7-day and 34-hour windows. EIP-7976 calldata floor: pair-form evidence carries ≤ 4 KiB of keys, below its execution gas; if K ever exceeds 64, store public keys on-chain at registration. EIP-8037: fresh slots only in offence records and in domain writes: a registration and an exit each write one domain-tree version per seat, about 1.6 M state gas, which EIP-8037 meters outside the EIP-7825 per-transaction cap (round 6, R6S-4).

15. Claims

  1. proven No false accusation succeeds (I5): each rule's accept set is two signatures by one key over conflicting messages, a signed object naming an invalid preimage, an aggregate whose PoP-backed bitmap includes the key, a signed L1 reference that is false, or code on the key; no rule infers fault from the absence of a landing.
  2. proven Deterrent equals debited minus the capped challenger share; self-reporting recovers at most 2,000 TAIKO.
  3. proven Two certificates or view changes over one (t, v, vcHash) at one redraw count intersect in ≥ 2Q(m) − m keys (12 at a full committee of 32 with Q = 22; pigeonhole), each fully slashable; with the floor rule the burn is ≥ 12 × B however seats are split. Across a redraw the committees differ and the intersection can be empty (certificate page §8 and §14 give the unslashable path and its probability).
  4. proven Rogue-key framing is impossible (standard BLS proof-of-possession argument).
  5. proven No slashing path depends on ecrecover succeeding.
  6. proven No offence outlives the bond: every window ends by termStart + EVIDENCE_WINDOW; every signed object's termStart ≤ activeUntil; exit waits the window past activeUntil and past key retirement.
  7. proven Share of primary terms is k/n (timeouts only on leader silence, round 4 R4H-4); primary share X costs X·H/(1 − X) seats; exclusion is unbounded. assumed (simulated, §5 table) the share of productive revenue equals the seat share below about 25 % of seats and exceeds it above; not proven linear (round 4, R4-C1).
  8. proven No owner-keyed progressive rule taxes a Sybil beyond the seat price.
  9. proven A chain-wide outage slashes nobody: no rule keys on the absence of a landing; MISS requires a VC a dark committee cannot form; S4c is fail-closed; a late landing only moves reward from the reserve, and since the ramps run from landableFrom an outage moves at most the maximum-ramp reward of the terms heading the backlog (round 6, R6H-2).
  10. proven No liveness rule waits for a slash.
  11. proven The accounting invariant, by construction.
  12. proven for the lander part, assumed for the attester part (an expectation over committee draws): the honest path does not lapse a reserve (I2 sizing, round 5, R5S-4): a self-landing skips the lander's reserve debit, the attester share it still pays (about 6 TAIKO per full term) is matched in expectation by its own attester income, and a reward credit to an owner with seats refills its reserve before its withdrawable balance, so only rewards paid to seatless landers leave the pool of reserves. 16. Alternatives rejected

  • A 50/50 challenger–burn split (halves the deterrent; key theft or "equivocate with a friend" pays up to half a bond); a challenger floor (self-reporting small debits nets a profit); challenger bonds or accusation-response games (no rule accepts a claim); affected-party compensation for reorg victims (not identifiable on L1); a treasury or DAO share of slashed funds (an operational role and an incentive to slash); BLS for the sequencer key (the guest verifies ecrecover); per-slot amounts for attester equivocation (dominated by full-ledger); MISS_PENALTY = 0 or 200 (squatting free, or entry unprofitable under griefing); a MISS refund when VC signers are later slashed (marginal); two-step seal-withholding games (vacuous once the seal is the committee's VC); replacement-triggered abandonment with a replacer share; any liveness penalty keyed on a landing deadline (rounds 2 and 3; see §3: it inferred fault from the absence of a landing); standalone rules for invalid takeover or statically invalid headers (software bugs, no protected asset); a "skipped certified block" slash on fallback leaders; progressive fee burns and consecutive-term cooldowns (bypassed by alternating addresses); term auctions; a separate attester bond; paying attesters on L2 from the Anchor's share (needs per-block bitmaps the guest cannot verify); attester reward escrow with claims; the data-request game as the per-block withholding rule; MAX_SEATS = 4,096 (a finite price for exclusion).
