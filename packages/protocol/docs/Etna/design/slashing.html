Slashing, bonds, rewards and anti-monopoly

Every offence provable on L1 has an evidence format a contract verifies from the evidence alone, submittable by anyone, with no challenger bond. False accusations are impossible rather than merely unprofitable: no rule accepts an unsigned claim. Bonds are sized to the value of a term and to the harm of a certified reorg. The price of primary share is linear in seats and unbounded at exclusion; the share of productive revenue follows it only below about a quarter of the seats (§5). Invariant I5: no accusation object exists; every rule's accept set is signatures by the accused over conflicting messages, a signed object with an invalid preimage, a signed L1 reference that is false, or code on the key, and no rule infers fault from the absence of a landing. Source: judge synthesis of the three B5/B6 designs, aligned with the certificate and landing pages.

What today does and why it fails

One bond rule exists, the late-proof liveness settlement, and it is dormant: mainnet minBond = livenessBond = 0, settlement is skipped while the prover whitelist is on, and half of any debit stays ownerless (00 §2.5). No contract ever sees a preconfirmation signature; both clients accept ten conflicting hashes per height and record nothing; the only sanction is roster removal by an admin. Provers are unpaid on-chain. This fails R6 and R1 and leaves TH4, TH5, TH8, TH9, TH10 and TH18 open.

1. Identity and ledger

  • Owner and keys. The slashable identity is the owner, the registry's operator record. At registration it binds a sequencer key (secp256k1 address, no code, TH18) and an attester key (BLS12-381, G1 public key, G2 signature, domain-separated) with a proof of possession verified by one pairing; without it the certificate-pair rule would be a framing vector. Keys are rotatable with the two-hour registry delay; a retired key stays bound to its owner for the evidence window. The owner address itself may be a contract (a treasury or multisig); only the sequencer key must be code-less.
  • Ledger. Today's TAIKO gwei ledger in the Inbox proxy; no L2 ledger, no ETH bonds. A debit of x is min(x, balance); the shortfall goes into the event; debits never revert.
  • Payout. Every debit splits into a challenger share min(10 %, 2,000 TAIKO) credited to the submitter's ledger entry, an affected-party share where a rule names one, and a burn accrued in pendingBurnGwei, swept by anyone to the dead address (the token has no burn function; a token pause can never block a slash). The offender can always be its own challenger, so the deterrent of a rule is the debit minus the challenger share; all sizing uses that number. Self-submitted evidence has no eligibility lever either (round 5, R5S-2): the suspension it produces changes no E_t and no rank, because suspended seats stay in the rank domain and are void views or empty committee slots when drawn (sequencing page §2), so an owner that reports itself loses a seat or its ledger and changes no draw of any term.
  • Floor, buffer and reserve. floor = seatCount × B_SEAT; registration and reactivation require 105 % of it plus the landing reserve LAND_RESERVE = 9,200 TAIKO per seat (two terms of reward exposure, landing page §5). A safety or structural debit below the floor triggers open-ended suspension until top-up plus reactivate(), whose eligibility effect is DELAY_REG-dated. MISS, the only liveness debit, never triggers that check: it is taken from the 5 % buffer above the floor, then from seat stake down to the hard floor at 50 %, the backstop, and it only emits an event before that; the hard floor itself suspends open-endedly with a DELAY_REG-dated effect (the crossing MISS is applied at a landing whose timing is choosable, so it is dated like an owner write; sequencing page §2) and returns through the same top-up plus reactivate() at τ' + DELAY_REG. Reward-type debits come from the reserve only: the lander ramp, the blob reward, the per-landing reward, a reward boost and the attester share are debited from the reserve and never from seat stake or the buffer, so an owner cannot drain its own class-A stake through self-directed rewards (round 2, S5); the reserve is ring-fenced from MISS (round 4, R4-C4), so a timeout can never empty it. A reward boost never exceeds R_BLK_MAX. Reward credits refill reserves first (round 5, R5S-4). Any reward-type credit (ramp, blob reward, per-landing reward, attester share, pin reward) whose rewardTo resolves to an owner with seats is credited to that owner's reserveGwei up to its required level (seatCount × LAND_RESERVE) and only the excess to its withdrawable balance; when the lander and the holder are the same owner the lander's debit and credit are both skipped; the attester share (0.1 TAIKO per landed block to the end certificate's signers, which never include the holder's own seats) is still debited, about 6 TAIKO per full term, and is offset in expectation by the attester income the owner's own seats earn, which refills its reserve first. Invariant I2, restated: no owner action short of a class-A slash of itself and of the self-directed reward drain costed in limitation L20, and no liveness event, can remove the funds that pay a drawn term's landers and attesters; the reserve is drained only by reward debits to other owners' landers, attesters and pinners, and every such payment to an owner with seats refills that owner's reserve. Residual (limitations L20): an owner can still empty its own reserve by letting its terms ramp and landing them with rewardTo a seatless address it controls (at most 4,561 TAIKO per term moved to a withdrawable balance); the lapse that follows is DELAY_REG-dated, so the unfunded window is the terms drawn inside DELAY_REG plus the terms still unlanded, and it costs the owner one SUSPEND(3) of lost revenue per cycle.
  • Withdrawal. While any seat is active or pending: the excess over 1.05 × floor + reserve; the reserve leaves the ledger only through requestExit plus the evidence window, like seat stake (round 4, R4H-3). After exit: everything, once the evidence window (7 days) has passed since the last seat's activeUntil and since the last key's retirement. Credits to addresses with no seats and no key history are withdrawable immediately.
  • Reserve lapse. When a reward debit takes the reserve below two terms of exposure (9,122 TAIKO = 2 × 4,561) the contract emits ReserveLow once per crossing, re-armed only when the reserve is back at its required level (seats × LAND_RESERVE) (hysteresis, round 5, R5S-4); a reserve below one term of exposure (4,561 TAIKO) makes the owner ineligible with a DELAY_REG-dated effect (never DELAY_S) for at least SUSPEND(3), stored in the same two-interval store as structural suspensions (round 4, R4-C2), and like every suspension it changes no rank (round 5, R5S-2). Sizing with the honest path included (round 5, R5S-4): a self-landed term costs the reserve only the attester share (the lander's debit and credit are skipped), so the honest drain is only the terms an owner leaves to third parties, about 210 TAIKO per term at the happy-path lag of 150 s (60 × 0.79 + 15 + 108 + 6 + 33 for the pin reward at 150 s) against 4,561 at the maximum ramp, offset by the lander, attester and pin income the owner's own seats earn, which is credited to its reserve first. Keeping the reserve funded is a sequencer duty (roles page §1) that the client performs with an automatic top-up when ReserveLow fires. Residual, stated plainly: no L1 rule can guarantee funds for a term drawn before the debits that emptied the reserve; the window is at most the terms drawn between the lapse and its DELAY_REG effect, and such a term is landed by its holder at gas cost or replaced under A1 with no penalty.
  • Committee attribution. The first landing that settles term t walks committeeOf(t) on-chain from the corrected Fenwick tree (sequencing page §8; never from calldata) and writes committeeRoot[t] (one overwritten slot); unsettled terms are settled by later landings (≤ SETTLE_MAX = 4 walks per landing) or by the permissionless recordAssignment(t) of the interfaces page, and the first party that pins a term is paid PIN_REWARD (a ramp to PIN_MAX = 400 TAIKO) from the term holder's reserve (round 5, R5H-2; §6), callable from termStart(t) until termStart + ROLE_HORIZON (34 h), inside which the registry's views are exact. Committee-dependent evidence supplies the key hashes and the public keys of the bits it verifies, checked against the pinned root.

Why "invalid takeover without preimage" and "parent lacks a certificate" have no standalone rule. Both are non-existence claims. Their provable forms are S6 (the header names an object; a supplied preimage that fails is the sequencer's own signed lie, and every attester of that header lied about V3). Their unprovable forms are harmless: honest nodes reject at V2/V3 before execution, nothing carrying such a header is landable, and the only way it becomes state is Q colluders certifying it, which never lands and is replaced.

Why false accusations are impossible. S1, S3a to S3c, S6 and S8 succeed only on the accused key's own signatures or code, over conflicting messages (a pair rule checks the inequality explicitly). S3d needs the accused's own signed L1 reference (inside the lock-vote preimage) plus an L1 fact about that reference. MISS needs a VC the landing verified. No rule infers fault from the absence of a landing: the abandonment rule of rounds 2 and 3 (S4a, with its outage gate and void waiver) was removed in round 4 (R4H-5, R4-C3) because a certified view is landable by anyone and paid from the holder's reserve, so a holder cannot abandon a provable view, and the only unlandable certified content is a guest-completeness bug, a false positive for any abandonment rule, handled by replacement plus the client blacklist. No rule accepts an unsigned claim; hence no challenger bond and no counter-accusation game. The challenger's only cost is gas: with Frame Transactions a trailing status VERIFY makes a losing or stale submission invalid and free; without them it reverts on AlreadySlashed for about 30k. proven

4. Bond sizing

Let V_term be one term's revenue (75 % base fee + tips + MEV, assumed ≈ 0.01 ETH; measurement is open item G8), P the TAIKO/ETH rate (assumed 1.5·10−4), H(X) the largest value users irrevocably act on within X seconds at a confirmation level.

  1. Attested or locked reorg. The cheapest provable attack on a locked block is a second certificate or view change: ≥ 12 attester keys plus the leader, all class A: ≥ 13 × B = 260,000 TAIKO ≈ 39 ETH at the assumed rate. This is the published credit bound for "locked", and it holds at every lock, already-landed locks included (round 5, R5S-1); raise B (linear, no liveness cost) before raising Q (which lowers the stall threshold). A FALLBACK VC by a later committee is not a provable attack on committee(t)'s locked blocks and is not priced here: L1 refuses it while a live VC of committee(t) is recorded and before termEnd(t) + 12 s, lands it only with a certificate at its lock, and lets committee(t)'s own VC at a higher lock supersede it until consumed (certificate page §5, round 5, R5S-5); the residual same-block race is limitation L5.
  2. Sequenced-level reorg. One uncertified tip block (S1): B − 2,000 ≥ H(1 s).
  3. Liveness. B ≥ 100 × V_term so a seat outweighs 100 terms of income; MISS_PENALTY = 50 TAIKO is a nuisance, not ruin, and since the bleed applies only to a leader silent to the whole committee an honest online leader is never bled (round 4, R4-C1), so no griefing rate enters the sizing; per-term reward exposure 4,561 TAIKO (240 + 15 + 3,900 + 6 + the 400-TAIKO maximum pin reward, round 5, R5H-2, sized in the fix pass) ≤ B/2 and LAND_RESERVE = 9,200 per seat covers two terms of it (9,122); the honest path does not drain it, because a self-landing skips the lander's debit, the attester share it still pays is offset in expectation by its own attester income, and every reward paid to an owner with seats refills that owner's reserve first (round 5, R5S-4).
  4. Sybil invariance. Class A takes the whole ledger and the floor is seatCount × B, so an owner with m committee slots loses ≥ m × B whatever the seat split.

B_SEAT = 20,000 TAIKO = 300 × V_term satisfies all four at the assumed inputs. The same seats make an owner sequencer and attester: a second bond would only lower the per-role Sybil price. assumed inputs; every TAIKO figure is a formula instantiated with them.

5. Anti-monopoly

  • Share. Sortition is uniform over eligible seats. An entity with k of n seats is primary with probability exactly k/n per term, history-free, whatever the split across addresses; committee slots k·K/n in expectation.
  • Why 100 % is unreachable. Registration has no gate but bond and a code-less key; its effect is future-dated from a 20-minute-old snapshot, so nothing an incumbent does after the entrant's transaction changes its draws; since round 5 an effective slot holds at most MUT_PER_SLOT = 2 mutations and a full slot queues a write to the next, so an incumbent that registers ahead of an entrant can delay the entrant's activeFrom by 60 s per 2 seats it registers (each held for at least MIN_TENURE at B_SEAT plus reserve: a one-day delay costs about 2,880 seats, about 87 M TAIKO), but cannot prevent the entry; no roster edit, vote or sponsor exists. The one arithmetic hole is a full seat array: MAX_SEATS = 65,535 (the registry's index type), and filling it costs 1.31 billion TAIKO, above the supply. proven given a registration is includable on L1 (T1; FOCIL-eligible).
  • Seat cap. CAP = 8 bounds an address, not an entity. Impossibility: no owner-keyed rule (cooldown, consecutive-term burn, per-owner decay) costs a Sybil-capable adversary more than the per-seat price, because the registry cannot distinguish m owners of one entity from m entities; such rules tax only honest large operators. Rejected accordingly. proven by indistinguishability.
  • Performance decay is the absence bleed and the hard floor only: 50 TAIKO per timed-out view, suspension when the ledger falls to half the floor. Strikes and the suspension ladder are reserved for structural offences (class B), which need the offender's own signed lie.
  • What a cartel can still do. With ≥ m − Q(m) + 1 slots (11 of 32) in a committee it can refuse to attest an honest primary's blocks; no certificate forms, no timeout forms either (the honest attesters attested the leader's two blocks, which stay uncertified, and an attester holding an attested block that is still uncertified never signs TIMEOUT; round 4 R4H-4, round 5 R5H-1), the primary keeps its term at the sequenced level, holds after two uncertified blocks, and loses that term's revenue; nothing is penalized and the term is not transferred. Attesters that attested but saw no certificate may redraw the committee twice (certificate page §5, REDRAW_MAX = 2), so blanking a term needs a blocking minority in the first draw and in each redraw (16 of 32 in the first draw). A blank term shifts revenue without shifting primaries: the cartel's own terms certify normally, so its share of productive revenue exceeds its seat share once it blanks a material fraction of honest terms. The committee is drawn without replacement from the pool left after excluding the primary by owner and the backups by seat, so the blocking probability is hypergeometric and depends on the owner structure. 6. Rewards and accounting

  • Sequencer income (L2, ETH): unchanged: coinbase gets 75 % of base fees plus tips and MEV; the Anchor keeps 25 %, owner-withdrawable, never load-bearing.
  • Forced-inclusion fees (L1, ETH): pull-credited to the consuming sequencer at landing, to the lander under escalation.
  • Lander reward (L1, TAIKO): the landing page's Dutch ramp per block, R_BLOB per blob and the per-landing reward r_land, debited from the responsible holder's landing reserve and credited to the rewardTo that the proof's journal binds, in the same transaction; a credit whose rewardTo resolves to an owner with seats goes to that owner's reserve first, up to seatCount × LAND_RESERVE, and only the excess to its withdrawable balance (round 5, R5S-4). TermRecord tracks bytesLanded; landing k of term t is paid r_land only if k ≤ ⌈bytesLanded_after / (6 × 130,044)⌉, so one-block landings earn at most one r_land per term and the per-term r_land exposure is exactly 3 × R_LAND_MAX (round 4, R4H-7). A self-landing (lander and holder one owner) skips the lander's debit and credit, so it costs L1 gas plus the attester share (about 6 TAIKO per full term), which the owner's own attester income offsets in expectation (round 5, R5S-4).
  • Attester reward (L1, TAIKO): 0.1 TAIKO per landed block (6 per full term), debited from each segment's holder's reserve, split equally among the end certificate's signers excluding the holder's own seats and credited to each signer's owner's reserve first (round 5, R5S-4); per block, so one-block landings earn nothing extra. Attesters that did not sign earn nothing, so refusing to attest costs income even though it is not slashable.
  • Pin reward (L1, TAIKO; round 5, R5H-2; sized in the fix pass): the first party that pins term t (by recordAssignment(t) or by the landing that settles it) is paid PIN_REWARD from the term holder's reserve, skipped when the pinner is the holder: 0 at termEnd(t), rising linearly to PIN_MAX = 400 TAIKO at termEnd(t) + LAND_WINDOW, which pays a 0.6 M-gas pin in full at 100 gwei; it is part of the per-term exposure (4,561) and makes prompt pinning, which keeps the mutation-ring correction M near zero for every later landing of the term, the paid path at the fee of the moment.
  • Landing announcement bond: announceLanding posts ANNOUNCE_BOND = 2,000 TAIKO on the ledger, forfeited 40 % to the eventual forced or replacement lander and 60 % burned if no covering landing follows; an announcement is refused once the head FI entry has been due at T_floor of the first unlanded term for longer than LAND_WINDOW (round 3, C3).
  • No per-term protocol fee, no treasury: a discretionary recipient would be an operational role (R1) and an incentive to slash.
  • Invariant (checkable from events): TAIKO.balanceOf(Inbox) = Σ balance + pendingBurnGwei. Every credit is a same-transaction debit of another entry; nothing is minted; a shortfall lowers a credit, never raises it. ETH: address(Inbox).balance = Σ unconsumed FI fees + Σ unconsumed FI bonds + Σ claimable ETH. TAIKO announcement bonds and landing reserves are ledger entries inside Σ balance; the reserve is a sub-balance that only reward-type debits and exit can lower. proven by construction.

7. Worked example (wei; B_SEAT = 2·1022 wei)

Owner A holds 3 seats plus the 5 % buffer and 3 × 9,200 TAIKO of landing reserve: balance 63,000 + 27,600 = 90,600 TAIKO = 9.06·1022 wei. Term 81,230, view 0, height 4,873,412.

  • S1. A's split-brain pair signs headers h and h'. Node N holds both and submits: ≈ 95k gas ≈ 1.9·1015 wei at 20 gwei. Debit 9.06·1022 (class A takes the whole ledger, reserve included); N credited min(9,060, 2,000) TAIKO = 2·1021 wei; 8.86·1022 wei queued for burn; A's seats ineligible from τ + 1,320 s. Had A self-reported, its loss is still 8.86·1022 wei.
  • S3a pair. Certificates c1, c2 at the same (t, 0, height), 22 and 23 bits, intersection 13 slots held by owners B (4 slots), C (2), D (1), E (3) and A (3). Total 3.78·1023 wei debited, 2·1021 to the challenger, the rest burned; the lower bound 13 × B = 2.6·1023 holds. The conflicting certificate could revert at most one 1-s block.
  • Late landing. Term 81,233 is certified (60 blocks, 15 blobs) and A's prover is down; 40 minutes after term end a third party lands it in three 6-blob landings at the maximum ramp: 60 × 4 + 15 × 1 + 3 × 1,300 = 4,155 TAIKO to the landers, 6 TAIKO to the attesters and PIN_MAX = 400 TAIKO (the pin ramp is at its maximum after 30 minutes) to the first of the three landings, which pins the term: 4.561·1021 wei debited from A's reserve (27,600 → 23,039 TAIKO); each credit to an owner with seats tops up that owner's reserve before touching its withdrawable balance (round 5, R5S-4). Had A landed the term itself, only the 6-TAIKO attester share would have been debited. A fourth one-block landing of the same term would earn its ramp and blob reward but no r_land. Nothing else changes; no rule fires for the delay.
  • MISS. Term 81,235: A is primary and dark; the committee's timeout VC is carried by the backup's landing; the inbox burns 5·1019 wei from A's buffer and nothing else changes. A's reserve is untouched and its balance stays above the floor.

11. Attacks defended and not defended

  • TH4 equivocation: defended. A holder that signs two headers at one height loses its whole ledger for at most one uncertified block; two certificates burn ≥ 13 seats ≈ 39 ETH for one 1-s block of attested reorg.
  • TH5 withholding: defended by worthlessness and by "certified implies landable by anyone". The seal is the committee's view change; the holder holds nothing the committee lacks; a certified view is landable by any lander, paid from the holder's reserve, so there is nothing to abandon and no penalty is needed (round 4, R4H-5).
  • TH8 bond griefing: defended. No accusation object; duplicates revert; no challenger bond; dust deposits meet the buffer and floor rules; a timeout VC needs Q attesters that attested nothing of the view that is still uncertified, so a minority cannot bleed a producing leader, and MISS never touches the reserve or eligibility. Residual: Q colluders can time out an honest leader early for 50 TAIKO (outside T11).
  • TH9 monopolisation: priced below one third of the seats, not defended above it. A party that pays the linear price holds the primary share it paid for; a blocking minority of a committee and its redraws can blank honest terms and take a larger share of productive revenue (§5 table, limitation L2); censorship inside its terms is bounded by forced inclusion.
  • TH10 squatting: defended by the bleed. A holder that gets no block attested for 5 s is timed out whatever headers it gossips (round 5, R5H-1, R5-C1), so a non-serving seat loses 50 TAIKO per drawn term (about 1,800 per day at 40 seats), reaches the 50 % hard floor after 200 draws and earns no attester income meanwhile.
  • TH18 EIP-8151: defended. No ecrecover on any attester path (BLS); the sequencer path has the two-branch rule and S8.
  • TH11 evidence races: defended with frames, degraded without. The cap was sized so one lost race per win leaves a challenger positive.
  • Key theft: partly defended. A thief can destroy the victim's bond but nets at most 2,000 TAIKO.
  • Not defended: blank terms by a cartel holding a blocking minority of a committee and of both redraws (≥ 16 of 32 in the first draw; unprovable, revenue effect in §5); value above the credit bounds (39 ETH per locked window; B − 2,000 per sequenced block); wallets must apply the level table.

12. Failure analysis

  • Challengers all offline. Absence penalties, settlement, roots and rewards happen on the landing path, so liveness needs no challenger; equivocation deterrence is deferred (evidence valid 7 days; discovery is immediate in practice because ≥ Q bonded nodes hold every conflicting pair). All malicious: they can only submit valid evidence, race for the cap, or withhold it.
  • Attesters all offline. No certificates or VCs: S3/S6 evidence cannot exist, every term is unattributed; nobody is slashed for a committee-wide outage, by design. All malicious: conflicting certificates burn ≥ 13 seats each; certifying an invalid lineage burns a seat per slot; certifying private data stalls the chain until replacement at no provable cost (limitation L3); refusing to attest is unslashable liveness harm bounded by one term.
  • Holders all offline. A VC per view, 50 TAIKO per view until the hard floor, open-empty terms, then dead mode. All malicious: equivocation costs the ledger; withholding is worthless; coordinated non-landing pays the ramp from their reserves to any lander who lands their certified views, and once replaceableFrom passes a replacement or forced batch lands from lastLanded until dead mode.
  • Landers and provers all offline. Rewards ramp; nothing is slashed; once the replacement predicate opens, a replacement or forced batch may land from lastLanded and the unlanded backlog is voided with no penalty to anyone (limitation L6); a landed replacement, never a record, is what voids it (round 4, R4H-5).
  • The token. Transfers reverting (pause or upgrade) block deposits, withdrawals and burn sweeps; every slash, reward and settlement is ledger-internal and unaffected.
  • DAO inactive. Parameters frozen; nothing else changes.

14. Robustness

6/4/2-s slots: every window is in seconds or terms; no rule reads blockhash, EIP-2935 or EIP-4788; worst evidence ≈ 600k gas = 6 % of a 10 M-gas block. Without Frame Transactions: losing evidence races pay ≈ 30k; no evidence path uses a shared nonce or blobs; this is the block least affected by EIP-8141 slipping. ePBS Empty slots and 4788 gaps: not consumed; an Empty slot delays an evidence transaction by one slot against 7-day and 34-hour windows. EIP-7976 calldata floor: pair-form evidence carries ≤ 4 KiB of keys, below its execution gas; if K ever exceeds 64, store public keys on-chain at registration. EIP-8037: fresh slots only in offence records and registration.

15. Claims

  1. proven No false accusation succeeds (I5): each rule's accept set is two signatures by one key over conflicting messages, a signed object naming an invalid preimage, an aggregate whose PoP-backed bitmap includes the key, a signed L1 reference that is false, or code on the key; no rule infers fault from the absence of a landing.
  2. proven Deterrent equals debited minus the capped challenger share; self-reporting recovers at most 2,000 TAIKO.
  3. proven Two certificates or view changes over one (t, v, vcHash) at one redraw count intersect in ≥ 2Q(m) − m keys (12 at a full committee of 32 with Q = 22; pigeonhole), each fully slashable; with the floor rule the burn is ≥ 12 × B however seats are split. Across a redraw the committees differ and the intersection can be empty (certificate page §8 and §14 give the unslashable path and its probability).
  4. proven Rogue-key framing is impossible (standard BLS proof-of-possession argument).
  5. proven No slashing path depends on ecrecover succeeding.
  6. proven No offence outlives the bond: every window ends by termStart + EVIDENCE_WINDOW; every signed object's termStart ≤ activeUntil; exit waits the window past activeUntil and past key retirement.
  7. proven Share of primary terms is k/n (timeouts only on leader silence, round 4 R4H-4); primary share X costs X·H/(1 − X) seats; exclusion is unbounded. assumed (simulated, §5 table) the share of productive revenue equals the seat share below about 25 % of seats and exceeds it above; not proven linear (round 4, R4-C1).
  8. proven No owner-keyed progressive rule taxes a Sybil beyond the seat price.
  9. proven A chain-wide outage slashes nobody: no rule keys on the absence of a landing; MISS requires a VC a dark committee cannot form; S4c is fail-closed; a late landing only moves reward from the reserve.
  10. proven No liveness rule waits for a slash.
  11. proven The accounting invariant, by construction.
  12. proven for the lander part, assumed for the attester part (an expectation over committee draws): the honest path does not lapse a reserve (I2 sizing, round 5, R5S-4): a self-landing skips the lander's reserve debit, the attester share it still pays (about 6 TAIKO per full term) is matched in expectation by its own attester income, and a reward credit to an owner with seats refills its reserve before its withdrawable balance, so only rewards paid to seatless landers leave the pool of reserves.
  13. assumed B_SEAT = 20,000, MISS = 50, ATT_REWARD_PER_BLOCK = 0.1 rest on V_term ≈ 0.01 ETH and the TAIKO/ETH rate; resolved by a week of mainnet coinbase and MEV measurement (G8, G10).
  14. assumed EIP-2537 gas as of Pectra with no repricing in Glamsterdam or Hegotá; devnet measurement of the 32-key aggregate path is open.
  15. open Whether the revenue shift from blank terms at and above the T11 boundary (§5 table) needs a further brake beyond two redraws, for example a larger REDRAW_MAX or a redraw seeded per attempt.

16. Alternatives rejected

  • A 50/50 challenger–burn split (halves the deterrent; key theft or "equivocate with a friend" pays up to half a bond); a challenger floor (self-reporting small debits nets a profit); challenger bonds or accusation-response games (no rule accepts a claim); affected-party compensation for reorg victims (not identifiable on L1); a treasury or DAO share of slashed funds (an operational role and an incentive to slash); BLS for the sequencer key (the guest verifies ecrecover); per-slot amounts for attester equivocation (dominated by full-ledger); MISS_PENALTY = 0 or 200 (squatting free, or entry unprofitable under griefing); a MISS refund when VC signers are later slashed (marginal); two-step seal-withholding games (vacuous once the seal is the committee's VC); replacement-triggered abandonment with a replacer share; any liveness penalty keyed on a landing deadline (rounds 2 and 3; see §3: it inferred fault from the absence of a landing); standalone rules for invalid takeover or statically invalid headers (software bugs, no protected asset); a "skipped certified block" slash on fallback leaders; progressive fee burns and consecutive-term cooldowns (bypassed by alternating addresses); term auctions; a separate attester bond; paying attesters on L2 from the Anchor's share (needs per-block bitmaps the guest cannot verify); attester reward escrow with claims; the data-request game as the per-block withholding rule; MAX_SEATS = 4,096 (a finite price for exclusion).

17. Open questions

  1. Should the absence bleed be paused for owners whose seats attested blocks in the same term, so a cartel cannot grief an owner that is demonstrably online?
  2. Burn destination: transfer to the dead address (supply unchanged) versus a DAO-added public burn (supply reduced). Governance choice; logic identical.
  3. Should B_SEAT be DAO-adjustable by constant upgrade with 7-day notice so exits are never trapped?
  4. Should nodes publish the credit bounds (B − 2,000 at "sequenced", 13 × B at "locked") beside the confirmation level so wallets apply them mechanically?
