Slashing, bonds, rewards and anti-monopoly

Every offence provable on L1 has an evidence format a contract verifies from the evidence alone, submittable by anyone, with no challenger bond. False accusations are impossible rather than merely unprofitable: no rule accepts an unsigned claim. Bonds are sized to the value of a term and to the harm of a certified reorg. The monopoly cost curve is linear in share and unbounded at exclusion. Source: judge synthesis of the three B5/B6 designs, aligned with the certificate and landing pages.

What today does and why it fails

One bond rule exists, the late-proof liveness settlement, and it is dormant: mainnet minBond = livenessBond = 0, settlement is skipped while the prover whitelist is on, and half of any debit stays ownerless (00 §2.5). No contract ever sees a preconfirmation signature; both clients accept ten conflicting hashes per height and record nothing; the only sanction is roster removal by an admin. Provers are unpaid on-chain. This fails R6 and R1 and leaves TH4, TH5, TH8, TH9, TH10 and TH18 open.

1. Identity and ledger

  • Owner and keys. The slashable identity is the owner, the registry's operator record. At registration it binds a sequencer key (secp256k1 address, no code, TH18) and an attester key (BLS12-381, G1 public key, G2 signature, domain-separated) with a proof of possession verified by one pairing; without it the certificate-pair rule would be a framing vector. Keys are rotatable with the two-hour registry delay; a retired key stays bound to its owner for the evidence window. The owner address itself may be a contract (a treasury or multisig); only the sequencer key must be code-less.
  • Ledger. Today's TAIKO gwei ledger in the Inbox proxy; no L2 ledger, no ETH bonds. A debit of x is min(x, balance); the shortfall goes into the event; debits never revert.
  • Payout. Every debit splits into a challenger share min(10 %, 2,000 TAIKO) credited to the submitter's ledger entry, an affected-party share where a rule names one, and a burn accrued in pendingBurnGwei, swept by anyone to the dead address (the token has no burn function; a token pause can never block a slash). The offender can always be its own challenger, so the deterrent of a rule is the debit minus the challenger share; all sizing uses that number.
  • Floor and buffer. floor = seatCount × B_SEAT; registration and reactivation require 105 % of it. A safety or structural debit below the floor triggers open-ended suspension until top-up. Liveness debits (absence, abandonment, reward payments) never trigger that check; they only emit an event; a hard floor at 50 % is the backstop.
  • Withdrawal. While seats exist: the excess over 105 % of the floor. After exit: everything, once the evidence window (7 days) has passed since the last seat's activeUntil and since the last key's retirement. Credits to addresses with no seats and no key history are withdrawable immediately.
  • Committee attribution. The landing of term t writes committeeRoot[t] (one overwritten slot) since it loads the keys anyway; unsettled terms are settled by later landings (≤ 4 per landing) or by a permissionless recordCommittee(t) within two hours of the term's start, inside which the registry's views are exact. Committee-dependent evidence supplies the key hashes and the public keys of the bits it verifies.

Why "invalid takeover without preimage" and "parent lacks a certificate" have no standalone rule. Both are non-existence claims. Their provable forms are S6 (the header names an object; a supplied preimage that fails is the sequencer's own signed lie, and every attester of that header lied about V3). Their unprovable forms are harmless: honest nodes reject at V2/V3 before execution, nothing carrying such a header is landable, and the only way it becomes state is Q colluders certifying it, which S7 turns into 23 seats.

Why false accusations are impossible. S1, S3a to S3c, S6 and S8 succeed only on the accused key's own signatures or code. S4a and S7 need an L1 clock fact plus a quorum certificate whose membership is pinned by a root the landing wrote, plus the outage gate. MISS needs a VC the landing verified. No rule accepts an unsigned claim; hence no challenger bond and no counter-accusation game. The challenger's only cost is gas: with Frame Transactions a trailing status VERIFY makes a losing or stale submission invalid and free; without them it reverts on AlreadySlashed for about 30k. proven

4. Bond sizing

Let V_term be one term's revenue (75 % base fee + tips + MEV, assumed ≈ 0.01 ETH; measurement is open item G8), P the TAIKO/ETH rate (assumed 1.5·10−4), H(X) the largest value users irrevocably act on within X seconds at a confirmation level.

  1. Attested or locked reorg. The cheapest provable attack on a locked block is a second certificate or view change: ≥ 12 attester keys plus the leader, all class A: ≥ 13 × B = 260,000 TAIKO ≈ 39 ETH at the assumed rate. This is the published credit bound for "locked"; raise B (linear, no liveness cost) before raising Q (which lowers the stall threshold).
  2. Sequenced-level reorg. One uncertified tip block (S1): B − 2,000 ≥ H(1 s).
  3. Liveness. B ≥ 100 × V_term so a seat outweighs 100 terms of income; MISS_PENALTY ≤ V_term × (1 − p)/p at p = 0.5 (entry stays profitable when half of a party's draws are griefed); SLASH_ABANDON above the maximum lander reward per term (paying a lander always dominates abandoning); per-term reward exposure ≤ B/2.
  4. Sybil invariance. Class A takes the whole ledger and the floor is seatCount × B, so an owner with m committee slots loses ≥ m × B whatever the seat split.

B_SEAT = 20,000 TAIKO = 300 × V_term satisfies all four at the assumed inputs. The same seats make an owner sequencer and attester: a second bond would only lower the per-role Sybil price. assumed inputs; every TAIKO figure is a formula instantiated with them.

5. Anti-monopoly

  • Share. Sortition is uniform over eligible seats. An entity with k of n seats is primary with probability exactly k/n per term, history-free, whatever the split across addresses; committee slots k·K/n in expectation.
  • Why 100 % is unreachable. Registration has no gate but bond and a code-less key; its effect is future-dated from a 20-minute-old snapshot, so nothing an incumbent does after the entrant's transaction changes its draws; no roster edit, vote or sponsor exists. The one arithmetic hole is a full seat array: MAX_SEATS = 65,535 (the registry's index type), and filling it costs 1.31 billion TAIKO, above the supply. proven given a registration is includable on L1 (T1; FOCIL-eligible).
  • Seat cap. CAP = 8 bounds an address, not an entity. Impossibility: no owner-keyed rule (cooldown, consecutive-term burn, per-owner decay) costs a Sybil-capable adversary more than the per-seat price, because the registry cannot distinguish m owners of one entity from m entities; such rules tax only honest large operators. Rejected accordingly. proven by indistinguishability.
  • Performance decay is the absence bleed and the hard floor only: 50 TAIKO per timed-out view, suspension when the ledger falls to half the floor. Strikes and the suspension ladder are reserved for structural offences (class B), which need the offender's own signed lie.
  • What a cartel can still do. With ≥ 11 of 32 committee slots it can refuse to attest an entrant's blocks; the honest 21 then time out honestly, a true VC forms, and the entrant loses 50 TAIKO for that view and nothing else. P[Bin(32, c) ≥ 11] = 0.04 / 0.36 / 0.79 at cartel seat share c = 0.2 / 0.3 / 0.4. Driving a one-seat entrant to the 50 % hard floor takes 200 of its own drawn views in which the cartel sits in the committee: about 139 days at c = 0.2, about a week at c = 0.33 (the T11 boundary), five days at c = 0.4, at 40 seats; the entrant can top up at any time, and the cartel forgoes its attester income in every griefed term. Eligibility is never removed by a timeout, so no minority holds a removal power. Not defended above the boundary: there the bleed is a slow but real lever (limitations L2).

6. Rewards and accounting

  • Sequencer income (L2, ETH): unchanged: coinbase gets 75 % of base fees plus tips and MEV; the Anchor keeps 25 %, owner-withdrawable, never load-bearing.
  • Forced-inclusion fees (L1, ETH): pull-credited to the consuming sequencer at landing, to the lander under escalation.
  • Lander reward (L1, TAIKO): the landing page's Dutch ramp, debited from the responsible holder's ledger and credited to rewardTo in the same transaction. Self-landing nets zero.
  • Attester reward (L1, TAIKO): 6 TAIKO per landing, debited from the holder, split equally among the end certificate's signers excluding the holder's own seats. Attesters that did not sign earn nothing, so refusing to attest costs income even though it is not slashable.
  • Lander bonus: the escrowed 40 % of an abandonment penalty, paid to the next ordinary landing's rewardTo (never to a replacement or forced landing).
  • No per-term protocol fee, no treasury: a discretionary recipient would be an operational role (R1) and an incentive to slash.
  • Invariant (checkable from events): TAIKO.balanceOf(Inbox) = Σ balance + pendingBurnGwei + pendingLanderBonusGwei. Every credit is a same-transaction debit of another entry; nothing is minted; a shortfall lowers a credit, never raises it. ETH: address(Inbox).balance = Σ unconsumed FI fees + Σ claimable ETH + Σ open demand fees. proven by construction.

7. Worked example (wei; B_SEAT = 2·1022 wei)

Owner A holds 3 seats plus the 5 % buffer: balance 6.3·1022 wei. Term 81,230, view 0, height 4,873,412.

  • S1. A's split-brain pair signs headers h and h'. Node N holds both and submits: ≈ 95k gas ≈ 1.9·1015 wei at 20 gwei. Debit 6.3·1022; N credited min(6,300, 2,000) TAIKO = 2·1021 wei; 6.1·1022 wei queued for burn; A's seats ineligible from τ + 1,320 s. Had A self-reported, its loss is still 6.1·1022 wei.
  • S3a pair. Certificates c1, c2 at the same (t, 0, height), 22 and 23 bits, intersection 13 slots held by owners B (4 slots), C (2), D (1), E (3) and A (3). Total 3.78·1023 wei debited, 2·1021 to the challenger, the rest burned; the lower bound 13 × B = 2.6·1023 holds. The conflicting certificate could revert at most one 1-s block.
  • S4a. Term 81,233's view change is recorded; nothing lands by termEnd + 3,600 s; term 81,234 landed 700 s after that term's end, so the outage gate passes. Holder debited 2·1021 wei: 2·1020 to the reporter, 8·1020 escrowed for the next lander, 1021 burned. No strike; A keeps its seats.
  • MISS. Term 81,235: A is primary and dark; the committee's timeout VC is carried by the backup's landing; the inbox burns 5·1019 wei from A and adds one strike. A's balance stays above the floor.

11. Attacks defended and not defended

  • TH4 equivocation: defended. A holder that signs two headers at one height loses its whole ledger for at most one uncertified block; two certificates burn ≥ 13 seats ≈ 39 ETH for one 1-s block of attested reorg.
  • TH5 withholding: defended by worthlessness plus S4a/S7. The seal is the committee's view change; the holder holds nothing the committee lacks.
  • TH8 bond griefing: defended. No accusation object; duplicates revert; no challenger bond; dust deposits meet the buffer and floor rules. Residual: a false VC by ≥ 11 non-attesting colluders costs the victim 50 TAIKO per griefed view and nothing else.
  • TH9 monopolisation: defended in cost, not possibility. A party that pays the linear price holds what it paid for; censorship inside its terms is bounded by forced inclusion.
  • TH10 squatting: defended. 50 TAIKO per drawn term (about 1,800 per day at 40 seats), the 50 % hard floor after 200 draws, no attester income.
  • TH18 EIP-8151: defended. No ecrecover on any attester path (BLS); the sequencer path has the two-branch rule and S8.
  • TH11 evidence races: defended with frames, degraded without. The cap was sized so one lost race per win leaves a challenger positive.
  • Key theft: partly defended. A thief can destroy the victim's bond but nets at most 2,000 TAIKO.
  • Not defended: cartel liveness griefing above the T11 boundary (≥ 11 of 32 slots; unprovable, priced above); value above the credit bounds (39 ETH per locked window; B − 2,000 per sequenced block); wallets must apply the level table.

12. Failure analysis

  • Challengers all offline. Absence penalties, settlement, roots and rewards happen on the landing path, so liveness needs no challenger; equivocation deterrence is deferred (evidence valid 7 days; discovery is immediate in practice because ≥ Q bonded nodes hold every conflicting pair). All malicious: they can only submit valid evidence, race for the cap, or withhold it.
  • Attesters all offline. No certificates or VCs: S3/S6/S7 evidence cannot exist, S4a cannot fire, every term is unattributed; nobody is slashed for a committee-wide outage, by design. All malicious: conflicting certificates burn ≥ 13 seats each; certifying an invalid lineage burns a seat per slot; certifying private data costs 23 seats; refusing to attest is unslashable liveness harm bounded by one term.
  • Holders all offline. A VC per view, 50 TAIKO plus a coalesced strike, suspension within a week, open-empty terms, then dead mode. All malicious: equivocation costs the ledger; withholding is worthless; coordinated non-landing costs 2,000 per term plus the ramp to any lander until dead mode.
  • Landers and provers all offline. Rewards ramp; nothing is voided or slashed while the outage gate sees no landing.
  • The token. Transfers reverting (pause or upgrade) block deposits, withdrawals and burn sweeps; every slash, reward and settlement is ledger-internal and unaffected.
  • DAO inactive. Parameters frozen; nothing else changes.

14. Robustness

6/4/2-s slots: every window is in seconds or terms; no rule reads blockhash, EIP-2935 or EIP-4788; worst evidence ≈ 600k gas = 6 % of a 10 M-gas block. Without Frame Transactions: losing evidence races pay ≈ 30k; no evidence path uses a shared nonce or blobs; this is the block least affected by EIP-8141 slipping. ePBS Empty slots and 4788 gaps: not consumed; an Empty slot delays an evidence transaction by one slot against 7-day and 34-hour windows. EIP-7976 calldata floor: pair-form evidence carries ≤ 4 KiB of keys, below its execution gas; if K ever exceeds 64, store public keys on-chain at registration. EIP-8037: fresh slots only in offence records and registration.

15. Claims

  1. proven No false accusation succeeds: each rule's accept set is two signatures by one key over conflicting messages, a signed object naming an invalid preimage, an aggregate whose PoP-backed bitmap includes the key, or an L1 fact with a root-pinned quorum and the outage gate.
  2. proven Deterrent equals debited minus the capped challenger share; self-reporting recovers at most 2,000 TAIKO.
  3. proven Two certificates or view changes over one (t, v, vcHash) intersect in ≥ 2Q(m) − m keys (12 at a full committee of 32 with Q = 22; pigeonhole), each fully slashable; with the floor rule the burn is ≥ 12 × B however seats are split.
  4. proven Rogue-key framing is impossible (standard BLS proof-of-possession argument).
  5. proven No slashing path depends on ecrecover succeeding.
  6. proven No offence outlives the bond: every window ends by termStart + EVIDENCE_WINDOW; every signed object's termStart ≤ activeUntil; exit waits the window past activeUntil and past key retirement.
  7. proven Share is k/n; share X costs X·H/(1 − X) seats; exclusion is unbounded.
  8. proven No owner-keyed progressive rule taxes a Sybil beyond the seat price.
  9. proven A chain-wide outage slashes nobody: S4a and S7 require a landing inside the outage window; MISS requires a VC a dark committee cannot form; S4c is fail-closed.

16. Alternatives rejected

  • A 50/50 challenger–burn split (halves the deterrent; key theft or "equivocate with a friend" pays up to half a bond); a challenger floor (self-reporting small debits nets a profit); challenger bonds or accusation-response games (no rule accepts a claim); affected-party compensation for reorg victims (not identifiable on L1); a treasury or DAO share of slashed funds (an operational role and an incentive to slash); BLS for the sequencer key (the guest verifies ecrecover); per-slot amounts for attester equivocation (dominated by full-ledger); MISS_PENALTY = 0 or 200 (squatting free, or entry unprofitable under griefing); a MISS refund when VC signers are later slashed (marginal); two-step seal-withholding games (vacuous once the seal is the committee's VC); replacement-triggered abandonment with a replacer share; standalone rules for invalid takeover or statically invalid headers (software bugs, no protected asset); a "skipped certified block" slash on fallback leaders; progressive fee burns and consecutive-term cooldowns (bypassed by alternating addresses); term auctions; a separate attester bond; paying attesters on L2 from the Anchor's share (needs per-block bitmaps the guest cannot verify); attester reward escrow with claims; the data-request game as the per-block withholding rule; MAX_SEATS = 4,096 (a finite price for exclusion).

17. Open questions

  3. Should B_SEAT be DAO-adjustable by constant upgrade with 7-day notice so exits are never trapped?
  4. Should nodes publish the credit bounds (B − 2,000 at "sequenced", 13 × B at "locked") beside the confirmation level so wallets apply them mechanically?
