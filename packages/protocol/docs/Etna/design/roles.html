Roles

Etna has two bonded roles that share one registration (sequencer and attester), three unbonded roles anyone plays with no registration (lander/prover, challenger, forced-inclusion requester), the passive node operator, and the DAO, whose only power is upgrading code. No role is admitted, removed or paused by anyone in day-to-day operation (R1).

1. Sequencer (bonded)

AspectRule
EntryregisterKeys (a code-less secp256k1 sequencer key, a BLS attester key with proof of possession) then register(count) with bond ≥ 1.05 × seats × B_SEAT + seats × LAND_RESERVE (B_SEAT = 20,000 TAIKO, at most 8 seats per address; LAND_RESERVE = 9,200 TAIKO per seat, two terms of the 4,561-TAIKO per-term reward exposure: 60 × 4 + 15 × 1 + 3 × 1,300 + 60 × 0.1 + the 400-TAIKO maximum pin reward) from which lander, attester and pin rewards, and nothing else, are paid; the reserve is non-withdrawable while the owner has any active or pending seat, liveness debits never touch it (round 4, R4H-3, R4-C4), and every reward-type credit the owner earns is paid into it first, up to seats × LAND_RESERVE, so the honest path does not drain it (the attester share it pays is offset in expectation by its own attester income; round 5, R5S-4; landing page §5 is normative). A fresh owner has a 5 % buffer above the floor (1,000 TAIKO per seat, 20 timeouts) before a liveness debit touches seat stake, and the 50 % hard floor is the only liveness-driven suspension. Effective DELAY_REG (2 h) later; seat indices are contract-assigned; the attester key's proof of possession covers (pubkey, owner, chainId) and a key already bound is rejected. Cost: bond plus about two fresh storage slots per first seat under EIP-8037. No admission decision exists.
ExitrequestExit(seats), any count of the owner's seats, each seat one mutation-ring entry (round 5, R5-C4: an owner with the 8-seat cap adds at most 16 entry or exit mutations a week, however it groups them), effective two hours later but never before a seat has been eligible for MIN_TENURE = 7 days (activeUntil ≥ activeFrom + MIN_TENURE; round 4, R4H-1: a seat is drawn and bled for at least a week, which prices registry churn); finalizeExit and full withdrawal seven days after the last seat's activeUntil and after the last key's retirement (the evidence window). Partial withdrawal of any excess over 1.05 × floor + reserve at any time; the reserve itself leaves the ledger only through requestExit plus the evidence window, like seat stake (round 4, R4H-3).
RightsPrimary holder of a term with probability seats/A, backup with the same weighting; computed 20 to 80 minutes ahead by every node (Sequencing rights).
DutiesProduce one block per second during its view, each passing every validity rule with its full payload gossiped (only blocks the honest attesters attest stop the committee's timeout: a bodiless, payload-less, invalid or future-stamped header gets nothing attested, so a holder that gossips headers without serving is timed out at 5 s and bled; round 5, R5H-1, R5-C1); carry the newest certificate (lag ≤ 1) or the opening view change; hold when more than two blocks are uncertified, serving the two real ones on request (the honest attesters that attested them never time the leader out, so a leader held by an abstaining minority keeps its view; a redrawn committee attests the one its redraw vote names); stop at a later view change or at term end; record on L1 every view change of the term's committee that it carries (recordViewChange; the FALLBACK gates rest on it, round 5 fix pass); serve range requests; get every certified block of its view landed by termEnd + LAND_WINDOW (30 min), landing itself if no one else does (its own landing costs it gas plus the attester share: the lander's debit and credit are both skipped when lander and holder are one owner, round 5, R5S-4; a third party is paid from its landing reserve: up to 4 TAIKO per block, 1 per blob and 1,300 per landing the term needs, at most three (round 4, R4H-7), plus the pin reward, up to PIN_MAX = 400 TAIKO, to whoever pins the term first); keep the landing reserve funded (the client tops up through deposit() when ReserveLow fires, once per crossing of two terms of exposure, re-armed at the required level; round 5, R5S-4).
RewardsL2: coinbase share of base fees (75 %), priority fees and ordering value, as today. L1: forced-inclusion fees of consumed entries (pull ledger); as a lander, attester (0.1 TAIKO per landed block, shared) or first pinner (PIN_REWARD, a ramp to PIN_MAX = 400 TAIKO per term) of other owners' terms, reward-type credits that refill its own reserve first, up to seats × LAND_RESERVE, with only the excess withdrawable (round 5, R5S-4). Self-landing costs only L1 gas plus the attester share (about 6 TAIKO per full term), which the owner's own attester income offsets in expectation.
SlashingS1 block equivocation (whole ledger); S6 naming an invalid certificate or view change (one seat, one strike; three strikes in a week suspend); S8 key acquires code (whole ledger); absence proven by a TIMEOUT view change (50 TAIKO burned from the 5 % buffer above the floor, then from seat stake, never from the landing reserve; never a strike: only the 50 % hard floor can end eligibility). A TIMEOUT is signed only by an attester that saw no new certificate for TIMEOUT seconds and holds no block of the view that it attested and that is still uncertified; an honest online leader gets its blocks attested, so it is never bled (round 4, R4H-4), and a leader that gets nothing attested, whatever headers it gossips, always is (round 5, R5H-1 and the fix pass; the certificate page is normative). A self-inflicted class-A or class-B suspension changes no rank and no draw (round 5, R5S-2). No abandonment offence exists: S4a was removed in round 4 (R4H-5, R4-C3) because a certified view is landable by anyone and paid from the reserve, so no holder can abandon a provable view.
All offlineEach view times out after 5 s; after four takeovers the term idles; certified absences bleed 50 TAIKO each until the hard floor; terms become open-empty (any bonded address); if nothing lands for 75 terms, dead mode lets any bonded address land from the last landed head. No DAO action.
All maliciousCannot finalize invalid state (ZK), land uncertified data (certificate check), skip due forced inclusions (landing reverts, forced batch bypasses them), block a registration, or hide equivocation. Withholding is worthless; coordinated non-landing costs the ramp, paid from the reserves to whoever lands, and a window nobody lands past the deadline machinery is replaced with no penalty; an honest entrant gets its share two hours later.

2. Attester (bonded; the same registration)

AspectRule
Entry and exitAs the sequencer; every registered seat is eligible for the per-term committee of up to 32 seats drawn by the cycle seed as ranks among the seats eligible at the term's start (a Fenwick prefix-count tree over the append-only seat array, corrected by the mutation ring for effects dated after the term's start; round 4, R4H-1), excluding the primary holder by owner and the backups by seat (sequencing page §8 owns the rule); the quorum scales with the committee's actual size. A suspended owner's seats stay in the rank domain: drawn as a committee slot, such a seat is an empty slot (m shrinks by one, Q(m) scales, later slots do not move), and drawn as a holder it is a void view, so no suspension, self-inflicted or not, changes E_t, a rank or a committee after the seed is fixed (round 5, R5S-2).
DutiesExecute every valid block of the current view through the engine, verify the produced hash equals the signed one, attest with a BLS signature over the header hash, one attestation per (term, view, height); sign TIMEOUT for (t, v) only when, for TIMEOUT = 5 s by its clock, it has seen no new certificate of the view and holds no block of the view that it attested and that is still uncertified (so a leader whose two real blocks it attested while a minority abstains is never timed out, while a leader gossiping bodiless or unattestable headers gets nothing attested and is; round 4, R4H-4, bounded in round 5, R5H-1 and restated on the attester's own attestations in the fix pass; the certificate page is normative), or TERM_END at term end; sign FALLBACK for an earlier term t only once termEnd(t) + END_GRACE + VC_FALLBACK × (own term − t) has passed, the same condition L1 checks at record and landing time (round 5, R5S-5); sign REDRAW(t, v, vcHash, n, h, phHash), n ≤ REDRAW_MAX = 2, naming the lowest uncertified block of the view it attested, when it has seen no certificate for TIMEOUT seconds; as a member of a redrawn committee, attest the named block with the staleness bound waived; record on L1 every view change it signed within TO_REBROADCAST of its formation if no record exists yet (the FALLBACK gates rest on it; round 5 fix pass); cast lock votes only at locks it holds, re-voting only upward; serve blocks_by_range for two hours; keep a slashing-protection record of the last signed header, attestation, lock vote and timeout.
RewardsATT_REWARD_PER_BLOCK = 0.1 TAIKO per landed block (6 per full term), debited from each block's holder's reserve, split equally among the end certificate's signers and credited to each signer's owner's reserve first (round 5, R5S-4); per block so range splitting earns nothing. Not signing earns nothing.
SlashingS3a attestation equivocation or a certificate pair (whole ledger, ≥ 12 keys per conflicting pair; keyed on (t, v, vcHash, redraw, height)); S3b lock lie (whole ledger; no lock is exempt, landed locks included, round 5, R5S-1: an honest TIMEOUT, TERM_END or FALLBACK vote is never below a certificate its key attested outside a reset, and after a reset only RESUME is signable); S3c non-monotone lock votes (whole ledger); S6 attesting a header whose named certificate is invalid (one seat, one strike); S3d signing a REPLACE or RESUME lock vote whose signed L1 reference did not satisfy the replacement predicate (one seat, one strike; three strikes in a week suspend; the interfaces page is normative for evidence types). Silence is never slashed, and neither is signing over data that never became available (round 2: no L1 rule can distinguish it from data that is available but unprovable; limitation L3).
All offlineNo certificates, no view changes: the leader produces at most two blocks and holds; at term end plus 12 s the next committee signs a fallback view change, which L1 records and lands only from that time, only while no VC of the term's own committee is recorded, and only with a certificate at its lock (round 5, R5S-5); the chain fails closed at the preconfirmation layer with only "sequenced" and "landed" levels; after 75 unlanded terms dead mode lets a bonded party land without certificates. Nobody is slashed for a committee-wide outage.
All malicious (≥ Q)Can certify data only they hold (an unslashable stall until the replacement window opens, 35 to 65 minutes; limitation L3), refuse to attest (a blank term: the leader keeps its view, nothing lands, nobody is penalized; below Q a minority can do nothing more), sign early timeouts (cost 0, one view), or revert a locked block (12 provable slashings at any lock, landed locks included; round 5, R5S-1). Cannot land beyond a lock, forge a proof, or touch a term with an honest committee: as the next term's committee it can sign a FALLBACK for the honest term, but L1 refuses it while the honest committee's own VC is recorded, before termEnd + 12 s, or without a certificate at its lock, leaving only the same-block race of limitation L5 (round 5, R5S-5).

3. Lander and prover (anyone)

AspectRule
Entry and exitNone. Any address with the certified data (served by any committee member or RPC node) and access to two ZK proving systems can prove; any address can land with a proof it holds, its own or one received on the proofs topic. Proofs are fungible because the blob bytes are a pure function of the certified chain.
DutiesNone enforced. Economic pull only.
RewardsPer landed block, a Dutch ramp from 0.5 to 4 TAIKO per block, 1 TAIKO per blob, and 0 to 1,300 TAIKO per landing over the 30 minutes after term end, the per-landing part paid to landing k of a term only if k ≤ ⌈bytes landed so far / (6 × 130,044)⌉, so one-block landings earn at most one per term and a term owes at most three (round 4, R4H-7); all debited from the term holder's landing reserve (never its seat stake) and credited on the L1 ledger in the same transaction (into the lander's own reserve first if it owns seats, round 5, R5S-4); plus PIN_REWARD (a ramp to PIN_MAX = 400 TAIKO) from the holder's reserve to the first party that pins the term, by recordAssignment or by the landing that settles it (round 5, R5H-2); plus 40 % of a forfeited announceLanding bond when landing the forced or replacement batch it failed to cover; plus forced-inclusion fees when landing a forced batch.
SlashingNone. A wrong or stale landing reverts; with Frame Transactions it is invalid and free, without them it costs about 25k gas plus a blob fee.
All offlineThe sequencer lands its own blocks; if it cannot, ranges stay certified and un-voided and the ramp waits for the first returning prover. Nothing is voided or penalized while no landing happens: only a landed replacement or forced batch past the deadline machinery voids, and no rule penalizes an unlanded view.
All maliciousCan only decline or race; no lander is trusted and no proof names one.

4. Challenger (anyone)

AspectRule
EntryNone; no bond.
DutiesNone enforced; every gossiped object is self-contained evidence.
Rewards10 % of the debit, capped at 2,000 TAIKO, credited on the L1 ledger; withdrawable immediately for addresses with no seats.
SlashingNone; false accusations are impossible by construction, so no bond is needed.
All offlineAbsence penalties, settlement and rewards are applied on the landing path, so liveness needs no challenger; equivocation deterrence is delayed, not lost (7-day windows). Since locked-block safety is a deterrent, a challenger-free week is a real degradation (open question on the certificate page).
All maliciousCan only submit valid evidence or withhold it.

5. Forced-inclusion requester (anyone)

AspectRule
EntryPost one blob (or a small calldata shape, FOCIL-eligible) with the fee; see Forced inclusion.
GuaranteeInclusion at a bounded L1 time after the request becomes due; escalation to a permissionless forced batch when sequencers ignore it; void, fee to the includer, no refund after seven days if nothing lands (replacing today's owner-only queue reset).
CostThe queue fee, doubling with backlog and escalating after skips, plus a 0.05 ETH bond refunded on consumption; both burned on void or skip.

6. Node operator (passive)

Runs the derivation and preconfirmation client with an L1 view. Validates every block by V1 to V6, follows certificates and view changes, labels confirmations sequenced / attested / locked / landed / final, serves range requests as a duty of RPC nodes, and derives from L1 blobs when behind. Has no validity role; a bad L1 view yields pending, a bad clock only mis-times signing. Not slashable.

7. DAO (upgrades only)

Can upgrade the Inbox and Anchor implementations, rotate ZK image ids (with a two-hour overlap or an emergency zero), add a third ZK system, change constants by upgrade. Cannot admit or remove any participant, pause landing or block production, void the forced-inclusion queue, or touch bonds. If the DAO never acts again: image windows stay open-ended, single-proof mode and replacement keep certified landing moving unless every ZK system is broken; certificate-free regimes (dead mode, no-committee, open-empty, forced batches) always need two systems and stall on a one-system outage until an image rotation (limitation L19), parameters freeze. proven by construction: no function on any liveness path is owner-gated.

8. Role lifecycles

Owner:   UNREGISTERED --registerKeys, register(seats)--> PENDING(2 h) --> ACTIVE --requestExit--> EXITING(2 h) --> EXPIRED --7 d--> WITHDRAWABLE
         ACTIVE --3 structural strikes in a week (class B evidence, one strike each; DELAY_S-dated)--> SUSPENDED(36 h, doubling) --> ACTIVE ; ACTIVE --below the hard floor (MISS bleed; DELAY_REG-dated)--> SUSPENDED_OPEN --top-up + reactivate()--> ACTIVE at τ' + DELAY_REG ; ACTIVE --class A evidence--> SLASHED (exit only)
         ACTIVE --reserve below one term of exposure (ReserveLow once per crossing of two terms, with hysteresis; DELAY_REG-dated, ≥ SUSPEND(3))--> SUSPENDED_RESERVE --top-up + reactivate()--> ACTIVE at τ' + DELAY_REG
         (every input that changes E_t or a rank is DELAY_REG-dated; a suspension of any kind changes neither: its seats stay in the rank domain and are void views or empty slots when drawn; round 4 R4-C2, round 5 R5S-2)
Term:    SCHEDULED (known 20 min ahead) --termStart--> VIEW 0 --REDRAW vote (≤ 2)--> VIEW 0, new committee --timeout VC--> VIEW 1..4 --term end VC--> next term
Block:   SEQUENCED (0.3 s) --Q attestations--> ATTESTED (0.7 s) --carried by a certified block--> LOCKED (1.7 s) --land--> LANDED (minutes) --> FINAL
Lander:  IDLE --certificate seen, reward ≥ cost--> PROVE --> SUBMIT --Landed(me)--> PAID ; --Landed(other) or expiry--> IDLE (free)

9. Claims

  1. proven No role has an admission, removal or pause gate; the DAO's only lever is code.
  2. proven Every bonded duty has an objective slashing condition or is deliberately unslashable (silence), and every unbonded role is safe to leave to anyone because it can only decline or race.
  3. assumed Reward magnitudes (ramp, attester share, pin reward, challenger cap) rest on an assumed per-term revenue of about 0.01 ETH; measurement is open item G8.
  4. proven An owner that follows §1 is never suspended by its own operation: a self-landing moves only the attester share out of its reserve (offset in expectation by its own attester income), every reward it earns refills the reserve first, and a timeout needs a leader that produced no attestable block for 5 s (round 5, R5S-4, R5H-1).
