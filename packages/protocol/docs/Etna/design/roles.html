Roles

Etna has two bonded roles that share one registration (sequencer and attester), three unbonded roles anyone plays with no registration (lander/prover, challenger, forced-inclusion requester), the passive node operator, and the DAO, whose only power is upgrading code. No role is admitted, removed or paused by anyone in day-to-day operation (R1).

1. Sequencer (bonded)

AspectRule
EntryregisterKeys (a code-less secp256k1 sequencer key, a BLS attester key with proof of possession) then register(count) with bond ≥ 1.05 × seats × B_SEAT (20,000 TAIKO per seat, at most 8 seats per address) plus a landing reserve of 6,200 TAIKO from which lander and attester rewards are paid. Effective DELAY_REG (2 h) later; seat indices are contract-assigned; the attester key's proof of possession covers (pubkey, owner, chainId) and a key already bound is rejected. Cost: bond plus about two fresh storage slots per first seat under EIP-8037. No admission decision exists.
ExitrequestExit (all seats), effective two hours later; finalizeExit and full withdrawal seven days after the last seat's activeUntil and after the last key's retirement (the evidence window). Partial withdrawal of any excess over 105 % of the floor at any time.
RightsPrimary holder of a term with probability seats/A, backup with the same weighting; computed 20 to 80 minutes ahead by every node (Sequencing rights).
DutiesProduce one block per second during its view; carry the newest certificate (lag ≤ 1) or the opening view change; gossip the full payload; hold when more than two blocks are uncertified; stop at a later view change or at term end; serve range requests; get every certified block of its view landed by termEnd + LAND_WINDOW (30 min), landing itself if no one else does (its own landing costs it only gas; a third party is paid from its landing reserve (up to 4 TAIKO per block plus 1,300 per landing)).
RewardsL2: coinbase share of base fees (75 %), priority fees and ordering value, as today. L1: forced-inclusion fees of consumed entries (pull ledger). Self-landing costs only L1 gas.
SlashingS1 block equivocation (whole ledger); S6 naming an invalid certificate or view change (one seat); S8 key acquires code (whole ledger); S4a certified view not landed by the hard deadline while other landings happened (2,000 TAIKO, no strike); absence proven by a timeout view change (50 TAIKO burned, never a strike: a timeout is manufacturable by an abstaining minority, so only the 50 % hard floor can end eligibility).
All offlineEach view times out after 5 s; after four takeovers the term idles; certified absences bleed 50 TAIKO each until the hard floor; terms become open-empty (any bonded address); if nothing lands for 75 terms, dead mode lets any bonded address land from the last landed head. No DAO action.
All maliciousCannot finalize invalid state (ZK), land uncertified data (certificate check), skip due forced inclusions (landing reverts, forced batch bypasses them), block a registration, or hide equivocation. Withholding is worthless; coordinated non-landing costs the ramp and the abandonment penalty; an honest entrant gets its share two hours later.

2. Attester (bonded; the same registration)

AspectRule
Entry and exitAs the sequencer; every registered seat is eligible for the per-term committee of up to 32 seats drawn by the cycle seed from the eligible list, excluding the primary holder by owner and the backups by seat (sequencing page §8 owns the rule); the quorum scales with the committee's actual size.
DutiesExecute every valid block of the current view through the engine, verify the produced hash equals the signed one, attest with a BLS signature over the header hash, one attestation per (term, view, height); sign timeouts after 5 s without a certificate only if it attested nothing in that window, or at term end; cast lock votes only at locks it holds, re-voting only upward; serve blocks_by_range for two hours; keep a slashing-protection record of the last signed header, attestation and timeout.
Rewards0.1 TAIKO per landed block (6 per full term), debited from each block's holder, split equally among the end certificate's signers; per block so range splitting earns nothing. Not signing earns nothing.
SlashingS3a attestation equivocation or a certificate pair (whole ledger, ≥ 12 keys per conflicting pair); S3b lock lie (whole ledger); S3c non-monotone lock votes (whole ledger); S6 attesting a header whose named certificate is invalid (one seat); S3d signing a REPLACE timeout whose L1 reference did not satisfy the replacement predicate (one seat). Silence is never slashed, and neither is signing over data that never became available (round 2: no L1 rule can distinguish it from data that is available but unprovable; limitation L3).
All offlineNo certificates, no view changes: the leader produces at most two blocks and holds; at term end plus 12 s the next committee signs a fallback view change; the chain fails closed at the preconfirmation layer with only "sequenced" and "landed" levels; after 75 unlanded terms dead mode lets a bonded party land without certificates. Nobody is slashed for a committee-wide outage.
All malicious (≥ Q)Can certify data only they hold (an unslashable stall until the replacement window opens, 35 to 65 minutes; limitation L3), refuse to attest (liveness only, one term), sign early timeouts (cost 0, one view), or revert a locked block (12 provable slashings). Cannot land beyond a lock, forge a proof, or touch a term with an honest committee.

3. Lander and prover (anyone)

AspectRule
Entry and exitNone. Any address with the certified data (served by any committee member or RPC node) and access to two ZK proving systems can prove; any address can land with a proof it holds, its own or one received on the proofs topic. Proofs are fungible because the blob bytes are a pure function of the certified chain.
DutiesNone enforced. Economic pull only.
RewardsPer landed block, a Dutch ramp from 0.5 to 4 TAIKO per block, 1 TAIKO per blob, and 0 to 1,300 TAIKO per landing over the 30 minutes after term end, debited from the term holder's landing reserve (never its seat stake) and credited on the L1 ledger in the same transaction; plus 40 % of any abandonment penalty escrowed since the previous landing; plus forced-inclusion fees when landing a forced batch.
SlashingNone. A wrong or stale landing reverts; with Frame Transactions it is invalid and free, without them it costs about 25k gas plus a blob fee.
All offlineThe sequencer lands its own blocks; if it cannot, ranges stay certified and un-voided and the ramp waits for the first returning prover. Nothing is voided or penalized while no landing happens (outage gate).
All maliciousCan only decline or race; no lander is trusted and no proof names one.

4. Challenger (anyone)

AspectRule
EntryNone; no bond.
DutiesNone enforced; every gossiped object is self-contained evidence.
Rewards10 % of the debit, capped at 2,000 TAIKO, credited on the L1 ledger; withdrawable immediately for addresses with no seats.
SlashingNone; false accusations are impossible by construction, so no bond is needed.
All offlineAbsence penalties, settlement and rewards are applied on the landing path, so liveness needs no challenger; equivocation deterrence is delayed, not lost (7-day windows). Since locked-block safety is a deterrent, a challenger-free week is a real degradation (open question on the certificate page).
All maliciousCan only submit valid evidence or withhold it.

5. Forced-inclusion requester (anyone)

AspectRule
EntryPost one blob (or a small calldata shape, FOCIL-eligible) with the fee; see Forced inclusion.
GuaranteeInclusion at a bounded L1 time after the request becomes due; escalation to a permissionless forced batch when sequencers ignore it; void, fee to the includer, no refund after seven days if nothing lands (replacing today's owner-only queue reset).
CostThe queue fee, doubling with backlog and escalating after skips, plus a 0.05 ETH bond refunded on consumption; both burned on void or skip.

6. Node operator (passive)

Runs the derivation and preconfirmation client with an L1 view. Validates every block by V1 to V6, follows certificates and view changes, labels confirmations sequenced / attested / locked / landed / final, serves range requests as a duty of RPC nodes, and derives from L1 blobs when behind. Has no validity role; a bad L1 view yields pending, a bad clock only mis-times signing. Not slashable.

7. DAO (upgrades only)

Can upgrade the Inbox and Anchor implementations, rotate ZK image ids (with a two-hour overlap or an emergency zero), add a third ZK system, change constants by upgrade. Cannot admit or remove any participant, pause landing or block production, void the forced-inclusion queue, or touch bonds. If the DAO never acts again: image windows stay open-ended, single-proof mode and replacement keep certified landing moving unless every ZK system is broken; certificate-free regimes (dead mode, no-committee, open-empty, forced batches) always need two systems and stall on a one-system outage until an image rotation (limitation L19), parameters freeze. proven by construction: no function on any liveness path is owner-gated.

8. Role lifecycles

Owner:   UNREGISTERED --registerKeys, register(seats)--> PENDING(2 h) --> ACTIVE --requestExit--> EXITING(2 h) --> EXPIRED --7 d--> WITHDRAWABLE
         ACTIVE --3 structural strikes (class B evidence)--> SUSPENDED(36 h, doubling) --> ACTIVE ; ACTIVE --below the hard floor--> SUSPENDED_OPEN --top-up--> ACTIVE ; ACTIVE --class A evidence--> SLASHED (exit only)
Term:    SCHEDULED (known 20 min ahead) --termStart--> VIEW 0 --timeout VC--> VIEW 1..4 --term end VC--> next term
Block:   SEQUENCED (0.3 s) --Q attestations--> ATTESTED (0.7 s) --carried by a certified block--> LOCKED (1.7 s) --land--> LANDED (minutes) --> FINAL
Lander:  IDLE --certificate seen, reward ≥ cost--> PROVE --> SUBMIT --Landed(me)--> PAID ; --Landed(other) or expiry--> IDLE (free)

9. Claims

  1. proven No role has an admission, removal or pause gate; the DAO's only lever is code.
  2. proven Every bonded duty has an objective slashing condition or is deliberately unslashable (silence), and every unbonded role is safe to leave to anyone because it can only decline or race.
  3. assumed Reward magnitudes (ramp, attester share, challenger cap) rest on an assumed per-term revenue of about 0.01 ETH; measurement is open item G8.
