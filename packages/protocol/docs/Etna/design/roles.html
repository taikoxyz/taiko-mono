Roles

Etna has two bonded roles that share one registration (sequencer and attester), three unbonded roles anyone plays with no registration (lander/prover, challenger, forced-inclusion requester), the passive node operator, and the DAO, whose only levers are upgrades (parameters are frozen in the code) and the Anchor's legacy withdraw. No role is admitted, removed or paused by anyone in day-to-day operation (R1).

Self-landing costs only L1 gas plus the attester share (about 6 TAIKO per full term), which the owner's own attester income offsets in expectation.
SlashingS1 block equivocation (whole ledger); S6 naming an invalid certificate or view change (one seat, one strike; three strikes in a week suspend); S8 key acquires code (whole ledger); absence proven by a TIMEOUT view change (50 TAIKO burned from the 5 % buffer above the floor, then from seat stake, never from the landing reserve; never a strike: only the 50 % hard floor can end eligibility). A TIMEOUT is signed only by an attester that saw no new certificate for TIMEOUT seconds, counted from the moment it first holds the view's opening object and restarted on each certificate (round 6, R6H-4), and holds no block of the view that it attested and that is still uncertified; an honest online leader gets its blocks attested, so it is never bled (round 4, R4H-4), and a leader that gets nothing attested, whatever headers it gossips and whether or not it publishes anything at all, is bled, outside the collusion and split cases of certificate page §1(d) (round 5, R5H-1 and the fix pass; the certificate page is normative). A self-inflicted class-A or class-B suspension changes no rank and no draw (round 5, R5S-2), and neither does a reserve lapse, DELAY_S-dated since round 6 (R6S-2). No abandonment offence exists: S4a was removed in round 4 (R4H-5, R4-C3) because a certified view is landable by anyone and paid from the reserve, so no holder can abandon a provable view.
All offlineEach view times out after 5 s; after four takeovers the term idles; certified absences bleed 50 TAIKO each until the hard floor; once every owner a term draws is suspended there, every drawn view is void and the term is open-empty (any bonded address; round 6, R6H-2: such a term no longer idles to its end); if nothing lands for 75 terms, dead mode lets any bonded address land from the last landed head. No DAO action.
All maliciousCannot finalize invalid state (ZK), land uncertified data (certificate check), skip due forced inclusions (landing reverts, forced batch bypasses them; a due entry is voided only by expiry or by a four-hour stall of the queue, which voids the entries due at its cut, for entries a guest can prove needs stuffing at the drain rate for four hours, priced on the forced-inclusion page, and refunds the voided entries but the first, round 6, R6H-1), block a registration, or hide equivocation. Withholding is worthless; coordinated non-landing costs the ramp, paid from the reserves to whoever lands, and a window nobody lands past the deadline machinery is replaced with no penalty; an honest entrant gets its share two hours later.

2. Attester (bonded; the same registration)

AspectRule
Entry and exitAs the sequencer; every registered seat can be drawn into the per-term committee of up to 32 seats, drawn by the cycle seed as ranks among the seats in the domain at the term's start, those registered and inside [activeFrom, activeUntil), suspended ones included (read from the version of a persistent domain tree effective at the term's start; round 4, R4H-1; round 5 fix pass), excluding the primary holder by owner and the backups by seat (sequencing page §8 owns the rule); the quorum scales with the committee's actual size. A suspended owner's seats stay in the rank domain: drawn as a committee slot, such a seat is an empty slot (m shrinks by one, Q(m) scales, later slots do not move), and drawn as a holder it is a void view, so no suspension, self-inflicted or not, changes E_t, a rank or a committee after the seed is fixed (round 5, R5S-2). The committee size m, and with it the 12-key bound behind a lock, therefore falls with the suspended share s of the domain (about 32(1 − s) slots): owners that park seats in a suspension before a seed is fixed shrink every committee those seats are drawn into, but parked seats sit in no committee, which lowers the parkers' own chance of a quorum or a blocking minority; with at most a third of the seats parked, inside T11, m falls below 8 with probability about 3·10−7 per term; nodes publish m; and the seats of an owner suspended or lapsed for more than STALE_EXIT = 7 days can be exited by anyone with exitStale (round 6, R6-C1). The walk reads each drawn owner's attester key valid at termStart(t), from the key's validity interval (round 6, R6S-4).
DutiesExecute every valid block of the current view through the engine, verify the produced hash equals the signed one, attest with a BLS signature over the header hash, one attestation per (term, view, opening object, redraw count, height); sign TIMEOUT for (t, v) only when, for TIMEOUT = 5 s by its clock, it has seen no new certificate of the view and holds no block of the view that it attested and that is still uncertified (so a leader whose two real blocks it attested while a minority abstains is never timed out, while a leader gossiping bodiless or unattestable headers gets nothing attested and is; round 4, R4H-4, bounded in round 5, R5H-1 and restated on the attester's own attestations in the fix pass; the certificate page is normative), the 5 s counted from the moment it first holds the view's opening object (for view 0, at termStart(t) once it holds the closing VC of term t − 1; for a later view, or one reopened by a REPLACE or RESUME VC, when it holds the VC that opened it; for a member of a redrawn committee, when the redraw forms) and restarted on each new certificate, so a leader that publishes nothing at all is timed out 5 s after its view opened, and no view is timed out before the attester holds the object that opened it (round 6, R6H-4); sign TERM_END for (t, v) at the earlier of holding a certificate, under the same opening object, of a block stamped termEnd(t) − 1, the term's last possible block under V4, that extends its lock (h ≥ lock_i, on the lock's chain; clock-free, and never a lock lie for such a block, since every header the key attested under that object carried a certificate at or below its lock; a certificate on another branch fires nothing), and termEnd(t) + END_GRACE by its clock, whether or not any block of the view arrived (certificate page §1; after the #22188 review, 2026-10-01); sign FALLBACK for an earlier term t only once termEnd(t) + END_GRACE + VC_FALLBACK × (own term − t) has passed, the same condition L1 checks at record and landing time (round 5, R5S-5), and only while it holds no VC of committee(t) for the view, sees none recorded and sees no certificate of committee(t) recorded above the lock it would sign (round 6, R6H-4, R6S-3); sign REDRAW(t, v, vcHash, n), n ≤ REDRAW_MAX = 2, when it attested a block of the view and has seen no certificate for TIMEOUT seconds; as a member of a redrawn committee, attest the view's valid blocks it received within STALE_MAX of their timestamps; record on L1 every view change it signed within TO_REBROADCAST of its formation if no record exists yet (the FALLBACK gates rest on it; round 5 fix pass); as a member of committee(t), if no TERM_END VC of it has formed by termEnd(t) + END_GRACE + 0.5 s, record the highest certificate it holds of the term's last view with recordCertificate (about 165k gas for a term already pinned, plus the committee walk when it is not, since the call pins the term first if needed), at once if it is the member whose bit is the lowest set bit of the highest certificate's bitmap of the view and otherwise only after one L1 block has passed without a record, and in either case only if no certificate as high is already recorded (round 6, R6S-3 and the fix pass): a blocking minority of the committee can withhold TERM_END so that no VC of the committee ever supersedes a later committee's lower FALLBACK, and the recorded certificate makes L1 refuse that FALLBACK without any quorum, reaching L1 within about one L1 block, or one more if the cartel holds both first senders (the leader and the member whose bit is the lowest set bit of the highest certificate's bitmap of the view), before any landing can consume the FALLBACK; the stagger keeps the gas a withholding cartel imposes on honest members to about 165k per record for a pinned term, or that plus one committee walk (about 1 M gas at 128 seats) when the term is not yet pinned, the usual case at termEnd + 2.5 s unless a recordAssignment(t) pin preceded it, normally one or two records per withheld term; cast lock votes only at locks it holds, re-voting only upward; serve blocks_by_range for RETAIN_SECONDS (two hours): every attester that attested a block executed it (V6) and holds its bytes, and the design adds no L1-accountable publication duty, because a failure to serve is unprovable on L1 (limitation L3) and an L1 publication duty costs what the bonded-fragment path of #22188 costs, the argument posted there (after the #22188 review, 2026-10-01); RewardsATT_REWARD_PER_BLOCK = 0.1 TAIKO per landed block (6 per full term), debited from each block's holder's reserve, split equally among the end certificate's signers and credited to each signer's owner's reserve first (round 5, R5S-4); per block so range splitting earns nothing. Not signing earns nothing.
SlashingS3a attestation equivocation or a certificate pair (whole ledger, ≥ 12 keys per conflicting pair; keyed on (t, v, vcHash, redraw, height)); S3b lock lie (whole ledger; no lock is exempt, landed locks included, round 5, R5S-1: an honest TIMEOUT, TERM_END or FALLBACK vote is never below a certificate its key attested outside a reset, and after a reset only RESUME is signable); S3c non-monotone lock votes (whole ledger); S6 attesting a header whose named certificate is invalid (one seat, one strike); S3d signing a REPLACE or RESUME lock vote whose signed L1 reference did not satisfy the replacement predicate (one seat, one strike; three strikes in a week suspend; the interfaces page is normative for evidence types). Silence is never slashed, and neither is signing over data that never became available (round 2: no L1 rule can distinguish it from data that is available but unprovable; limitation L3).
All offlineNo certificates, no view changes: the leader produces at most two blocks and holds; at term end plus 12 s the next committee signs a fallback view change, which L1 records and lands only from that time, only while no VC of the term's own committee is recorded, only with a certificate at its lock (round 5, R5S-5), and never below a certificate of the term's own committee recorded for the view (round 6, R6S-3; an offline committee has none); the chain fails closed at the preconfirmation layer with only "sequenced" and "landed" levels; after 75 unlanded terms dead mode lets a bonded party land without certificates. Nobody is slashed for a committee-wide outage.
All malicious (≥ Q)Can certify data only they hold (an unslashable stall until the replacement window opens, 35 to 65 minutes; limitation L3), refuse to attest (a blank term: the leader keeps its view, nothing lands, nobody is penalized; a blocking minority can also withhold its TERM_END lock votes and so delay every handoff out of a committee it blocks by 12 to 22 s, until a later committee's FALLBACK, and can do nothing more; round 6, R6-C2), sign early timeouts (cost 0, one view), or revert a locked block (12 provable slashings at any lock at a full committee, landed locks included; round 5, R5S-1). Cannot land beyond a lock, forge a proof, or touch a term with an honest committee: as the next term's committee it can sign a FALLBACK for the honest term, but L1 refuses it while the honest committee's own VC is recorded, before termEnd + 12 s, without a certificate at its lock (round 5, R5S-5), or below the honest committee's highest certificate recorded for the view, which the honest leader and the member whose bit is the lowest set bit of the highest certificate's bitmap of the view record at termEnd + 2.5 s whenever no TERM_END VC has formed, and the other honest members one L1 block later if no record has appeared, so the refusal holds even when a blocking minority of the honest committee withholds TERM_END (round 6, R6S-3). What remains is limitation L5: a FALLBACK consumed by a landing in the same L1 block as, or before, the honest record, which needs L1 ordering against that record as well, and orphans every descendant of the FALLBACK lock until that landing, minutes of blocks, with no slashable key.

3. Lander and prover (anyone)

AspectRule
Entry and exitNone. Any address with the certified data (served by any committee member or RPC node) and access to two ZK proving systems can prove; any address can land with a proof it holds, its own or one received on the proofs topic. Proofs are fungible because the blob bytes are a pure function of the certified chain.
DutiesNone enforced. Economic pull only.
RewardsPer landed block, a Dutch ramp from 0.5 to 4 TAIKO per block, 1 TAIKO per blob for up to 15 blobs per term in landing order (round 6 fix pass: later blobs earn nothing, so the per-term exposure of 7,161 bounds every landing pattern), and 0 to 1,300 TAIKO per landing over the 30 minutes after landableFrom(t) = max(termEnd(t), lastLanded.at), the L1 time from which the term could first land, fixed in the term record by the first landing that reaches the term (round 6, R6H-2: nobody can land during an outage, so no term is charged for that time; the first landing after an outage builds on the old lastLanded, so the at most four terms it reaches pay the maximum, also in a later landing that finishes one of them, and every term it does not reach starts a fresh ramp from a later landing), the per-landing part paid to landing k of a term only if k ≤ ⌈bytes landed so far / LANDING_UNIT⌉, LANDING_UNIT = 254,000 bytes, a constant at every slot time (round 4, R4H-7; round 6, R6H-3): the sequencer chooses its block sizes and the lander its range boundaries, and since V9 holds every block's record to one blob (130,035 bytes), a greedily packed landing that is not its term's last carries more than 390,132 − 130,044 = 260,088 bytes of payload even in the three blobs (LANDING_BYTES = 390,132 bytes) one L1 block carries at 2-s slots, while at most about 12,500 bytes of a term are not counted in bytesLanded, so bytesLanded after k greedy landings exceeds (k − 1) × 254,000 (landing page §5); so one-block landings earn at most one per term, a term at the V10 cap of 5 × 254,000 = 1,270,000 bytes needs at most five landings at every slot time (two six-blob landings at 12-s slots) and each of them is paid, however its blocks pack, and the break-even fee is ≈ 100 gwei per landing at every slot time; all debited from the term holder's landing reserve (never its seat stake) and credited on the L1 ledger in the same transaction (into the lander's own reserve first if it owns seats, round 5, R5S-4); plus PIN_REWARD (a ramp to PIN_MAX = 400 TAIKO, also from landableFrom(t)) from the holder's reserve to the first party that pins the term, by recordAssignment or by the landing that settles it (round 5, R5H-2), a recordAssignment pin being paid only for a term at or below the first unlanded term, so pinning a backlog during an outage pays nothing beyond its head (round 6, R6H-2); plus 40 % of a forfeited announceLanding bond when landing the forced or replacement batch it failed to cover; plus forced-inclusion fees when landing a forced batch.
SlashingNone. A wrong or stale landing reverts; with Frame Transactions a landing in the direct-to-builder shape (a trailing status VERIFY) is invalid and free, while a public-mempool-shape landing whose parent was consumed by another landing is still valid and pays the inbox's first check, about 30k gas of frame execution plus the intrinsic cost and the blob fee, with its gate nonce burned (only losers of the same gate nonce in the same block are invalid and free; frame-transactions page; after the #22188 review, 2026-10-01); without frames it costs about 25k gas plus a blob fee.
All offlineThe sequencer lands its own blocks; if it cannot, ranges stay certified and un-voided and the ramp waits for the first returning prover, who is paid the maximum only for the terms the first landing after the outage reaches, every other term ramping from a later landing (round 6, R6H-2), so the backlog's holders self-land their terms at no debit or pay a fresh ramp. Nothing is voided or penalized while no landing happens: only a landed replacement or forced batch past the deadline machinery voids, and no rule penalizes an unlanded view.
All maliciousCan only decline or race; no lander is trusted and no proof names one.

4. Challenger (anyone)

AspectRule
EntryNone; no bond.
DutiesNone enforced; every gossiped object is self-contained evidence.
Rewards10 % of the debit, capped at 2,000 TAIKO, credited on the L1 ledger; withdrawable immediately for addresses with no seats.
SlashingNone; false accusations are impossible by construction, so no bond is needed.
All offlineAbsence penalties, settlement and rewards are applied on the landing path, so liveness needs no challenger; equivocation deterrence is delayed, not lost (7-day windows). Since locked-block safety is a deterrent, a challenger-free week is a real degradation (open question on the certificate page).
All maliciousCan only submit valid evidence or withhold it.

5. Forced-inclusion requester (anyone)

AspectRule
EntryPost one blob (or a small calldata shape, FOCIL-eligible) with the fee; see Forced inclusion.
GuaranteeInclusion in an L1-landed block within about 112 minutes of the save against any sequencer and attester behaviour, plus proving; escalation to a permissionless forced batch when sequencers ignore it; a blob entry still unconsumed after seven days (no landing of two or more blocks for a week) expires: it derives to the empty FI block, its fee goes to the includer and its bond is refunded (replacing today's owner-only queue reset). The one exception to the bound is a stall (round 6, R6H-1): once no block has consumed every due entry for four hours of anchored time, which absent a guest-completeness bug needs saturating stuffing, every entry due at the stall's cut, fiClearTip + 4 h, is voided and refunded in full, bond and fee, to the requester the entry recorded at save time, which withdraws per entry with withdrawFiRefund(id) (round 6 fix pass), except the stored head, whose bond and fee are burned as the presumed poison; an entry that became due after the cut is served with content. The protocol cannot tell a poison entry from an honest entry that sat at the head through a four-hour outage, and burns either. A voided requester re-posts, and the 112-minute bound applies to the re-post.
CostThe queue fee, doubling with backlog, plus a 0.05 ETH bond refunded on consumption, on expiry and on a stall's void, burned with the fee only for the first entry a stall voids (round 6, R6H-1); every refund is withdrawn per entry by the requester the entry stores, with withdrawFiRefund(id) against the status the consuming landing wrote into the kind byte of the entry's own word (an overwrite of a word already read, so a landing that consumes 64 entries from 64 fresh addresses writes no slot per requester; round 6 fix pass). The fee floor is set at save time: it is multiplied by 4level, where the escalation level rises by one at each stall, counted once however many landings void it, and falls by one per ESCALATION_DECAY = 7 days since the last stall, and by four more while a stall is in progress at save time. The surcharge, the part above the unescalated fee, is refunded once the entry is consumed with content, and an entry a stall voids behind the first gets its whole fee back, so an honest request posted during a stall, after one or while landing is merely slow pays no escalation unless it is a stall's first void; it must still front the surcharge at save time, and after an attack that surcharge falls only fourfold per week. The schedule this covers: an attacker can pre-post any number of unprovable entries before a stall at the unescalated price, but all of them due at the cut are voided with the first, and the only entry that can seed the next stall is one saved during this one, which pays the next stall's price, so with stalls less than a week apart the k-th costs its poster at least 0.05 + 0.001 × 4k−1 ETH however they are spaced (each stall restarts the decay clock, so a daily drip's fourteenth costs at least 0.001 × 413 ≈ 67,000 ETH); only stalls a week or more apart return to the floor price, so the long-run rate the escalation lets through is one cheap stall a week, at about 0.05 ETH and four hours of halted landing each.

6. Node operator (passive)

Runs the derivation and preconfirmation client with an L1 view. Validates every block by V1 to V6, follows certificates and view changes, labels confirmations sequenced / attested / locked / landed / final, with "landed (provisional)" for a one-leaf landing in degraded proving mode, and for any landing whose chain holds an unfinalized provisional record, until its checkpoint is written, and shows each label with its exceptions (certificate page §4; after the #22188 review, 2026-10-01), serves range requests as a duty of RPC nodes, and derives from L1 blobs when behind. Has no validity role; a bad L1 view yields pending, a bad clock only mistimes signing. Not slashable.

Bridge relayers stay outside the protocol's roles, and Etna adds no role for them. On L2 an L1→L2 message is processable once an anchored L1 block at or after the one that sent it has its hash recorded on L2 or its checkpoint revealed: a relayer reveals that checkpoint before claiming whenever no sequencer or other party has revealed it yet, or, if the optional extended proof is adopted, reveals and verifies in one transaction (anchor-free blocks, issue #22147).

7. DAO (upgrades only)

The DAO's only powers are upgrades, parameters frozen in the code (changed only by an upgrade) and the Anchor's withdraw of its legacy balance (Anchor page §7, never on a liveness path). It can upgrade the Inbox, the Anchor, both SignalServices, the Bridge, the Vaults and the quota manager (Etna needs the Anchor and the L2 SignalService upgraded once, before the fork: the Anchor's current-root guard on anchorV4, and the L2 SignalService's permissionless checkpoint reveal and pin with its syncer path closed by the same guard; anchor-free blocks, issue #22147; and it needs the compatible upgrades of the shared contracts before T0, below), rotate ZK image ids (with a two-hour overlap or an emergency zero), add a third ZK system, change constants by upgrade, and, after a proof conflict in single-proof mode, name the sound leaf by an upgrade (below). Cannot admit or remove any participant, pause landing or block production, void the forced-inclusion queue, or touch bonds. The shared contracts (after the #22188 review, 2026-10-01; working assumption A5 revised; this replaces the open question AD6, which had kept their pause and owner powers outside this design): the Bridge, both SignalServices, the ERC20, ERC721 and ERC1155 Vaults and the quota manager they call are upgraded in place before T0, on L1 by the DAO and on L2 through the DelegateController, independently of the fork, to compatible implementations that remove pause and unpause and remove or freeze every owner-only operational function (changeBridgedToken, the quota setters), with addresses and storage unchanged (Anchor page §4). The schedule this covers is a pauser key, or the DAO's own operational key short of an upgrade vote, halting bridging or remapping a bridged token: after the upgrades no such action exists, so no address other than the DAO can stop bridging, remap a token or mint a bridged token; the DAO keeps two DAO-only entry points that are not upgrades, DefaultResolver.registerAddress (the Bridge and the Vaults resolve their remote peers by name through it) and the owner branch of BridgedERC20 mint and burn (every bridged-token proxy was initialized with the vault's owner), each as slow as an upgrade because it passes the same governance; both are recorded in limitation L-OP as residual DAO operational powers (after the #22188 review, 2026-10-01; verifier X4); the price is the loss of an emergency brake (limitation L-OP), a human decision the user may reverse (Anchor page §19). If the DAO never acts again: image windows stay open-ended, single-proof mode and replacement keep certified landing moving unless every ZK system is broken, and a single-proof landing, being provisional, becomes final by the 24-h rule with no DAO action (landing page §7; after the #22188 review, 2026-10-01); certificate-free regimes (dead mode, no-committee, open-empty, forced batches) always need two systems and stall on a one-system outage until an image rotation (limitation L19), parameters freeze; a forced-inclusion entry that no guest can prove (a guest-completeness bug) stalls every landing path for at most four hours of anchored time, after which every entry due at the stall's cut is voided, and each further stall needs an entry saved during the previous one, priced at save time with the stall in progress and at an escalation level that falls by one quadrupling only per week without a stall, so the halt is bounded by about four hours per stall, stalls less than a week apart number about 1 + log4(budget / 0.001 ETH) however they are spaced, and over any period of T days a budget buys at most about T / 7 + 1 + log4(budget / 0.001 ETH) stalls, the long-run rate being one cheap stall a week (round 6, R6H-1); a DAO guest fix shortens it but is never a prerequisite. The one state that waits for the DAO is CONFLICT: a valid proof from the other ZK leaf over a provisional landing's start state and inputs with a different end state, which no rule can resolve objectively; the DAO's conflict resolution is an upgrade that names the sound leaf and rolls lastLanded and the forced-inclusion head back to the start of the oldest unfinalized record if needed, at most 24 h of landings (limitation L-PF; after the #22188 review, 2026-10-01), not an operational power. proven by construction: no function on any liveness path is owner-gated, and the only DAO action any path can wait for is an upgrade.
