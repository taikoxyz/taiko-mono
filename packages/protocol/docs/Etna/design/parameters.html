Parameters

One table for every constant the design names. Units are seconds, gwei of TAIKO, wei, gas, L1 block counts (only where L1 itself is block-denominated) or counts. R5 audit: no parameter in any block is denominated in L1 slots or epochs; the only block-denominated values are the L1-given blockhash and EIP-2935 windows (L2's own EIP-2935 window counts L2 blocks and no rule reads it; L2's EIP-4788 ring is keyed by L2 timestamp, so HISTORY_BUFFER_LENGTH is in seconds); the attester-policy anchor lag is in seconds (round 5 fix pass). Amounts in TAIKO are formulas instantiated with assumed inputs (a term's revenue ≈ 0.01 ETH, 1 TAIKO ≈ 1.5·10−4 ETH) and are open until measured. This page is normative for constants (round 4, D9): where another page states a different value, this table governs. Round 4 removed SLASH_ABANDON, LANDER_BONUS_BPS, OUTAGE_WINDOW and DEADLINE_HARD with the abandonment rule (R4H-5), renamed ABANDON_GRACE to REPLACE_GRACE, and added MIN_TENURE, REDRAW_MAX and ANNOUNCE_BOND. Round 5 removed the per-landing attester total in favour of ATT_REWARD_PER_BLOCK alone (R5S-6) and added PIN_REWARD (R5H-2); its fix pass replaced the mutation ring and the MUT_PER_SLOT cap with a persistent domain tree. Anchor-free blocks (issue #22147) removed ANCHOR_GAS_LIMIT, the golden-touch address and the three Anchor metadata slot numbers, added BEACON_ROOTS_ADDRESS and HISTORY_STORAGE_ADDRESS on L2, HISTORY_BUFFER_LENGTH and the extraData layout and kind values, and made FI_GAS_LIMIT an FI block's whole gas limit. Round 6 added LANDING_BYTES and LANDING_UNIT (R6H-3), ESCALATION_DECAY (R6H-1) and STALE_EXIT (R6-C1), cut V9's record bound (and MAX_ENVELOPE) to one blob and TERM_BYTES_MAX to 5 × LANDING_UNIT, which lowers the client throughput target to about 20 KiB/s (R6H-3), re-derived the exposure, LAND_RESERVE and ReserveLow once, per seat (R6S-2, R6H-2, R6H-3), and redefined FI_SKIP and SKIP_ESCALATION over the stall clock, the escalation now decaying and refunded to every entry but a stall's first void (R6H-1). The critique revision (after the #22188 review, 2026-10-01) restated END_GRACE as the clock fallback of a TERM_END signal that is normally clock-free, and added LAND_CALLDATA_MAX, the frame gate's calldata bound, and DEGRADED_FINALITY, the provisional window of single-proof landings. The round-6 fix pass capped R_BLOB at 15 paid blobs per term, so the exposure bounds every landing pattern, made the escalation refund exact (the surcharge FI_FEE × (4e − 1), the bounty undiluted) and moved requester refunds to per-entry withdrawals, so a landing writes no requester slot.

3. What must be measured before freezing

  • A term's revenue (base-fee share, tips, MEV) on mainnet for a week: sets B_SEAT, MISS_PENALTY, ANNOUNCE_BOND, the reward band, LAND_RESERVE and the attester share (open items G8, G10).
  • Mainnet gas of the RISC0 and SP1 leaves; the EIP-2537 aggregate path for 32 keys (≈ 165k gas per certificate or view-change verification assumed, pairing plus key aggregation and calldata); the registry walk (the round-6 persistent-tree simulation measured about 5.4 M gas at 4,096 seats and 10.1 M at 65,535, so the round-5 estimates were 11 to 26 % low; the figures above now use these simulated values, still assumed until measured); the forced-inclusion queue walk.
  • Gossip and execution latency on a 32-attester devnet: sets TIMEOUT, STALE_MAX, CERT_LAG_MAX and validates the 1-s cadence.
  • Guest cost of 64 KZG versioned hashes and of a 4,096-header L1 chain.
  • Gas of revealCheckpoint on L2 (about 100k assumed, the issue #22147 review's figure).
  • Blob-fetch latency under sparse blob pools: sets FI_DELAY.
  • The calldata of a maximal landing (proof, certificate, committee proofs and segment descriptors): sizes LAND_CALLDATA_MAX, assumed 8,192 bytes (after the #22188 review, 2026-10-01).
