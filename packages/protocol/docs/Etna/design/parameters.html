Parameters

One table for every constant the design names. Units are seconds, gwei of TAIKO, wei, gas, L1 block counts (only where L1 itself is block-denominated) or counts. R5 audit: no parameter in any block is denominated in L1 slots or epochs; the three block-denominated values are the L1-given blockhash and EIP-2935 windows and the attester-policy anchor lag, whose wall-time range is stated. Amounts in TAIKO are formulas instantiated with assumed inputs (a term's revenue ≈ 0.01 ETH, 1 TAIKO ≈ 1.5·10−4 ETH) and are open until measured. This page is normative for constants (round 4, D9): where another page states a different value, this table governs. Round 4 removed SLASH_ABANDON, LANDER_BONUS_BPS, OUTAGE_WINDOW and DEADLINE_HARD with the abandonment rule (R4H-5), renamed ABANDON_GRACE to REPLACE_GRACE, and added MIN_TENURE, REDRAW_MAX and ANNOUNCE_BOND. Round 5 removed the per-landing attester total in favour of ATT_REWARD_PER_BLOCK alone (R5S-6) and added PIN_REWARD and MUT_PER_SLOT (R5H-2).

1. Dependency chain

DELAY_S  = LOOKBACK + 2·TERM                              = 1,320 s   (dates a class A or B suspension, which changes no rank and no E_t: a suspended seat drawn as a holder is a void view, drawn as a committee slot an empty slot; an owner may trigger it against itself and gains nothing, since no draw moves; round 5, R5S-2)
DELAY_REG > CYCLE + LOOKBACK − TERM                       = 7,200 s   (no grinding when the seed is fresh: every input that changes E_t or a rank, entry and exit, the only ones, is DELAY_REG-dated; reactivation and a reserve lapse are DELAY_REG-dated too but act through the suspension flag, and no suspension changes E_t or a rank; round 4 R4-C2, round 5 R5S-2)
MIN_TENURE = 604,800 s (7 d) ≤ activeUntil − activeFrom   (a seat is eligible, drawn and bled for at least a week; bounds the mutation ring: an owner with CAP seats produces ≤ 16 entry or exit mutations per week, each exited seat one mutation, and suspensions produce none; round 4 R4H-1, round 5 R5-C4)
MUT_PER_SLOT = 2 per 60-s effective slot                    (a registry write whose slot is full is queued to the next slot and its DELAY grows accordingly; M ≤ 2 × ROLE_HORIZON / 60 = 4,096 in the absolute worst case, which needs about 4,096 seat entries or exits inside one 34-h window (MIN_TENURE forbids both for one seat), about 4,096 × 30,200 ≈ 124 M TAIKO of seats held for at least a week; the stalest pin then costs about 0.6 M + 4,096 × 2.1k ≈ 9.2 M gas, inside the 16.8 M EIP-7825 per-transaction cap as a standalone recordAssignment; round 5, R5H-2, and the fix pass: at 8 per slot the bound was 4,096 and about 35 M gas, which no transaction could carry)
ROLE_HORIZON = COMMITTEE_RING·TERM                        = 122,880 s (34 h: the landing horizon; roles pinned for every settled term; mutations effective inside it are the correction set M, gathered once per pin)
SUSPEND(3) ≥ ROLE_HORIZON + DELAY_S                       = 129,600 s (the two-interval store never drops a live interval; a reserve lapse uses the same store and the same minimum)
RECYCLE_GRACE ≥ ROLE_HORIZON + DELAY_S                    = 129,600 s (a recycled seat never changes a decided term)
DEAD_TERMS·TERM > LAND_WINDOW_MAX + REPLACE_GRACE         = 4,500 s   (a slow but live chain never enters dead mode; 75 terms)
RETAIN_SECONDS ≥ LAND_WINDOW_MAX + REPLACE_GRACE + slack  = 7,200 s   (attesters serve data until any landing is possible)
ROTATION_OVERLAP ≥ LAND_WINDOW_MAX + REPLACE_GRACE + prove= 7,200 s   (an image rotation never orphans an in-window proof)
ANCHOR_MAX_AGE + TERM + LAND_WINDOW_MAX + REPLACE_GRACE   = 5,760 s   < EIP-2935 window at 2-s slots (16,382 s)
FI_EXPIRY + ANCHOR_MAX_AGE + LAND_WINDOW_MAX + REPLACE_GRACE ≈ 7 d 95 min < blob retention (≈ 18 d; ≥ 9 d assumed)
60·R_BLK_MAX + 15·R_BLOB + 3·R_LAND_MAX + 60·ATT_REWARD_PER_BLOCK + PIN_MAX = 240 + 15 + 3,900 + 6 + 400 = 4,561 TAIKO   (per-term reward exposure at the maximum ramp; V10 caps a term's sequencer bytes at 15 blobs, three paid landings; FI blocks exempt, round 4 R4H-6; PIN_MAX added in round 5, R5H-2, and sized in the fix pass)
LAND_RESERVE = 9,200 per seat ≥ 2 × 4,561 = 9,122; ≤ B_SEAT / 2 = 10,000   (two terms of exposure per seat; CAP seats hold 73,600 ≈ 16 terms against ≈ 7 expected inside one LAND_WINDOW_MAX at 8 of 72 seats, P(> 16) ≈ 0.02 %; the happy path moves only the attester share, offset in expectation, since a self-landing skips the lander's debit and credit and reward credits to an owner with seats refill its reserve first; a single seat survives one maximum-ramp third-party landing; round 4 R4-C4, round 5 R5S-4)
ReserveLow below 2 × 4,561 = 9,122, once per crossing; re-armed at the required level (seats × 9,200); lapse below 4,561   (hysteresis so the event carries information; the lapse is DELAY_REG-dated; round 5, R5S-4)
Registration bond ≥ 1.05 × seats × B_SEAT + seats × LAND_RESERVE   (the 5 % buffer, 1,000 TAIKO per seat = 20 MISS debits, absorbs liveness debits before seat stake; the reserve is ring-fenced from MISS and non-withdrawable while a seat is active; round 4, R4H-3)
Q(m) = ⌊2m/3⌋ + 1; 2Q(m) − m                              (provable double-signers behind a locked block: 12 at m = 32, 6 at m = 16, 4 at m = 8; m counts the committee's non-empty slots, a suspended seat's slot being empty; round 5, R5S-2)
m − Q(m) + 1 abstainers blank a term                      (11 at m = 32; a redraw vote needs ⌊m/2⌋ + 1 = 17 attesters that attested and saw no certificate, REDRAW_MAX = 2; round 4, R4-C1)
FALLBACK VC gate: block.timestamp ≥ termEnd(t) + END_GRACE + VC_FALLBACK × (signerTerm − t)   (the L1 gate on recording or landing a FALLBACK VC signed by committee(signerTerm); committee(t)'s own TIMEOUT or TERM_END VC at a higher lock supersedes it until consumed; round 5, R5S-5)
K_MIN_CERT = 8 ≤ committee size, else no-committee mode   (4 provable double-signers at m = 8; below that a certificate says little)
Launch condition: ≥ 8 owners, ≥ 72 seats in the domain at arm time   (a heuristic over a Sybil-able, revocable count that no rule enforces or protects, chosen so that a small cartel blanks few terms at launch; read by the DAO before armEtna(T0); launch may begin in no-committee mode if it fails; 44 = K + CAP + V_MAX is the bare full-committee minimum with no slack; round 4, R4-C5)
DEGRADE_AFTER = 7,200 s < ROLE_HORIZON                    (single-proof mode arms long before the horizon; certified landings only, L19)

3. What must be measured before freezing

  • A term's revenue (base-fee share, tips, MEV) on mainnet for a week: sets B_SEAT, MISS_PENALTY, ANNOUNCE_BOND, the reward band, LAND_RESERVE and the attester share (open items G8, G10).
  • Mainnet gas of the RISC0 and SP1 leaves; the EIP-2537 aggregate path for 32 keys; the registry walk; the forced-inclusion queue walk.
  • Gossip and execution latency on a 32-attester devnet: sets TIMEOUT, STALE_MAX, CERT_LAG_MAX and validates the 1-s cadence.
  • Guest cost of 64 KZG versioned hashes and of a 4,096-header L1 chain.
  • Blob-fetch latency under sparse blob pools: sets FI_DELAY.
