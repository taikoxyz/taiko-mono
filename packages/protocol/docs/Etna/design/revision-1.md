# Historical revision after round01 — superseded

This is a historical coordination record, not a current specification. Later rounds replace several rules below, including early-fragment duties, deadlines, fee allocation and canonical admission pricing. Use the [current HTML reference](index.html) and [architecture navigation map](architecture.md); immutable iteration reports retain the review history. The term revision here names a research draft, not a deployed upgrade.

1. **Nonexclusive L1 DA staging is mandatory for canonical eligibility.** A new permissionless DARegistry records actual current-transaction BLOBHASH (one blob) or keccak of full bounded calldata. Stage records identify full manifest/body commitment and all execution-affecting context. No stage updates/reserves the Inbox head, grants sequencing priority, assigns a worker or publishes a checkpoint. Canonical `submitSegment` still republishes full matching blob/body plus valid proof in one atomic action. This adds a prior generic DA transaction and duplicate publication cost, explicitly disclosed; it is not a pre-proposed unproved canonical segment. Literal R7 permits earlier public copies; no claim if user separately requires first-ever DA only with proof.
2. **Timing:** public-data age W=360 seconds; origin maximum age900 seconds; staging record maximum age900 seconds at acceptance; unsafe horizon900 L2 blocks. Proof target180s; healthy inclusion assumption120s; retrieval/notice budget60s is explicit. W supplies time for a funded peer to retrieve and prepare the same branch's proof, not a promise of inclusion before the transaction becomes eligible. Typical landing7–8min, illustrative worst formation60+stage inclusion120+age360+final inclusion120=660s plus header acquisition; not guaranteed under arbitrary faults. All timing uses seconds. DA retention must cover900s plus retrieval; calldata/archive fallback and restaging when necessary.
3. **Origin reuse:** nondecreasing number/timestamp; equal number requires exact same header. No segment requires a fresh L1 header merely because byte/gas cap seals early.900s freshness forces eventual snapshot advance; FIFO bound becomes t_enqueue+1020+(1+ceil((p+1)/4))*Delta. Snapshots remain exact queue tail at origin, parent-frozen work unchanged.
4. **Canonical owner assent:** add `address producer`, `bytes32 blockHashesRoot`, `uint256 stageId` to SegmentStatement and immutable AcceptedReceipt as needed. EIP-712 ProducerAuthorization binds exact statement hash (which excludes signature), stageId and domain; a recovered EOA producer or registered immutable signing key must authorize it. Contract owners can register such keys with objective on-chain self-authorization. Anyone may relay/pay, and producer need not be beneficiary. No generic EIP1271 result is re-evaluated during slashing. The execution proof binds ordered per-block hashes/root and every statement field. Receipt is evidence of checked owner authorization, not merely a named recipient.
5. **Same-owner canonical conflict:** a bonded publisher DutyClaim X versus an authorized accepted block Y at same revision/base accepted head/L2 number and different block hash is objective evidence. Prove Y's membership using bounded fixed-depth Merkle proof against accepted blockHashesRoot. Slash X's reserved position only once, existing10%reporter/90%sink split. No slash of an honest loser merely because someone ELSE won. Cross-address economic identity cannot be inferred. Candidate body for descendants of unaccepted segments must define baseAcceptedHead and membership context exactly; avoid equating stale historical head with immediate parent incorrectly.
6. **Publication-by covenant:** a sealed segment's known DA key/context may be signed using a separate reserved position (new claim kind), with issuedAt constrained to its preexisting bucket window and publishBy=issuedAt+240. A matching actual staging record by that time discharges the covenant, regardless of who stages. A later stage cannot erase default. Permissionless signature+record/absence evidence; slash reserved position10%reporter90%sink. Do not pretend an early block-fragment keccak equals a later blob versioned hash. Early1s DutyClaims retain bounded availability challenges; the seal claim is issued when exact complete stage key is known. Any participating producer can incur objective liability by these duties; malformed ordinary submissions pay gas. Do not claim every private computation is punishable: mature public competing branches may still win; R6 review must distinguish this from first-reveal-at-landing.
7. **Small-entry exposure:** position reserve10 TAIKO, bucket60 positions=600 TAIKO; issuance60s; retention1200s; evidence3600s; challenge response600s. Release later of evidence close and admitted response deadline. One claim/sec locks roughly36,000 TAIKO plus one60-position bucket; four fragments/sec144,000 plus bucket, with precise worst windows. This replaces60.48m; it is a bounded service penalty, not insurance against unlimited MEV. More stake buys no canonical exclusivity or admission cap. Unbonded canonical producer/stager remains permitted (producer authorization is mandatory); contract identity key registration is objective and no whitelist. ETH denomination for staging/gas, TAIKO services unchanged.
8. **Anti-censorship resource price:** force fee remains0.001ETH*(1+floor(pending/50));50% credited to proof-bound beneficiary,50% irrevocably unwithdrawable ETH sink ledger. Same-owner enqueue/process cannot recover sink. At empty queue, filling1000 requests without intermediate consumption costs10.5ETH total fee,5.25ETH net sink plus gas/proof/DA. FIFO still gives finite predecessor bound, not uniform queue latency. Discuss linear service collateral and irreversible queue price as limited economic deterrence, not identity diversity or guaranteed fair race outcomes. Delayed discovery after1h evidence horizon explicitly accepted limitation for bounded soft services, never custody safety.
9. **Proof jobs:** accept only with exact live staging receipt/context known; deadline=max(acceptedAt+300, stage.publishedAt+360+120). Require origin.ts+900>=deadline+120, and stage.publishedAt+900>=deadline+120. Offers expire600s. Receipt historic cancellation/default semantics unchanged. No exclusive proving right.
10. **Surplus:** total ledger liabilities<=held balance for each asset. Unsolicited donations are uncredited surplus, cannot halt withdrawals or grant a claim; exact internal ledger conservation is separate. No owner sweep.

New HTML staging page is normative for exact staging content/key/record, Merkle block-hash encoding, producer authorization, reorgs/expiry and public-data lead. ## Pipeline commitment clarification

Head.segmentCommitment=contextHash/taskHash, not full statementHash. Receipts separately store full statementHash and authorization/payment fields. Execution-identical changes of beneficiary, job, producer or stageSalt cannot alter the precomputed parent head and invalidate all descendants. The execution-only context is known at sealing, before L1 stage/job registration. This is normative across the pages.
