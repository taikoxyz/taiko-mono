Liveness and safety arguments

This page assembles the per-block arguments into two end-to-end sketches. Each step names the page that carries it and the assumption it consumes (T1 to T13 in 01-threat-model.md). Nothing here is a formal proof; the red-team rounds in iterations/ attack exactly these chains.

1. The properties

PropertyStatementLevel
P1 state safetyNo L1 checkpoint is ever written for a state root that is not the result of executing the certified chain from the previous checkpoint.landed
P2 preconf safetyA locked block is reverted only by ≥ 2Q(m) − m provably slashable attesters (12 at a full committee; m counts the non-empty slots, so the count falls with the suspended share of the domain, round 6, R6-C1), by a landed replacement or forced batch after the deadline machinery, by an L1 reorg, or, after a landing accepted with one leaf in single-proof mode, a DAO resolution of a proof conflict that voids that provisional landing and every block built on it (landing page §7; after the #22188 review, 2026-10-01). Round 4 restated the design property-first. The five invariants the rules are derived from, each owned by the page that states it: I1 sortition is a pure function of state at S(t): the holders and committee the inbox computes at landing time equal what nodes computed at termStart(t), whatever registry mutations became effective in between (Sequencing); I2 a drawn term's lander reward exists at landing time whatever the owner does, short of a class-A slash of itself: no other owner action and no liveness event removes the funds, the reserve is drained only by reward debits to other owners' landers, attesters and pinners, every such payment to an owner with seats refills that owner's reserve first, a self-landing moves only the attester share, and the reserve holds two terms per seat (14,400 against 2 × 7,161, five paid landings of LANDING_UNIT = 254,000 bytes counted at every slot time, round 6, R6H-3) of the worst-case drain (round 5, R5S-4; Landing §5). Two round-6 rules make the sizing hold against the owner's and the outage's schedule: the guarantee is per seat and read at every draw, so an owner that directs its own terms' rewards to a seatless address before a draw, the one owner-triggerable drain, still moves its own money but lapses 22 minutes after its reserve crosses seats × 7,161, and every draw it keeps is funded by about one term per seat (R6S-2); and the ramps run from landability, landableFrom(t) = max(termEnd(t), lastLanded.at), because nobody can land during an outage, so an outage of any length charges the maximum only to the terms the first landing after it reaches, not to the backlog, and an owner at its required level keeps its draws through it from four seats up, while one with three seats or fewer can still lapse if that landing reaches more of its terms than it has seats (R6H-2). The residual is the terms drawn between a crossing and its effect, plus those still unlanded at it, which the holder lands at gas cost or which are replaced with no penalty (limitation L20); I3 no minority takes a view from a producing leader: a view changes hands only when the leader is provably silent to the whole committee or the term ends, and an abstaining minority can at most withhold certification (Certificates); I4 nothing voids a locked block except a landed replacement after the deadline machinery or 2Q(m) − m slashings, or, after a landing accepted with one leaf in single-proof mode, a DAO resolution of a proof conflict that voids that provisional landing and every block built on it (landing page §7; after the #22188 review, 2026-10-01), at any lock and against any committee: S3b exempts no lock and a later committee's FALLBACK is gated by L1 time, first record and a certificate at its lock (round 5, R5S-1, R5S-5; Landing §6) and refused below a recorded certificate of committee(t) (round 6, R6S-3); I5 no accusation object exists, and no rule infers fault from the absence of a landing (Slashing §3).

2. Safety argument (P1, P2)

  1. A landing writes a checkpoint only after the verifier accepts a proof over a journal whose parent root and parent hash are read from inbox storage, whose blob hashes are read from blobhash, whose anchor tip is checked against blockhash or EIP-2935, whose consumed queue entries and forced-inclusion stall clock are read from storage, and whose claim that no further entry was due (fiNotDueAt, the latest anchor time at which the range relied on none at its new head) is checked by inequality against the stored save time of the entry there, which an entry saved after the proof always satisfies, so no third party can make the landing revert by saving an entry (round 6, R6H-1), and whose end header hash is bound by an aggregate certificate verified on L1 (Landing L3, L5, L6, L7, L8, L11). proven
  2. The guest proves execution from that parent to that end, that every header's signature recovers to the term's leader, that every anchored L1 header hashes to the parentBeaconBlockRoot of each block that anchors it and chains to the tip (so every L1 block hash an L2 header records, and every checkpoint revealed from it on L2, belongs to an L1 block canonical at landing; anchor-free blocks, issue #22147), and that forced-inclusion blocks are exactly the consumed entries. So a false root needs an unsound proof (T3) accepted by ZK_K = 2 distinct systems, or one system in single-proof mode plus a colluding quorum that certified a header committing to the false root (T11). A single-proof landing is provisional (after the #22188 review, 2026-10-01): its checkpoint is written only after a proof of the same journal from a different leaf or DEGRADED_FINALITY = 24 h without a conflicting proof, so the false root also needs that no honest prover of the other leaf contradicts it within a day; when the other leaf is unusable for that long, the premise of the mode, the window adds delay, not evidence, and the mode's safety rests on T11 as before. TEE never counts (A3). assumed T3 and T11; the common-mode guest bug is the stated residual.
  3. What lands is what was certified (V7): the end certificate binds the end header hash, the guest chains signed headers from the last landed block, and view changes pin where views end; a block without a certificate on itself or a descendant cannot be landed. proven (Certificates §6).
  4. A locked block cannot be reverted cheaply. A certified block that carries C(h) has Q signers who verified C(h); a view change with a lock below h has Q signers; the intersection is ≥ 2Q − K = 12 when both are signed by the committee of one redraw count, each of whom signed a lock below a certificate they had verified, which is the objective lock-lie offence S3b (scoped to the opening object, at any redraw count, with no lock exempt: round 3 had exempted every lock that was a landed block, which let a Q cartel void locked blocks with a timeout at a lock its own lander had just landed, and round 5 deleted the exemption, R5S-1). The exemption is not needed for any honest signer: an attester's lock is at least the carried certificate it verified at V3 and its lock votes are monotone, so no honest TIMEOUT, TERM_END or FALLBACK vote sits below a certificate the same key attested under the same opening object, and after a landed replacement or forced batch the RESET state lets it sign only RESUME, which S3b never covers. Cost ≥ 12 × B_SEAT = 240,000 TAIKO at a full committee, (2Q(m) − m) × B_SEAT for a committee of m non-empty slots, plus, for a certificate pair, the leader; m falls with the suspended share of the domain, because a suspended owner's drawn seats are empty slots, and nodes publish it (round 6, R6-C1). Across a redraw the two committees differ and the intersection can be empty: reverting a locked block then needs ⌊m/2⌋ + 1 REDRAW signers of the certifying committee plus Q of the redrawn one, slashable only for keys in both, with probability about 6·10−7 per view at one third of the seats (certificate page §14; limitations L16). proven within one redraw count, given the certificate formats; assumed T11 across a redraw.
  5. Handoff ambush (W1) needs the same 12 double-signers: the next leader's first block carries the recorded term-end view change; landing withheld blocks above its lock needs a second view change for the same view. The honest view change is recorded on L1 within seconds, long before any landing of withheld blocks can be proven. The only other signer set that can close the view is a later committee with a FALLBACK VC, and that set is gated: L1 refuses the record and the landing before termEnd(t) + END_GRACE + VC_FALLBACK × (signerTerm − t), while any VC of committee(t) is recorded for the view, and unless a certificate exists at the FALLBACK lock (so no uncertified private block lands through it, V7 across committees); committee(t)'s own TERM_END VC at a higher lock supersedes a recorded FALLBACK until a landing consumes it (round 5, R5S-5). That supersession needs Q of committee(t), and a blocking minority of committee(t) can withhold TERM_END so that no committee(t) VC ever forms; the schedule is closed by a gate that needs no quorum (round 6, R6S-3): anyone may record a certificate of committee(t) for the view on L1 (recordCertificate), L1 refuses a FALLBACK whose lock is below the highest one recorded, a FALLBACK recorded before a higher certificate is dead, and when no TERM_END VC has formed by termEnd(t) + END_GRACE + 0.5 s, committee(t)'s last leader and the member whose bit is the lowest set bit of the highest certificate's bitmap of the view must record that certificate at once and every other member one L1 block later if no record has appeared. The certificate exists before term end, so the record reaches L1 within about one L1 block, or one more if a cartel holds both first senders, before any landing can consume a FALLBACK, which needs a proof of blocks built on it; the stagger holds the gas a withholding cartel imposes on honest members to about 165k per record for a term already pinned, plus the committee walk (about 1 M gas at 128 seats, 5.4 M at 4,096) when it is not, since recordCertificate pins the term first if needed, normally one or two records per withheld term (limitation L2); the gate does not rest on a lower FALLBACK lock being an objective lie, since committee(t + k) keys attested nothing under (t, v). What remains is a FALLBACK consumed by a landing in the same L1 block as, or before, the certificate record, needing Q of a later committee, a next leader that builds on the FALLBACK lock and L1 ordering against the record (T1); it orphans every descendant of the FALLBACK lock until that landing, minutes of blocks, with no slashable key: limitation L5. proven inside one committee; assumed T11 and T1 for the cross-committee residual.
  6. Replacement voids but never falsifies: a replacement landing starts from lastLanded with a certified REPLACE view and a valid proof, so P1 holds through it; P2 is weakened only for blocks that were certified but unlanded past the deadline machinery, which is announced by an L1 predicate every node evaluates identically. A recorded REPLACE VC voids nothing by itself: it is accepted only with lockHeight == lastLanded.height, lockPhHash == lastLanded.phHash and block.timestamp > replaceableFrom, and until the replacement or a forced batch lands, landing the original stays valid and whichever lands first wins (round 4, R4H-5, R4-C3). proven from the landing rules.
  7. Randomness and rights cannot be bent into safety violations: a biased seed changes who sequences, never what is valid; a holder under a wrong assignment cannot land (rights are checked at landing from the same function nodes use).

3. Liveness argument (P3, P4)

  1. Block production. While one honest eligible seat exists, the hash walk finds it as primary or backup with probability approaching 1 over a few terms (open-empty terms admit any bonded address; a term is open-empty when its domain is empty or every holder view it draws is void, so a term whose drawn owners are all suspended or lapsed is open to any bonded address too, round 6, R6H-2). A view whose leader is silent to the whole committee (no new certificate for TIMEOUT = 5 s while no committee member holds an attested block of the view that is still uncertified; round 5, R5H-1: header gossip without an attestable payload is silence; the 5 s run from the moment each attester holds the view's opening object, so a leader that publishes nothing at all is silent too, round 6, R6H-4) is closed by Q = 22 of 32 committee signatures (T9: gossip reaches honest nodes within about a second; T11: fewer than m − Q(m) + 1 = 11 of 32 abstainers in the committee and its redraws). assumed T9, T11.
  2. Certification. With ≥ Q(m) of the term's m committee seats honest and online, every valid block is certified within about 0.7 s and every honest view change forms within one extra round trip. If fewer are online, no block is certified: the chain degrades to "sequenced" only and a later committee's fallback closes the term (any depth). An abstaining minority of ≥ m − Q(m) + 1 makes the term blank: the leader keeps its view (the honest attesters attested its two real blocks, which stay uncertified, and an attester holding such a block never signs a timeout; round 5, R5H-1), nothing of it lands and nobody is penalized; attesters that attested but saw no certificate for TIMEOUT seconds may vote a committee redraw for the same leader and view (⌊m/2⌋ + 1 signatures, at most REDRAW_MAX = 2 redraws), so blanking needs a blocking minority in the first draw and in every redraw (round 4, R4H-4, R4-C1; invariant I3); by withholding its TERM_END lock votes too, such a minority delays every handoff out of a committee it blocks by 12 to 22 s, since the next leader then waits for a FALLBACK of committee(t + 1) from termEnd(t) + 12 s, or of committee(t + 2) from + 22 s (round 6, R6-C2); if the committee walk finds fewer than 8 non-empty seats (a suspended owner's drawn seat is an empty slot, round 6, R6-C1), no-committee mode makes landings certificate-free. At term end the grid continues (R4; after the #22188 review, 2026-10-01): an attester signs TERM_END as soon as it holds the certificate of a block stamped termEnd − 1 that extends its lock, the term's last possible block under V4, so the view change forms at about termEnd and the next leader's first block, stamped termEnd, follows about 0.5 s later; only a handoff whose last block is missing or uncertified waits for the clock fallback at termEnd + END_GRACE = 2 s and empties at most four grid slots, once per term, whatever the outgoing leader withholds; the 1-s grid is a rewarded duty, not a validity rule. The quorum scales with the committee size, so a small honest registry certifies normally with a published, weaker collusion bound.
  3. Landing. Any certified prefix is landable by anyone holding the data, which ≥ Q attesters hold and serve for two hours; blob bytes are deterministic so proofs are fungible; the lander reward ramps to a level that exceeds proving and landing cost in a 100-gwei hour assumed, at every slot time, because the per-landing reward is paid per LANDING_UNIT = 254,000 bytes and, since V9 holds every block's record to one blob, a greedy landing that is not the term's last carries more than 260,088 bytes of payload even in the three blobs of a 2-s-slot L1 block while at most about 12,500 bytes of a term go uncounted, so bytesLanded after k greedy landings exceeds (k − 1) × 254,000 and a lander is paid for each of the at most five landings a term at the V10 cap needs, however its blocks pack (landing page §5; round 6, R6H-3), and from landability, so after an outage only the terms the first landing reaches are charged the maximum (round 6, R6H-2). Two distinct ZK systems are needed in normal mode; if none lands for two hours, one leaf lands a certified range provisionally: lastLanded advances, rewards are paid and every deadline runs, but the L1 checkpoint is written only after a second-leaf proof of the same journal or DEGRADED_FINALITY = 24 h without a conflicting proof, and a conflict halts landing until a DAO upgrade names the sound leaf (after the #22188 review, 2026-10-01). Landing needs an L1 builder to include a blob transaction (T1); FOCIL does not protect blobs, so a total builder boycott stalls landing but voids nothing.
  4. Nothing voids by clock within the landing horizon. A proving or L1 outage shorter than the landing horizon (the smaller of the 34-hour role horizon and the EIP-2935 window: 27 h at 12-s slots, 4.55 h at 2-s slots; the role horizon, for which roles stay computable on L1, is a different quantity) leaves every certified block certified and penalizes nobody (there is no abandonment rule: S4a was removed in round 4, R4H-5 and R4-C3); the first landing after the outage clears the backlog, and it is the only landing the outage makes expensive: the ramps run from landableFrom(t) = max(termEnd(t), lastLanded.at), and since nobody can land during the outage, the at most four terms the first range after it reaches are charged the maximum ramp, each term's ramp start being fixed by the first landing that reaches it, while every other term ramps from a later landing (round 6, R6H-2); a reserve lapse needs a reserve below one term per seat (round 6, R6S-2), so an owner at its required level keeps its draws from four seats up, while one with three seats or fewer can still lapse if that first range reaches more of its terms than it has seats. Replacement needs a landed proof, so an outage alone cannot trigger it: a REPLACE VC is recordable only at lastLanded after replaceableFrom, the record has no closing effect, and landing the original stays valid until a replacement or forced batch lands. Beyond the landing horizon, unlanded ranges are lost without a slash (limitations L14); and once no block has consumed every due forced-inclusion entry for four hours of anchored time (FI_SKIP, measured on the stall clock fiClearTip), every entry due at the cut, fiClearTip + FI_SKIP, is voided, refunded except the stored head, whose bond and fee are burned, while an entry that became due after the cut is served with content (round 6, R6H-1). A chain whose blocks keep consuming the due entries keeps resetting that clock, so a proving or L1 outage during which L2 blocks are still produced voids no entry. These are the design's two clock rules. proven within the landing horizon.
  5. Recovery floor. If every registered owner and every committee is dark, after 75 unlanded terms dead mode lets any address with B_SEAT of bond sequence from the last landed block and land without a certificate; the first L1 landing wins. Recovery needs one honest bonded party and two working ZK systems, no DAO (certificate-free landings never degrade to one system, limitation L19). The first block of a dead-mode view, like the first block of a view opened by a REPLACE or RESUME view change, may be a sequencer block although a forced-inclusion entry is due, once per opening, so a chain reopened from lastLanded can advance its anchor before the obligation binds (an honest entry waits at most one block per opening). An entry that no guest can prove therefore halts landing only until its stall voids it, four hours of anchored time (round 6, R6H-1): before the trigger the attacker can pre-post any number of entries, but all those due at the cut are voided together, 64 per landing, and the count such a landing must meet is taken no later than the cut, so the waived block plus the void set's empty FI blocks always meets it. That ordinary landing is the dependable path: a forced batch ends a stall only if its anchor lies between the cut and the due time of the first unprovable entry not yet due at the cut, which it would have to serve with content, a window an attacker can shrink to one L1 block or none. Each further stall needs an entry saved during the previous one, which the inbox prices with the stall in progress and at an escalation level that falls by one quadrupling only per week without a stall, so with stalls less than a week apart the k-th costs at least 0.05 + 0.001 × 4k−1 ETH however they are spaced, and over any period of T days a budget buys at most about T / 7 + 1 + log4(budget / 0.001 ETH) stalls of about four hours each, with no DAO action; the long-run rate the escalation lets through is one cheap stall a week. proven
  6. Bridging resumes with the first landing in normal mode, since the checkpoint is written in the same transaction; in degraded single-proof mode the checkpoint of a provisional landing waits for a second-leaf confirmation or DEGRADED_FINALITY = 24 h, so L2→L1 withdrawals and every message that needs an L1 checkpoint wait up to a day (after the #22188 review, 2026-10-01); L1→L2 messages resume with the first block whose anchor is at most 30 minutes stale (ANCHOR_MAX_AGE): L2's EIP-4788 contract records that block's anchor hash (an L1 execution block hash), and anyone, the sequencer by client policy or a relayer before claiming, reveals the checkpoint from it, so no single party's silence stops L1→L2 bridging while blocks are produced (anchor-free blocks, issue #22147).

4. Accountability argument (P6)

Every harmful deviation falls into one of three bins. Impossible: finalizing a false root, landing uncertified data, landing beyond a lock, excluding a registrant, pausing the chain, and, since the shared contracts' pause and owner-only operational powers are removed by compatible in-place upgrades, stopping bridging, remapping a bridged token or minting one by any address other than the DAO (A5, revised after the #22188 review, 2026-10-01: R1 holds for the custody contracts at the cost of an emergency brake, limitation L-OP; the DAO keeps two DAO-only entry points that are not upgrades, DefaultResolver.registerAddress (the Bridge and the Vaults resolve their remote peers by name through it) and the owner branch of BridgedERC20 mint and burn (every bridged-token proxy was initialized with the vault's owner), each as slow as an upgrade because it passes the same governance; both are recorded in limitation L-OP as residual DAO operational powers (after the #22188 review, 2026-10-01; verifier X4)). Worthless: withholding (unextendable, unlandable, times out in 5 s), squatting a view with bodiless or payload-less headers (timed out at 5 s and bled like a dead leader, since such headers get nothing attested and only an attested block that stays uncertified stops a timeout; round 5, R5H-1; a holder backed by a blocking minority of the committee keeps its view without MISS, a term that minority could blank anyway, certificate page §1(d)), producing unprovable content (replaced after the deadline machinery, no penalty), posting a forced-inclusion entry that no guest can prove (it halts landing only until a four-hour stall voids it with every entry due at the stall's cut, its bond and fee are burned, and the next stall needs an entry saved during this one, at four times the previous seed's fee, less one quadrupling per full week between the stalls; round 6, R6H-1), refusing to attest (loses income; a minority below Q can blank a term and, withholding TERM_END too, delay the handoff out of it by 12 to 22 s, round 6, R6-C2, but never take it, bleed its leader or stall a term with an honest majority), not landing a certified view (anyone lands it, paid from the holder's reserve). Slashable: every signature pair over conflicting messages, every signed object naming an invalid preimage, a signed L1 reference that is false (S3d), a registered key that acquired code. No rule accepts an unsigned claim and no rule infers fault from the absence of a landing (invariant I5), so no honest party can be slashed by accusation; silence is never slashed because a colluding committee could manufacture it. proven (Slashing §3).

5. Locality argument (P7)

Rights, committees and seeds are functions of L1 data at least 20 minutes old (LOOKBACK) and of registry writes future-dated by at least 22 minutes, so every node below finality computes the same answers as the inbox does at landing: the inbox evaluates a term as ranks among the seats in the domain at termStart(t), those registered and inside [activeFrom, activeUntil), suspended ones included, from the version of its persistent domain tree effective at termStart(t), so registry churn between term start and landing changes no assignment (invariant I1; round 4, R4H-1), and every input that changes E_t or a rank (entry and exit, the only two) is DELAY_REG-dated while a suspension of any kind changes neither, a suspended seat being a void view or an empty committee slot when drawn (round 4, R4-C2; round 5, R5S-2: self-submitted evidence can suspend an owner 22 minutes later, so round 4's premise that such a suspension could not be owner-triggered was false, and the rule now holds because such a suspension moves no rank); and the committee walk reads each drawn owner's attester key valid at termStart(t), from a per-key validity interval, so a key rotation that takes effect between term start and landing, up to the 34-hour role horizon later, changes neither the committee root nor any holder check (round 6, R6S-4). Validity rules read only signed objects and L1 blocks at least 48 s old, expressing disagreement as pending, never invalid. Takeover is decided by the existence of a view change, a signed object; a node's clock only decides whether it signs a timeout. Dead mode is the single rule that reads current L1 state, only adds rights, and is arbitrated by the landing contract. proven (Sequencing claims 1, 2; Certificates claim 1).
