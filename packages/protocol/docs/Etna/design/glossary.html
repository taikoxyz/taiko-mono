Glossary

Canonical names used across the design. Where the source syntheses used different words for one thing, the alias is listed so the notes remain readable.

TermMeaningAliases avoided
TermA 60-second sequencing interval on the grid termStart(t) = T0 + 60t, with T0 set by the DAO's armEtna(T0) after initEtna. All rights and committees are per term.epoch, slot
Cycle60 terms; the unit for which one randomness seed is fixed.
ViewThe position within a term after v takeovers: view 0 is the primary holder, view 1 to 4 the backups. Sentinels: 254 forced batch, 255 open-empty, dead mode and no-committee mode.rank
Holder / leaderThe owner entitled to sequence in (term, view); "leader(t, v)" and "holder(t, v)" are the same function.operator, preconfer, proposer
OwnerThe registered identity that holds seats and bond; the slashable party. May be a contract.
SeatOne unit of bond (B_SEAT) and one registry slot; the unit of sortition weight and of committee sampling.
Sequencer key / attester keyThe owner's code-less secp256k1 key that signs headers, and its BLS12-381 key that signs attestations, timeouts, lock votes, redraw votes and view changes. Each key has a validity interval (validFrom, validUntil), and a term's committee walk reads the attester key valid at termStart(t); at registration the sequencer key signs keccak(DOMAIN_KEYREG ‖ chainId ‖ owner) as proof of control, so nobody can bind another party's address (round 6: R6S-4 for the key history, the judge's note on registerKeys for the proof).
Snapshot S(t)The highest L1 block with timestamp ≤ termStart(t) − LOOKBACK; the L1 state from which term t is computed.
Rule DEvery registry write takes effect only after a delay ≥ LOOKBACK + 2·TERM (DELAY_REG for entry and exit, the only inputs that change E_t or a rank; DELAY_S for class A or B suspensions and, since round 6, reserve lapses (R6S-2), which change no rank: a suspended seat stays in the domain and is a void view or an empty committee slot when drawn, round 5, R5S-2), so a term's rank domain is fixed at its snapshot and read at landing from the domain-tree version effective at its start.future-dated effect
SeedThe per-cycle randomness derived from an accumulator of block.prevrandao values mixed by inbox transactions.
Hash walkThe deterministic sequence of eligible ranks keccak(seed ‖ 0x01 ‖ t ‖ k) mod E_t from which holders (and, with domain 0x02, committees) are drawn; rank r names the r-th seat in the domain at termStart(t) in seat-id order (suspension is read after the draw, round 5, R5S-2).sortition
Eligible rankA seat's position, in seat-id order, among the E_t seats in the domain at termStart(t) (registered and inside [activeFrom, activeUntil); suspension is read after the draw, round 5). The contract answers "which seat has rank r at term t" from the version of its persistent domain tree effective at termStart(t); seat ids never move (round 4, R4H-1; a versioned tree since the round-5 fix pass).eligible list, active set, list position
Domain treeA persistent (versioned) segment tree over seat ids counting the seats in the domain. Every entry or exit writes a new version by path copying, tagged with its effective time τ + DELAY_REG, which is monotone in transaction order; term t reads the latest version effective at termStart(t), so later writes change nothing about it and a walk's gas does not depend on them. It replaced round 4's Fenwick tree and mutation ring (round 5 fix pass).mutation ring, Fenwick tree
SuspensionA per-owner flag (class A or B evidence and, since round 6, a reserve lapse, DELAY_S-dated; the hard floor, DELAY_REG-dated) read only after a seat is drawn: the owner's seats stay in the rank domain, a view drawn on them is void and a committee slot drawn on them is empty. It changes no E_t and no rank, so no owner write, self-submitted evidence included, changes a committee after the seed is fixed (round 5, R5S-2). An owner continuously suspended or lapsed for longer than STALE_EXIT = 7 days without reactivating can have its seats exited by anyone with exitStale(owner, seats), effective τ + DELAY_REG like exitSlashed (round 6, R6-C1).ineligibility
Pin / PIN_REWARDWriting a term's assignment digest and committee root on L1, by recordAssignment(t) or by the first landing that settles the term; the first pinner is paid PIN_REWARD from the term holder's reserve, a ramp from 0 at landableFrom(t) to PIN_MAX = 400 TAIKO over LAND_WINDOW, which pays a pin at the launch registry (about 0.8 M gas) in full up to about 75 gwei (round 5, R5H-2, sized in the fix pass), part of the per-term exposure; a recordAssignment pin is paid only for a term at or below the first unlanded term (round 6, R6H-2).settle
CommitteeUp to 32 seats drawn per term from the seats in the domain at termStart(t) (excluding the primary holder by owner and the backups by seat) whose attestations certify blocks; a drawn seat whose owner is suspended is an empty slot (m shrinks by one, no re-draw), so m falls with the suspended share of the domain and the 12 provable double-signers behind a locked block hold at a full committee (round 6, R6-C1); quorum Q(m) = ⌊2m/3⌋ + 1 of its actual size m (22 of 32). May be redrawn up to twice per view (see Redraw).attester set
RedrawA vote REDRAW(t, v, vcHash, n), n ≤ REDRAW_MAX = 2, by attesters that attested a block of the view but saw no certificate for TIMEOUT; ⌊m/2⌋ + 1 of them redraw the committee for the same leader, view and opening object, and the redrawn committee attests the view's blocks it received within STALE_MAX of their timestamps (the staleness bound is measured at receipt, round 5 fix pass). It has no conflicting form, no slashing rule and no penalty; the redraw count enters every signed message and equivocation key of the view.stall vote
Signed header (PH)The sequencer-signed header of a block; phHash is its hash and the object attestations sign. Its blockHash commits to the whole L2 header, including parentBeaconBlockRoot (the anchor hash) and extraData, so PH binds the anchor and the block kind; vcHash and the carried certificate live in PH and in the blob record, not in the block.envelope hash
AttestationOne attester's BLS signature over (term, view, opening object, redraw count, height, phHash).
Certificate C(h)An aggregate of ≥ Q attestations over one block; about 100 bytes; carried by the block one or two heights above.quorum cert
Carried certificateThe certificate a block includes for its parent or grandparent (lag ≤ 1).parent certificate
LockAn attester's highest certified block whose chain it holds; monotone within a term; reset only by a landed replacement or forced batch.cursor
TimeoutAn attester's signed statement that a view should close, carrying no lock. Kind TIMEOUT is signed only when, for TIMEOUT seconds, the attester saw no new certificate of (t, v) and holds no block of the view that it attested and that is still uncertified (the leader liveness signal, read from the attester's own attestations since the round-5 fix pass: a producing leader is never timed out, a non-serving one always is); the window starts when the attester first holds the view's opening object (for view 0, at termStart(t) once it holds term t − 1's closing VC) and restarts on each new certificate, so a leader that publishes nothing is timed out too (round 6, R6H-4); kind TERM_END at term end; kind FALLBACK by a later committee, gated by L1 time (accepted from termEnd(t) + END_GRACE + VC_FALLBACK × (t' − t), only while no live VC of committee(t) is recorded, landed only with a certificate at its lock, round 5, R5S-5, and refused, or dead once recorded, below a certificate of committee(t) recorded for the view, round 6, R6S-3); kinds REPLACE and RESUME after a reset (certificate page §6). It carries the redraw count like every attester message.TO
View change VC(t, v)Q lock votes aggregated over one lock (timeouts carry no lock), naming the opening object it closes; closes view v, opens the next, and is the seal of the view's batch. Kinds: TIMEOUT, TERM_END, FALLBACK, REPLACE, RESUME.takeover certificate, TC, timeout certificate, seal
Lock voteAn attester's signed vote for where a view's batch ends (lock height and hash, plus the signed L1 reference for REPLACE and RESUME: the L1 block at which A1 held for REPLACE, the L1 block containing the forced or replacement landing resumed from for RESUME, certificate page §1); monotone per key; no lock is exempt from the lock-lie rule S3b, already-landed locks included (round 5, R5S-1); Q of them over one lock form the view change. Distinct from the timeout, which carries no lock.seal vote
Landing reserveLAND_RESERVE = 14,400 TAIKO per seat (two terms of reward exposure, 2 × 7,161 = 14,322, the exposure counting five paid landings, round 6, R6H-3) an owner keeps on the ledger above its seat stake and buffer; only reward-type payments (ramp, blob reward, per-landing reward, attester share, pin reward) are debited from it, never MISS; a reward credit to an owner with seats refills that owner's reserve first, and a self-landing skips the lander's debit and credit, so the honest path does not drain it (the attester share it still pays is offset in expectation by its own attester income) (round 5, R5S-4); it is not withdrawable while any seat is active or pending and leaves only through exit plus the evidence window, and an owner that launders it through a seatless rewardTo cannot take it below one term per seat without a lapse (round 6, R6S-2).reward pool
LapseThe suspension of an owner whose landing reserve is below seats × 7,161 TAIKO, one term of exposure per seat (round 6, R6S-2, replacing round 5's absolute line): DELAY_S-dated, since like every suspension it changes no rank and no E_t; at least SUSPEND(3) long, in the two-interval store; ended only by a top-up to seats × LAND_RESERVE plus reactivate(). Checked at every draw, so every draw an owner keeps is funded by at least one term per seat.reserve lapse, ineligibility
ReserveLowThe event the inbox emits once per crossing of an owner's reserve below (seats + 1) × 7,161 TAIKO, one term above the lapse line (14,322 for one seat, 64,449 for eight), re-armed only when the reserve is back at seats × 14,400; the client tops up to that level on it, at most one term per seat (round 5, R5S-4; round 6, R6S-2).
Certificate recordrecordCertificate(C, cp): anyone records on L1 a certificate of committee(t) for a view (t, v, vcHash) with its committee proof (one 32-key aggregate verification, ≈ 165k gas, assumed, plus the committee walk, about 1 M gas at 128 seats and 5.4 M at 4,096, when the term is not yet pinned, since recordCertificate pins the term first if needed), and the inbox keeps the highest (height, phHash); a FALLBACK VC whose lock is below it is refused, and one recorded earlier is dead. When no TERM_END VC has formed by termEnd(t) + END_GRACE + 0.5 s, the view's leader and the member whose bit is the lowest set bit of the highest certificate's bitmap of the view record the view's highest certificate, and every other member only after one L1 block without a record, so a withheld term normally costs one or two records, each 165k when the term is already pinned and the walk more when it is not (round 6, R6S-3; round 6 fix pass).
Blank termA term whose certification an abstaining committee minority withholds: no certificates, the leader keeps its view, nothing of it lands, nobody is penalized and the term is not transferred; the residual of limitation L2.stalled term
Range / segmentA contiguous run of certified blocks that a landing covers, spanning up to four consecutive views; each view's part is a segment. Lander-chosen, at most 256 blocks and 6 blobs.batch
LandingThe single L1 action land that carries a range's blobs and ZK proof and finalizes it; the propose-with-proof primitive.propose, prove, batch submission
JournalThe ZK proof's public input structure; its hash is what the verifier checks.commitment, public input
Canonical blob encoding (E1)The deterministic byte layout of a range's blobs, a pure function of the certified blocks, making proofs fungible.
Lander / proverAnyone who submits a landing; anyone who produces a proof. No registration.proposer, designated prover
Reward rampThe per-block lander reward rising linearly from R_BLK_MIN at landableFrom(t) = max(termEnd(t), lastLanded.at) to R_BLK_MAX after LAND_WINDOW, lastLanded.at being the time of the landing that the first range reaching t builds on (then fixed in t's term record), so no term is charged for time in which nothing could land (round 6, R6H-2); the per-landing reward and the pin reward ramp from the same origin; a Dutch auction with the holder as reserve bidder.
DeadlinetermEnd + LAND_WINDOW, the economic deadline at which the reward ramp of a term landable from its end reaches its maximum; replaceableFrom (landing page A1, with REPLACE_GRACE) is the only later instant with an effect, and it opens replacement, never a penalty.proving window, hard deadline
ReplacementA landing accepted after replaceableFrom that starts a new view from the last landed block, voiding certified but unlanded descendants once it lands; until then landing the original stays valid and whichever lands first wins. The abandonment penalty, outage gate and void waiver of rounds 2 and 3 were removed in round 4 (R4H-5, R4-C3): certified implies landable by anyone, and the reserve pays for it.forced reorg
Single-proof modeAfter two hours without a landing, one ZK system suffices for certified ranges until the next landing; such a landing is provisional (see Provisional landing; after the #22188 review, 2026-10-01).degraded mode
Provisional landingA landing accepted with one ZK leaf in single-proof mode (after the #22188 review, 2026-10-01): it advances lastLanded, pays rewards and runs every deadline, but its L1 checkpoint is written only when a proof of the same journal from a different leaf confirms it (confirmProvisional) or DEGRADED_FINALITY = 24 h pass with no conflict (finalizeProvisional), in order behind every earlier provisional record; the record (the start it extended with the forced-inclusion head at that point, end height, hash, state root, journal hash, leaf, time) lives in a ring of 12, and finalization writes the start of the oldest record still unfinalized as the checkpoint. A proof conflict is a valid proof from a different leaf over the same inputs with a different end state: the inbox enters CONFLICT, no landing or checkpoint write proceeds until a DAO upgrade names the sound leaf, and the provisional landing and everything built on it may be voided: lastLanded and the forced-inclusion head return to the start of the oldest unfinalized record, at most 24 h of landings, whose consumed entries are due again. Labelled "landed (provisional)" (landing page §7).degraded landing, soft landing
Open-empty termA term in which the walk finds no seat in the domain, or every holder view it draws is void (every drawn owner suspended or lapsed), treated exactly alike (round 6, R6H-2); any bonded address may sequence with view 255.open term
Dead modeAfter 75 unlanded terms, any bonded address may sequence from the last landed block and land without a certificate; first landing wins.
No-committee modeA term whose committee has fewer than K_MIN_CERT = 8 non-empty slots (m < 8: the committee walk's seats, less those whose owners are suspended or lapsed at termStart(t)): sortition holders, no certificates, certificate-free landings, sequenced level only. Between 8 and 32 the quorum scales with the committee size.committee-degraded mode
Forced inclusion (FI)A user request (blob or calldata) that must occupy one L2 block, of kind FI (or FORCED inside a forced batch), once due at an anchored L1 time. The block's canonical record in a landing carries no payload (about 250 bytes: header core and signature, the request's own blob or calldata being the payload witness), so an FI block adds no blob to any landing, is exempt from V10's byte cap and earns no lander reward of its own (round 6 fix pass).
Forced batchA certificate-free landing of exactly the due FI entries by anyone, with view 254 and blocks of kind FORCED, once replacement is permitted.permissionless batch
Empty FI blockThe FI block with no transactions that an undecodable payload or a void entry (expired, or voided by a stall) yields; its anchor, coinbase and gas limit follow the forced-inclusion page F3 like any FI block.
CursorPer block, the index of the next unconsumed FI entry. It is derived, never stored: after a landing it is fiQueue.head, and a block of kind FI or FORCED advances its parent's cursor by one (anchor-free blocks, issue #22147); the stall clock is derived alongside it (see FI_SKIP).fiIndex
AnchorThe L1 block an L2 block is anchored to: its hash is the L2 header's parentBeaconBlockRoot and its number is PH.anchorNumber, checked by V5. No transaction carries it (anchor-free blocks, issue #22147). The Anchor contract (proxy 0x1670…10001) remains as the L2 fee treasury.
Block kindThe last byte of the 7-byte extraData, basefeeSharingPctg(1) ‖ termId(4) ‖ view(1) ‖ kind(1): 0 NORMAL (a sequencer block), 1 FORCED (a forced-batch block, view 254), 2 GENESIS_CHILD (the first Etna block), 3 FI (a forced-inclusion block). Header rules H1 to H3 check it against PH.
L2 beacon-roots contractL2's EIP-4788 contract at 0x000F3df6D732807Ef1319fB7B8bB8522d0Beac02. Its standard pre-execution call stores each L2 block's parentBeaconBlockRoot, which on L2 is an L1 execution block hash, keyed by L2 timestamp and readable for 8,191 s. Distinct from L1's EIP-4788 contract, whose beacon roots Etna never reads.
RevealrevealCheckpoint(l2Timestamp, headerRlp) on the L2 SignalService: anyone presents the RLP of an L1 header (at most 1,536 bytes) whose keccak L2's EIP-4788 contract recorded for that L2 timestamp, or, with timestamp 0, that a pin holds, and the L1 checkpoint it decodes is stored (idempotent; a conflicting one reverts). By client policy a sequencer reveals each new anchor; relayers reveal before claiming.
Origin pinpinCurrentOrigin() on the L2 SignalService: anyone stores the current block's anchor hash permanently (one bool, never cleared), so it stays revealable after its ring slot is reused; a pin queued through forced inclusion has no deadline (adopted from the Astra candidate, PR #22188). Distinct from the L1 assignment pin above.pin (bare), L2 pin
Current-root guardThe rule that the Anchor's legacy anchorV4 and the L2 saveCheckpoint run only when L2's EIP-4788 contract returns a zero root for the current block (a Shasta header), and that reveals and pins run only when it returns a non-zero one (an Etna header); it closes the legacy writers from the first Etna block and reopens them under a rollback, with no fork time stored in a contract (adopted from the Astra candidate, PR #22188).fork-time gate
Checkpoint{blockNumber, blockHash, stateRoot} of the other chain, held by a SignalService (unchanged interface): written into the L1 SignalService by the inbox once per landing accepted with two leaves unless an unfinalized provisional record precedes it on its chain (then the landing writes none and is subsumed by the one checkpoint written when the newest record it follows is finalized), and once per finalization of a provisional record, once per finalization pass (at a confirmation or after 24 h, once every earlier record is final), writing the start of the oldest record still unfinalized or lastLanded (landing page §7; after the #22188 review, 2026-10-01), and into the L2 SignalService by a reveal (anchor-free blocks, issue #22147).
Shared contractsThe L1 and L2 SignalService, the Bridge, the ERC20, ERC721 and ERC1155 Vaults and the quota manager: addresses and storage unchanged (R2); implementations upgraded in place before T0, bridged through the DelegateController on L2 and independent of the fork, to remove pause and unpause and every owner-only operational function (changeBridgedToken, the quota setters), so that no address other than the DAO can stop bridging, remap a token or mint a bridged token; the DAO keeps two DAO-only entry points that are not upgrades, DefaultResolver.registerAddress and the owner branch of BridgedERC20 mint and burn, each as slow as an upgrade (limitation L-OP); the price is the loss of an emergency brake (limitation L-OP; after the #22188 review, 2026-10-01, A5 revised).frozen-bytecode contracts
Anchor tipThe newest L1 block a landing's range anchors; the inbox checks it against L1's blockhash or EIP-2935 and the guest chains every anchored header to it.origin block
Confirmation levelssequenced (signed, ~0.3 s) → attested (certificate, ~0.7 s) → locked (certificate carried, ~1.7 s) → landed (on L1 with proof; "landed (provisional)" for a one-leaf landing of single-proof mode, whose checkpoint is written after a second-leaf confirmation or 24 h, and for any landing whose chain holds an unfinalized provisional record, which is subsumed by that record's finalization checkpoint; both revertible by a DAO resolution of a proof conflict) → L1-safe (one L1 descendant) → final (L1-finalized). Landing announcementannounceLanding(first, end) with a TAIKO ledger bond of ANNOUNCE_BOND = 2,000: defers the forced path by LAND_CHAIN_GRACE once per range, a single deferral shared with a recorded REPLACE view change (whichever comes first; the forced-inclusion bound stays 112 min, round 5, R5-C3); forfeited (40 % to the eventual forced or replacement lander, 60 % burned) if no covering landing follows; refused once the head forced-inclusion entry has been due for longer than LAND_WINDOW. Opening objectWhat a view's first block carries in vcHash: the view change (or, for sentinel views 254 and 255, keccak("ETNA_DEAD" ‖ lastLanded.blockHash ‖ t)) whose hash, vcHash, opens a view; carried, with the redraw count, in every header, attestation, timeout, lock vote and certificate of the view and in every equivocation key.view id
StrikeOne unit of the structural-offence ladder (class B only): one offence adds one strike, three strikes in a week suspend an owner (round 5, R5S-6), and the suspension changes no rank. Timeouts never strike.decay

Gate contract (G_LAND)The shared-nonce Frame-Transaction sender for the public-mempool landing shape; deployed only once EIP-8141 is Final, an immutable address in the inbox that only a DAO upgrade changes, beside the direct land that sits behind no switch. It pins the selector and word 0 (_rewardTo) of landFor and bounds the calldata to [68, LAND_CALLDATA_MAX] bytes (assumed 8,192); its race is free only between same-nonce losers, since a stale copy whose parent moved still pays the early revert (after the #22188 review, 2026-10-01).
Trailing status VERIFY (A7)The Frame-Transaction shape in which a frame after the action checks its success, making any reverting landing invalid and free.
Golden touchShasta's system account with a public key, the only sender its Anchor accepts; Etna gives it no role. The current-root guard makes the Anchor's legacy entry point and the L2 saveCheckpoint revert in every block whose header records a non-zero root, that is every Etna block, and the clients drop its carve-outs at the fork, so after T0 a golden-touch transaction is an ordinary, fee-paying one that reaches nothing privileged.
Abandonment (Shasta tail)abandonShastaTail(Proposal[]): once T0 + DRAIN_DEADLINE (24 h) has passed with proposals still unproven, anyone presents the unproven proposals in order; the inbox checks each against its ring buffer, voids them (their ordinary content is lost, announced in advance), moves the forced-inclusion head back by the number of forced sources they consumed and sets those entries' fee to zero (the Shasta proposer was paid at proposal time; a leak of at most 10 × fee per abandoned proposal), so no forced request accepted by Shasta is discarded by the migration except by the stall rule; each call starts at lastFinalizedProposalId + 1 and moves that pointer past the ids it processed, so no entry is requeued twice and no abandoned proposal is also finalized; the requeued entries are due at the fork (after the #22188 review, 2026-10-01).drain void, tail void
Landing horizonThe time after which an unlanded range can no longer be landed: the smaller of ROLE_HORIZON (34 h, the role horizon, for which roles stay pinned) and L1's EIP-2935 window (27 h at 12-s slots, 4.55 h at 2-s slots), so a different quantity from ROLE_HORIZON. The one time-based void.
FI_SKIP / stallThe four-hour stall rule, the one clock rule in forced inclusion. The stall clock fiClearTip is the latest parent-anchor time at which a block's cursor had no due entry, or the cut of a stall whose void set is used up, derived per block like the cursor, stored at each landing and set to the drain block's timestamp at the fork; once a block's parent anchor reaches the cut fiClearTip + FI_SKIP, every entry due at the cut is void (the empty FI block), up to 64 per landing, the next landings continuing, and the clock restarts at the cut once that set is used up, so entries due later are served with content. A void-by-stall landing caps L8's T_floor at the cut, so it is always satisfiable. The first entry a stall voids loses its bond and whole fee, the others are refunded. The fee floor is multiplied by 4level + inProgress: the escalation level rises by one per stall and falls by one per ESCALATION_DECAY = 7 days since the last stall, and inProgress is 1 while a stall is in progress at save time; the multiplied part, the surcharge, is refunded to every entry but a stall's first void, so no honest request pays it. With stalls less than a week apart the k-th costs at least 0.05 + 0.001 × 4k−1 ETH; a week or more apart each returns to the floor price (round 6, R6H-1; round 1, F4; round 3, H1).skip, head skip
Pull ledger / per-entry refundThe two ways ETH leaves the forced-inclusion queue, both pulled, never pushed (round 6, R6H-1; round 6 fix pass). The ETH pull ledger has one slot per includer (the range's sequencer or a forced batch's rewardTo) and holds the fees of the entries it consumed less each entry's surcharge, withdrawn with withdrawFiFees; whatever a requester paid above the required price FI_FEE × 4e stays in it as the includer's bounty, undiluted. A requester's refunds are not ledger entries: the consuming landing or forced batch writes the entry's status (consumed with content, voided and refundable, burned; claimed after withdrawal) into two bits of the kind byte of the entry word it already read, and the requester withdraws per entry with withdrawFiRefund(id): the bond plus the surcharge FI_FEE × (4e − 1) for an entry consumed with content or expired, the bond plus the whole fee for an entry a stall voids behind the first, nothing for a burned or legacy entry. So a landing that consumes 64 entries of 64 fresh requesters writes no fresh slot (a per-requester ledger would have written 64 at ≈ 98k state gas each, ≈ 6.3 M), and a requester whose address reverts blocks nothing.claimable balance, refund ledger
