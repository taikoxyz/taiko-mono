Glossary

Canonical names used across the design. Where the source syntheses used different words for one thing, the alias is listed so the notes remain readable.

TermMeaningAliases avoided
TermA 60-second sequencing interval on the grid termStart(t) = T0 + 60t, with T0 set by the DAO's armEtna(T0) after initEtna. All rights and committees are per term.epoch, slot
Cycle60 terms; the unit for which one randomness seed is fixed.
ViewThe position within a term after v takeovers: view 0 is the primary holder, view 1 to 4 the backups. Sentinels: 254 forced batch, 255 open-empty, dead mode and no-committee mode.rank
Holder / leaderThe owner entitled to sequence in (term, view); "leader(t, v)" and "holder(t, v)" are the same function.operator, preconfer, proposer
OwnerThe registered identity that holds seats and bond; the slashable party. May be a contract.
SeatOne unit of bond (B_SEAT) and one registry slot; the unit of sortition weight and of committee sampling.
Sequencer key / attester keyThe owner's code-less secp256k1 key that signs headers, and its BLS12-381 key that signs attestations, timeouts, lock votes, redraw votes and view changes.
Snapshot S(t)The highest L1 block with timestamp ≤ termStart(t) − LOOKBACK; the L1 state from which term t is computed.
Rule DEvery registry write takes effect only after a delay ≥ LOOKBACK + 2·TERM (DELAY_REG for every owner-triggerable input, DELAY_S only for evidence-driven suspensions), so a term's eligible set is fixed at its snapshot; the mutation ring restores it at landing.future-dated effect
SeedThe per-cycle randomness derived from an accumulator of block.prevrandao values mixed by inbox transactions.
Hash walkThe deterministic sequence of eligible ranks keccak(seed ‖ 0x01 ‖ t ‖ k) mod E_t from which holders (and, with domain 0x02, committees) are drawn; rank r names the r-th seat eligible at termStart(t) in seat-id order.sortition
Eligible rankA seat's position, in seat-id order, among the E_t seats eligible at termStart(t). The contract answers "which seat has rank r at term t" from a Fenwick prefix-count tree over the seats eligible now, corrected by the mutation ring; seat ids never move (round 4, R4H-1).eligible list, active set, list position
Mutation ringThe log of (effectiveTime, seatId, +1/−1) eligibility changes inside ROLE_HORIZON, applied to the tree lazily in (effectiveTime, seatId) order by the first call after the effective time and before that call computes any right; entries after termStart(t) are undone when term t is evaluated.
CommitteeUp to 32 seats drawn per term from the seats eligible at termStart(t) (excluding the primary holder by owner and the backups by seat) whose attestations certify blocks; quorum Q(m) = ⌊2m/3⌋ + 1 of its actual size m (22 of 32). May be redrawn up to twice per view (see Redraw).attester set
RedrawA vote REDRAW(t, v, vcHash, n), n ≤ REDRAW_MAX = 2, by attesters that attested a block of the view but saw no certificate for TIMEOUT; ⌊m/2⌋ + 1 of them redraw the committee for the same leader, view and opening object. It has no conflicting form, no slashing rule and no penalty; the redraw count enters every signed message and equivocation key of the view.stall vote
Signed header (PH)The sequencer-signed header of a block; phHash is its hash and the object attestations sign.envelope hash
AttestationOne attester's BLS signature over (term, view, opening object, redraw count, height, phHash).
Certificate C(h)An aggregate of ≥ Q attestations over one block; about 100 bytes; carried by the block one or two heights above.quorum cert
Carried certificateThe certificate a block includes for its parent or grandparent (lag ≤ 1).parent certificate
LockAn attester's highest certified block whose chain it holds; monotone within a term; reset only by a landed replacement or forced batch.cursor
TimeoutAn attester's signed statement that a view should close, carrying no lock. Kind TIMEOUT is signed only when, for TIMEOUT seconds, the attester received no header of (t, v) with a valid leader signature above its lock and no new certificate (the leader liveness signal; a producing leader is never timed out); kind TERM_END at term end; kind FALLBACK by a later committee; kinds REPLACE and RESUME after a reset (certificate page §6).TO
View change VC(t, v)Q lock votes aggregated over one lock (timeouts carry no lock), naming the opening object it closes; closes view v, opens the next, and is the seal of the view's batch. Kinds: TIMEOUT, TERM_END, FALLBACK, REPLACE, RESUME.takeover certificate, TC, timeout certificate, seal
Lock voteAn attester's signed vote for where a view's batch ends (lock height and hash, plus the signed L1 reference for REPLACE and RESUME); monotone per key; Q of them over one lock form the view change. Distinct from the timeout, which carries no lock.seal vote
Landing reserveLAND_RESERVE = 8,400 TAIKO per seat (two terms of reward exposure) an owner keeps on the ledger above its seat stake and buffer; only reward-type payments (ramp, blob reward, per-landing reward, attester share) are debited from it, never MISS; it is not withdrawable while any seat is active or pending and leaves only through exit plus the evidence window; a reserve below one term's exposure makes the owner ineligible with a DELAY_REG-dated effect for at least SUSPEND(3).reward pool
Blank termA term whose certification an abstaining committee minority withholds: no certificates, the leader keeps its view, nothing of it lands, nobody is penalized and the term is not transferred; the residual of limitation L2.stalled term
Opening objectWhat a view's first block carries in vcHash: a view change, a dead-mode marker, or a forced marker.
Range / segmentA contiguous run of certified blocks that a landing covers, spanning up to four consecutive views; each view's part is a segment. Lander-chosen, at most 256 blocks and 6 blobs.batch
LandingThe single L1 action land that carries a range's blobs and ZK proof and finalizes it; the propose-with-proof primitive.propose, prove, batch submission
JournalThe ZK proof's public input structure; its hash is what the verifier checks.commitment, public input
Canonical blob encoding (E1)The deterministic byte layout of a range's blobs, a pure function of the certified blocks, making proofs fungible.
Lander / proverAnyone who submits a landing; anyone who produces a proof. No registration.proposer, designated prover
Reward rampThe per-block lander reward rising linearly from R_BLK_MIN at term end to R_BLK_MAX after LAND_WINDOW; a Dutch auction with the holder as reserve bidder.
DeadlinetermEnd + LAND_WINDOW, the economic deadline at which the reward ramp reaches its maximum; replaceableFrom (landing page A1, with REPLACE_GRACE) is the only later instant with an effect, and it opens replacement, never a penalty.proving window, hard deadline
ReplacementA landing accepted after replaceableFrom that starts a new view from the last landed block, voiding certified but unlanded descendants once it lands; until then landing the original stays valid and whichever lands first wins. The abandonment penalty, outage gate and void waiver of rounds 2 and 3 were removed in round 4 (R4H-5, R4-C3): certified implies landable by anyone, and the reserve pays for it.forced reorg
Single-proof modeAfter two hours without a landing, one ZK system suffices for certified ranges until the next landing.degraded mode
Open-empty termA term in which the walk finds no eligible seat; any bonded address may sequence with view 255.open term
Dead modeAfter 75 unlanded terms, any bonded address may sequence from the last landed block and land without a certificate; first landing wins.
No-committee modeA term whose committee walk finds fewer than 8 eligible non-holder seats: sortition holders, no certificates, certificate-free landings, sequenced level only. Between 8 and 32 the quorum scales with the committee size.committee-degraded mode
Forced inclusion (FI)A user request (blob or calldata) that must occupy one L2 block once due at an anchored L1 time.
Forced batchA certificate-free landing of exactly the due FI entries by anyone, with view 254, once replacement is permitted.permissionless batch
CursorPer block, the index of the next unconsumed FI entry; carried in the anchor calldata.fiIndex
Anchor (anchorV5)The golden-touch first transaction of every block carrying the L1 checkpoint and the block's self-description.
Checkpoint{blockNumber, blockHash, stateRoot} of the other chain, written into a SignalService by its authorized syncer (unchanged interface).
Anchor tipThe newest L1 block a landing's range anchors; the inbox checks it against blockhash or EIP-2935 and the guest chains every anchored header to it.origin block
Confirmation levelssequenced (signed, ~0.3 s) → attested (certificate, ~0.7 s) → locked (certificate carried, ~1.7 s) → landed (on L1 with proof) → L1-safe (one L1 descendant) → final (L1-finalized).safe, unsafe, finalized
Landing announcementannounceLanding(first, end) with a TAIKO ledger bond of ANNOUNCE_BOND = 2,000: defers the replaceable window by LAND_CHAIN_GRACE once per range; forfeited (40 % to the eventual forced or replacement lander, 60 % burned) if no covering landing follows; refused once the head forced-inclusion entry has been due for longer than LAND_WINDOW. A recorded REPLACE view change, accepted only at the landed lock after replaceableFrom, defers the forced path once in the same way; it is dead (ignored by the first-recorded rule, no priority) once lastLanded has moved above its lock, so it never blocks the original view's own closing view change.landing intent
Opening objectThe view change (or, for sentinel views, keccak("ETNA_DEAD" ‖ lastLanded.blockHash ‖ t)) whose hash, vcHash, opens a view; carried, with the redraw count, in every header, attestation, timeout, lock vote and certificate of the view and in every equivocation key.view id
StrikeOne unit of the structural-offence ladder (class B only); three strikes in a week suspend an owner. Timeouts never strike.decay
Class A / B / C offenceSafety (whole ledger), structural (one seat), liveness (MISS only: 50 TAIKO burned, from the buffer then seat stake, never the reserve).
MIN_TENURE7 days: requestExit sets activeUntil ≥ activeFrom + MIN_TENURE, so every seat is drawn and bled for at least a week and every entry in the mutation ring is paid for.
Gate contract (G_LAND)The shared-nonce Frame-Transaction sender for the public-mempool landing shape; deployed only once EIP-8141 is Final.
Trailing status VERIFY (A7)The Frame-Transaction shape in which a frame after the action checks its success, making any reverting landing invalid and free.
Golden touchThe public-key system account that signs every anchor transaction with a fixed nonce, as today.
Landing horizonThe time after which an unlanded range can no longer be landed: 34 h (roles pinned) or the EIP-2935 window if shorter. The one time-based void.
FI_SKIPThe four-hour rule that voids a due head forced-inclusion entry no landing has consumed; the one clock rule in forced inclusion.
Published constantsprotocolParams() and its hash, the single source of every guest-visible constant (TH25).
Etna: a fully permissionless based-rollup protocol (design research, not implementation). Every claim is tagged proven assumed open. Source documents: README, 00, 01, 02, 03. Learning site: learn/.
