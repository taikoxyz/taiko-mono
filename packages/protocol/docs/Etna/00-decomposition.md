## 4. What is explicitly dropped and why

- **The Dutch admission rent, the one-blob staging segment and the mandatory public-data staging gate of candidate B.** Dropped for cost, not for an absolute ceiling: the one-blob cap is per segment, so faster sealing raises throughput, but at 67.2 ETH per day at a 60-s cadence and 283.2 ETH per day at 15 s (B's own figures, with about 137 B/s rent-free), as both agents and the comparison agreed. The staging gate goes with the proof-race layer that D1 does not select.
- **Candidate B's accountability registry** (collateral buckets, signed duty claims with evidence windows, `proveCanonicalConflict`). Dropped as a mechanism, because the committee layer carries its own slashing (S3); S3's review states which of its patterns (signed duty claims, evidence windows) are reused and which are not.
- **Bonded fragment publication at L1 calldata prices.** About 64 gas per byte makes every published byte cost what calldata costs; the merged design keeps availability through the committee's execution requirement and the retention duty, and records the residual (a failure to serve is unprovable on L1) as a limitation.
- **Pause and owner-only operational powers on the shared contracts.** Removed by compatible upgrades: the "Remove pause powers", "Remove owner mint/burn authority" and selector-audit rows of B's retained-surface table, adopted by A as README A10 (whose limitation L-OP is declared and, at `06be4421`, unwritten).
- **The anchor transaction.** B adopted taikoxyz/taiko-mono#22147 and committed it; A announced the adoption (README A9), uncommitted at `06be4421`.
