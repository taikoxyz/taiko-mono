Documentation Consistency Audit

Aspect 6 of the DeepSeek independent adversarial review of PR #22034 (v14 design). Review date 2026-08-21. Eleven files audited line-by-line against each other and against the deployed contracts and the model checker. Companions: index · soundness · attacks · auction sims · L2 vs L1 timestamps · economics · model checker.

Verdict: remarkably consistent for a 14-revision document. Every arithmetic claim checks out (77-min tail, 225 epochs/day, 7-slot ≈ 22%, 55+margin freshness floor, W_a+2 ≤ K, the 30 d vs 18 d retention infeasibility), every parameter value is stable across all eleven files, and the PR-number cross-references verify against GitHub. The inconsistencies that exist are the classic residue of a long revision chain: one invariant-vs-definition disagreement (the r12-5 tuple fix applied to only 1 of 6 sites), one genuinely stale artifact (the slides deck, correctly labeled v9 but with materially outdated content), two stale section numbers from the v13 renumbering, and an absent v1–v6 baseline that the doc leans on with "as v3" forward references.

6.4 The slides deck — correctly labeled, materially stale

STALE ARTIFACT (documented in README, still wrong)

The deck self-labels "v9" everywhere; README:16 documents that the proposal governs. But its content is a genuine v9 snapshot carrying six v10+ reversals — a reader of the deck learns the opposite of the current design on:

  • T_max framed as "the complete idleness/censorship bound" (slides 6:214, 20:608, 22:653) — v10 renamed it the tenure-renewal/re-pricing bound (r9-F1);
  • "No discretionary content" in Total Anarchy (slide 19:575) — reversed by v11 §9;
  • Bridge refund via "the bridge's unprocessed-message path" (slide 19:583) — superseded by the terminal-cancellation handshake (v10→v14);
  • Withdrawal gate "no in-flight verdicts" (slide 16:512) — superseded by the single challenge horizon (v12);
  • "Forgiveness never covers whoever drove the stall" (slide 18:561) — replaced by the data-established-epochs tolling rule (v10);
  • Verification numbers (slide 21): "8 rounds / 6.0 M states / 11/11 mutants / 3×4, 3×5" — now 24 mutants, default 2×6 ≈1.4 M states.

Re-syncing is tracked as §13-T.13; until then the deck misleads. (Removed mechanisms — H_force, the seal window, "awaits max-tenure decision" — are correctly absent; r8-W4's fixes hold.)
